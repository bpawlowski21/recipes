Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post (3 slides). A thinner, lighter peanut sauce, good for summer, with minimal cooking — the edamame cooking water is reused to soak the rice noodles and thin the peanut butter.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup frozen shelled edamame
- 2 nests vermicelli rice noodles
- 1 cup red cabbage
- 1 red bell pepper
- 2 medium carrots
- ½ English cucumber
- Bunch fresh cilantro

**Sauce**
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- ½ cup cooking water from the edamame
- 1 tbsp tahini
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp Tamari
- 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp garlic paste
- ¼ tsp ginger powder
- 1–2 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp maple syrup

**Toppings:** fresh cilantro, sesame seeds

## Directions

1. Boil the edamame for 5 to 7 minutes.
2. Add the noodles to a large bowl. When the edamame is done, strain 1/2 cup of the cooking water into a container and set aside. Strain the rest of the water over the noodles, cover, and let sit until the noodles soften. Drain.
3. Make the sauce: add the peanut butter and the reserved edamame water to a bowl and stir, continuing to stir as you add each remaining sauce ingredient.
4. In a large bowl, add the veggies, noodles, sauce, and cilantro. Toss well.

## Notes

- The saved poster used a spoonful of pre-made garlic paste (garlic cloves blended and stored in the fridge) in place of fresh garlic.
