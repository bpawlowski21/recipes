## Ingredients

- 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil, plus additional for drizzling
- 2 jars (12 ounce each) marinated artichokes, drained
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- Zest from 1 lemon
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves (or 1 teaspoon dried)
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- Kosher salt and black pepper to taste
- 1 pound long-cut pasta
- 3 tablespoons salted butter
- 3 tablespoons raw walnuts, roughly chopped
- 1 cup fresh basil, roughly chopped, plus more for serving
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 8 ounces burrata cheese, torn

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Combine olive oil, artichokes, garlic, lemon zest, thyme, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper in a baking dish. Roast 15-20 minutes until artichokes are crispy and golden.
2. Bring salted water to a boil in a large pot. Cook pasta per package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
3. In the same pot, melt butter and walnuts over high heat until butter browns and nuts toast, about 3-5 minutes. Remove walnuts and return drained pasta to pot.
4. Add basil and parmesan, tossing to combine. Thin with reserved pasta water as desired. Stir in roasted artichokes and their oils.
5. Divide among plates and top with burrata, oil drizzle, reserved walnuts, and fresh herbs.
