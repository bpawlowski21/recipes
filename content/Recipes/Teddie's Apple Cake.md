Source: Amanda Hesser and The New York Times. Handwritten tasting notes on the original scan. Original page:

![[Teddie's Apple Cake (handwritten).pdf]]

## Ingredients

- Butter, for greasing pan
- 3 cups (384 grams) flour, plus more for dusting pan
- 1½ cups (317 grams) vegetable oil
- 2 cups (400 grams) sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 3 cups peeled, cored and thickly sliced apples, such as Honeycrisp or Granny Smith*
- 1 cup chopped walnuts (omitted)
- 1 cup raisins (omitted)
- Vanilla ice cream, for serving, optional

*A mix of Fuji, York, and Cameo also work well — use whatever you have on hand or prefer to bake with.

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Butter and flour a 9-inch tube pan. Beat the oil and sugar together in a mixer (fitted with a paddle attachment) while assembling the remaining ingredients. After about 5 minutes, add the eggs and beat until the mixture is creamy.
2. Sift together 3 cups of flour, the salt, cinnamon and baking soda. Stir into the batter. Add the vanilla, apples, walnuts (if using) and raisins (if using) and stir until combined.
3. Transfer the mixture to the prepared pan. Bake for 1 hour and 15 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool in the pan before turning out. Serve at room temperature with vanilla ice cream, if desired.

## Notes

- Total time: 1 hour 30 minutes. Yield: 8-10 servings.
- Printed ingredient list already notes walnuts and raisins as omitted ("I omit") by the transcriber of this print.
- Handwritten tasting notes added to the scan: "so good! Stays crispy for days!" and "Made for Rosh 2024" (i.e., Rosh Hashanah) — a confirmed successful bake, kept for reference alongside the recipe.
