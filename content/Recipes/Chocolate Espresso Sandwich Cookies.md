## Ingredients

**For the cookies:**
- 1 1/2 cups plus 3 tablespoons flour
- 3/4 cup plus 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon instant espresso powder
- 15 tablespoons (1 7/8 sticks) butter

**For the filling:**
- 8 ounces white chocolate
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/4 teaspoon instant espresso powder

## Directions

- Heat cream to a boil in a small saucepan over medium heat. Remove from heat, add white chocolate and espresso powder. Let sit 1 minute, then whisk until melted. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes until thickened.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Combine all dry cookie ingredients in a large bowl. Cut butter into cubes and add to mixer in batches until dough forms.
- Roll dough to 1/8-inch thickness on floured surface. Cut into two-inch rounds with cookie cutter. Place on baking sheet and reroll scraps.
- Bake 12-15 minutes. Cool completely on rack.
- Place half the cookies upside down. Whisk filling to lighten, then dollop 1 teaspoon onto each cookie's center. Top with remaining cookies and press gently until filling reaches edges.
