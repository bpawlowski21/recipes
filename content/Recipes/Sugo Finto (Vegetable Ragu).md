Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. "Naturally vegan: Sugo finto/scappato. It means fake or escaped sauce. It's prepared exactly like a classic ragù sauce, but without meat, so 'escaped' in the sense that the meat has escaped from the pot. It's basically a vegetable ragu and it's sooo good! The trick is to cook it long and slow."

## Ingredients

- 1 big onion
- 2 carrots
- 2 stalks celery (peel the top layer)
- 2–3 sage leaves, chopped
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 rosemary sprig
- A handful of parsley, chopped
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 250 ml red wine
- 600g tomato passata / chopped tomatoes (plus vegetable stock to fill the bottle/tin)
- Salt, pepper to taste

## Directions

1. Chop all the herbs and dice the onion, carrot, and celery.
2. Heat the olive oil in a pan, then add the onion, carrot, and celery, and let it cook 10 minutes. Add the garlic, herbs, and bay leaf, and cook another 10 minutes.
3. Add the wine and let it cook until evaporated.
4. Add the tomato passata and fill up the bottle/can with vegetable stock, adding it to the pot too. Leave to cook on low heat about 20–30 minutes — the more the better, really; keep checking on it and add water if necessary. The sauce will reduce and become thicker.
5. Season pasta with it, or have it with polenta or bread — a real treat!
