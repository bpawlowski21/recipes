## Ingredients

**For the Cookies:**
- 1/3 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 3 Tablespoons vegetable oil
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup powdered sugar
- 1 egg, room temperature
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/8 teaspoon almond extract
- 1 Tablespoon milk
- 2 cups cake flour
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

**For the Frosting:**
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 3 cups powdered sugar
- 2 Tablespoons milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/8 teaspoon almond extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Food coloring

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 325°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Using a stand mixer with whisk attachment, beat room temperature butter and vegetable oil until blended (about 2 minutes).
- Gradually add granulated sugar while beating, then powdered sugar. Mix until combined. Scrape bowl and add egg, milk, vanilla, and almond extract; beat until combined.
- Add cake flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Mix until no streaks remain without overmixing.
- Scoop dough using a 1-ounce cookie scoop, roll into balls, and place 2 inches apart on baking sheet. Gently flatten with palm.
- Bake at 325°F for 13 minutes until edges set but centers slightly underdone. Cool completely on wire rack.
- Make frosting by combining all frosting ingredients in stand mixer with paddle attachment. Beat on low, gradually increasing to high speed until creamy and slightly fluffy. Add food coloring as desired.
- Frost cooled cookies with offset spatula and add sprinkles.
