## Ingredients

- 4 large pitas, toasted, cut into wedges
- 2 cups prepared hummus (homemade or store-bought)
- Olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups (8 ounces or 225 grams) cherry tomatoes, chopped small
- 8 ounces (225 grams) small cucumbers, washed, unpeeled, chopped small
- 1/4 medium red onion, chopped small
- Juice of half a lemon
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Sumac and/or za'atar (optional)
- 1 to 2 tablespoons finely chopped parsley, or a mix of parsley, mint, and chives

## Directions

1. Spread hummus on a large plate using the back of a spoon, creating swirls and cavities.
2. Drizzle lightly with olive oil to freshen it up.
3. In a bowl, combine tomatoes, cucumbers, onion, lemon juice, approximately 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil, and salt and pepper to taste. If using sumac, add about 1/4 teaspoon.
4. Stir in herbs.
5. Heap the salad mixture on top of the hummus, arrange pita wedges around it, and finish with additional za'atar, sumac, and/or fresh herbs before serving.
