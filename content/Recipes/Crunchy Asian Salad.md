## Ingredients

- 1 env. (0.7 oz.) GOOD SEASONS Italian Dressing Mix
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp. soy sauce
- 2 pkg. (3 oz. each) ramen noodle soup mix
- 2 pkg. (16 oz. each) coleslaw blend (cabbage slaw mix)
- 4 green onions, sliced
- ½ cup PLANTERS Dry Roasted Sunflower Kernels
- ½ cup PLANTERS Sliced Almonds, toasted
- 1 chopped apple, peeled (Wendy's addition)
- Optional: mandarin oranges (Wendy's addition)

## Directions

- PREPARE dressing mix in small bowl as directed on envelope. Stir in sugar and soy sauce.
- BREAK apart noodles; place in large bowl. Discard seasoning packet or reserve for another use. Add coleslaw blend, onions, sunflower kernels and nuts to noodles; mix lightly.
- ADD dressing; toss to coat.

## Notes

- Dressing can be prepared ahead of time; refrigerate up to 24 hours before using. Noodles can also be crumbled the day before; place in resealable plastic bag along with the sunflower kernels and nuts, sealed and stored at room temperature until ready to assemble.
- Substitute: can prepare using GOOD SEASONS Asian Sesame Dressing Mix.
- Wendy's handwritten additions: can add mandarin oranges; add 1 chopped, peeled apple.
