## Ingredients

**Pastry**
- 1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons (10 tablespoons total; 85 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea or table salt
- 1/2 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 4 tablespoons (55 grams) cold unsalted butter, diced
- 2 tablespoons plain yogurt or sour cream
- 2 to 3 tablespoons cold water

**Balsamic Onions**
- 2 small or 1 large red onion, halved and thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons (30 grams) unsalted butter
- 2 teaspoons brown sugar, light or dark
- 1/2 teaspoon fine sea or table salt
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- Freshly ground black pepper

**Assembly**
- 2 teaspoons smooth Dijon mustard
- 2 teaspoons minced fresh thyme leaves (optional)
- 1 (8.5-ounce or 250-gram) wheel of brie
- 1 egg, beaten with 1 teaspoon water
- Sesame seeds, ideally toasted (optional)

## Directions

1. **Make the pastry:** combine flour, sugar, and salt in a medium bowl. Sprinkle butter over and work it in with a pastry blender or fingertips until it resembles small peas. Sprinkle sour cream and 2 tablespoons water over and stir/mash to combine; it should form large clumps. Add the remaining tablespoon of water if needed. Bring together into a single mass, pat into a flatter packet on parchment paper, wrap tightly, and chill until firm, 1 to 2 hours in the fridge or about 20 minutes in the freezer.
2. **Make the balsamic red onions:** heat a large skillet over medium heat and add the butter. Once melted, cook the onions, stirring, until softened and beginning to lightly brown at the edges, about 5 minutes. Add sugar, salt, balsamic vinegar, and pepper and cook, stirring, until the balsamic reduces to a dark, jammy puddle, about 3 to 5 more minutes. Transfer to a bowl to cool.
3. **Assemble:** heat oven to 400°F. Unwrap chilled dough, line a small baking sheet with parchment, flour the counter and dough top, and roll into a large, thin circle, about 10 inches (check size by placing the brie in the middle). Thinly spread the first teaspoon of Dijon in the middle, scatter half the red onions over it, and sprinkle with half the thyme if using. Place brie on top. Thinly spread remaining Dijon on top of the brie, followed by remaining onions and thyme. Bring the sides of the dough up to wrap the brie, making little folds to help it fit, and press edges together gently at the top.
4. Transfer the pastry-wrapped brie to the baking sheet. Brush sides, pleats, and top of pastry with egg wash and sprinkle with sesame seeds if using.
5. **Bake** for 30 minutes, or until golden brown all over, rotating the tray if needed for even color. Let rest on a cooling rack for 10 minutes if possible.
6. Transfer brie to a rimmed plate (it will be runny when cut) and serve with crackers or baguette slices.
