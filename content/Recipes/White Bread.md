## Ingredients

- 4½ teaspoons instant yeast (two 0.25-ounce packets)
- ¾ cup + 2⅔ cups warm water, divided
- ¼ cup (52 g) granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 3 tablespoons (43 g) unsalted butter, cubed, room temperature — brown the butter first for extra flavor
- 9 to 10 cups (1200 g) all-purpose flour
- 3 tablespoons (43 g) unsalted butter, melted (for brushing)

## Directions

1. Dissolve yeast in ¾ cup warm water in mixer bowl; let sit 5 minutes. Add remaining water, sugar, salt, room temperature butter, and 5 cups flour; stir to combine.
2. Using dough hook on low speed, gradually add remaining flour until dough is soft and tacky but not sticky. Knead 7-10 minutes until soft ball forms and clears bowl sides.
3. Place dough in greased bowl, turning to coat completely. Cover with plastic wrap and let rise in draft-free area until doubled, about 45 minutes to 1 hour.
4. Turn dough onto floured surface; gently press to remove air pockets. Divide in half (or in thirds for three loaves). Working with one piece, pat into a 9x12-inch rectangle, then roll tightly from short end. Seal seams and ends; place in greased loaf pans. Cover and let rise until doubled, 30-45 minutes.
5. Position oven rack on lowest setting; preheat to 400°F.
6. Brush loaves with melted butter. Bake 30-35 minutes, rotating halfway through, until golden brown (internal temperature should reach 195°F).
7. Remove from oven and brush with additional melted butter. Cool 10 minutes, then remove from pans and cool completely before slicing.

Yields 2 loaves (20 servings).
