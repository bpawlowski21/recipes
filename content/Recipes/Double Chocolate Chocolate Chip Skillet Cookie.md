## Ingredients

- 8 ounces semi-sweet chocolate, divided (only 4 ounces are melted)
- 3/4 cup butter
- 1 1/4 cups sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 cup flour
- 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Toppings: vanilla ice cream, hot fudge, mini chocolate chips

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Heat an oven-proof skillet over medium-low heat. Add butter and 4 ounces chocolate to the skillet. Let melt, stirring often to prevent burning. Remove from heat and immediately add sugar. Stir well until sugar dissolves.
- Let cool for 15 minutes before adding eggs. Add eggs one at a time to temper the chocolate mixture. Stir in vanilla.
- Fold in flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Ensure the mixture is no longer warm or hot before adding the remaining 4 ounces of chocolate chips or chunks.
- Bake for 20-22 minutes. Remove from oven and let cool slightly before adding ice cream and desired toppings. Scoop into dishes or eat straight from the skillet.
