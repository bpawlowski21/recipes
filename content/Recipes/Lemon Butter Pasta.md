## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 2 tablespoons drained capers
- 1/2-1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 3/4 pound angel hair pasta
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 6 tablespoons cold salted butter
- 1 lemon, sliced, seeds removed
- 1 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 1 zucchini or yellow summer squash, very thinly sliced
- 3-4 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves (or 2 teaspoons dried thyme)
- 8 ounces ricotta cheese, whipped, if desired

## Directions

1. Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add olive oil, breadcrumbs, capers, and red pepper flakes. Cook, stirring occasionally, until breadcrumbs are toasted and capers are fried, about 4 minutes. Remove and season with salt.
2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook pasta according to package directions until al dente. Drain.
3. Set skillet over medium-high heat. Add 2 tablespoons butter and lemon slices. Sear until caramelized, about 1 minute per side. Remove lemon and finely chop. Mix with basil.
4. Add remaining butter, zucchini, garlic, and thyme to skillet. Season with salt and pepper. Cook until zucchini is golden, 5 minutes. Remove from heat, stir in pasta and half the lemon-basil mixture.
5. Spread ricotta into bowls. Add pasta. Top with breadcrumbs and remaining lemon-basil. Twirl pasta into cheese and serve.
