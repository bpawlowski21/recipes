## Directions

**Key technique:** To use discard starter in a yeast roll recipe, substitute it for an equal amount of the recipe's flour and liquid (water, milk, etc.). Example: for 1 cup (227g) of discard starter, reduce the recipe's flour by ~113g and its liquid by ~113g.

**Three recipe applications demonstrated:**
- **Golden Pull-Apart Butter Buns** — add 126g discard starter; reduce flour from 418g to 355g; reduce water from 152g to 89g.
- **Crusty European-Style Hard Rolls** — add 233g discard starter to replace overnight starter; omit 120g flour + 113g water + yeast.
- **Whole Grain Dinner Rolls** — add 100g discard starter; reduce all-purpose flour from 180g to 141g; reduce whole wheat flour from 57g to 44g; reduce water from 141g to 91g.

**Important considerations:**
- Choose recipes containing liquid ingredients (milk, water, juice).
- For sweeter recipes, limit discard to one-third or less of total flour weight.
- Adjust liquid amounts if starter consistency resembles pancake batter.
