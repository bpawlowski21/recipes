## Ingredients

**For the Lemongrass Ginger Syrup:**
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 kaffir lime leaves
- 1 stalk lemongrass, smashed and sliced
- 1 (2-inch) piece ginger, peeled and thinly sliced

**For the Cocktail:**
- 1½ oz gin (preferably Rutte)
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¾ oz lemongrass-ginger syrup
- ½ oz Imbue Bittersweet vermouth
- Lemon twist, for garnish

## Directions

1. To make the syrup, combine sugar, kaffir lime leaves, lemongrass, ginger, and ½ cup water in a small saucepan over medium-high heat; cook until sugar has dissolved (about 3 minutes), then let sit for 2 hours and strain.
2. To make the cocktail, combine gin, lemon juice, syrup, and vermouth in a cocktail shaker filled with ice; shake vigorously, then strain into a chilled coupe glass and garnish with a lemon twist.
