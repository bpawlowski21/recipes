## Ingredients

**Pastry Cream**
- 1 cup (235 ml) heavy cream
- 1 cup (235 ml) whole milk
- ⅓ cup (65 g) + 3 tablespoons granulated sugar, divided
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 5 large egg yolks, room temperature
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla extract

**Choux Pastry**
- 1 cup (236 ml) water
- ½ cup (113 g) unsalted butter
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
- 4 large eggs

**Chocolate Topping**
- 2 oz (55 g) semisweet chocolate or ⅓ cup chocolate chips
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1½ cups (185 g) powdered sugar
- 2-4 tablespoons hot water

## Directions

**Pastry Cream**
1. Combine cream, milk, ⅓ cup sugar, and salt in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Stir until sugar dissolves and mixture simmers. Remove from heat and cool 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk egg yolks with remaining 3 tablespoons sugar for 15 seconds until sugar begins dissolving.
3. Sprinkle cornstarch over egg mixture and whisk until no lumps remain.
4. Slowly drizzle about ½ cup warm cream into egg mixture while whisking constantly. Gradually whisk in remaining cream (tempering the eggs to prevent cooking).
5. Pour mixture back into saucepan and return to medium heat. Whisk constantly until thickened.
6. Remove from heat and pour through fine mesh strainer into a heatproof bowl.
7. Cut butter into 4 pieces and whisk in one tablespoon at a time until melted and combined. Whisk in vanilla extract.
8. Place plastic wrap in direct contact with pastry cream surface. Cool at room temperature 30-60 minutes, then refrigerate 3-4 hours.

**Choux Pastry**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine water, butter, and salt. Bring to a rolling boil.
3. Sprinkle flour over boiling water. Reduce heat to medium-low and stir constantly with a spatula until moisture is absorbed and mixture forms a ball pulling away from pot sides.
4. Remove from heat and stir several minutes to cool. Add eggs one at a time, stirring well after each addition until smooth and velvety.
5. Transfer pastry to a piping bag fitted with ½-inch round tip. Pipe 5-inch strips onto prepared sheets, spacing 2 inches apart.
6. Bake 30 minutes or until light golden brown and pastry appears dry.
7. Remove from oven. Use a sharp knife to pierce each end of each eclair to the center. Return to oven and turn off heat. Crack door open and let sit 10-15 minutes.
8. Remove and cool completely on baking sheet.

**Assembly**
1. Once cooled, use a wooden skewer to pierce through both ends of each eclair to ensure a clear path for filling.
2. Fit piping bag with small pointed tip. Fill bag with chilled pastry cream. Insert tip into one end and fill. Repeat from opposite end. Avoid overfilling.
3. Prepare chocolate topping by melting chocolate and butter together. Whisk in powdered sugar and water until desired consistency is reached.
4. Dip or spread chocolate over tops of eclairs.
5. Allow chocolate to harden before serving.
