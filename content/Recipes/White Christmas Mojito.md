## Ingredients

- Juice of 1 lime
- 8 fresh mint leaves
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2 tablespoons white rum
- 1 tablespoon coconut rum
- 1/4 cup canned unsweetened coconut milk
- Sparkling water, for topping
- Pomegranate arils, for garnish

## Directions

1. In a glass, muddle lime juice, sugar, and mint leaves until leaves break down.
2. Fill glass halfway with ice.
3. In a blender, combine white rum, coconut rum, and coconut milk; pulse until smooth.
4. Pour mixture over ice and stir to combine.
5. Top with sparkling water. Garnish with fresh mint and pomegranate arils.
