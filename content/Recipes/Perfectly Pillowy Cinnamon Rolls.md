## Ingredients

**Tangzhong**
- 1/2 cup (113g) milk, whole preferred
- 3 tablespoons (24g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour

**Dough**
- 2/3 cup (151g) milk, cold, whole preferred
- 2 1/2 cups (300g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 1 teaspoon (6g) table salt
- 2 tablespoons (25g) granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 4 tablespoons (57g) unsalted butter, softened

**Filling**
- 1 tablespoon (14g) butter, melted
- 1/2 cup (107g) light brown sugar, packed
- 2 tablespoons (14g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 3 to 4 teaspoons (8g to 10g) ground cinnamon
- 1/16 teaspoon table salt

**Icing**
- 3 tablespoons (42g) butter, melted, divided
- 1/2 teaspoon King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 1/16 teaspoon table salt
- 1 1/2 cups (170g) confectioners' sugar, sifted
- 1 to 2 tablespoons (14g to 28g) milk, heavy cream, or buttermilk

## Directions

- Combine tangzhong ingredients in a small saucepan and whisk until smooth. Cook over medium heat, stirring regularly, until thickened and paste-like (1-3 minutes). Transfer to a mixing bowl.
- Add cold milk, then flour and remaining dough ingredients to the tangzhong in order listed. Mix until combined, then knead until smooth, elastic, and tacky (up to 15 minutes by hand, 10-12 minutes with mixer).
- Shape dough into a ball, place in bowl, and cover. Let rise 60-90 minutes until puffy.
- While dough rises, combine melted butter with remaining filling ingredients, mixing until texture resembles damp sand. Set aside.
- Transfer dough to greased surface and press into a 10" x 12" rectangle, about 1/2" thick.
- Sprinkle filling over dough, leaving 1/2" strip along one long side uncovered.
- Roll dough into a log from the filled side.
- Score into eight equal pieces (1 1/2" to 2" each). Cut using dental floss, bench knife, or sharp knife.
- Place rolls on prepared baking sheet with 2" spacing between each roll and from pan edges. Tuck spiral ends underneath to prevent unraveling.
- Cover and let rise 30-60 minutes until puffy.
- Preheat oven to 375°F. Bake 14-18 minutes until light golden brown and internal temperature reads 190°F.
- Brush hot rolls with 1 1/2 tablespoons melted butter. Cool 10-15 minutes.
- Mix remaining butter with vanilla, salt, confectioners' sugar, and milk until smooth. Ice rolls before serving.
- Store cooled, un-iced rolls wrapped at room temperature for a couple days, or freeze up to 1 month.
