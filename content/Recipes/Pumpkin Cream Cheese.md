## Ingredients

- 8 oz. cream cheese, softened
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup pumpkin puree
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup (or agave)
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon

## Directions

- Mix all of the ingredients together in a bowl. Cover bowl and chill in the fridge for at least an hour before using.

## Notes

- Wendy's handwritten additions on the scan: a sprinkle of pumpkin pie spice, and a note to mix it in the Cuisinart (food processor) rather than by hand.
