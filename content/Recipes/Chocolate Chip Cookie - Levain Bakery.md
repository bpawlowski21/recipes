"This Levain bakery copycat is the real deal - huge gooey chocolate chip cookies that are sure to leave you drooling." A different, larger-format Levain copycat recipe from the other saved [[Chocolate Chip Cookies (Christina Marsigliese)]] — this one uses cold butter and no walnuts, that one uses room-temp butter and walnuts and makes smaller cookies.

Prep Time: 15 minutes · Calories: 392 kcal

## Ingredients

- 1 cup salted butter, cold
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- 3 cups flour
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup semi sweet chocolate chips
- 1 cup dark (or milk) chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a cookie sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat. Set it inside of another cookie sheet (you'll be baking on a stack of 2 cookie sheets) and set aside.
2. Cut the cold butter into small portions and add to the bowl of a stand mixer along with the brown sugar and granulated sugar. Mix with the paddle attachment until there are no pieces of butter left and everything is incorporated, about 5 minutes.
3. Scrape the sides of the bowl and add the eggs and vanilla. Mix until the egg is completely combined.
4. Add the flour, corn starch, baking powder, and salt. Mix until a soft dough forms.
5. Add in the chocolate chips, mixing until evenly distributed.
6. Use a food scale to weigh out 6 oz of dough for each cookie (a generous scoop from a #12 cookie scoop, or about ¾ cup, if you don't have a scale).
7. Roll into a ball and then pinch and squish the dough so there are large dimples in the dough.
8. Arrange 5 on the prepared cookie sheet (remember, double layer of cookie sheets) and then bake.
9. Bake at 400°F for 10-13 minutes or until the tops of the cookies are golden brown. The cookies should spread and expand slightly while baking.
10. Remove from the oven and allow the cookies to cool on the hot pan for at least 25 minutes before enjoying.
11. Repeat with remaining dough and enjoy!
