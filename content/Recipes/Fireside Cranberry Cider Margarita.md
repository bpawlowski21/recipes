## Ingredients

- 2 oz mezcal or silver tequila
- 0.5 oz orange liqueur
- 2 oz apple cider
- 2 oz no-sugar cranberry juice
- 1 tbsp orange juice
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 0.25 cup ginger beer

**Gingered Rim**
- 1 tbsp coarse sugar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 0.25 cup candied ginger pieces, finely chopped
- 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon

## Directions

- Mix rim ingredients on a small plate. Run a lime or orange wedge around the glass rim and coat in the sugar mixture.
- Fill glass with ice.
- Combine mezcal/tequila, apple cider, cranberry juice, and orange liqueur in a cocktail shaker. Shake to combine, fill with ice, and shake 1 minute. Pour into the prepared glass and top with ginger beer.
- Garnish with apple slices, cinnamon sticks, and rosemary. Optionally light the cinnamon stick or star anise for a smoky effect.
- Mocktail: omit tequila; use 0.25 cup apple cider with 0.25-1 tsp apple cider vinegar.
