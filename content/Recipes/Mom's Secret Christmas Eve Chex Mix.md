## Ingredients

- 3 cups corn chex
- 3 cups rice chex
- 2 cups whole wheat chex
- 2 cups mixed nuts
- 2 cups bite-size pretzels
- 2 cups garlic-flavor bite-size bagel chips (or regular bagel chips)
- 1 1/2 sticks (3/4 cup) salted butter, melted
- 4 tablespoons worcestershire sauce
- 2 teaspoons seasoned salt
- 3/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- Pinch of pepper

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 250°F.
- In a large bowl, combine the chex cereals, mixed nuts, pretzels, and bagel chips. Set aside.
- Melt butter in a separate bowl and stir in worcestershire sauce, seasoned salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and a pinch of pepper. Pour over cereal mixture and toss well for 3-5 minutes until evenly coated.
- Bake for 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes.
- Spread on paper towels to cool for about 15 minutes. Store in an airtight container.
