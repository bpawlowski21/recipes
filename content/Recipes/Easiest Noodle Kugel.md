## Ingredients

- 12 ounces wide egg noodles (use the whole bag, per Wendy's note)
- 1 cup chopped apples (approx 2), or 2 chopped apples per Wendy's note
- sprinkle of dried cranberries
- 5 large eggs (or egg beaters)
- 1 cup sour cream (reduced fat)
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted, cooled
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 3 cups lowfat milk (Wendy notes this quantity is a must)
- 2 cups 1% cottage cheese (Wendy increases from the printed 1 cup, and notes to whip it)
- 3 cups cornflakes, coarsely crushed
- 1/4 cup (packed) dark brown sugar

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Butter 13 x 9 x 2-inch glass baking dish. Spread uncooked noodles over bottom of prepared dish and sprinkle with apples & cranberries. Whisk eggs, sour cream, butter, vanilla and sugar in large bowl until smooth. Whisk in milk & cottage cheese and pour mixture over noodles. Let kugel stand 5 minutes.
- Mix cornflakes and brown sugar in bowl; sprinkle evenly over kugel.
- Bake kugel until set in center, about 1 hour. Cut kugel into squares. Serve warm or at room temperature.

## Notes

- Wendy's typed adaptation (forwarded email to herself, Sept 19, 2010, "Fwd: easiest noodle kugel - WN ADAPTED"): use the whole 12-ounce bag of noodles, 3 cups lowfat milk, 2 cups 1% cottage cheese (whipped), 2 chopped apples, 1 tsp vanilla, and a sprinkle of dried cranberries.
- Handwritten blue-ink correction on the printed ingredient list emphasizes "3 cups lowfat milk" as a must, and bumps the cottage cheese from 1 cup to 2 cups.
- Distinct from the separate "Noodle Kugel" (Martha Stewart, apple-and-cream-cheese version) also transcribed from this scan — kept as two separate recipes since the ingredients and method differ meaningfully.
