## Ingredients

**For the Strawberries:**
- 1 pound strawberries, sliced horizontally (3 cups sliced)
- ¼ cup granulated sugar, or more to taste
- Zest of 1 lemon

**For the Shortcakes:**
- 2 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
- 4 teaspoons baking powder
- 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1/8 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 1 stick (1/2 cup) cold salted butter, cut into small cubes and refrigerated until ready to use
- 2/3 cup half-and-half, plus more as needed
- 2 tablespoons heavy cream
- 1 tablespoon sanding sugar or turbinado sugar

**For the Whipped Cream:**
- 1¼ cups heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 2/3 cup crème fraîche
- 3 tablespoons honey

## Directions

1. Toss strawberries, sugar, and lemon zest together; let sit 10-15 minutes to release juices. Chill until ready.
2. Preheat oven to 425°F.
3. Pulse flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in food processor about 5 times. Add butter and pulse 10-15 times until pea-sized pieces remain. Transfer to bowl and stir in half-and-half until just moistened (don't overwork); add more half-and-half as needed.
4. Transfer to floured surface and gently knead twice. Form into a 6×1-inch square. Cut in half, then cut across each half twice to create six shortcakes. Brush tops with cream and sprinkle with sanding sugar. Bake 12-13 minutes until golden.
5. Beat cream and vanilla in stand mixer on medium-high speed until soft peaks form (2-3 minutes). Add crème fraîche and honey; beat 30 seconds until incorporated. Chill.
6. Slice shortcakes horizontally. Spoon ¾ cup strawberries and juices onto each, top with ¾ cup whipped cream, and close with other shortcake half.
