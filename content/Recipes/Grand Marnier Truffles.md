## Ingredients

- 1 pound (454g) dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 1 cup (227ml) heavy cream
- 6 tablespoons (85g) unsalted butter, room temperature, cut into small cubes
- 1/4 cup (57ml) Grand Marnier
- 1/2 cup (42g) cocoa powder, sifted

### Tempered Chocolate (Optional coating)
- 24 ounces (567g) dark chocolate, finely chopped
- Flaky sea salt

## Directions

- Place chopped chocolate in a medium heatproof bowl.
- Heat cream over medium heat until it reaches a boil, then remove from heat.
- Pour hot cream over chocolate; let sit one minute, then whisk until smooth.
- Stir in butter and Grand Marnier; whisk vigorously until smooth and shiny.
- Cool to room temperature, then cover and refrigerate for 2 1/2 hours until firm enough to scoop.
- Place cocoa powder in a shallow dish. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Roll truffle filling into small balls using 2 teaspoons per truffle; transfer to prepared sheets.
- Refrigerate rolled truffles at least 1 hour before dipping or coating.
- Dip chilled truffles in tempered chocolate using a fork or candy dipper; allow excess to drip off.
- Sprinkle each truffle with flaky sea salt.
- Let chocolate set about 1 hour before serving. Store in airtight container at room temperature up to 1 week.
