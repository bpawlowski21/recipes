## Ingredients

**Dry**
- 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour
- ¼ cup (50g) granulated sugar
- ¼ cup (50g) brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon ground nutmeg (optional)

**Wet**
- 1 cup (236ml) buttermilk
- 1 large egg
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 tablespoons (42g) unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled

**Topping**
- 4 tablespoons salted butter, melted
- ½ cup (100g) granulated sugar
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F and lightly grease a 12-count donut pan with baking spray.
2. Whisk together flour, both sugars, baking powder, baking soda, cornstarch, salt, and nutmeg in a large bowl.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk buttermilk, egg, and vanilla. While whisking, slowly drizzle in melted butter until combined (slight curdling is normal).
4. Fold wet ingredients into dry ingredients using a spatula until just combined — avoid overmixing to prevent dense, dry donuts.
5. Pipe or spread batter evenly into prepared donut pans.
6. Bake for 10-12 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the thickest part comes out clean. Cool several minutes, then transfer to cooling rack.
7. Brush warm donuts with melted salted butter, then dip in cinnamon-sugar mixture. Serve warm.
