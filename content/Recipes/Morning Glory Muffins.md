## Ingredients

**Dry**
- 1¼ cups (150g) whole wheat flour
- ½ cup (60g) all-purpose flour
- ¾ cup (159g) brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon ground ginger
- ½ teaspoon salt

**Wet**
- ¾ cup unsweetened applesauce
- ½ cup (114ml) melted coconut oil
- 1 large apple, shredded
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract

**Mix-ins**
- 2 cups grated carrot (approximately 3 medium)
- ½ cup raisins
- ½ cup flaked coconut
- ⅓ cup chopped walnuts

## Directions

1. Line a muffin tin with paper liners. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Whisk together flours, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, ginger, and salt in a large bowl.
3. In a separate bowl, combine applesauce, oil, shredded apple, and vanilla extract.
4. Fold wet ingredients into dry mixture until just combined. Then fold in carrot, raisins, coconut, and walnuts.
5. Divide batter evenly among prepared muffin cups, filling to the top of liners.
6. Bake at 400°F for 10 minutes, then reduce heat to 350°F and bake 10-12 additional minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
7. Cool in pan for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack. Serve warm, at room temperature, or chilled.
