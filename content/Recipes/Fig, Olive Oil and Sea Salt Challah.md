## Ingredients

**Bread**
- 2 1/4 teaspoons (1 packet) active dry yeast
- 1/4 cup (85 grams) plus 1 teaspoon honey
- 2/3 cup warm water (110-116°F)
- 1/3 cup (80 ml) olive oil, plus more for the bowl
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons flaky sea salt (such as Maldon), or 1 1/2 teaspoons table salt
- 4 cups (500 grams) all-purpose flour

**Fig Filling**
- 1 cup (5 1/2 ounces or 155 grams) stemmed and roughly chopped dried figs
- 1/8 teaspoon freshly grated orange zest (or more as desired)
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) water
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) orange juice
- 1/8 teaspoon sea salt
- Few grinds black pepper

**Egg Wash**
- 1 large egg
- Coarse or flaky sea salt for sprinkling

## Directions

1. **Make dough (mixer method):** Whisk yeast and 1 teaspoon honey into warm water; let stand until foamy. Combine yeast mixture with remaining honey, olive oil, and eggs in mixer bowl. Add salt and flour; mix until dough begins holding together. Switch to dough hook; run at low speed 5-8 minutes. Transfer to an oiled bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and let rise 1 hour until almost doubled. (By hand: proof yeast as directed, mix wet ingredients, add salt and flour, mix with a wooden spoon until it comes together, then knead on a floured surface 5-10 minutes until smooth and elastic; let rise as directed.)
2. **Prepare fig paste:** In a small saucepan, combine figs, zest, water, juice, salt, and pepper. Simmer over medium heat about 10 minutes until figs are soft. Season to taste. Cool to lukewarm, then process in a food processor until a fine paste forms. Cool completely.
3. **Insert figs:** Divide risen dough in half. Roll first half into a wide, imperfect rectangle. Spread half of the fig filling over the dough, stopping short of the edges. Roll into a tight log, trapping the filling inside. Gently stretch the log to a comfortable width and divide in half. Repeat with remaining dough and filling.
4. **Weave challah:** Arrange four dough ropes in a tic-tac-toe pattern. Weave so one side goes over and the other goes under where they meet. Take the four legs coming from underneath the center and move each leg to the right (jumping it). Take legs from the right side and jump each over the leg before, moving left. Repeat jumps if extra rope remains. Tuck odd bumps under to form a round.
5. **Final rise and bake:** Transfer to a parchment-covered baking sheet. Beat egg and brush over challah. Let rise 1 hour (preheat oven to 375°F at the 45-minute mark). Brush with egg wash again and sprinkle with sea salt. Bake 35-40 minutes until beautifully bronzed; internal temperature should reach 195°F. Cool on a rack before serving.
