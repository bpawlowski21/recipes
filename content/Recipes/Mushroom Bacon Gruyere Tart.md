Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta") — despite the folder, this recipe is not vegan (eggs, cheese, bacon, Greek yogurt). Can be made vegetarian by leaving off the bacon, and any cheese can be substituted.

## Ingredients

- 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed (from a 17.3 oz package — author uses Pepperidge Farm)
- ¼ cup Greek yogurt
- 2 large eggs
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper
- 1 cup shredded Gruyère (any cheese works)
- 1 cup sautéed mushrooms
- ½ cup crumbled crispy bacon
- Fresh thyme leaves, for garnish
- Egg wash (1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp water)

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Unroll one sheet of pastry onto the baking sheet. Lightly score a 1-inch border around the edge, being careful not to cut all the way through. Prick the center with a fork. Brush the outer border with egg wash.
3. Bake 12–15 minutes, until light golden brown. Remove from the oven and push down any puffy places in the center with the back of a spoon.
4. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper. Add the yogurt, garlic, and cheese and mix well to combine.
5. Pour the filling into the center of the tart shell and spread evenly.
6. Top with mushrooms and bacon and bake 12 more minutes, or until the filling is set. Sprinkle with fresh thyme.
