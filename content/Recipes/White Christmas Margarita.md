Prep Time: 4 minutes

**Equipment:** Cocktail shaker, jigger, strainer, rocks glass

## Ingredients

- 2 oz blanco tequila
- 1 oz white cranberry juice
- ½ oz lime juice
- ½ oz orange liqueur
- ½ oz simple syrup
- Frozen cranberries, for garnish
- Rosemary sprig, for garnish
- Salt, for rim

## Directions

1. In a cocktail shaker, combine the white cranberry juice, simple syrup, lime juice, orange liqueur, and blanco tequila.
2. Add ice to the shaker and shake vigorously until well-chilled.
3. Strain the mixture into a glass filled with fresh ice.
4. Top with frozen cranberries for a pop of color and a sprig of rosemary for a festive touch.
5. Serve immediately and enjoy your holiday-inspired margarita.

## Notes

- Pro Tips: rim the glass with sugar or a mix of sugar and salt before pouring the drink. Chill the glass in the freezer beforehand for an extra-refreshing experience. Gently smack the rosemary sprig before garnishing to release its natural aroma.
