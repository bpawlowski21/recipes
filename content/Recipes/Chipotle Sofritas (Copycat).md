## Ingredients

**For the marinade:**
- 1 medium green bell pepper, stemmed, seeded, and chopped (about 1/2 cup)
- 1 medium tomato, stemmed and quartered
- 1/2 medium onion, peeled and quartered
- 1/2 cup water (plus additional for braising)
- 2 chipotle chilies plus 2 tablespoons adobo sauce
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 teaspoon red wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon ancho chile powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon salt and freshly ground black pepper

**For the tofu:**
- 1 tablespoon olive oil or rice bran oil
- 1 (16 ounce) package extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 8 slices

## Directions

- Combine bell pepper, tomato, onion, water, chipotle chilies, adobo sauce, garlic, red wine vinegar, ancho chile powder, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper in a food processor. Pulse until slightly chunky.
- Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Brown tofu slices until golden (about 10 minutes), working in batches if needed. Drain on paper towels.
- Pulse browned tofu until it resembles 1/4-inch coarse crumbles. Transfer to a bowl and mix with marinade.
- Cover and marinate at least 30 minutes or overnight.
- Heat skillet over medium-high heat. Add marinated tofu and marinade with 1/4 cup water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer about 10 minutes until heated through. Adjust water as needed for desired consistency. Season to taste.
