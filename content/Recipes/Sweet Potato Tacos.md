## Ingredients

- 2 pounds (about 4 medium) sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1/2 to 3/4-inch cubes
- Olive oil
- 1 heaped teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 heaped teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 to 3 teaspoons chili powder, to taste
- Chipotle powder, cayenne, or hot sauce, to taste
- 1/2 teaspoon sweet smoked paprika
- 12 small (6-inch) or 6 medium (8 to 9-inch) flour tortillas
- 1 15-ounce can refried black beans
- 1 lime, in wedges
- Optional toppings: sliced avocado, pickled red onions, pickled jalapeños, chopped fresh cilantro, hot sauce, or taco slaw

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 400°F.
2. Toss sweet potatoes with 2 tablespoons olive oil, then add salt, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, chili powder, chipotle powder, and paprika. Toss to evenly coat.
3. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper (or coat directly with 1 additional tablespoon olive oil for crispier results) and spread potatoes in a single layer.
4. Roast for 40 to 45 minutes, tossing once or twice for even color.
5. Warm tortillas over a gas flame with tongs for light charring, or wrap in foil and warm in the oven for 5 minutes.
6. To assemble: schmear refried black beans on each tortilla, add a spoonful or two of roasted sweet potatoes, squeeze lime juice over top, and finish with desired toppings.
7. Assemble as far as beans, potatoes, lime, and avocado in the kitchen, then let everyone add remaining toppings individually. Keep warm nested in a casserole dish for about 15 minutes.
