## Ingredients

- 7 ounces (198 grams) almond paste
- 1/4 cup (50 grams) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg white
- Two pinches of sea salt
- 1¼ cup (130 grams) sliced almonds, blanched
- 6 ounces semi- or bittersweet chocolate, chopped or in chips (about 1 cup)
- Colored sprinkles (optional)

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Cut or tear almond paste into chunks and place in a stand mixer bowl with paddle attachment. Add sugar and beat at medium-high speed for 3-5 minutes until paste breaks down completely (cover the bowl with a dishtowel to prevent splashing). Add egg white and salt, then beat until uniform and creamy.
3. Place sliced almonds in a shallow bowl and have water nearby for wetting hands.
4. Wet hands and scoop 1 tablespoon of dough. Roll into a 4-inch log, then drop into the almond bowl. Wipe hands dry and roll the log through almonds until coated. Transfer to the prepared tray and arc into a horn shape, pressing loose almonds back on.
5. Repeat with remaining dough.
6. Bake for 15 minutes until almonds are lightly toasted and horns are puffed. Cool completely.
7. Melt chocolate in a small bowl, dip cookie ends, and return to parchment to set. Add sprinkles if desired.
