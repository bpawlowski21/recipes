## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 1 cup roughly chopped mushrooms
- 2 tablespoons salted butter
- 1 pound dry orzo pasta
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves, plus more for topping
- 3 cups low sodium vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup dry white wine (Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc)
- 3 cups broccoli florets, roughly chopped
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- Zest of 1/2 a lemon
- 1/2 cup whole milk or canned coconut milk
- 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1/2 cup grated asiago or manchego cheese
- Fresh basil, for serving

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook until fragrant, about 5 minutes. Stir in mushrooms and season with salt and pepper. Cook 2-3 minutes until softened. Add butter, orzo, broccoli, and thyme; cook until golden and toasted, about 5 minutes. Add wine, broth, bay leaf, garlic powder, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Simmer 8-10 minutes until orzo is al dente, stirring often.
3. Stir in lemon zest, milk, and 1/2 cup cheddar. Transfer to an oven-safe dish if needed. Scatter remaining cheeses on top.
4. Bake 10-15 minutes until cheese melts. Switch oven to broil and broil 1-2 minutes until cheese becomes crispy. Serve warm with fresh thyme and basil.
