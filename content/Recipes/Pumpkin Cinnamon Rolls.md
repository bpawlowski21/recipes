## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 3½ cups all-purpose flour (420g)
- ¼ cup granulated sugar (50g)
- ¼ cup light brown sugar (55g)
- 2¼ teaspoons instant yeast
- 1½ teaspoons cinnamon
- 1½ teaspoons ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ cup whole milk (120mL)
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter (57g)
- ¾ cup pumpkin purée (168g)
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract (15mL)

**Filling**
- ½ cup unsalted butter, room temperature (113g)
- ¾ cup packed light brown sugar (165g)
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon ground ginger
- ½ teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg

**Cream cheese glaze**
- 6 ounces cream cheese, room temperature (170g)
- 1 cup powdered sugar (120g)
- 3 tablespoons maple syrup
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

## Directions

**Dough**
1. In stand mixer bowl, combine flour, sugars, yeast, spices, and salt; whisk to blend.
2. Warm milk and butter to 110°F, then add to dry ingredients along with pumpkin purée, egg, and vanilla.
3. Using dough hook, mix on low speed approximately 15 minutes until smooth and tacky.
4. Transfer to oiled bowl, cover, and allow to rise in warm location until doubled (about 1 hour).
5. Butter a 13x9-inch baking dish.
6. Punch down dough and roll on floured surface into a 12x18-inch rectangle.

**Filling**
7. Spread softened butter across dough, leaving a ½-inch border on one long side.
8. Combine brown sugar and spices; sprinkle over buttered surface and gently press with hands.
9. Starting from the long side opposite the border, roll tightly into a log.
10. Cut into 12 rounds (1½ inches thick); place in buttered baking dish.
11. Cover and let rise in warm place until nearly doubled (about 1 hour).
12. Preheat oven to 350°F and bake uncovered for 30 minutes until golden brown.

**Cream cheese glaze**
13. Beat cream cheese until smooth; sift in powdered sugar.
14. Mix in maple syrup and vanilla until well combined.
15. Spread glaze over warm rolls before serving.
