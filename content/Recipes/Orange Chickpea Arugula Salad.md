## Ingredients

**Dressing**
- 1 tsp orange zest
- ½ cup orange juice
- ¼ cup olive oil
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp agave syrup
- 4 tbsp chopped red onion
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp salt and pepper
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- ½ tsp chili flakes

**Salad**
- 4 cups arugula
- 400g chickpeas
- 1 cucumber
- ½ cup fresh cilantro
- ½ cup pine nuts
- ½ red onion
- 1 orange

## Directions

1. To make the dressing, throw all the dressing ingredients into a blender or food processor. Blend until smooth.
2. Chop up all the salad ingredients and assemble in a big bowl.
3. Serve the salad with the dressing and enjoy.
