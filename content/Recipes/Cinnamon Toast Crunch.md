## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon plus 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 1/4 cups white whole wheat or whole wheat pastry flour (or additional all-purpose flour)
- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 sticks (1 cup) cold salted butter, cut into 1/2 inch pieces
- 1/2 cup cold water
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2-4 tablespoons salted butter, melted

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Combine cinnamon and sugar in a small bowl.
- In a food processor, combine whole wheat flour, all-purpose flour, remaining cinnamon, salt, and cold butter. Pulse until pea-sized clumps form. Add vanilla and cold water (1 tablespoon at a time) until dough forms a ball. Add extra water if needed.
- Turn dough onto floured surface and form into a ball. Divide in half. Roll each piece thin (about 1/8-inch thick). Transfer to parchment paper, brush with melted butter, sprinkle generously with cinnamon sugar, and cut into 1/2-inch squares.
- Transfer parchment to baking sheet. Space squares 1/2 inch apart.
- Bake 12-14 minutes until golden brown. Cool completely. Store in airtight container up to 1 week. Enjoy with milk, on ice cream, or by the handful.
