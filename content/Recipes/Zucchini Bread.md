## Ingredients

- 2 cups (13 ounces or 370 grams) grated, packed zucchini, not wrung out, grated on the large holes of a box grater
- 2 large eggs
- 2/3 cup (160 ml) neutral oil (safflower), olive oil, or melted unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup (95 grams) packed dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (100 grams) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon fine sea or table salt
- 1 1/4 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1/8 teaspoon ground or freshly grated nutmeg
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 2 cups (260 grams) all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons (25 grams) raw or turbinado sugar

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Lightly coat a 6-cup or 9x5-inch loaf pan with nonstick spray.
2. Place grated zucchini in a large bowl and add oil, eggs, sugars, vanilla, and salt. Use a fork to mix until combined.
3. Sprinkle cinnamon, nutmeg, baking soda, and baking powder over surface of batter and mix until combined, then give it 10 extra stirs for extra security that ingredients are well-dispersed.
4. Add flour and mix until just combined.
5. Pour into prepared loaf pan and smooth the top.
6. Sprinkle with raw or turbinado sugar, do not skimp.
7. Bake for 55 to 60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the middle and top of the cake comes out batter-free.
8. Let cool completely in the pan.
9. Leave in pan, unwrapped, overnight or 24 hours before removing carefully and serving in slices.
10. Store at room temperature for 4 to 5 days. Wrap only the cut end in foil and return to the baking pan, leaving the top exposed to maintain crunchiness.
