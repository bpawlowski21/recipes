Karli Bitner, "Cooking with Karli" (noted by hand on the printed card). "Making bagels at home does not have to be difficult. This asiago cheese bagel recipe yields the extraordinarily flavorful bagels with the most delightful bagel texture known to man."

Prep Time: 30 mins · Cook Time: 15 mins · Rest Time: 30 mins · Total Time: 1 hr 30 mins · Calories: 360

## Ingredients

- 2 cups warm water (105°F–113°F)
- 1½ tbsp dry active yeast
- 1 tbsp brown sugar, packed
- 4½–4¾ cups bread flour
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 cups shredded asiago cheese (can sub parmesan or cheddar, too!)

## Directions

1. In a small bowl, add the warm water, dry active yeast, and brown sugar. Stir to combine and set aside.
2. In a large bowl or the bowl of a stand mixer, add the 4½ cups of bread flour, salt, and ½ cup of shredded cheese. Stir to combine.
3. Your yeast mixture should be bubbly and foamy — if not, your bagels will not rise; discard and try again.
4. Add the yeast mixture to the dry ingredients.
5. **If using a stand mixer,** use the dough hook and knead for 10 minutes — you'll most likely need to add the extra ¼ cup of flour to keep the dough workable but soft.
6. **If mixing by hand,** stir the dry ingredients into the yeast mixture until a shaggy dough forms. Pour the contents onto a clean surface and knead by hand for 10 minutes, adding extra 2-3 tbsp of bread flour as needed to keep the dough soft but workable.
7. Knead by pushing the dough, turning, and folding it in half, repeating this for 10 minutes.
8. Once the dough has finished kneading, allow the dough to rest for 5 minutes.
9. Use a sharp knife or bench scraper to cut the dough into 8 equal-sized pieces (cut the dough in half, then each half in half again, and then once more).
10. Take each portion of dough and knead into a tight ball. Once in a ball, press your thumb into the center of the ball and gently squeeze and pull the dough while rotating it around your thumb to create the bagel shape. Place onto a parchment- or silicone-lined cookie sheet.
11. Repeat with the remaining dough. Cover the cookie sheet with plastic wrap and allow the bagels to rise for 30 minutes.
12. Preheat the oven to 425°F once the bagels have risen for 30 minutes.
13. In a large pot, boil about 4 inches of water. Once at a rolling boil, carefully drop the risen bagels into the boiling water one by one — may need to do this in a few batches.
14. Boil the bagels for about 1 minute, flip over to the other side, and boil for an additional minute.
15. Use a large slotted spoon or spider strainer to remove the bagels and return to the lined cookie sheet.
16. Repeat with the remaining bagels. While the next batch of bagels are boiling, top the boiled ones with shredded cheese.
17. Once all of the bagels have boiled and are topped with shredded cheese, bake in the 425°F oven for 15-17 minutes or until golden brown.
18. Allow the bagels to cool for 10 minutes before cutting and enjoying.
19. Store in a zip-top bag at room temperature for up to 3 days.
