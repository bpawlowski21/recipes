![[Spicy Baked Tofu Burrito Bowl (handwritten).pdf]]

Tofu dreams really do come true. This vegan burrito bowl with spicy baked tofu, cilantro lime rice, roasted pepper, black beans and creamy avocado is the ultimate recipe for meal prep.

## Ingredients

**Bowl:**
- 1 batch cilantro lime rice or Vegan Mexican Rice
- 1 can black beans
- 1 avocado
- 1 red pepper
- 1 orange pepper
- 1 batch spicy tahini dressing

**Tofu:**
- 1 block extra firm tofu
- 2 tablespoons adobo sauce (handwritten substitution — printed ingredient was "sriracha," crossed out)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 3 tablespoons corn starch or arrowroot starch
- 1-2 teaspoon coconut sugar or brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1½ teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- ¼ teaspoon chili powder
- ½-1 teaspoon salt

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees Fahrenheit.
2. Remove the tofu from the package and drain out excess liquid.
3. Place between two towels and put a heavy object on top (a few books, tea pot, pan etc) and let press for 5-10 minutes. It helps crisp up the tofu really well.
4. While the tofu is pressing, mix together the coconut sugar, corn starch, spices, and salt.
5. Chop the tofu into cubes and add to a large mixing bowl.
6. In a small bowl, mix together the oil and adobo sauce (originally sriracha, per handwritten substitution).
7. Add half of the oil/adobo mixture to the tofu and toss lightly. Set the rest aside for later.
8. Toss with the spice/arrowroot mixture until well coated.
9. Add to a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
10. De-seed and chop the peppers into strips and place on the same baking sheet but away from the tofu (the tofu crisps best when it's not touching anything).
11. Drizzle the peppers with olive oil, just 1-2 tsp so they don't stick, and add a sprinkle of salt.
12. Bake for 20 minutes and then brush the rest of the adobo mixture on the tofu.
13. Bake for 10-15 more minutes or until the tofu is crispy on the outside and the peppers have softened.
14. Assemble the bowls with cilantro lime rice, black beans (warmed on the stove if desired), peppers, tofu, avocado, spicy tahini dressing, and salsa if desired.

## Notes

- You can use red, orange, or yellow bell peppers, but orange and red have the strongest flavor when roasted.
- If you don't want the tofu to be too spicy, feel free to sub the (adobo sauce/sriracha) for a less spicy hot sauce such as cholula.
- Some brands of tofu are much firmer than others. Even if it's labeled "extra firm tofu," they're not all created equal. SoyBoy is noted as one of the firmest brands, good for baking. If it seems too soft, try pressing the liquid out for longer.
- The arrowroot starch can be subbed for corn starch or tapioca starch. Be sure to use the full 3 tbsp since it's the key ingredient for making the tofu crispy.
- Nutrition (printed, per ¼ of recipe): 543 kcal | 59g carbs | 22.6g protein | 21.1g fat | 13.1g fiber.
- Handwritten on the original: the "sriracha" ingredient (2 tablespoons) is crossed out and replaced with "adobo sauce" — a genuine tested substitution, carried through in the ingredient list and directions above.
