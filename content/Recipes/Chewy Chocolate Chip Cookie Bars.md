## Ingredients

- 11 tablespoons (156 g) unsalted butter
- 2 cups plus 2 tablespoons (454 g) light or dark brown sugar, packed
- 1 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon butterscotch or vanilla-butternut flavor (optional)
- 3 large eggs
- 2 1/4 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon espresso powder (optional)
- 2 3/4 cups (330 g) all-purpose flour
- 3 cups (510 g) chocolate chips (or combination of flavored chips/nuts)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9" x 13" pan.
- Melt butter and stir in brown sugar. Add salt, vanilla, and optional flavor until combined.
- Cool mixture slightly, then add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Scrape bowl midway through.
- Add baking powder, espresso powder, and flour, stirring to combine.
- Stir in chocolate chips or desired additions.
- Scoop the batter into the prepared pan, spreading it to the edges with a wet spatula or fingers, smoothing the top.
- Bake 30-32 minutes until risen with shiny, golden tops. Center may appear moist — this is intentional.
- Remove from oven and press down risen edges. Allow complete cooling before cutting; overnight is recommended.
