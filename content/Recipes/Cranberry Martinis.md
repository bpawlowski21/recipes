Yields: 6 drinks.

Infuse the vodka with the orange zest and cranberries at least two days ahead. Use a vegetable peeler to make strips of orange zest — it's so easy. Best of all, the entire cocktail mixture can be prepared and refrigerated for up to three days. Just shake with ice before serving.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup fresh cranberries
- 6 (1-by-3-inch) strips orange zest, plus extra for serving
- 1 cup water
- 1 (750-milliliter) bottle good vodka, such as Grey Goose
- 1 cup cranberry juice cocktail, preferably tart
- 1/4 cup triple sec
- Ice

## Directions

**Several days ahead:**
1. Place the sugar, cranberries, 6 strips of orange zest, and 1 cup of water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, lower the heat, and simmer for 5 minutes, until the cranberries start to pop open. Pour the vodka into a large pitcher, add the cranberry mixture, and store covered in the refrigerator for at least 2 days and up to 5 days.

**When ready to serve:**
1. Strain the vodka mixture, reserving the cranberries and discarding the orange zest. Stir in the cranberry juice cocktail and Triple Sec. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice, add the drink mixture, shake for a full 30 seconds (it's longer than you think!), and strain into martini glasses. Rub the rim of each glass with fresh orange zest, spoon a few cranberries into each glass and serve ice cold.
