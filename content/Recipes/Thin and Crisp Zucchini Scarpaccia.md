## Ingredients

- 2 lbs (907 grams) zucchini or other summer squash (about 4 small-medium)
- 2 teaspoons (7 grams) kosher salt
- 3/4 cup (96 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup (60 g) cornmeal, plus more for sprinkling
- 1/2 cup packed (50 g) grated Parmigiana Reggiana (using the small-grate setting on a box grater)
- 1 tsp (4 g) garlic powder (salt-free)
- 1 tsp (4 g) silk chili, Aleppo pepper, or crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 small yellow or white onion (7 ounces | 200 grams pre-trimming), peeled and thinly sliced
- Butter for greasing the pan
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- Flaky sea salt

## Directions

1. Set a colander inside a large bowl.
2. Using a mandoline or a knife, thinly slice the zucchini. The zucchini should be very thin but not paper thin — you should be able to fold each round in half, and it will bend. Transfer the zucchini to the colander, and season with the salt. Toss to distribute, then let it drain. Ideally, allow the zucchini to drain for at least one hour. I have gotten away with less time, and I have also allowed it to drain for over 4 hours.
3. Once the zucchini has drained, use your hands to press it hard into the colander to squeeze out as much water as possible into the bowl (you're going to use the water). Lay a large clean tea towel on a work surface. Transfer the zucchini to the towel. Wrap it up into a beggar's purse, then wring it out over the bowl to extract as much water from the zucchini as possible — don't be afraid to be aggressive. Transfer the liquid to a liquid measure, reserving the bowl. Add enough water to the liquid measure to get 1 heaping cup (250 grams). Set aside. Dry out the reserved bowl.
4. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
5. In the reserved bowl, whisk together the flour, cornmeal, garlic powder, silk chili, and parmesan. Add the 1 cup (250 grams) of zucchini water to the bowl, and whisk until you have a pancake-like batter.
6. Add the zucchini and sliced onion, and using a spatula, fold the vegetables into the batter — it will feel like mostly vegetables with just enough batter to coat them.
7. Butter a 13×18-inch sheet pan or a 12×16-inch Grandma pan (see notes above) with softened butter. Sprinkle with 2 tablespoons cornmeal, then shake and rotate the pan to distribute it evenly.
8. Spread the zucchini mixture into the pan, and use your hands to spread it evenly, pressing it down into a thin layer. Pour the two tablespoons of olive oil into a small dish, then brush it evenly over the top. Sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
9. Transfer to the oven and bake for 40 to 45 minutes or until the edges are crisp. Let cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a cutting board and cut into 12 squares.
10. Serve immediately or at room temperature.
