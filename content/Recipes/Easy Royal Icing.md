## Ingredients

- 4 cups (500 g) powdered sugar
- 2 1/2 tablespoons meringue powder
- 6 tablespoons water
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (or other oil-free flavoring)

## Directions

- Ensure your bowl and all utensils are clean and grease-free, as this is essential for proper whipping.
- In a stand mixer bowl with whisk attachment (or large bowl with electric hand mixer), combine powdered sugar, meringue powder, and water.
- Beat on low speed until combined, then add vanilla extract or alternative flavoring.
- Gradually increase speed to high and beat for 5+ minutes until the mixture forms firm, fluffy stiff peaks that hold their shape.
- Divide icing into bowls and tint with gel food coloring as desired.
- Keep one portion at stiff consistency for outlining. For the flooding icing, add water one teaspoon at a time until the mixture drizzles from a spoon and holds its shape briefly before dissolving.
- Pipe outlines with stiff icing, fill with flooding icing, and smooth with a toothpick or scribing tool.
- Allow cookies to dry completely (several hours to overnight) before stacking or transporting.
