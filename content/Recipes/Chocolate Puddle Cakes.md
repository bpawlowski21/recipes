## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons (55 grams) unsalted butter, cut into chunks, plus more for dishes
- 2 teaspoons cocoa powder, plus more for dusting
- 3 ounces (85 grams) bittersweet chocolate, roughly chopped
- 1/8 teaspoon fine sea salt or a couple pinches of flaky salt
- 1 to 2 tablespoons (13 to 25 grams) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 egg yolk

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 450°F. Butter two 6-ounce ramekins, ensuring complete coverage. Coat bottom and sides with cocoa powder, then tap out excess.
2. Place butter and chocolate in a heatproof bowl. Melt about two-thirds of the way using microwave or water bath, then whisk until completely melted. Whisk in sugar and salt. Add egg and yolk, whisking vigorously 20-30 extra times until smooth and glossy. Add cocoa powder and whisk to combine.
3. Divide batter between prepared ramekins. Bake 7-9 minutes (7 for most molten center, 9 for less oozy but still gooey).
4. Transfer to a cooling rack for 1 minute. Place a dessert plate over the ramekin, flip, count to ten, then lift the cup off. Repeat with second cake.
5. Dust with cocoa powder or powdered sugar. Serve with whipped cream, ice cream, or plain.
