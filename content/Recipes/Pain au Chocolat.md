## Ingredients

### Dough
- 1 1/2 cups (340g) water, milk, or a combination
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 4 1/2 cups (540g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 2 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 2 1/2 teaspoons (15g) table salt
- 4 tablespoons (57g) unsalted butter, softened

### Butter block
- 24 tablespoons (340g) unsalted butter, cold

### Chocolate filling
- 16 to 32 Pain au Chocolat Sticks (85g to 170g total)

### Glaze
- 1 large egg beaten with a pinch of salt

## Directions

- Mix water, sugar, 2 cups flour, yeast, salt, and butter until blended; add remaining flour and knead until dough begins to smooth out.
- Place in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise about an hour until puffy.
- Refrigerate covered dough 8-16 hours (overnight recommended).
- Prepare the butter: halve three sticks lengthwise, pound between floured paper until malleable, then roll to 8"x8".
- Roll chilled dough into a 12" square; center butter at a 45° angle and fold four flaps to enclose.
- Roll to 10"x20" and perform a letter fold (fold in thirds like a business letter). Turn 90° and repeat.
- Perform a book fold: fold short edges to center, fold in half lengthwise, then turn 90°.
- Wrap in floured plastic and refrigerate 8-24 hours.
- Remove half the dough; roll to 8"x24" and cut into eight 4"x6" pieces.
- Place chocolate at one end of each piece, roll into a tube, and place seam-side down on parchment. Flatten slightly and cover.
- Let rise in a warm place about an hour until puffy.
- Preheat oven to 425°F.
- Brush egg glaze on tops; bake 18-20 minutes until golden brown.
- Cool briefly before serving.
