## Ingredients

### Brown Butter Donuts
- 2½ tablespoons (35g) unsalted butter, melted until browned
- 1¼ cups (150g) all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ½ cup (106g) light brown sugar, packed
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- ½ cup (113ml) whole milk

### Cinnamon Sugar Coating
- 4 tablespoons (57g) unsalted butter, melted
- 1 cup (199g) granulated sugar
- 1½ teaspoons ground cinnamon

## Directions

- Heat oven to 350°F and generously spray a 6-mold donut pan with non-stick spray.
- Brown the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat, stirring frequently until golden and nutty-scented. Cool slightly.
- Combine flour, salt, baking powder, and nutmeg in one bowl. In another, blend browned butter with sugar, then whisk in egg and vanilla. Stir in milk.
- Gently fold dry ingredients into wet mixture without overmixing.
- Pipe or spoon batter into pan. Bake 10-11 minutes until donuts spring back when pressed.
- Cool in pan 5 minutes, then transfer to cooling rack.
- Mix melted butter with combined sugar and cinnamon in separate bowls.
- Dip cooled donuts in butter, then roll in cinnamon-sugar mixture until well coated.
