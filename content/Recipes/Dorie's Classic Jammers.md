## Ingredients

**Dough**
- Butter, sugar, salt (creamed base — see Directions)
- Egg yolks
- Flour

**Streusel**
- Flour, sugar, butter (per Dorie's Cookies streusel method)

**Assembly**
- Jam, for filling

*(Full ingredient quantities were on a page not captured in this scan crop — see attached PDF for the printed ingredient list.)*

## Directions

1. Beat the butter, sugar, and salt together until well combined.
2. Add the egg yolks, then the flour, mixing to form a dough.
3. Turn the dough out and shape. **Handwritten correction:** "Keep in a ball, refrigerate overnight" (instead of shaping into a disk).
4. ~~Roll the dough 1/4-inch thick between parchment and freeze for at least 1 hour or refrigerate at least 2 hours~~ — this rolling/chilling step was crossed out on the physical page (not used).
5. **Make the streusel:** combine flour, sugar, and butter per the streusel method (steps 1-3 on the printed page).
6. Pack the streusel into a container and refrigerate (dated 12.24.21 in the margin).
7. Arrange a rack in the oven. **Handwritten correction:** use a 24-well mini-muffin tin (instead of a 12-well tin).
8. **Assemble the cookies (handwritten method):** using the mini-muffin tin, make 13g balls of dough, flatten into a disc, and place one in each well. No need to roll the dough and cut with a cutter — this replaces the printed method of rolling and cutting rounds.
9. Spoon jam into the center of each disc.
10. **Bake:** 11 minutes, rotate the baking sheet, then bake 9 to 11 minutes more, until golden.
11. Repeat with remaining dough and streusel.
