## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (8 Tbsp; 113g) unsalted butter, melted & slightly cooled
- 1/4 cup (50g) packed light or dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 6 Tablespoons (86g) pumpkin puree
- 1 1/2 cups (188g) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
- 1/2 cup (90g) semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Directions

- Whisk melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until lumps dissolve. Add vanilla and blotted pumpkin puree; mix until smooth.
- Whisk flour, salt, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and pumpkin pie spice in a large bowl. Combine wet and dry ingredients with a spoon. Fold in chocolate chips.
- Cover and refrigerate dough for 30 minutes to 3 days.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.
- Scoop dough (about 1.5 tablespoons per cookie) into balls. Space 3 inches apart. Flatten slightly with back of spoon.
- Bake 11-12 minutes until edges appear set. Centers will look soft. Optional: press chocolate chips on warm cookies. Cool on sheets 10+ minutes, then transfer to wire rack.
- Store covered at room temperature up to 1 week.
