## Ingredients

**Crust:**
- 3 cups finely ground graham crackers
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 sticks butter, melted

**Filling:**
- 24 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Directions

- Mix graham crackers, sugar, and melted butter; press into the prepared pan.
- Bake 10 minutes. Let cool completely.
- Beat cream cheese with sugar until smooth.
- Beat in eggs, sour cream, and vanilla.
- Spread over the crust; bake until the edges are set but the center is still loose, about 30 minutes.
- Let cool, then chill until set.
