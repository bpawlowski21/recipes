## Ingredients

- 4 cups dried multigrain penne pasta
- 4 firm, ripe peaches, halved and pitted
- 3 lemons
- 4 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 5-oz. package fresh baby arugula
- 2 cups fresh blueberries
- Sea salt, to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 4 teaspoons pure maple syrup

## Directions

- Cook pasta per package directions; drain and rinse with cold water. Chill until needed.
- Place four peach halves cut-side down on grill rack. Grill covered over medium-high for 8 minutes until grill marks appear; cool. Cut remaining peach halves into ½-inch pieces.
- For dressing, extract 1 tsp. lemon zest and squeeze ½ cup juice from lemons. Whisk together zest, juice, mustard, and garlic in a large bowl. Remove half of dressing; set aside. Add pasta, chopped peaches, arugula, and blueberries to bowl; toss to coat. Season with salt and pepper.
- Top servings with grilled peach halves and drizzle with maple syrup. Serve reserved dressing on the side.
