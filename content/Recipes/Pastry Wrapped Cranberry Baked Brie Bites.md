## Ingredients

- 2 cups fresh cranberries
- 2-4 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 4 sage leaves, plus more for serving
- 1/2 cup orange juice
- Kosher salt
- 2 teaspoons orange zest
- 2 sheets (1 box) frozen puff pastry, thawed
- 1 (6-8 ounce) wheel Brie, cut into 12 cubes
- 1 egg, beaten
- Coarse sugar for sprinkling (optional)
- Flaky sea salt and honey for serving (optional)

## Directions

- Combine cranberries, brown sugar, cinnamon, sage, and orange juice in a medium pot over high heat. Bring to a boil and break down cranberries with a fork. Simmer 10 minutes until thickened by one-third. Remove sage, stir in orange zest and salt.
- Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Roll out both puff pastry sheets to approximately 12x9 inches. Cut into thirds along creases, then cut each into 4 pieces for 24 rectangles total. Top half the rectangles with one brie cube and about 1 teaspoon cranberry jam. Brush edges with beaten egg. Cover with remaining rectangles and seal by crimping with a fork. Place on baking sheet and freeze 20 minutes or refrigerate until ready to bake.
- Brush tops with beaten egg, make 2 small slits, and lightly sprinkle with coarse sugar. Bake 15-20 minutes until pastry is golden and brie has melted.
