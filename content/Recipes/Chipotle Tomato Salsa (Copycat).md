## Ingredients

- 1 pound tomatoes, cut into 1/4-inch pieces
- 1/4 cup finely chopped red onion (from 1 small)
- 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh cilantro (from 1 bunch)
- 3 jalapeño peppers, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- Salt to taste
- Tortilla chips for serving

## Directions

- In a medium bowl, combine tomatoes, red onion, cilantro, jalapeño, lemon juice, and lime juice.
- Add salt to taste.
- Stir all ingredients together until combined.
- Serve with tortilla chips.
- Yield: about 2 cups. Prep time: 10 minutes.
