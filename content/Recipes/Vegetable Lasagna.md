## Ingredients

**Veggies and Spinach**
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 large carrots, chopped (about 1 cup)
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1 medium zucchini, chopped
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 5 to 6 ounces baby spinach

**Tomato Sauce**
- 1 large can (28 ounces) diced tomatoes
- ¼ cup roughly chopped fresh basil, plus more for garnish
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes

**Remaining Ingredients**
- 2 cups (16 ounces) low-fat cottage cheese, divided
- ¼ teaspoon salt, to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 9 no-boil lasagna noodles
- 8 ounces (2 cups) freshly grated low-moisture, part-skim mozzarella cheese

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Warm the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add carrots, bell pepper, zucchini, onion, and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are golden on the edges, 8–12 minutes.
3. Add the spinach in batches, stirring frequently until wilted, about 3 minutes total. Remove from heat.
4. Drain the canned tomatoes in a mesh sieve for about a minute. Transfer to a food processor with the basil, olive oil, garlic, salt, and red pepper flakes.
5. Pulse about 10 times until the tomatoes reach a spreadable consistency. Transfer to a bowl (should yield just over 2 cups). Set aside.
6. Blend half the cottage cheese (1 cup) in the food processor until smooth, about 1 minute. Transfer to a large mixing bowl.
7. Pulse the cooked vegetables and spinach in the food processor 5–7 times until finely chopped but not puréed. Transfer to the cottage cheese mixture. Add the remaining cottage cheese, ¼–½ teaspoon salt, and black pepper. Stir to combine.
8. Spread ½ cup tomato sauce on the bottom of a 9"×9" baking dish. Layer 3 noodles (snap the ends to fit). Spread half the cottage cheese mixture over the noodles. Top with ¾ cup sauce and ½ cup mozzarella.
9. Add 3 more noodles, then the remaining cottage cheese mixture and ½ cup mozzarella (no sauce in this layer).
10. Top with the final 3 noodles, ¾ cup sauce, and 1 cup mozzarella.
11. Cover with parchment paper or foil (don't let it touch the cheese). Bake covered 18 minutes. Remove the cover, rotate the pan 180°, and continue baking 10–15 minutes until the top is spotty brown.
12. Cool 15–20 minutes. Garnish with fresh basil, slice, and serve.
