Handwritten on the back of a postcard mailed to [[Wendy Nash]] at 326 East 34th St, Apt D4, New York, NY 10016 — postmarked Niagara Falls, NY, January 6, 1995, signed "Love you, K." Original postcard scan:

![[Dill Shrimp Recipe (postcard, 1995).pdf]]

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups mayonnaise
- ⅓ cup lemon juice
- ¼ cup sugar
- ½ cup sour cream
- 1 large red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 Tbsp. dried dill
- ½ tsp. salt
- 2 lbs. cooked jumbo shrimp

## Directions

- Mix together all ingredients. Add shrimp. Chill overnight or for a few hours.

## Notes

- Sent as a handwritten recipe on the back of a "Square Cards KA 762" postcard (Buffalo Bill Cody / USA Easter egg stamps), addressed to Wendy Nash, postmarked Niagara Falls, NY, Jan 6 1995, signed "Love you, K." — K's full identity isn't recorded on the card.
