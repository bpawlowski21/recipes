## Ingredients

**Base**
- ½ cup coconut flour
- 1 cup ground almonds (almond flour)
- ½ cup almond (or peanut) butter
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- 3 tbsp coconut oil
- ½ tsp sea salt, optional

**Topping**
- 1 cup (140g) vegan dark chocolate
- 1–2 tbsp almond or peanut butter, to swirl

## Directions

1. Put all the base ingredients into a large bowl and mix thoroughly. It should be slightly sticky, not crumbly — if the almond butter isn't runny enough, add a bit more oil/syrup.
2. Press the mixture into cupcake cases, packing it down tightly. Refrigerate 30 minutes.
3. Melt the chocolate, pour onto the base, top each one with a little almond/peanut butter, and swirl through. Refrigerate again to set.

## Notes

- Store in the fridge — will last 4–5 days.
