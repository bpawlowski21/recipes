## Ingredients

- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
- 3/4 cup (150 g) sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon almond extract (optional)
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 1/4 cups (295 g) all-purpose flour

### Glaze (Optional)
- 1 cup (125 g) powdered sugar
- 3-4 tablespoons milk
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Sprinkles for decorating

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F.
- Combine butter and sugar in a large bowl; beat with electric mixer until creamed.
- Add vanilla extract, almond extract (if using), and salt; stir until well-combined, scraping bowl sides.
- Add egg yolk and beat until combined.
- With mixer on medium-low, gradually add flour until completely combined, scraping bowl as needed.
- Form approximately 1/2 cup dough into a log.
- Insert dough log into cookie press fitted with desired tip; squeeze cookies onto ungreased baking sheet, spacing 1 inch apart.
- Add sprinkles if desired; bake 9-10 minutes.
- Cool 5-10 minutes on sheet, then transfer to wire rack to cool completely.
- For glaze: whisk powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla until proper consistency; drizzle or dip cookies and top with sprinkles.
