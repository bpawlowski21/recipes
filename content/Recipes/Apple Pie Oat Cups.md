## Ingredients

**Dry**
- 2.5 cups oats (quick, minute, or rolled all work)
- ¼ cup unflavoured collagen powder (protein powder or oat flour also work)
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 2 tsp apple pie spice (or 1.5 tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp nutmeg, ⅛ tsp ginger)
- ¼ tsp salt

**Wet**
- ¼ cup maple syrup
- 1 cup milk of choice
- ½ cup unsweetened apple sauce
- ¼ cup peanut butter
- 1 egg or flax egg
- 1 medium apple, diced

## Directions

1. Bake at 350°F for 24–27 minutes.
