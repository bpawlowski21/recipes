## Ingredients

- 1 cup warm water or milk
- 2¼ teaspoons active dry yeast (1 packet)
- 2 teaspoons honey
- 2½-3 cups all-purpose flour, plus 1 teaspoon (plus more as needed)
- ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
- 7 tablespoons salted butter, at room temperature
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- 3 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- ½ cup light brown sugar
- ¾ cup mini semi-sweet chocolate chips or chopped chocolate

## Directions

1. In a stand mixer bowl with dough hook, combine milk, yeast, and honey. Wait 5-10 minutes until bubbly. Add flour, salt, and 1 tablespoon butter. Knead 3-4 minutes until smooth. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled, 1-2 hours.
2. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line baking sheet with parchment paper.
3. Mix granulated sugar with 1 teaspoon cinnamon in a bowl.
4. Roll dough into a rectangle about ¼ inch thick (16×14 inches). Spread with 4 tablespoons butter and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar. Sprinkle chocolate over half the dough. Fold dough in half over chocolate. Cut into 12 strips, twist each into a loose knot, and place on baking sheet.
5. Melt remaining 2 tablespoons butter and combine with 2 teaspoons cinnamon, brown sugar, and 1 teaspoon flour. Spoon clumps over knots.
6. Bake 8-10 minutes. Remove and spoon any melted sugar back over rolls. Return to oven and bake another 8-10 minutes until golden.
7. Enjoy warm or at room temperature.
