## Ingredients

- 8 ounces dried spaghetti or tonnarelli
- 4 ounces aged pecorino romano, finely grated
- A lot of freshly ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Bring a pot of well-salted water to boil. Cook pasta to one minute shy of package instructions and taste for desired doneness, cooking longer if needed.
2. While pasta cooks, combine all pecorino (except a spoonful for garnish) and lots of freshly ground black pepper in a bowl. Add 1 tablespoon cold water and use an immersion blender to work it into a paste, adding additional cold water one tablespoon at a time as needed. Aim for a thickness resembling cream cheese or frosting — approximately 4 to 5 tablespoons total. Blend thoroughly until smooth. (Alternatively, use a food processor, even grinding the cheese in it if preferred, though this requires longer processing.)
3. Before pasta finishes, scoop out a cup of hot cooking water and set aside. Drain pasta quickly in a colander and immediately transfer the hot pasta to a large bowl. Add three-quarters of the cheese-pepper paste in dollops and toss to combine.
4. Pour in one small ladleful of pasta water and toss vigorously to loosen the paste into a lightly creamy consistency coating the spaghetti. Taste and add remaining cheese-pepper paste if desired. Add more pasta water only as needed.
5. Finish with reserved pecorino and a few grinds of black pepper. Serve immediately.
