## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup (142 g) light brown sugar, packed
- 2/3 cup (131 g) granulated sugar
- 8 tablespoons (113 g) unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup (92 g) vegetable shortening
- 3/4 teaspoon table salt
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon almond extract (optional)
- 1 teaspoon cider vinegar or white vinegar
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 large egg
- 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 cups (340 g) semisweet chocolate chips

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F and lightly grease two baking sheets.
- Combine sugars, butter, shortening, salt, extracts, vinegar, and baking soda in a large bowl, beating until smooth.
- Mix in the egg, beating thoroughly and scraping bowl sides.
- Blend in flour, then chocolate chips.
- Scoop 1 1/4" dough balls onto sheets with 2" spacing between them.
- Optionally sprinkle sea salt on cookies before baking.
- Bake 11-12 minutes until edges are chestnut brown and tops are light golden.
- Cool cookies on pan until they set, then transfer to storage.
- Keep in airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze for extended storage.
