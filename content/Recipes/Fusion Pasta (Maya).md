Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post by the same author as the other "Maya" recipes in this vault ("Veganuary Nr. 11"). "This is inspired by @gnochgnoch and just trust me... it hits the spot!" Prep time: 25 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 2–3 tbsp sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 onion
- A thumbsize piece of ginger
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- ½ lemon
- 1 tbsp maple syrup or agave
- 200g pasta/noodles
- Optional: chopped parsley and 3 tbsp vegan Parmesan

## Directions

1. Sauté the onion first, then add the ginger and garlic.
2. Stir in the tomato paste, soy sauce, lemon, and maple syrup/agave.
3. Cook the pasta, then add it to the pan with some pasta water.
4. Top with parsley and vegan Parmesan if using.
