## Ingredients

**Ginger Syrup:**
- 1/2 cup honey
- 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced
- 1-2 jalapeños, sliced (to taste)

**Cocktail:**
- Salted chili sugar for rim (1/4 cup sea salt, 1-2 tsp sugar, 1 tsp chili powder, mixed)
- 1 1/2 oz tequila
- 1/2 oz mezcal (or additional tequila)
- Juice from 1/2 lemon
- 1/3 cup pomegranate or cranberry juice
- Sparkling water, for topping

## Directions

1. Make ginger syrup by bringing 1/2 cup water, honey, ginger, and jalapeños to a boil. Boil 2-3 minutes, then cool. (Makes enough for about 6 drinks.)
2. Rim glass with the salt-sugar-chili mixture, then fill with ice.
3. In a cocktail shaker, combine 2-3 tablespoons ginger syrup, tequila, mezcal, lemon juice, and pomegranate juice. Add ice and shake vigorously.
4. Strain into prepared glass filled with ice. Top with sparkling water. Garnish with mint.
