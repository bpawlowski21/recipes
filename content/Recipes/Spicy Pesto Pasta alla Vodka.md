## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 shallot, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1-2 teaspoons crushed red pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup tomato paste
- 1/4 cup vodka
- 3/4 cup basil pesto (homemade or store-bought)
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 cup heavy cream or canned coconut milk
- 1 pound short cut pasta
- 3 tablespoons salted butter
- 1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese, plus more for serving
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, roughly chopped

## Directions

- Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add shallot, garlic, and oregano; cook until shallots begin to caramelize (about 5 minutes). Reduce heat to low, add tomato paste and red pepper flakes; cook 4-5 minutes. Stir in vodka, cook 2 minutes. Stir in pesto and cream. Season with salt and pepper. Keep warm over low heat.
- Bring salted water to a boil in a large pot. Add pasta and cook according to package directions until al dente. Before draining, reserve 1 1/2 cups pasta water. Drain pasta.
- Add pasta, 1/2 cup pasta cooking water, and butter to the vodka sauce; toss until butter melts. Add Parmesan and basil. Thin sauce with additional pasta water if needed.
- Divide pasta among plates and top with basil and cheese. Serve immediately.
