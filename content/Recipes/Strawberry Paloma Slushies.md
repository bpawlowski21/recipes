## Ingredients

- 4 limes
- 1/4 cup (85 g) honey or agave syrup
- 3 to 4 cups strawberries
- 1 cup (240 ml) tequila
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 4 cups (720 g) ice cubes

## Directions

1. Peel three limes by removing tops and bottoms, then cutting away remaining peel and pith in wide strips. Discard peels. Slice the remaining lime thinly.
2. Place peeled limes in a blender with honey or agave, strawberries, tequila, and salt. Blend until combined.
3. Add ice in batches and blend until smooth and completely chilled.
4. Pour into four glasses and garnish with lime slices. Serve immediately.
