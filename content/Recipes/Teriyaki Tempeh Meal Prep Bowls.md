Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post (comments section, 2 slides). Meal-prep bowls with homemade teriyaki sauce.

Macros per portion (makes 3): 544 cals, 33P, 78C, 12F.

## Ingredients

**Teriyaki Sauce**
- 340g tempeh (1.5 package)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1.5 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 inch cube grated ginger
- 3 tbsp water + ¾ tbsp cornstarch, mixed together

**Bowl**
- 1 small red onion
- 1 green pepper
- 1 red pepper
- 1.5 cups chopped broccoli
- 1 cup baby corn (1 can)
- Salt & pepper to taste
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- 0.75 cups cooked brown rice per meal
- Optional: sesame seeds & green onions

## Directions

1. Make the teriyaki sauce: add the sauce ingredients into a pan, mix, and bring to a simmer, then thicken with the cornstarch slurry. Cook a few minutes, stirring continuously, until thickened.
2. Add the tempeh and toss to coat.
3. Cook your veggies of choice with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
4. Divide into three containers along with 0.75 cups of cooked brown rice in each portion, and top with sesame seeds and green onions.

## Notes

- Tip: try adding chili flakes for extra spice.
