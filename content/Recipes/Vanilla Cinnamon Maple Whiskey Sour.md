## Ingredients

- 2 ounces (1/4 cup) bourbon
- 1 ounce (2 tablespoons) fresh lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup
- 1 dash vanilla extract (about 1/4 teaspoon)
- 1 pinch cinnamon (about 1/8 teaspoon)
- Cinnamon stick and rosemary, for garnish

**Vanilla-Sugared Rosemary Garnish**
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla bean seeds
- Fresh rosemary sprigs

## Directions

- Add bourbon, lemon juice, maple syrup, vanilla extract, and cinnamon to a cocktail shaker with ice. Shake well and strain into a glass.
- Garnish with vanilla-sugared rosemary and cinnamon stick; optionally ignite the cinnamon stick for a smoky effect.
- For the garnish: mix sugar with vanilla bean seeds, lightly dampen rosemary sprigs with water, coat thoroughly in the vanilla sugar, and let dry on a parchment-lined baking sheet for 1 hour (can be made 1 day ahead).
- Mocktail: omit bourbon and substitute 2 ounces apple cider.
