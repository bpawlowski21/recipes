"Try my delicious Holiday White Cosmopolitan for a chic holiday cocktail. Perfect for parties with its festive and balanced flavor."

Handwritten substitution note (signed "MH"). Original scan:

![[Holiday White Cosmopolitan (annotated).pdf]]

**Equipment:** Cocktail shaker, jigger, citrus juicer, cocktail pick for garnish, bowl for sugared cranberries, wire rack for sugared cranberries

## Ingredients

- 1.5 ounce vodka *(handwritten: citrus vodka, if on hand)*
- 1 ounce white cranberry juice
- ¾ ounce orange liqueur
- ½ ounce lime juice, freshly squeezed
- Ice

**Sugared Cranberries**
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- Fresh cranberries
- Simple syrup

## Directions

**Sugared Cranberries**
1. Add a small splash of simple syrup to a bowl, then add fresh cranberries to the bowl and gently swirl to coat them in the syrup.
2. Remove cranberries from the bowl, using a slotted spoon. Place the coated cranberries to a wired rack, then allow them to dry for a few minutes so they're not completely wet.
3. Add sugar to a bowl, then add the cranberries and roll them in the sugar until they're coated. Place them on the wired rack again and allow to completely dry.

**Holiday White Cosmopolitan**
1. Place a coupe glass or martini glass in the freezer to chill.
2. Add vodka, white cranberry juice, fresh lime juice, and orange liqueur to a cocktail shaker. Add ice, then shake until chilled.
3. Strain into your chilled martini glass or coupe glass. Garnish with three sugared cranberries on a cocktail pick.
