## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup sunflower oil or avocado oil
- 2 large green bell peppers, stemmed, cored, and sliced
- 1 large red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt to taste

## Directions

- In a large skillet over medium-high heat, warm the oil until it shimmers. Add the peppers, onions, oregano, and about 1/2 teaspoon of salt.
- Cook while stirring occasionally until the vegetables are softened but retain a crisp-tender texture, about 7 minutes. Transfer to a serving dish and serve hot.
- Servings: 4 (1/2 cup each). Total time: 15 minutes (5 min prep, 10 min cook).
