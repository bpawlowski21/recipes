## Ingredients

- 1 cup (8 oz/225 g) butter, melted and cooled
- 2 cups (12 oz/340 g) dark brown sugar, loosely packed
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 cups (10 oz/284 g) all-purpose flour
- 1½ cups (9 oz/255 g) white chocolate chips

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Butter and line an 8-inch square tin with parchment paper.
- In a medium bowl, whisk together melted butter and brown sugar until combined with no lumps.
- Whisk in eggs, vanilla extract, and salt.
- Using a rubber spatula, fold in flour, then white chocolate chips.
- Spread batter evenly into prepared pan and bake approximately 30 minutes, until the top is golden brown and the middle is just set.
- Allow to cool completely before removing from tin.
- Cut into 9 squares and serve. Store in an airtight container at room temperature up to 3 days, or freeze up to 4 weeks.
