## Ingredients

- 1 pound part skim mozzarella, shredded
- 8 ounces feta cheese, crumbled
- 2 cups shredded provolone cheese
- 3 large eggs
- 2 cups shredded Tuscan kale
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 pound pizza dough (homemade or store-bought)
- 1/2 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh chopped dill
- 2 cloves garlic, grated
- Crushed red pepper flakes
- 2 tablespoons salted butter, at room temperature

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 450°F. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Combine mozzarella, feta, and provolone in a medium bowl. Add 1 egg and mix well.
3. Massage kale with 1 tablespoon olive oil, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.
4. Divide pizza dough in half. Roll each into a rough oval (about 10 inches). Transfer to baking sheets. Spoon cheese mixture into centers, leaving 1-inch borders. Fold edges over to form "boats." Top with kale.
5. Bake 10 minutes until crust is golden and cheese melts. Crack an egg into each center. Bake another 3-5 minutes until egg white is barely set.
6. Combine remaining olive oil, basil, parsley, dill, garlic, crushed red pepper, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.
7. Remove from oven. Add 1 tablespoon butter to each center; stir butter and egg into cheese. Brush herb sauce over warm khachapuri. Serve immediately.
