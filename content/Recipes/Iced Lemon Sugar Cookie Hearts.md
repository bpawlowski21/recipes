## Ingredients

**Lemon Cookies:**
- 1 cup (227g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1 cup (199g) granulated sugar
- 2 large egg yolks, room temperature
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 2 teaspoons lemon zest, finely grated
- 3 cups (360g) all-purpose flour, plus more for kneading
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

**Lemon Glaze:**
- 2 cups (227g) confectioners' sugar, sifted
- 1/4 cup (57ml) lemon juice, freshly squeezed
- 2 teaspoons lemon zest, finely grated
- 1/4 cup yellow cookie decorating sugar (optional)

## Directions

- Combine butter and sugar in mixer bowl; beat on medium until combined, then medium-high until pale and fluffy (about 1 minute).
- Reduce to medium speed; add egg yolks and egg, beating until well combined. Beat in lemon zest.
- On low speed, gradually add flour and salt; beat until combined.
- Sprinkle flour on work surface; scrape dough onto surface and knead gently for 30 seconds. Divide in half and press each into a 6-inch disc. Wrap tightly and refrigerate at least 3 hours (or up to 2 days).
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Remove dough from fridge; let sit 15 minutes. Roll one disc to 1/4-inch thickness on floured surface.
- Cut hearts with cookie cutters; transfer to baking sheets with 1-inch spacing.
- Bake 10 minutes until set but not golden. Cool on sheet 15 minutes, then transfer to cooling rack.
- Repeat with remaining dough. Chill and reroll scraps as needed.
- For glaze: Whisk confectioners' sugar, lemon juice, and zest in shallow bowl until thick but pourable.
- Transfer glaze to piping bag with fine tip. Outline each heart, let set 1-2 minutes, then fill center. Sprinkle with colored sugar.
