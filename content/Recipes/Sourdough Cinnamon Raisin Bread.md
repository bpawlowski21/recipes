## Ingredients

**Dough:**
- 1/2 cup (113g) sourdough starter, ripe or discard
- 3 cups (360g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 2 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1 1/4 teaspoons (8g) table salt
- 1 large egg
- 5 tablespoons (71g) unsalted butter, softened
- 2/3 cup (150g) water, lukewarm

**Filling:**
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1 large egg, beaten with 1 tablespoon water
- 1/2 cup (75g) raisins

## Directions

- Combine all dough ingredients and knead to form a soft, smooth dough (6-8 minutes on medium-low speed).
- Place dough in greased container; allow to rise 1.5-2 hours until doubled.
- While rising, prepare filling by mixing sugar, cinnamon, and flour.
- Deflate dough and transfer to lightly greased work surface.
- Roll dough into approximately 6" x 20" rectangle.
- Brush with egg wash; sprinkle filling and raisins, leaving 1" bare strip on short edge.
- Roll dough into log starting from covered end; seal ends and long seam.
- Transfer seam-side down to greased 9" x 5" loaf pan; cover and let rise 1 hour.
- Preheat oven to 350°F.
- Brush loaf top with egg wash if desired; bake 40-45 minutes, tenting after 15-20 minutes. Interior should reach 190°F.
- Remove from oven, loosen edges, turn out of pan. Brush with butter if desired.
- Cool completely before slicing; store wrapped at room temperature for several days or freeze.
