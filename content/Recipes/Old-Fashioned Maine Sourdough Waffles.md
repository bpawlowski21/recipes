## Ingredients

**Starter:**
- 1 cup (120g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1 cup (113g) King Arthur Golden Wheat Flour
- 2 tablespoons (25g) granulated sugar
- 2 cups (454g) buttermilk
- 1 cup (227g) unfed sourdough starter or discard

**Batter:**
- 2 large eggs
- 4 tablespoons (57g) melted butter or 4 tablespoons (50g) vegetable oil
- 3/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda

## Directions

- Mix flours and sugar in a medium bowl.
- Stir in buttermilk and sourdough starter. Cover with plastic wrap and let sit at room temperature overnight.
- Beat together eggs, butter (or oil), salt, and baking soda. Stir into the starter mixture; it will immediately begin forming bubbles.
- Place a rack on a baking sheet, put it in the oven, and preheat to 200°F.
- Preheat a waffle iron, grease when hot, and cook according to manufacturer's instructions until golden brown.
- Transfer finished waffles to the oven to keep warm while cooking remaining batter.
- Freeze leftover waffles for several weeks and reheat in the toaster as needed.
