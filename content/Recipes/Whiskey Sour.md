## Ingredients

- 2 ounces whiskey (bourbon preferred)
- 1 ounce fresh lemon juice
- 3/4 to 1 ounce simple syrup (maple syrup can substitute)
- Ice
- Garnish: orange peel and cocktail cherry

## Directions

- Add the whiskey, lemon juice, and simple syrup to a cocktail shaker filled with ice and shake until very cold.
- Strain into a lowball or Old Fashioned glass. Serve with ice, an orange peel, and a cocktail cherry.
