## Ingredients

**Squash**
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 cups 1/2-inch cubes peeled butternut squash (1 1/2 pounds; from a 2 1/4-pound whole squash)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper, to taste

**Dressing**
- 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 clove garlic, grated or smashed into a paste
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 3 tablespoons olive oil

**Salad**
- 5 cups baby arugula
- 1/2 cup pomegranate seeds (or finely diced green apple if you can't find pom seeds) — Wendy's note: to peel a pomegranate, do it in a bowl of water, or just buy pre-packaged seeds
- 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds, lightly toasted
- 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese (4 ounces)
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Directions

- Roast the squash: preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil and pour 2 tablespoons of the oil on the lined sheet. Place the oiled sheet in the oven (nothing on it) and heat until very hot but not smoking, about 8 minutes.
- In a large bowl, toss the squash with the remaining 1 tablespoon oil, the salt, pepper, and cayenne until coated. Using an oven mitt, remove the hot baking sheet and quickly pour the squash onto the sheet (it should sizzle); make sure the flat sides of the squash hit the hot oiled pan. Roast until the undersides are golden, about 15 minutes. Remove the squash, shake the pan (or flip with tongs), return to the oven, and roast until the new underside is golden brown, 10 to 15 minutes more. Let cool to room temp or just warm.
- Make the dressing: in a screw-top jar, shake the vinegar, mustard, honey, garlic, salt, pepper, and oil until creamy. (If it separates, just shake it again.)
- Make the salad: arrange the arugula on a platter. Top with the roasted squash, pomegranate seeds, and pumpkin seeds. Scatter the onion and goat cheese on top. Drizzle with the dressing to taste and sprinkle with pepper.

## Notes

- Serves 4-6.
- Wendy has made this for Thanksgiving in 2022, 2023, and 2024 — her note calls it a "favorite Thanksgiving salad," adding "very good, doesn't always get eaten."
- Wendy's substitution note: Asian pear, cut into small cubes, can stand in for the pomegranate/apple.
