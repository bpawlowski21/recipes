Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post ("Veganuary 15/25 — 25 recipes in under 25 mins: Dubu Jorim (두부조림)"). "This is a very very simple yet delicious tofu recipe from Korea. I recommend serving it with rice and garlic eggplant." Prep time: 25 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 400g tofu, pat dry, cut in thick slices
- 3 cloves garlic
- A thumbsize piece of ginger
- 2 spring onions
- 1 tbsp chili flakes
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- ¼ cup (60ml) soy sauce
- ¼ cup (60ml) water
- 2 tbsp syrup or sugar

## Directions

1. Fry the tofu slices in oil on both sides until golden.
2. Finely chop the garlic, ginger, and spring onion.
3. Mix the chili flakes, sesame oil, soy sauce, water, and syrup/sugar together with the chopped garlic, ginger, and spring onion.
4. Add the sauce to the tofu and let it simmer 5–10 minutes.

## Notes

- Recommended to serve with rice and garlic eggplant.
