## Ingredients

**Pancakes**
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
- 1/8 teaspoon ground ginger
- 2 tablespoons chopped walnuts (optional)
- 2 tablespoons golden raisins (optional)
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup buttermilk, plus more if needed
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 2 cups finely grated carrots (about 3/4 pound)
- 3 tablespoons butter for griddle

**Cream Cheese Topping**
- 4 ounces cream cheese, room temperature
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 2 to 3 tablespoons milk
- 1/2 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- Dash of ground cinnamon

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 200°F and position rack in upper third to keep finished pancakes warm.
2. Whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and optional nuts and raisins in a large bowl.
3. In a separate small bowl, whisk together egg, brown sugar, buttermilk, and vanilla. Stir in grated carrots, then combine with dry ingredients until just incorporated. Let rest five minutes.
4. While batter rests, prepare topping: beat cream cheese until fluffy and lump-free. Whisk in powdered sugar, two tablespoons milk, vanilla, and cinnamon. Add remaining milk if desired for thinner consistency.
5. Melt one tablespoon butter over medium heat in a cast-iron skillet or griddle. Adjust batter consistency with additional buttermilk if needed.
6. Spoon 2-3 tablespoons batter per pancake onto the hot pan. Flip once, cooking approximately 2 minutes per side until golden.
7. Transfer cooked pancakes to warm serving dish in oven. Repeat with remaining batter, adding more butter as needed.
8. Serve warm with cream cheese topping.
