## Ingredients

- 4 large leeks
- Olive oil
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- 4 large garlic cloves, minced
- 300ml oat milk
- 100ml white wine
- 1 sprig rosemary
- 3 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 2 x 400g cans cannellini beans, drained & rinsed
- Sea salt & freshly ground black pepper
- Crusty bread, to serve

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan assisted)/gas 7.
2. Trim the top from the leeks at the point they start to flare (save the tops for other recipes). Place on a baking tray and roast 35–40 minutes, or until fully charred on the outside.
3. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in an oven-proof pan over medium heat. Once hot, add the flour and garlic and whisk vigorously to form a paste. Continue whisking about a minute, to cook off the flour.
4. Add the oat milk a little at a time, whisking continuously, until smooth. Add the white wine and rosemary sprig, then bring to a gentle simmer and cook, stirring frequently, 5–6 minutes, until thickened and glossy. Remove the rosemary, then stir in the nutritional yeast. Season to taste with salt and black pepper.
5. Chop each burnt leek in half lengthways and use a spoon to scoop out the soft flesh in the middle. Roughly chop the leek flesh and add it to the sauce along with the cannellini beans. Stir well to combine, then transfer the whole pan into the oven and bake 15–20 minutes, until golden on top. Serve with crusty bread and a good crack of black pepper.
