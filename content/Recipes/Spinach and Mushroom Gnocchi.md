## Ingredients

- 1 package gnocchi (17.5 oz)
- 1 can coconut milk (14 oz)
- ½ cup vegetable broth
- 16 oz mushrooms
- 8 oz spinach (about 8 handfuls)
- Drizzle of olive oil
- 1 small onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- ¼ cup nutritional yeast
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Directions

1. In a pan over medium heat, drizzle a generous amount of olive oil. Add the chopped onion and minced garlic. Cook 3–4 minutes, until they start to caramelize.
2. Add the chopped mushrooms, coconut milk, and vegetable broth. Cook 5–6 minutes, until the mushrooms start to soften (test by gently sticking a fork in them).
3. Add the nutritional yeast, spinach, and salt and pepper. Cook until the spinach has fully softened and darkened in color. Remove from heat.
4. Cook the gnocchi according to package instructions (or use a different pasta if preferred).
5. Drain the gnocchi and add to the sauce, mixing well.
6. Serve and enjoy — top with vegan parmesan if desired.
