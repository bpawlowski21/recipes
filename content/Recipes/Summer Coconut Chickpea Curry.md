## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup + 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 can (14 ounce) chickpeas, drained
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 2 zucchini or summer squash, diced
- 2 ears sweet corn, kernels removed
- 1 shallot, chopped
- 1 inch fresh ginger, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1 1/2 tablespoons yellow curry powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 can (14 ounce) coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons tahini
- Juice and zest from 1/2 a lemon
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
- 8 ounces Halloumi cheese, sliced into 1/4 inch pieces
- 2 cups cooked basmati rice
- Sesame seeds, green onions, and Persian cucumbers for serving (optional)

## Directions

1. Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add chickpeas, season with salt and pepper, and cook until beginning to crisp (about 5 minutes). Reserve 1/2 cup chickpeas for topping if desired.
2. Add zucchini, corn, shallot, garlic, and ginger to the remaining chickpeas. Season with salt and pepper. Cook 5-10 minutes until vegetables begin to soften.
3. Stir in curry powder and cayenne; cook until fragrant (about 1 minute). Add coconut milk, 1/3-1/2 cup water, and tahini. Bring to a simmer and cook 5-10 minutes until sauce thickens. Add water if needed. Remove from heat and stir in lemon juice, zest, and cilantro.
4. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add halloumi and cook until golden (about 3 minutes per side).
5. Divide cooked rice among bowls, spoon curry over top, and garnish with halloumi, reserved chickpeas, and cucumbers.
