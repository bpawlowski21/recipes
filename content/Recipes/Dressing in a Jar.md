## Ingredients

- 1–2 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp minced shallot
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- ¾ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp maple syrup
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- ⅓ cup olive oil

## Directions

1. Combine all ingredients in a jar, seal, and shake to emulsify.
