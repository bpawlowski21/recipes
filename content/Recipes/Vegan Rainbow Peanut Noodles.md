Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram comment screenshot. A different, veggie-loaded peanut noodle recipe from the other saved [[Peanut Sesame Cucumber Noodle Salad]] and [[Spicy Peanut Sesame Noodles with Edamame]], also by healthygirlkitchen.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz whole wheat thin spaghetti (or any noodle you like)
- 1 cup shredded purple cabbage
- 1 cup carrot ribbons
- 1 cup baby spinach
- 1.5 cups bell pepper strips
- 1 cup shelled cooked edamame
- ½ cup cilantro

**Secret healthygirl peanut sauce**
- ½ cup peanut butter
- 2 tsp sriracha
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- ¼ cup water
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp garlic
- 1 tsp ginger

## Directions

1. Cook the noodles, prep the veggies, mix the sauce ingredients together, then combine!
