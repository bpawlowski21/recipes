A thick, creamy and flavorful smoothie bowl packed with fruits, veggies, antioxidants and plant-powered protein for a filling and delicious breakfast!

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cups unsweetened vanilla almond milk
- 1 cup frozen blueberries
- 1 cup frozen spinach
- 1 tbsp cacao powder
- 1 tbsp hemp seeds
- 1 Medjool date (optional for sweetness, or try stevia/banana/maple syrup to taste)
- 1 scoop vanilla brown rice protein
- Optional: 1 tsp spirulina, dash vanilla extract
- Toppings of choice: cacao nibs, fresh fruit, granola, cereal, coconut, goji berries, etc.

## Directions

1. Place all ingredients in a high-speed blender and blend until smooth and creamy. It will be thick.
2. Pour into bowl and top with desired toppings.
