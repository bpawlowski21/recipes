## Ingredients

- One bar (3.5oz/100g) Lindt Dark Chocolate, chopped
- One bar (3.5oz/100g) Lindt Milk Chocolate, chopped
- 2/3 cup (160ml) cream—heavy, light or half & half

**Accompaniments**
- Pound cake cut into cubes
- Fresh fruit: grapes, strawberries, bananas, apples, pears, pineapple
- Dried fruit: apricots, pineapple, peaches, papaya, mango
- Marshmallows

## Directions

- In a saucepan, heat the cream just until it starts to boil.
- Remove the saucepan from the heat and add the chopped chocolate.
- Stir until melted.
- Pour the chocolate mixture into the fondue pot and set on the table to enjoy.
