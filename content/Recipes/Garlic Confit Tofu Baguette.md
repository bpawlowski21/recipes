Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram comment screenshot (intro not fully captured, but describes shaved/marinated tofu with roasted-garlic mayo on a toasted baguette — "the toasted baguette, pickled veggies, and fresh fixings take this sammie over the top").

## Ingredients

- 1 block super firm tofu, drained and pressed
- Salt, pepper, and olive oil, as specified below
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 bulb garlic, sliced in half lengthwise
- 1 cup vegan mayo
- ½ lemon, juiced
- Pickled veggies of your choosing (post used watermelon radish and carrot)
- Baby arugula
- Tomato slices
- Toasted baguette rolls

## Directions

1. Flip the tofu on its side and use a vegetable peeler to shave off thin strips. It's okay if some pieces break apart or crumble. Add the tofu to a large mixing bowl with 2 tbsp olive oil, soy sauce, maple syrup, smoked paprika, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Mix together and set aside 1 hour.
2. Preheat oven to 350°F. Add the garlic bulb to a cooking vessel with 3 tbsp olive oil and salt. Roast 45 minutes.
3. Once cooled, squeeze the garlic into a bowl with the mayo, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Use a whisk to smash the cloves and mix the mayo together.
4. Add the tofu to a parchment-lined tray in one even sheet. Roast 35 minutes at 350°F.
5. Take out and add to the toasted baguette with the mayo and all the remaining fixings. Wrap in parchment, slice in half, and dig in.
