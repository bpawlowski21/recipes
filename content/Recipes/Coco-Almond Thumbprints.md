## Ingredients

**Macaroons:**
- 2 cups (200g) sliced or slivered almonds
- 1 cup (120g) shredded sweetened coconut
- 1/2 cup (100g) sugar
- 2 large egg whites

**Chocolate Filling:**
- 3 ounces (85g) semisweet or bittersweet chocolate, chopped
- 3 tablespoons heavy cream

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Prepare baking sheet with parchment paper or mat.
- Combine almonds, coconut, and sugar in food processor, pulsing until nuts are ground with some larger pieces remaining.
- Gradually add egg whites while pulsing until dough holds together when squeezed.
- Drop spoonfuls onto baking sheet, roll into balls, and create indentations using your finger or wooden spoon.
- Bake 14-16 minutes, rotating midway, until firm and golden on bottom.
- For filling: Heat chocolate and cream together until melted; stir until smooth.
- Spoon chocolate ganache into each cookie's indent, allowing it to set at room temperature or refrigerate 10-20 minutes.
