## Ingredients

**Graham cracker crust**
- 1½ cups graham cracker crumbs (about 12 full sheets)
- ½ cup salted butter, melted
- 5 tablespoons granulated sugar
- Pinch salt

**Lemon cheesecake**
- 3 packages (8 ounces each) cream cheese, room temperature
- 8 ounces sour cream, room temperature
- 1¼ cups granulated sugar
- 4 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup lemon curd

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Line a 9 or 10-inch springform pan with parchment paper on the bottom and grease the sides.
2. Combine graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, sugar, and salt. Press firmly into the prepared pan as an even layer.
3. Fill a 9x13-inch pan with water and place in oven to create a water bath for the cheesecake.
4. Beat softened cream cheese, sour cream, and sugar together for 2 minutes until smooth. Add vanilla extract, then eggs one at a time, beating until just combined.
5. Pour batter over crust. Drop pan on counter to remove air bubbles. Bake for 55 minutes at middle rack, rotating halfway through.
6. Turn off oven and open door slightly. Leave cheesecake inside for one hour to cool gradually.
7. Remove from oven, run a knife along edges, and cool on a rack for 1 hour. Pour lemon curd evenly over top. Cover with foil and refrigerate overnight.
8. Release springform pan when serving. Top with whipped cream and lemon zest. Slice into 16 servings.
