## Ingredients

**Polenta Fries**
- 1 cup quick cooking polenta
- 2 cloves garlic, grated
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh sage
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tablespoons salted butter, melted
- 1/4 cup fresh chopped basil
- 2 tablespoons fresh chopped oregano

**Lemon Aioli**
- 1/2 cup avocado oil based mayo
- 1-2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper

## Directions

1. Bring 2 cups water to a boil in a medium saucepan. Slowly whisk in polenta, 1 clove garlic, thyme, sage, 1/4 cup parmesan, and season with salt. Stir until thick, about 3-5 minutes. Cover and let sit 5 minutes.
2. Line an 8×8 baking dish with parchment paper. Spread polenta evenly and freeze 15-20 minutes until set (or 1 hour in the fridge).
3. Preheat oven to 425°F. Remove polenta and cut into 1/4 inch thick fries. Toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Bake 15 minutes, flip carefully, then bake 15-20 minutes more until crispy.
4. Mix melted butter, 1 clove garlic, remaining parmesan, basil, and oregano. Toss hot fries with herb butter.
5. Combine all aioli ingredients and mix until smooth. Serve fries with lemon aioli and ketchup.
