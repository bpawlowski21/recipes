## Ingredients

- 1/2 medium red onion, coarsely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 tablespoons adobo sauce
- 2 tablespoons ancho chile powder (or 1 ounce dried ancho chiles, soaked)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil or rice bran oil, plus more for cooking
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 4 pounds boneless, skinless chicken
- Water (as needed to reach 1 cup marinade)

## Directions

**Make the marinade:**
- Combine red onion, garlic, adobo sauce, ancho chile powder, oil, cumin, oregano, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper in a food processor. Blend until smooth, then add water to reach 1 cup total.
- Place chicken in a freezer-safe bag, pour in marinade, seal, and massage to coat evenly. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes or overnight.

**Cook (choose one method):**
- Stovetop: Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large skillet until shimmering. Cook chicken, turning occasionally, until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
- Grill: Preheat to medium-high heat. Grill chicken in batches, turning occasionally, until 165°F internally (10-15 minutes). Add oil between batches.
- Oven: Preheat to 400°F. Arrange chicken in oiled baking dish, cover with parchment paper, and bake 30-40 minutes until 165°F internally.

**Finish:**
- Rest chicken 10 minutes tented with foil. Chop into pieces and season with salt. Serve in tortillas or bowls with desired toppings.
