Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. "I love dates, dark chocolate and tahini together so I made it into an official dessert."

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups chocolate chips
- 1 tsp coconut oil
- 20 dates, cut into small pieces
- 2–3 tablespoon tahini
- Flaky sea salt, to taste

## Directions

1. Melt together the chocolate chips and coconut oil (the post did this in three 30-second intervals in the microwave).
2. Add the date pieces to a bowl. Pour the chocolate over the dates and mix until evenly coated.
3. Spoon the date-chocolate mixture into small clusters on a parchment-lined sheet pan (a lined muffin tin also works).
4. Sprinkle salt over the clusters and drizzle with tahini.
5. Freeze the clusters for about 10 minutes, or refrigerate about 30 minutes, until set.
