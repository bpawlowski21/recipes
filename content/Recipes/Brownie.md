## Ingredients

- 1 cup unsalted butter (226g)
- 2 cups granulated sugar (400g)
- ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder (75g) — use dutch processed (darker, richer); add 1 tsp espresso powder (marked as a must-do)
- 3 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract (15 mL)
- 1 teaspoon salt — use sea salt, not table salt
- 1 cup all-purpose flour (120g)
- 1½ cups semisweet chocolate chips (270g) — chopped chocolate is preferred, bittersweet or semisweet

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease an 8x8-inch baking pan with baking spray and line it with parchment paper (marked as a must-do).
2. In a large microwave-safe bowl, melt the butter in the microwave in 20-second intervals, stirring between each one until fully melted, about 2 minutes. Add the sugar and cocoa (plus espresso powder) and whisk vigorously for 30 seconds, until the sugar is dissolved.
3. Whisk in the eggs, vanilla, and salt. Add the flour and chocolate chips and mix together with a spatula until just combined. Spread the batter into the prepared pan. (You can sprinkle the top with more chocolate chips, if desired.)
4. Bake for about 35 to 40 minutes, inserting a toothpick into the center to check doneness, and removing it with several moist crumbs for fudgy brownies or only a few crumbs for cakey brownies. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before slicing.

## Notes

- Use a metal baking pan. Using a metal baking pan will have the brownies ready in about 35 to 40 minutes. A glass baking pan will likely take 5 to 10 minutes longer, conduct heat differently, and may cause the brownies to sink in the center. Metal also helps create crisp brownie edges.
- Must line the pan with parchment.
- Handwritten test notes from two separate bakes:
  - "2.11.24 — made for Superbowl. Cut into bite sized squares."
  - "6.7 & 6.11.2024 — for Father's Day, use Guittard bittersweet wafers (red bag at Whole Foods) — or combine with mini chips, did ½ / ½." (A set of initials is written next to this note but is not clearly legible from the scan.)
- Dutch-processed cocoa (darker, richer) plus 1 teaspoon espresso powder is marked as a required addition ("* must"), not optional.
- Use sea salt rather than table salt.
- Chopped chocolate (bittersweet or semisweet) is noted as preferred over standard chocolate chips.
