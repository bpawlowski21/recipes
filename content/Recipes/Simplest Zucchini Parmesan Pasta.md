## Ingredients

- 1 pound pasta (any cut)
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 3-4 medium zucchini/yellow summer squash, shredded or chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh thyme
- Chili flakes, to taste
- Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
- 2 tablespoons salted butter
- 1 cup grated parmesan
- 1/2 cup grated pecorino or manchego cheese
- 1 cup fresh basil, chopped

## Directions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook pasta according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 1/4 cups pasta water before draining.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add zucchini, garlic, thyme, chili flakes, salt, and pepper. Cook until golden, about 5 minutes. Stir in butter and mash the zucchini with a spoon or potato masher until chunky.
3. Add 1 cup reserved pasta water and bring to a simmer. Add cooked pasta, parmesan, and pecorino cheese. Toss until melted, adding more pasta water if needed to thin the sauce.
4. Remove from heat and toss in fresh basil. Serve topped with basil and freshly cracked pepper.
