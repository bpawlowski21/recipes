## Ingredients

**Enchilada Filling:**
- 1 Tbsp avocado oil (or neutral oil/vegan butter)
- 1 cup diced red onion
- 1 medium red or orange bell pepper, diced
- ¼ tsp sea salt
- 1 (15-oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup red enchilada sauce

**Cornbread Topping:**
- 1 cup cornmeal (fine or medium grind)
- ¼ cup unbleached all-purpose flour (or GF flour)
- 1½ tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ⅔ cup unsweetened plain almond milk (or other dairy-free milk)
- 2 Tbsp avocado oil (or melted vegan butter)
- 2–3 Tbsp maple syrup (optional)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C).
- Heat a 10-inch cast-iron or oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Add oil, then sauté onion for 4–5 minutes until translucent and lightly browned.
- Add bell pepper and sea salt; cover and sauté 5 minutes until softened.
- Stir in black beans and enchilada sauce; turn off heat. (Transfer to a baking dish if skillet isn't oven-safe.)
- In a bowl, combine cornmeal, flour, baking powder, and salt. In a measuring cup, whisk dairy-free milk, oil, and maple syrup together.
- Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients; stir until just combined. Let rest 5 minutes.
- Spread cornmeal mixture over filling, leaving about a 1-inch gap around edges.
- Bake 20 minutes until crust is fully set.
- Serve hot. Store leftovers in the refrigerator up to 3–4 days or freeze up to 1 month.
