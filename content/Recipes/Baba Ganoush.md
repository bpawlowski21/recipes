## Ingredients

- 2 eggplants
- 1 garlic head
- Olive oil
- Salt
- ½ cup tahini
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Juice from 1 lemon
- Salt & pepper to taste
- ½ tsp cumin
- ½ tsp paprika
- Cold water

## Directions

1. Cut both eggplants in half, drizzle with olive oil and salt, and place face down on a parchment-lined baking tray. Cut off the top of the garlic head, place on aluminum foil, drizzle with olive oil and salt, and wrap. Place on the baking tray. Bake about 50–60 minutes at 200°C/400°F.
2. Make the tahini sauce by mixing all the tahini ingredients, adding as much water as necessary until smooth and creamy.
3. Once the eggplants and garlic are ready, remove the inside of the eggplants and mash with a knife, and squeeze out the garlic. Add both into the tahini sauce and mix well.
4. Top with more olive oil and chopped parsley. Serve with pita bread (optional). Enjoy!
