"Irresistible chewy, buttery chocolate chip cookies with crisp crunchy caramelized toffee edges and soft chewy insides!"

Prep Time: 10 mins · Cook Time: 12 mins · Chill Time: 30 mins

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (113g) unsalted butter, softened
- ½ cup (100g) granulated sugar
- ½ cup (110g) packed dark brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon (5ml) pure vanilla extract
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 1 cup plus 1 tablespoon (150g) all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 1¼ cups (6oz/170g) dark chocolate wafers, or chopped dark chocolate block

For best results, use a kitchen scale to weigh the ingredients.

## Directions

1. Combine softened butter with both sugars and vanilla extract in a large bowl or the bowl of a stand mixer. Mix by hand using the "creaming method" with a wide rubber spatula to smear the butter and sugar together and mix vigorously until smooth and slightly fluffed. If using a stand mixer, secure the paddle attachment and mix on medium-low speed for 2-3 minutes. It should lighten in color and look quite fluffy. Scrape down the sides of the bowl and mix in the egg until evenly incorporated. The mixture should look a bit whipped at this point.
2. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl and whisk to blend evenly. Add to the butter mixture all at once and mix gently until just combined. Add the chocolate and fold it in until evenly distributed.
3. Cover the dough in the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 1 hour.
4. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Lightly grease two large cookie sheets with soft butter.
5. Use a 1.35-oz cookie scoop to portion dough onto prepared baking trays, leaving about 4 inches of space since they'll spread a lot — only 6 cookies per tray. Do not flatten. Bake for about 10-12 minutes until nicely browned around the edges like the color of copper. The center should be just lightly golden and just started to puff up — that's how you know it won't be completely raw in the middle even though it looks underdone.
6. Remove trays from the oven and transfer to wire racks. Let cookies cool for 2 minutes on trays before transferring individually to the racks to finish cooling.

## Notes

- Recipe by Christina Marsigliese, Founder & Author of Scientifically Sweet.
- Wendy's handwritten adaptations:
  - Uses Guittard Bittersweet Baking Chocolate wafers, or 70% Callebaut dark callets, as the dark chocolate.
  - No need to chill the dough any longer than needed — some spread is desired.
