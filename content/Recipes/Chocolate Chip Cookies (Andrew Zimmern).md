## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 8 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
- 1/2 cup white sugar
- 1/3 cup brown sugar
- 3 tablespoons light corn syrup
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- 1/2 cup pecans, toasted and chopped
- 3/4 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F.
- Sift flour and baking soda together.
- In a standing mixer with paddle attachment, cream butter, sugars, and corn syrup until well combined (about 1 minute).
- Add milk, egg yolk, vanilla, and salt. Beat until mixed, then add dry ingredients. Mix on low speed until batter forms — avoid overbeating.
- Fold in toasted pecans and chocolate chips by hand.
- Drop golf ball-sized portions of batter 2 inches apart on parchment-lined sheets.
- Bake one sheet at a time until brown and flat (approximately 10 minutes).
- Cool on a cookie rack.
