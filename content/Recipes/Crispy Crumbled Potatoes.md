## Ingredients

- 4 medium/large Russet potatoes
- Kosher salt
- 2 cups vegetable or peanut oil
- Finishes of your choice (pecorino and fried rosemary, smoked paprika and aioli, or simply salt)

## Directions

1. **Boil the potatoes:** place potatoes in a medium/large pot, cover with an inch or two of cold water and a big spoonful of salt. Bring to a boil over high heat; once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook until a toothpick or skewer inserts without crunchy resistance, about 45 to 55 minutes for 10-ounce potatoes. Drain and chill in the refrigerator for several hours, overnight, or up to three days.
2. **Crumble and fry:** once potatoes are fully cool in the center, heat oil in a 9-inch heavy frying pan to 350°F. Line a tray with paper towels or a torn paper bag. Messily crumble and tear potatoes with your hands into large and small chunks, including the skin. Add 1/3 to 1/2 of the potatoes to the oil at a time. Fry for 8 to 10 minutes, turning pieces as they brown underneath, until deep golden brown all over. Remove with a slotted spoon, shake off excess oil, and drain on towels, showering immediately with salt. Repeat with remaining potatoes. Eat immediately.
3. **Rewarm** potatoes in a 350°F oven on a large tray if needed.
