## Ingredients

- 6 large eggs, beaten
- 1 1/2 cups whole milk
- 2 tablespoons real maple syrup
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 12 cups roughly torn, day-old croissants (about 12-16 croissants)
- 1 jar (10 ounces) high quality fruit preserves (blueberry, strawberry, or blackberry)
- 8 ounces cream cheese, cut into small cubes
- 6 tablespoons cold salted butter, thinly sliced
- 1 cup fresh berries (blueberries, strawberries, or blackberries)
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Powdered sugar and maple syrup, for serving

## Directions

- Grease a 9x13 inch baking dish with butter.
- Whisk together eggs, milk, maple syrup, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl. Add torn croissants and gently toss. Arrange half the croissants in the prepared dish. Dollop jam and cream cheese over them. Add remaining croissants on top. Pour remaining egg mixture over croissants.
- Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes or up to overnight. Preheat oven to 375°F when ready to bake.
- Lay thinly sliced butter over the croissants. Bake for 45-50 minutes until golden and crisp. Cover loosely with foil if browning too quickly.
- Whip cream until stiff peaks form. Stir in 1 tablespoon powdered sugar or maple syrup if desired.
- Serve warm topped with whipped cream, fresh berries, and maple syrup.
