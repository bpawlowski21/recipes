## Ingredients

### Levain
- 74g medium-protein bread flour
- 15g caster sugar
- 74g water
- 30g ripe sourdough starter (100% hydration)

### Tangzhong
- 41g medium-protein bread flour
- 166g whole milk

### Main Dough
- All tangzhong (from previous step)
- 328g medium-protein bread flour
- 148g high-protein bread flour
- 95g unsalted butter
- 38g caster sugar
- 181g water
- 11g salt
- All levain (from previous step)

### Additional
- 1 large egg + 1 tablespoon whole milk (egg wash)
- Coarse sea salt for topping (optional)

## Directions

- **Prepare levain** (Day One, 9:00 p.m.): Combine levain ingredients. Cover and let ripen at ~77°F for 12 hours overnight.
- **Make tangzhong** (Day Two, 8:00 a.m.): Whisk flour and milk over medium-low heat until thickened into a paste (5-8 min). Spread on a plate to cool.
- **Mix** (9:00 a.m.): Cut butter into ½-inch pats. Combine water, flours, levain, sugar, cooled tangzhong, and salt; mix on low 1-2 min, then medium 4-5 min until strengthened. Rest 10 min. Add butter one pat at a time on low (5-8 min total), then mix on medium 1-2 min until smooth.
- **Bulk fermentation** (9:30 a.m.-1:00 p.m.): At 74-76°F, three sets of stretch-and-folds at 30-min intervals over 3½ hours.
- **Chill** (1:00-1:15 p.m.): Refrigerate uncovered dough 15-25 min to firm for shaping.
- **Shape** (1:15 p.m.): Butter pan. Divide into sixteen 70g pieces, shape as tight balls, arrange in pan, cover.
- **Proof** (1:45-4:15 p.m.): At 74-76°F, proof 2-3 hours until puffy and light.
- **Bake** (4:15 p.m.): Preheat 400°F. Egg wash, sprinkle sea salt. Bake 20 min, rotate, reduce to 350°F, bake 20 more min until golden and internal temp 204°F. Cool 30 min before serving.
