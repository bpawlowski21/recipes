## Ingredients

**Chai Tea Simple Syrup**
- 4 chai tea bags
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

**Chai White Russian**
- 2 ounces (1/4 cup) vodka
- 1 1/2 ounces (3 tablespoons) Kahlua
- 1-2 ounces (2-4 tablespoons) chai tea syrup
- 1-2 ounces (2-4 tablespoons) heavy cream
- Cinnamon sticks and star anise, for garnish

## Directions

**Chai Tea Simple Syrup:**
- In a medium pot, bring 1 cup water to a boil. Remove from heat, add the chai tea bags, cover and steep 10-15 minutes. Remove the tea bags and add the honey. Bring to a boil over high heat. Boil 3-5 minutes, then remove from heat. Stir in the vanilla. Let cool. Keep in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.

**Chai White Russian:**
- Fill a glass with ice. Add the vodka, Kahlua, and chai tea syrup. Stir gently. Add the heavy cream and stir to combine. Garnish with cinnamon and star anise, if desired.
