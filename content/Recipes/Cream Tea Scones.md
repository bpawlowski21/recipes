## Ingredients

- 3 cups (360g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1/4 to 1/3 cup (50g to 67g) granulated sugar, to taste
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon table salt
- 1 1/3 to 1 1/2 cups (301g to 340g) heavy cream or whipping cream, plus additional for brushing
- 1 teaspoon King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 1 to 2 cups (113g to 227g) chopped dried fruit, chocolate chips, nuts, or a combination, optional
- Coarse sparkling sugar for topping

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
- Combine 1 1/3 cups cream with vanilla in a measuring cup. Drizzle over the dry ingredients while tossing gently, adding more cream by the tablespoon as needed until the dough comes together. Stir in optional mix-ins.
- Lightly flour the work surface. Divide dough in half and pat each into a 5 1/2" circle, 3/4" thick.
- Brush circles with cream and sprinkle with sparkling sugar.
- Place on the baking sheet and cut each circle into six wedges, separating them slightly with about 1" spacing.
- Freeze for 15 minutes while the oven preheats to 425°F.
- Bake 14-15 minutes until golden and fully baked through.
- Serve warm, split, and topped with butter and jam or preserves.
- Store cooled scones airtight at room temperature for several days or freeze for longer storage.
