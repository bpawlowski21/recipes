## Ingredients

- 4½ cups sifted all-purpose flour, plus more for rolling
- 1 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt, plus more for sprinkling
- 2 tablespoons mild olive oil
- ¾ cup plus up to ½ cup warm water

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 500°F (260°C). Position a pizza stone on the bottom rack, or use a 10x15-inch baking sheet.
2. Combine flour, salt, oil, and ¾ cup water in a large bowl until dough forms. Add water gradually if needed; avoid making it sticky.
3. Let dough rest 10-15 minutes.
4. Divide dough into 8 pieces. Using a pasta maker, run each piece through repeatedly at decreasing thickness settings until reaching the thinnest setting. Alternatively, roll thin with a rolling pin on a floured surface.
5. Trim rolled dough into rectangles. Prick surface with a fork. Optionally brush lightly with water and sprinkle salt.
6. Place rectangles on pizza stone or baking sheet without touching. Bake 30-90 seconds until golden and bubbly.
7. Flip pieces using tongs; bake 15-30 seconds until the reverse side is golden and lightly blistered. Watch closely to prevent burning.
8. Cool before serving. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to a couple days.
