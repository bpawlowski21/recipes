## Ingredients

- 1 tsp olive oil
- 6-8 baby bok choy, halved lengthwise
- 1 T sesame oil
- 2 T seasoned rice vinegar
- 2 T lite soy sauce or wheat-free tamari (if avoiding gluten)
- 1 T water
- Freshly cracked black pepper
- Sesame seeds for serving

## Directions

- Brush indoor grill pan with olive oil and bring to med-high heat. Place bok choy cut-side down on preheated pan and immediately cover. Steam-grill bok choy for 2-3 minutes or until well-marked and bright green. Meanwhile, whisk together sesame oil, vinegar, soy sauce or tamari, water and black pepper to taste.
- Remove bok choy to plate. Drizzle with vinaigrette to taste and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!

## Notes

- If you're gluten-sensitive, be sure to choose wheat-free tamari over soy sauce. Extra dressing is great drizzled over rice, grilled shrimp, or steamed tofu.
