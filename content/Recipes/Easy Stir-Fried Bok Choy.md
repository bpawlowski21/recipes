## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 large shallots, minced
- 2 pounds baby bok choy, halved or quartered
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper (optional)

## Directions

- Heat vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat, swirling to coat the entire pan surface.
- Add minced garlic and shallots, stirring constantly for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
- Add the bok choy, soy sauce, and sesame oil, tossing to coat all ingredients evenly.
- Cover the pan and steam for 1-2 minutes, then uncover and toss again.
- Re-cover and continue cooking for an additional 3-5 minutes until bok choy reaches desired tenderness.
- Sprinkle with crushed red pepper flakes if desired and serve immediately.
