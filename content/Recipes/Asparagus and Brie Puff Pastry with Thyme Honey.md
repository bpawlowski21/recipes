## Ingredients

- 1 bunch asparagus, ends trimmed
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt and pepper
- 2 sheets frozen puff pastry, thawed
- 8 ounces brie, cut into 8 slices
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 pinch crushed red pepper flakes

**Thyme Honey:**
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 tablespoons salted butter
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Toss asparagus with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
3. Roll out pastry sheets on a floured surface and cut into 8 squares. Place brie on each square, add asparagus, then wrap two corners up and over to enclose. Transfer to baking sheet. Brush with egg and bake 20-25 minutes until golden brown.
4. While baking, melt honey, butter, and thyme together in a small saucepan over low heat.
5. Serve pastries warm, drizzled with thyme honey and topped with crushed red pepper flakes.
