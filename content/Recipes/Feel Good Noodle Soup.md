Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. "Sometimes we just need a little hug in a bowl and this soup can you that!" Prep time: 35 minutes.

## Ingredients

**Tofu sticks**
- 400g tofu, cut in half and scored
- 2 tbsp each soy sauce, white vinegar, maple syrup, and hoisin sauce

**Broth**
- 1 onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- A thumbsize piece of ginger
- 600ml water
- A handful lime leaves (optional)
- 1 tsp gochujang (or 1 tbsp red curry paste)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 3 tbsp canned coconut milk

**Else**
- 4 servings of noodles
- Marinated cucumber (1 tbsp each soy sauce, white vinegar, sugar, and 2 cloves garlic per cucumber)
- Cilantro

## Directions

1. Mix the tofu marinade ingredients well and bake at 200°C/400°F for 25 minutes, brushing with the glaze every now and then.
2. For the broth: fry the onion, garlic, and ginger in oil for a few minutes. Add the water and lime leaves, and let it simmer 5–10 minutes.
3. Mix the gochujang, soy sauce, lime juice, sugar, and coconut milk, and add to the soup. Let it simmer 10 minutes.
4. Serve the broth with noodles, tofu sticks, marinated cucumber, and cilantro, and enjoy.
