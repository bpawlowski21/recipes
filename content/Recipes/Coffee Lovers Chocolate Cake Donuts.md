## Ingredients

**Coffee Chocolate Donuts**
- 3/4 cup Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 1 and 2/3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup light brown sugar, packed
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon espresso powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/3 cup refined coconut oil, melted (canola or vegetable oil may substitute)
- 3/4 cup hot coffee

**Coffee Chocolate Fudge Frosting**
- 7 ounces semi-sweet chocolate, roughly chopped
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon espresso powder

## Directions

**Donuts**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Generously grease donut pan wells. If using only one pan, bake in two batches.
2. Whisk cocoa, flour, both sugars, baking powder, espresso powder, and salt in a large bowl.
3. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together eggs, sour cream, vanilla, and oil.
4. Combine wet and dry ingredients with light stirring. Carefully add hot coffee and stir until just blended.
5. Spoon or pipe batter into prepared pans, filling each well 3/4 to full.
6. Bake 10-12 minutes until risen and lightly springy when touched.
7. Cool in pan several minutes before gently removing and transferring to cooling rack.

**Frosting**
1. Combine chopped chocolate and heavy cream in a microwave-safe bowl.
2. Heat until cream bubbles, about 1 minute.
3. Remove and stir in espresso powder.
4. Whisk until chocolate fully melts and frosting is smooth.
5. Dip or spread frosting onto donut tops.
