## Ingredients

- 1 (15oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 tablespoons plain Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup buffalo sauce
- 4 oz cream cheese
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- Green onions (optional for garnish)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350 degrees
- Combine all ingredients in a food processor and pulse until blended but still chunky
- Transfer mixture to a small baking dish and bake for 20 minutes until heated through
- Optional: top with green onions before serving
