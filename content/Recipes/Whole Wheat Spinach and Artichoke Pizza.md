## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound whole wheat pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 3-4 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice + zest of 1/2 lemon
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- Kosher salt and pepper, to taste
- 2 cups fresh baby spinach
- 1 (12 oz) jar marinated artichoke hearts, drained
- 8 ounces fresh mozzarella, torn
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese or crumbled blue cheese
- 1/2 Meyer lemon, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons raw pine nuts
- Crushed red pepper flakes
- Fresh basil and microgreens, for serving

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 425°F and grease a baking sheet with olive oil.
- On a floured surface, roll dough thin. Transfer to baking sheet and spread with olive oil.
- Distribute garlic, lemon zest, basil, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes evenly across dough.
- Layer spinach, artichokes, and cheeses. Top with lemon slices and pine nuts.
- Bake 10-15 minutes until crust is crispy and cheese melts.
- Remove from oven and finish with lemon juice, fresh basil, and microgreens before serving.
