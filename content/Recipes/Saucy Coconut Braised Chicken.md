## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds boneless chicken thighs or breasts
- 1 tablespoon ground turmeric
- 3-4 tablespoons Thai red curry paste
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt
- 2 shallots, thinly sliced
- 1 zucchini or summer squash, diced
- 1 1/2 cups roughly chopped broccoli florets
- 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1 inch fresh ginger, grated
- 1 can (14 ounce) full fat coconut milk
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 2 teaspoons honey
- 8 ounces rice noodles
- Juice and zest from 1 lime
- 1/4 cup fresh Thai basil and/or mint

## Directions

**Stovetop method**
1. Combine chicken with turmeric, 1 tablespoon curry paste, and 1 tablespoon oil. Season with salt.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear chicken on both sides until browned (about 2 minutes). Remove from pot.
3. Add zucchini, shallots, garlic, ginger, and salt to pot. Cook 5 minutes until softened. Add broccoli, cook 2-3 minutes. Stir in remaining curry paste, cook 1 minute until fragrant. Add coconut milk, 1/2 cup water, chicken, fish sauce, and honey. Stir well. Partially cover and simmer 10-15 minutes until chicken is cooked through. Season to taste.
4. Cook rice noodles according to package directions.
5. Divide noodles among bowls. Top with curry, vegetables, chicken, fresh basil, mint, and lime.

**Slow cooker method**
- Follow steps 1-2 as above, then transfer chicken to slow cooker. Add shallots, garlic, ginger, 1 cup broth, coconut milk, curry paste, fish sauce, and salt. Cover and cook on low 6-8 hours or high 3-4 hours. Add zucchini and broccoli during final hour. Remove lid and cook 30 minutes on high to thicken sauce. Stir in lime juice and season. Finish as directed above.
