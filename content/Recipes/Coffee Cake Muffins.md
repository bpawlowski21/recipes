## Ingredients

**Muffins**
- 1¾ cups (220 g) all-purpose flour
- ½ cup (100 g) light brown sugar, firmly packed
- ½ cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ¾ teaspoon table salt
- ⅓ cup (75 g) unsalted butter, melted
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) neutral cooking oil
- 1 large egg
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 cup (240 g) sour cream

**Streusel topping**
- 5 tablespoons (70 g) unsalted butter
- 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
- ⅓ cup (66 g) light brown sugar, firmly packed
- ⅓ cup (66 g) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon table salt

**Glaze (optional)**
- 1 cup (125 g) powdered sugar
- 2-3 tablespoons whole milk

## Directions

**Muffin batter**
1. Whisk together flour, sugars, cornstarch, baking powder, cinnamon, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk melted butter and oil until smooth. Add egg and vanilla, then sour cream, whisking until combined.
3. Pour wet ingredients into dry and gently fold together until just combined.
4. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a 12-count muffin tin with paper liners. Let batter rest 15-30 minutes while preparing streusel.

**Streusel**
5. Melt butter and cool slightly.
6. Whisk together flour, sugars, cinnamon, and salt.
7. Drizzle cooled butter over dry ingredients and use a fork to claw together until crumbly (avoid overmixing).

**Assemble and bake**
8. Divide rested batter evenly into prepared muffin cups.
9. Divide streusel evenly over each muffin.
10. Bake at 425°F for 8 minutes, then reduce heat to 350°F (without opening door) and bake 12-14 minutes until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
11. Cool in tin for 7-10 minutes, then transfer to cooling rack.

**Glaze (optional)**
12. Whisk powdered sugar and milk until smooth, then drizzle over cooled muffins and allow to set before serving.
