## Ingredients

**Sourdough Version:**
- 250g whole grain rye flour (scant 2 cups)
- 250g bread flour (scant 2 cups)
- 370g water (1 2/3 cups)
- 100g active sourdough starter (~1/2 cup stirred down)
- 9g salt (1.5 tsp)
- 125-170g semi-sweet chocolate chips/chunks
- 125g dried cherries

## Directions

- Mix all ingredients together using a dough whisk or stiff spatula.
- Optional: Cover and rest 20-30 minutes, then perform two stretch-and-fold sessions.
- Cover and ferment several hours (approximately 9 hours at 68°F).
- Shape into a boule using letter-fold technique.
- Proof 1-3 hours in floured basket.
- Preheat oven with baking vessel for 30 minutes.
- Bake: 500°F for 20 minutes covered, 450°F for 10 minutes covered, 450°F for 10-20 minutes uncovered.
- Internal temperature should reach 205°F when done.
