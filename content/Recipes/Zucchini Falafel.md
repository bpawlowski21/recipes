Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. "We still got loads of zucchini at home, so here's another delicious way to use it up! Those falafel are crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, super flavoursome and complete with the yogurt-tahini-dip. You can either shallow fry, fry, air-fry or bake the falafel."

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (360g) cooked chickpeas
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- A bunch of parsley
- 1 medium zucchini, shredded
- 7 tbsp (chickpea) flour
- 1 tsp each salt, ground coriander, and cumin
- Squeeze of lemon
- Oil for frying

**Dip**
- 4 tbsp vegan yogurt
- 1 tbsp tahini
- Salt to taste
- ½ tsp each garlic powder and chili flakes
- Squeeze of lemon

## Directions

1. Pulse the chickpeas, onion, garlic, and parsley in a food processor.
2. Combine with the remaining falafel ingredients.
3. Shape into falafel.
4. Fry until golden brown, or bake at 200°C/400°F for 25 minutes.
5. Make the dip by mixing all the dip ingredients together.
