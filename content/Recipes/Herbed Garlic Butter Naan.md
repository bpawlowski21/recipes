## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 3/4 teaspoon active dry yeast (optional)
- 3/4 cup warm whole milk
- 1 cup full fat plain greek yogurt
- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) salted butter, melted and divided in half
- 2-4 cloves garlic, grated
- 3/4 cup chopped mixed herbs (parsley, cilantro, chives, and/or dill)

## Directions

- Combine water, honey, and yeast in stand mixer bowl; let sit 5-10 minutes until bubbling. Add milk, yogurt, flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Mix with dough hook until incorporated (2-4 minutes). Dough should be sticky. Knead into ball, cover with plastic wrap, and let rise in warm place for 1 hour until doubled, or refrigerate overnight.
- Divide dough into 8 equal balls. Roll each into large oval approximately 8 inches long and 1/4-inch thick.
- Heat cast iron skillet over medium-high heat until very hot. Brush both sides of naan with half the melted butter. Drizzle skillet with olive oil and wipe with paper towel. Place naan on hot skillet, cover with lid, and cook 1 minute until bubbles form. Flip and cook uncovered 1-2 minutes until toasted spots appear. Remove and wrap in kitchen towel. Repeat with remaining naan.
- Melt remaining butter with garlic over low heat until lightly browned and garlic turns golden. Remove from heat and add herbs. Brush garlic herb butter over warm naan and serve.
- Storage: keep in airtight container up to 3-4 days or freeze up to 3 months.
