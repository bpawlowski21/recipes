## Ingredients

**Rice + Vegetables:**
- 1 cup extra-firm tofu (8 ounces)
- 1 cup long- or short-grain brown rice (rinsed thoroughly)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup chopped green onion
- 1/2 cup peas
- 1/2 cup carrots, finely diced

**Sauce:**
- 3 Tbsp tamari or soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp peanut butter
- 2-3 Tbsp organic brown sugar, muscovado sugar, or maple syrup
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1-2 tsp chili garlic sauce
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (optional)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 400°F (204°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Wrap tofu in an absorbent towel and place something heavy on top to press out liquid.
- Dice pressed tofu into 1/4-inch cubes, arrange on baking sheet, and bake 26-30 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
- Prepare rice by bringing 12 cups water to boil, add rinsed rice, and boil uncovered for 30 minutes. Strain and return to pot (off heat), cover, and steam for 10 minutes.
- While rice and tofu cook, whisk together all sauce ingredients in a bowl. Adjust seasonings to taste.
- Add baked tofu to sauce and marinate for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
- Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Using a slotted spoon, transfer tofu to pan (reserving sauce) and cook 3-4 minutes until deeply golden, stirring occasionally. Remove and set aside.
- Add garlic, green onion, peas, and carrots to the hot pan. Sauté 3-4 minutes, then season with 1 Tbsp tamari.
- Add cooked rice, tofu, and remaining sauce. Stir and cook over medium-high heat 3-4 minutes.
- Serve immediately with optional chili garlic sauce or sriracha. Garnish with peanuts or cashews if desired.
