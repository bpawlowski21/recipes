## Ingredients

**Perfect Pico de Gallo**
- 2 cups diced tomatoes
- ½ cup chopped white onion
- ½ cup chopped cilantro
- 2 tablespoons chopped jalapeño chile
- 1 tablespoon lime juice

**Cherry Tomato-Chipotle Salsa**
- 4 green onions
- ¼ cup cilantro leaves
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- ½ chipotle chile in adobo sauce, drained
- 2 cups halved cherry tomatoes

**Salsa Verde**
- 8 tomatillos, husked
- 1 serrano or jalapeño chile, seeded
- 1 garlic clove
- ½ small onion
- ¼ cup cilantro leaves
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- Water

**Freestyle Master Recipe (template)**
- 2 cups produce of choice
- ½ cup onion
- ¼ cup fresh herbs
- 1–2 tablespoons acid (lime or lemon juice, or vinegar)
- Chile, to taste
- Salt, to taste

## Directions

**Perfect Pico de Gallo**
- Stir together all ingredients.

**Cherry Tomato-Chipotle Salsa**
- Pulse green onions, cilantro, lime juice, and chipotle in a food processor until finely chopped.
- Add cherry tomatoes and pulse until chunky.

**Salsa Verde**
- Simmer tomatillos, chile, garlic, and onion in water 10–15 minutes until soft.
- Transfer to a blender with the cilantro and lime juice; blend with some of the cooking water to reach desired consistency.

**Freestyle Master Recipe**
- Stir together all ingredients; add salt to taste.
- Let stand at room temperature at least 15 minutes and up to 4 hours.
