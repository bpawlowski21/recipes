## Ingredients

- 1 bunch green onions, thinly sliced
- 1 cup (100 grams) grated parmesan cheese
- 2 tablespoons (15 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon (10 grams) chopped fresh thyme leaves
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 2 pounds (905 grams) yukon gold potatoes, unpeeled, cut into very thin (1/8-inch) rounds
- 12 ounces (340 grams) any green or yellow zucchini or other summer squash, cut into very thin (1/8-inch) rounds
- 2 tablespoons (25 grams) olive oil

## Directions

1. **Prepare oven and pan:** Heat oven to 375°F. Line a deep 10- to 11-inch ovenproof skillet or cast-iron pan with parchment paper, pressing it across the bottom and pleating up the sides.
2. **Prepare filling:** Set aside 1/4 cup sliced green onions. Toss remaining green onions, all parmesan, flour, thyme, 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, and about 3/4 teaspoon pepper in a medium bowl.
3. **Assemble torte:** Layer 1/3 of potatoes in concentric circles in prepared pan. Layer 1/2 of squash atop potatoes. Drizzle with 2 teaspoons oil and sprinkle with 1/3 of cheese mixture. Repeat with 1/3 potatoes, 1/2 squash, 2 teaspoons oil, and 1/3 cheese mixture. Top with last 1/3 of potatoes, drizzle with remaining oil, and sprinkle with remaining cheese mixture. Press gently to flatten.
4. **Bake covered:** Cover pan tightly with foil and bake 45 to 50 minutes until potatoes are almost tender.
5. **Finish baking:** Remove foil and bake another 30 to 35 minutes until browned on top and potatoes are tender.
6. **Serve:** Cool 10 minutes in pan. Use parchment paper to lift torte onto a serving plate. Sprinkle with reserved green onions. Cut into 8 wedges and serve immediately.
