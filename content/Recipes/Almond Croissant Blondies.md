"These Almond Croissant Blondies combine a soft white chocolate blondie base with a buttery almond frangipane topping and sliced almonds. Inspired by the flavors of an almond croissant, these rich bars bake in two stages for a chewy blondie bottom and a golden almond layer on top."

Prep Time: 20 minutes · Cook Time: 55 minutes · Cooling: 1 hour · Total Time: 2 hours 15 minutes · Calories: 310kcal

## Ingredients

**Almond Blondie Batter**
- 1¼ cup chopped white chocolate (212g)
- ¾ cup unsalted butter (170g)
- 1 cup white granulated sugar (200g)
- 2 large eggs
- 1 large yolk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- ½ teaspoon almond extract
- 1½ cups all-purpose flour (190g)
- ⅔ cup almond flour (65g)
- ¼ teaspoon salt

**Almond Croissant Topping**
- 7 tablespoons unsalted butter (100g)
- ½ cup granulated sugar (100g)
- 1 large egg
- 1 heaping cup almond flour (100g)
- ½ teaspoon almond extract
- ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
- ½ cup sliced almonds

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (325°F if convection/gas or if your oven runs hot). Grease and line an 8×8" square pan with parchment paper. Don't use a glass pan — it can cook unevenly.

**Almond Blondie Batter**
2. Mix the unsalted butter and white chocolate in a large bowl. Melt together in the microwave (15-30 second increments, stirring in between) or over a double boiler.
3. Add the granulated sugar and whisk to combine.
4. Add the eggs one at a time, whisking until combined before adding the next.
5. Add the egg yolk along with the vanilla and almond extract, and whisk to combine.
6. Add the flour, almond flour, and salt, mix on low or fold with a spatula.

**Baking**
7. Pour the blondie batter into the prepared pan.
8. Bake in two rounds. First for about 30 minutes (25 if your oven runs hot/convection), until set around the edges and top but not fully baked through.
9. While the blondies bake the first round, make the almond croissant topping.

**Almond Croissant Topping**
10. Melt the butter in the microwave. Add the sugar, whisk to combine.
11. Add the egg, almond extract, and vanilla, and mix.
12. Add the almond flour and stir to combine.
13. Remove the blondies from the oven after about 30 minutes.
14. Pour the almond croissant topping over them, spread in an even layer.
15. Sprinkle the sliced almonds on top.
16. Return to the oven and bake for another 25 minutes.
17. Remove from the oven. Let cool before slicing and serving.
18. Sprinkle powdered sugar on top before serving.

## Notes

- **White chocolate:** don't use candy melts — use good quality white chocolate with over 20% cocoa butter (e.g. Callebaut).
- **Storage:** air-tight container at room temperature up to 2 days, fridge up to 6 days, or freeze up to 2 months (thaw overnight in fridge).
- **For easy removal:** leave a slight parchment overhang on two sides as handles.
- **Baking time:** oven temps vary — start checking doneness at 25-30 minutes. Blondies shouldn't be fully baked, just set around edges/center but still soft. Avoid baking until a toothpick comes out completely clean, as this can lead to dryness.
- **Oven:** if using convection, may need to reduce temperature by 25°F and monitor closely.
- **Baking pan:** don't use glass — a steel or metal pan is better; glass heats slowly and can leave an underdone center with overcooked edges.
