The secret to sensational batches is an all-in-one dough. Mix it in 10 minutes, shape it, top it, and bake it. Use this dough to make pretzel cookies, icebox cookies, jam-striped cookies, and drop cookies (see "Jam-Stripe Cookies" and "Technique - Drop Cookies, Three Ways" in this vault).

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 large egg yolks
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour (spooned and leveled)

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, using an electric mixer, beat butter, sugar, and salt until light and fluffy. Add yolks, one at a time, scraping the bowl after each addition. Add vanilla. With mixer on low, add flour; mix just until combined. Wrap in plastic; chill until firm, about 1 hour.
2. Citrus or Spice: whisk 1 tablespoon grated lemon, lime, or orange zest (or a combination), or 1 tablespoon pumpkin-pie spice into the flour before adding it to the butter mixture.

## Notes

- Wendy's handwritten note (dated 12/21/22): "I make these every year!" Note: make small balls before refrigerating — forming the balls ahead of time is a huge time saver. 23g balls make about 32 cookies.
- Almond variation on a pink sticky note attached to this page (dated 12/17/17): 1 1/2 sticks butter, 1/2 cup almond paste (beat until oils release), 3/4 cup sugar, 1 egg, 1/2 teaspoon vanilla, 1/2 teaspoon almond extract. Flour amount not captured on the note.
