## Ingredients

- 1 1/3 cups (227g) bittersweet chocolate or chocolate chips, chopped
- 8 tablespoons (113g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 2/3 cup (135g) granulated sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 teaspoons espresso powder, optional
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1 2/3 cups (200g) all-purpose flour
- Confectioners' sugar, for coating

## Directions

- Melt chocolate and butter together in a saucepan or microwave-safe bowl until smooth.
- In a separate bowl, beat sugar, eggs, vanilla, and espresso powder. Stir in chocolate mixture, baking powder, and salt, then fold in flour. Refrigerate dough 2-3 hours or overnight until firm.
- Preheat oven to 325°F. Prepare baking sheets with grease or parchment paper.
- Place confectioners' sugar in a shallow bowl. Using a teaspoon or cookie scoop, form dough into 1 1/4" balls and drop into sugar.
- Coat 5-6 balls at a time by shaking the bowl, then place on prepared sheets with 1 1/2" spacing between cookies.
- Bake 10-12 minutes, rotating pan positions midway through. Cookies will flatten and develop a distinctive streaked appearance.
- Cool on rack. Store at room temperature, well-wrapped, for several days, or freeze for extended storage.
