## Ingredients

- 2 cups granulated or brown sugar
- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter or unsalted Passover margarine, melted and cooled
- 3 eggs (or 3/4 cup Egg Beaters)
- 1 tablespoon brewed coffee
- 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder, sifted (64 grams)
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 scant cup matzoh cake meal
- 1/2 cup finely chopped toasted walnuts (optional)

## Directions

- Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a 7 by 10-inch rectangular baking pan, a 9-inch square pan, or an 8- or 9-inch springform pan.
- In a bowl, mix the sugar into the melted butter, then the eggs, coffee, cocoa, salt, cake meal, and toasted walnuts if using.
- Spoon the batter into the prepared pan and bake for about 25 minutes. Do not overbake. The brownies should be set and seem dry to the touch, but there should not be a crust around the sides. Cool in the pan. Cut into squares or (if baked in a round pan) into wedges.

## Notes

- Just wonderful even when it is not Passover — fudgy, dense, delicious. Another "most requested" recipe. From *A Treasury of Jewish Holiday Baking* by Marcy Goldman, © 2009 Whitecap Books, Ltd.
- Printed notes section on the page (a submitted variation, not Wendy's handwriting): add 1 tsp vanilla and 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips, and use 1 cup white sugar plus 1 cup brown sugar in place of the plain granulated/brown sugar called for above.
- Wendy's handwritten annotations (dated 4.11.2025) walk through applying that variation: whisk the eggs, whisk again, add the vanilla, fold in with a silicone spatula, then add the chocolate chips — batter will be very thick. "Do not overbake" underlined for emphasis. She also converted the cocoa powder to 64 grams, and wrote 200g / 190g next to the two sugars (white/brown) as weight equivalents, signed "-W".
