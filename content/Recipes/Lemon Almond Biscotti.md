## Ingredients

### Biscotti
- 6 tablespoons (85g) butter, softened
- 2/3 cup (135g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 3/4 to 1 1/4 teaspoons almond extract, to taste
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 2 to 3 tablespoons (28g to 43g) freshly squeezed lemon juice, to taste
- 2 large eggs
- 2 cups (240g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour

### Glaze (optional)
- 1/2 cup (57g) confectioners' sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons King Arthur Lemon Juice Powder
- 2 to 3 teaspoons milk

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F and grease or line a large baking sheet with parchment.
- Beat butter, sugar, salt, zest, almond extract, and baking powder until creamy. Add lemon juice and eggs, then incorporate flour at low speed until a smooth dough forms.
- Shape dough into a 13"x3"x3/4" log on the prepared sheet, smoothing the top and sides.
- Bake 25 minutes, then cool 10-25 minutes on the pan. Reduce oven heat to 325°F.
- Lightly spritz the log with water to soften the crust; wait 5 minutes, then slice crosswise into 1/2" pieces with a serrated knife, cutting perpendicular to the pan.
- Stand biscotti upright on the baking sheet and bake 30-35 minutes until very dry and golden at the edges.
- Cool on a rack. For the glaze, combine confectioners' sugar, lemon juice powder, and milk, and drizzle over cooled biscotti.
- Store completely cooled biscotti at room temperature, well wrapped, for two weeks, or freeze longer.
