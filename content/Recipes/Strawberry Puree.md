Prep Time: 5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1½ pounds fresh strawberries
- ½ medium lemon
- ¼ cup granulated sugar

## Directions

1. Trim and quarter 1½ pounds strawberries (about 4 cups). Place in the bowl of a food processor fitted with the blade attachment or a blender. Juice ½ medium lemon until you have 1 tablespoon. Add the lemon juice and ¼ cup granulated sugar to the food processor or blender. Blend on high speed for 1 minute. Scrape down the sides of the bowl and blend until smooth, 30 seconds to 1 minute more.

## Notes

- Storage: refrigerate strawberry purée in an airtight container for up to 5 days. The purée can also be frozen in a freezer-safe container for up to 3 months.
