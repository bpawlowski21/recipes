"This low-effort, high-impact chickpea salad sandwich is the antidote to your sad desk lunch! Chickpeas are mashed up and coated in a zingy, creamy dressing with bites of crisp veggies for textural fun. Sandwich a scoop between toasted, garlic-rubbed bread, or make it your own!"

Prep Time: 20 mins · Cook Time: 5 mins · Total Time: 25 mins · Diet: Gluten Free, Vegan, Vegetarian

A different, dressing-forward chickpea salad sandwich from the other saved [[Chickpea Salad Sandwich]] (Instagram, unattributed) — this one uses capers, dill, and lemon zest instead of bell pepper and spring onion.

## Ingredients

- ½ heaping cup (90g) roughly chopped red onion (about ½ small onion)
- ½ heaping cup (90g) roughly chopped celery (optional)
- ½ cup (15g) fresh dill, tightly packed (no tough stems)
- 2 tablespoons (25g) capers, drained
- 2 (15-ounce/425g) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 tablespoons (36g) dill pickle relish (or 2 dill pickles, roughly chopped)
- 1 fat clove garlic, minced/grated/crushed
- 1 small lemon, zested and juiced
- ⅓ to ½ cup (75 to 112g) vegan mayo
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard (or whole grain mustard)
- Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper

**For serving**
- 10 to 12 slices sandwich bread
- 1 to 2 cloves garlic, halved (if toasting bread)
- A few pats vegan butter (optional, if toasting bread)
- Sandwich fixings of choice (crisp lettuce, sliced tomatoes/cucumbers, pickles, etc.)

## Directions

1. In a food processor, combine the onion, celery (if using), dill, and capers. Pulse repeatedly (don't continuously blend) until finely chopped.
2. Add the chickpeas and pulse again until crumbled into small pieces but not pureed. Transfer to a large bowl.
3. Fold in the dill pickle relish, garlic, lemon zest, 1 tablespoon (15mL) lemon juice, mustard, black pepper to taste, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, and mayo (½ cup for a creamier, richer mouthfeel; ⅓ cup for a lighter vibe).
4. Mix well with a spatula until well-combined and creamy. Taste, adding more lemon juice, mustard, salt, or pepper as desired.
5. For toasted sandwiches: lightly toast each slice of bread. While still warm, rub each slice with the cut side of a halved garlic clove. If desired, spread a thin layer of vegan butter on one slice.
6. Assemble the sandwiches: scoop a generous amount of chickpea salad onto each slice of bread. Top with sandwich fixings and a pinch of salt and pepper (optionally drizzle veggie toppings with extra virgin olive oil). Top with the remaining slice and slice in half, or serve open-faced.

## Notes

- If you don't love celery, omit it or replace with the same amount of cucumber or red bell pepper, or ½ to 1 teaspoon celery seeds.
- The lemon zest adds a bright lemony flavor — omit or use just half the zest if you don't want that, then taste and add more as desired.
- Mayo-free alternative: replace the mayo with ¼ cup (56g) well-stirred tahini, 4oz (112g) creamy coconut yogurt, and 1 tablespoon (14g) extra virgin olive oil.
- Makes 5 to 6 sandwiches using standard sandwich bread; bakery-style bread yields 4 larger sandwiches.
- Without a food processor: finely chop the onion, celery, dill, and capers by hand; mash the chickpeas with a fork or potato masher until chunky; mix in the chopped veggies/herbs and dressing ingredients.
