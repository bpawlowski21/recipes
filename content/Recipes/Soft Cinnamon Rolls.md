## Ingredients

### Tangzhong (starter)
- 5 tablespoons (71g) water
- 5 tablespoons (71g) milk, whole preferred
- 3 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon (25g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour

### Dough
- All of the tangzhong (above)
- 4 cups plus 2 tablespoons (495g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 3 tablespoons (21g) King Arthur Baker's Special Dry Milk or nonfat dry milk
- 1 3/4 teaspoons (11g) table salt
- 1 tablespoon instant yeast
- 3/4 cup (170g) milk, whole preferred, lukewarm
- 2 large eggs
- 6 tablespoons (85g) unsalted butter, melted

### Filling
- 3/4 cup (142g) light or dark brown sugar, packed
- 4 teaspoons ground cinnamon

### Icing
- 2 cups (227g) confectioners' sugar
- Pinch of table salt
- 2 tablespoons (28g) butter, melted
- 1/2 teaspoon King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 2 to 3 tablespoons (28g to 43g) milk or heavy cream

## Directions

- Combine tangzhong ingredients in a small saucepan and whisk until smooth. Heat over medium, whisking constantly, until thick (about one minute), then remove from heat.
- Mix tangzhong with remaining dough ingredients until combined. Let rest covered for 20 minutes.
- Knead dough by hand, mixer, or bread machine until smooth, elastic, and somewhat sticky.
- Shape into a ball and let rise in a greased covered bowl 60-90 minutes until puffy.
- Combine brown sugar and cinnamon for the filling.
- Deflate dough and divide in half; shape each into a rough rectangle, then roll one piece into an 18"x8" rectangle.
- Sprinkle half the filling onto the dough. Starting with a long edge, roll into a log and cut into 12 slices (1 1/2" each), seam underneath. Repeat with the second piece and remaining filling.
- Grease a 9"x13" pan and space rolls inside. Cover and let rise 45-60 minutes until crowded and puffy.
- Preheat oven to 350°F with rack in the bottom third.
- Bake uncovered 22-25 minutes until set (internal temperature ~188°F).
- While baking, whisk icing ingredients to a thick, spreadable consistency.
- Turn rolls out onto a rack and spread with icing. Serve warm.
- Store cool rolls at room temperature for a couple days or freeze up to one month.
