"Vegetarian Avgolemono Soup (Greek Lemon Chickpea Soup)." "Craving a bowl of cozy Mediterranean comfort food? This vegetarian avgolemono soup is rich, creamy, and packed with bright lemon flavor! Inspired by the Greek classic, it keeps the same signature tang and velvety texture (just with a vegetarian twist). Bonus: it all comes together in one pot!" Prep Time: 15 mins · Cook Time: 25 mins · Total Time: 40 mins

## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp olive oil (15 mL)
- 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
- 1 cup diced carrots (about 2 to 3 carrots)
- 1 cup diced celery (about 3 to 4 ribs)
- ½ cup chopped green onion (about 4 green onions)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 6 cups vegetable broth (1.4 L)
- 1 cup white rice, any type (used arborio; 200 g)
- 2 (15-oz) cans chickpeas, undrained (425 g cans)
- 2 bay leaves
- ½ tsp ground black pepper
- ¼ tsp salt
- 3 large eggs
- ½ cup lemon juice (120 mL)
- 2 Tbsp chopped fresh dill

## Directions

1. **Flavor Base:** heat 1 Tbsp olive oil over medium in a large pot. Add the onion, carrots, celery, green onion, and garlic. Cook until the onion is tender and the celery is bright green, about 7 minutes.
2. **Assemble:** stir in the vegetable broth, white rice, chickpeas (do not drain — add the chickpea liquid), bay leaves, black pepper, and salt. Cover and cook until the rice is tender.
3. **Eggs:** separate the egg whites and yolks from 3 large eggs. Whisk the egg whites until they're frothy. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg yolks. Drizzle the yolks into the whites, then whisk in ½ cup lemon juice.
4. **Temper Eggs:** while whisking, slowly drizzle a ladle-full of the hot broth from the soup into the eggs. Continue adding broth until the egg mixture is warm.
5. **Serve:** stir the egg mixture and 2 Tbsp chopped fresh dill into the pot of soup and remove from heat. Taste and add salt and pepper as needed. Serve warm!

## Notes

- If using brown or wild rice, it will take a lot longer to cook and you'll need to use more broth.
- Can I use orzo instead of rice? Yes! It's a great substitute and will cook a little faster than rice — adjust the cooking time accordingly.
- Can I make this soup vegan? Yes! Swap the eggs for ½ cup of Just Egg, tempering it the same way by slowly whisking in hot broth. The texture won't be quite the same, but it'll still be tasty!
