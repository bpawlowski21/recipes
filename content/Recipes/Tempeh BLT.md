Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram comment screenshot. A second, differently-marinated tempeh bacon recipe from the one saved as [[Tempeh Bacon Sandwich]] — "even better the day after."

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp tamari
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 pinch cayenne
- 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 150g tempeh

## Directions

1. Cut the tempeh into 0.5cm thick slices.
2. Add all the other ingredients to a container and mix well to combine.
3. Place the tempeh into the container and make sure it's thoroughly coated. Marinate in the fridge for a minimum of 2 hours, ideally overnight.
4. Cook on a lightly oiled pan on medium heat for a couple of minutes on each side.

## Notes

- For a healthier BLT: use lightly toasted bread (rye sourdough recommended), a little Dijon mustard, some hummus, sliced tomato, the cooked tempeh, crispy lettuce, and avocado.
