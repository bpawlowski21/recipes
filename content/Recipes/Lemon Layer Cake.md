## Ingredients

**For the Cake**
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 2 tablespoons lemon zest (about 1 lemon)
- 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
- 4 eggs, room temperature
- 1 1/3 cups buttermilk
- 2 teaspoons lemon extract
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 2/3 cups all purpose flour
- 2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 3/4 teaspoon salt

**For the Frosting**
- 1/2 cup butter
- 4 oz cream cheese
- 3 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1-2 tablespoons milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon lemon extract
- Pinch of salt
- Optional: 2 lemons, thinly sliced, for garnish

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 325°F; grease and flour three 6-inch or two 8-9 inch cake pans.
- Combine sugar and lemon zest; rub together to infuse flavors.
- Beat softened butter and oil until homogenous (3 minutes).
- Gradually add sugar mixture, beating on high until pale yellow and fluffy (3 minutes).
- Add eggs one at a time, scraping bowl between additions; add extracts and beat 3 minutes more.
- Sift flour, salt, and baking powder together. Alternate dry ingredients and buttermilk in 4 batches, beginning and ending with buttermilk.
- Divide batter among pans; bake 30 minutes until golden and springs back when pressed. Cool completely on racks.
- Optional: for dried lemon garnish, thinly slice lemons, sprinkle with sugar, and bake at 300°F for ~2 hours until dry.
- Beat butter and cream cheese; add powdered sugar, milk, extracts, and salt until fluffy (2 minutes).
- Layer cakes with frosting between layers and on exterior; garnish with dried lemon slices if desired.
