"This sticky tofu is crispy on the outside with a sweet sticky glaze. The perfect dinner with rice and veggies and easy to make in 15 minutes."

Prep Time: 10 mins · Cook Time: 5 mins · Total Time: 15 mins · Calories: 441

Handwritten substitution note in red pen next to the sweet soy sauce. Original scan:

![[Crispy Sticky Tofu (annotated).pdf]]

## Ingredients

- 300 g extra firm tofu
- 3 tbsp sweet soy sauce *(handwritten alternative: or 2½ Tbsp soy sauce + ½ Tbsp honey)*
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1-2 garlic cloves
- ½ tbsp red pepper flakes, or more if you like it spicy
- 4 tbsp corn starch
- 3-4 tbsp water
- 3 tbsp oil
- Garnish: sesame seeds or chopped green onions

## Directions

1. Drain and press tofu with either a tofu press or in between a clean tea towel with something heavy on top to remove the excess water.
2. In a small bowl or ramekin, add the sweet soy sauce, vinegar, grated garlic, and red pepper flakes. Add 1 tablespoon of cornstarch and 3 tablespoon water, stir or whisk to combine.
3. Slice tofu into triangles or tofu cubes and coat in the remaining corn starch.
4. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat and add the tofu pieces in a single layer, making sure they don't touch. Pan fry for a few minutes on both sides until you have golden and crispy tofu.
5. Pour the marinade into the pan, which will become very bubbly instantly. Quickly turn all the tofu pieces over so they are coated on both sides and serve on a dish straight away.
6. Garnish with the sesame seeds and enjoy your Crispy Sticky Tofu!
