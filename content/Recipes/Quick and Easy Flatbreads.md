## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup (170g) water, lukewarm
- 1 tablespoon (14g) olive oil
- 1 teaspoon (6g) table salt
- 2 cups plus 2 tablespoons (254g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour, divided
- 1 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 teaspoon baking powder

## Directions

- Weigh your flour, or measure by gently spooning it into a cup and sweeping off any excess.
- Combine all ingredients using 2 cups (240g) of the flour in a stand mixer bowl. Using the paddle attachment, mix on medium speed to make a shaggy dough, about 30-45 seconds, scraping the bowl midway.
- Dust a 12"-round work surface area with the remaining 2 tablespoons (14g) flour. Transfer dough there and knead by hand, incorporating more flour as needed, until springy and just slightly tacky, about 2-3 minutes.
- Leave the dough on the surface, place the mixing bowl upside-down over it, and let rest 30 minutes.
- Divide the dough into eight equal pieces (55g-60g each). Form each into a ball, cover, and let rest 5 minutes.
- Preheat a large skillet (12" or larger) over medium heat, or an electric griddle to 325°F.
- Working one piece at a time, roll into a rough circle or oval about 1/4" thick and 6" in diameter.
- Dry-fry the flatbreads in batches of two to four, 2-3 minutes per side until golden brown. Transfer to a rack to cool slightly.
- Store well wrapped at room temperature for up to 5 days; freeze for longer storage.
