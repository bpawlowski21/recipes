## Ingredients

- 3 oranges (2 navel and 1 cara cara orange suggested)
- 2 heads of endive
- 2 ounces soft goat cheese or chevre, crumbled
- 1/3 cup sliced almonds or chopped marcona almonds, well-toasted
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Olive oil for drizzling
- Sherry vinegar
- 1 tablespoon minced chives
- Flaky or other sea salt, to finish

## Directions

1. Cut the top and bottom off oranges, exposing the flesh. Rest on either end and cut away peels and white pith. Using your knife, cut between each membrane and orange segment to release the segments. Chop once or twice more for appropriately sized pieces.
2. Trim end off endives and arrange individual leaves on a medium platter.
3. Add a few orange chunks to each leaf, then add goat cheese crumbles and almonds.
4. Season with black pepper, then drizzle with a thin stream of olive oil.
5. Add a few droplets of sherry vinegar to each boat.
6. Scatter chives over each piece and finish with sea salt.
