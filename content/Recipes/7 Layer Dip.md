Prep Time: 15 minutes · Total Time: 15 minutes

A delicious seasoned dip filled with our favorite fiesta flavors!

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cups salsa
- 16 ounces refried beans, canned
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 2 tablespoons taco seasoning
- 1 cup guacamole, prepared
- 1 1/2 cups cheddar cheese
- 1/2 cup black olives (or tomatoes)
- 4 sliced green onions
- 1 package Impossible Meat (plant-based ground), browned with 1 tsp taco seasoning, then cooled

## Directions

1. Place salsa in a sieve to drain any liquid (this keeps your dip from getting runny).
2. Combine refried beans and milk (or use the liquid from the salsa if you prefer) until smooth. Spread in the bottom of a 7x11 pan.
3. Add the browned, cooled Impossible Meat mixture as a layer between the beans and the cream cheese/sour cream mix.
4. Combine cream cheese, sour cream, and taco seasoning with a mixer on medium. Spread over refried beans (and Impossible Meat layer).
5. Spoon guacamole over cream cheese and gently spread. Repeat with drained salsa.
6. Top with cheese, olives/tomatoes, and green onions.
7. Chill 1 hour before serving. Serve with tortilla chips or crackers.

## Notes

- Wendy's tested adaptation (handwritten on the printed copy): use a 7x11 pan (not 9x13), 2 tablespoons taco seasoning (not 1), and add a layer of browned plant-based ground "meat" (1 package Impossible Meat browned with 1 tsp taco seasoning, cooled) between the bean layer and the cream cheese/sour cream layer.
