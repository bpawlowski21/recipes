## Ingredients

### Crust
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 1/2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1 cup (227g) unsalted butter, very cold, cut into cubes
- 1/2 cup full-fat sour cream, cold

### Apple Pie Filling
- 4 large apples, peeled and cored
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons apple pie spice
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour

### Egg Wash
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 2 tablespoons sparkling sugar (optional)

## Directions

### Crust
- Whisk together flour, salt, baking powder, and sugar in a large bowl.
- Cut in cold butter until mixture resembles coarse meal with pea-sized pieces remaining.
- Stir in sour cream until shaggy; knead briefly on floured surface.
- Pat into a log, roll to 12"x14", fold like a letter, flip and repeat.
- Fold in thirds again, wrap, and chill for 1 hour.

### Apple Pie Filling
- Peel, core, and dice apples.
- Brown butter in large skillet over medium heat (1 minute).
- Add apples with salt and both sugars; cook 8 minutes until soft.
- Mix in spice and lemon juice; cook 1-2 more minutes.
- Sprinkle flour on top, mix well, and thicken for about 2 minutes.
- Remove from heat and cool at least 15 minutes.

### Assembly
- Preheat oven to 425°F; line baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Roll dough into 18"x18" square; cut 16 apple shapes (or squares).
- Divide filling among 8 pieces using 1 tablespoon each.
- Brush edges with egg wash.
- Cut vents in remaining 8 pieces; top filled pieces.
- Press edges with fork to seal.
- Brush with egg wash and sprinkle with sparkling sugar.
- Bake 15-18 minutes until golden brown with bubbling filling.
- Cool 10 minutes before serving.
