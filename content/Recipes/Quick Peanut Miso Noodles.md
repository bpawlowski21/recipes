## Ingredients

- 1 serving noodles of choice (post used udon)
- 1 tbsp parsley, chopped (optional)

**Peanut Miso Sauce**
- 1 tbsp miso paste
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- ½ tbsp sesame oil
- ½ tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 2 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 1 tbsp sriracha (optional)

## Directions

1. In a bowl, mix the ingredients for the peanut miso sauce until smooth.
2. Cook noodles per package instructions.
3. Add the noodles to the bowl with the sauce and toss to combine. Top with parsley.
