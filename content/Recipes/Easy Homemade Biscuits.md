## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour (250g)
- 1 Tablespoon baking powder
- 1 Tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 6 Tablespoons unsalted butter, very cold (85g)
- ¾ cup whole milk (177ml)

## Directions

1. Chill butter in freezer for 10-20 minutes before starting.
2. Preheat oven to 425°F and line a cookie sheet with parchment paper.
3. Combine flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a large bowl; mix well.
4. Shred cold butter using a box grater and add to flour mixture, stirring until it resembles coarse crumbs.
5. Add milk and stir with a wooden spoon until combined without overworking.
6. Transfer dough to a floured surface and gently work together, adding flour if too sticky.
7. Fold dough in half over itself and flatten, rotating 90 degrees and repeating 5-6 times.
8. Flatten dough to 1" thick using your hands (not a rolling pin).
9. Cut biscuits with a floured 2¾" round cutter, pressing straight down.
10. Place biscuits less than ½" apart on baking sheet.
11. Re-work remaining dough to create additional biscuits until you have at least 6.
12. Bake at 425°F for 12 minutes until tops turn lightly golden.
13. Brush with melted salted butter if desired. Serve warm.
