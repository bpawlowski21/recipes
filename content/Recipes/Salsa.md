## Ingredients

- 1 can (28 ounce) whole tomatoes, with juice
- 2 (15 oz) cans diced fire roasted tomatoes, with juice
- 1 (4oz) can chopped green chilies
- ½ cup chopped onion
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and chopped
- ½ tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 cup cilantro leaves and stems
- 2 limes, juiced

## Directions

1. Combine everything in a food processor and pulse to combine until you get the consistency you want.
2. Check for seasoning.
