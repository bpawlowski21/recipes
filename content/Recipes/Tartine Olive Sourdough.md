## Ingredients

**Leaven (prepared overnight):**
- 31g ripe sourdough starter
- 110g whole wheat flour
- 110g water

**Dough:**
- 250g leaven (25%)
- 800g white bread flour (80%)
- 200g whole wheat flour (20%)
- 20g salt (2%)
- 730g water (plus 50g reserved)
- 3 cups pitted olives (~2 cups kalamata and 1 cup green), loosely chopped
- 2 teaspoons herbes de Provence
- Zest of one lemon

## Directions

- **Prepare leaven** (evening before, 10:30 p.m.): mix starter, whole wheat flour, and water; cover, rest overnight at room temperature.
- **Mix & autolyse** (9:00 a.m.): add leaven to bowl, pour in 730g water and dissolve, add both flours and mix until incorporated. Cover, autolyse 40 minutes. Add salt and reserved 50g water, squeeze to incorporate, pull dough sides up and over itself repeatedly until sticky.
- **Bulk fermentation** (9:45 a.m.-1:00 p.m.): six sets of stretch and folds spaced 30 minutes apart (10:30, 11:00, 11:30, 12:00, 12:30, 1:00). After the first set, add olives, herbes de Provence, and lemon zest, incorporating with thumb and index finger. Rest undisturbed 1:00-3:30 p.m.
- **Pre-shape** (3:40 p.m.): transfer to unfloured surface, divide in half, flour tops, cover with inverted bowls 30 minutes.
- **Shape** (4:10 p.m.): shape each piece into an oblong batard; place in rice-flour-lined proofing baskets.
- **Proof** (4:30 p.m.): refrigerate baskets overnight.
- **Score & bake** (7:30 a.m. next day): preheat oven with combo cooker to 500°F for 1 hour. Score loaf with a single long diagonal slash at 45°. Bake in combo cooker at 450°F for 20 minutes covered, then remove lid and bake 35 more minutes. Repeat with second loaf.
