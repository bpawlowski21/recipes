## Ingredients

### Crumb Topping
- 1/4 stick cold unsalted butter, cut into tiny cubes
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 3½ tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon

### Donuts
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar, packed
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 cup full-fat sour cream
- 2½ tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 1 large egg, at room temperature

### Vanilla Glaze
- 1/2 cup confectioners' sugar
- 1/2 tablespoon whole milk
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 6-mold donut pan with non-stick spray.
- Combine sugar, flour, and cinnamon; work in cold butter cubes until mixture resembles coarse meal. Chill in freezer.
- Whisk together dry ingredients (flour, brown sugar, salt, baking powder). In a separate bowl, whisk wet ingredients (sour cream, butter, egg). Gently fold wet into dry — avoid overmixing.
- Spoon or pipe batter into prepared pan. Divide crumb topping evenly among donuts. Bake 10-11 minutes until they spring back when lightly pressed.
- Cool donuts in pan for 10 minutes before transferring to wire rack.
- Whisk confectioners' sugar, milk, and vanilla together. Drizzle over cooled donuts and serve immediately.
