Saved from Apple Notes as "Lentil shepherds pie."

Prep Time: 30 minutes · Cook Time: 45 minutes · Total Time: 1 hour 15 minutes · Vegetarian, gluten-free.

## Ingredients

**Mashed Potatoes**
- 2 pounds russet potatoes
- ½ teaspoon fresh rosemary, chopped, plus more for topping
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons butter
- ¼ cup sour cream
- 1 teaspoon black pepper, divided
- 1¼ teaspoon kosher salt, divided
- ½ cup shredded Parmesan cheese, plus more for topping

**Lentil Filling**
- 1 pound French lentils (2¼ cups)
- 1 large yellow onion, minced (about 2 cups)
- 3 medium or 2 large carrots, diced
- 1 stalk celery, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 6 tablespoons miso
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce or tamari
- 1 cup frozen corn kernels
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 teaspoon dried sage

## Directions

1. Boil and peel the potatoes, then dice them. Cook in boiling water 10 minutes until fork-tender. Drain and mash with the milk, butter, sour cream, ½ teaspoon pepper, 1 teaspoon salt, ½ cup Parmesan, and rosemary. Set aside.
2. Cook the French lentils in 6 cups water for 25 minutes until tender. Drain excess liquid.
3. Mince the onion, dice the carrots and celery, and mince the garlic.
4. Preheat oven to 400°F.
5. Heat olive oil in a large skillet. Sauté onion, carrots, and celery for 5 minutes until translucent. Add garlic and cook 1 minute. Stir in the lentils, broth, miso (dissolved in ½ cup water), balsamic vinegar, soy sauce, corn, peas, sage, ¼ teaspoon salt, and ½ teaspoon pepper. Simmer 10–12 minutes until the liquid reduces but the filling stays moist.
6. Transfer the lentil filling to a 9×13" baking pan. Spread the mashed potatoes over the top. Top with a thin layer of Parmesan and the reserved rosemary. Bake 25 minutes. Optional: broil briefly until browned. Serve warm.
