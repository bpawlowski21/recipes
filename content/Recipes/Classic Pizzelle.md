## Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- 3/4 cup (149g) granulated sugar
- 3/8 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 3/4 cups (210g) unbleached all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 8 tablespoons (113g) unsalted butter, melted

## Directions

- In a large bowl or stand mixer bowl, combine eggs, sugar, salt, and vanilla; beat until well blended.
- Mix in the flour and baking powder until smooth.
- Add melted butter and mix until smooth; batter will be thick and soft.
- Heat your pizzelle iron and grease according to manufacturer's instructions. The batter will stiffen as the iron heats.
- Cook pizzelle per iron instructions, typically 45 seconds to 2 1/2 minutes until browned.
- Remove from iron and cool on a rack. Trim ragged edges with scissors if desired.
- Dust cooled pizzelle with confectioners' sugar, if desired.
- Store in an airtight container for up to 1 week or freeze for up to 1 month.
