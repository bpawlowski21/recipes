## Ingredients

**Dough:**
- 4 cups (480g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 3 tablespoons (27g) non-diastatic malt powder or 2 tablespoons (26g) light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup (28g) King Arthur Better Cheddar Cheese Powder (optional)
- 1 1/4 teaspoons (8g) table salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 1/3 to 1 1/2 cups (301g to 340g) lukewarm water

**Filling:**
- 6 ounces (170g) cream cheese
- 2 tablespoons (14g) King Arthur Better Cheddar Cheese Powder (optional)
- 3/4 cup (85g) shredded cheddar cheese
- 1/3 cup (13g) chives, parsley, or other fresh herbs (optional)

**Water Bath:**
- 2 quarts (1814g) water
- 2 tablespoons (18g) non-diastatic malt powder or 1 tablespoon (13g) light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon (14g) granulated sugar

**Topping (Optional):**
- Everything Bagel Topping

## Directions

- Measure flour by spooning gently into a cup and sweeping off excess.
- Combine all dough ingredients, starting with lesser water amount. Mix and knead by hand, mixer, or bread machine until very soft and smooth, adding water as needed.
- Cover dough and let rise until puffy, about 1 1/2 hours.
- While dough rises, mix cream cheese, cheese powder, shredded cheese, and herbs for filling.
- Scoop filling by heaping tablespoonful into 12 balls; place on parchment-lined baking sheet.
- Freeze cheese balls until ready to use; no covering needed.
- Turn dough onto lightly greased surface. Divide into 12 equal pieces and round each into a ball.
- Cover dough balls lightly and let rest 10 to 15 minutes.
- Preheat oven to 425°F. Heat water, malt, and sugar to gentle boil in large, wide pan.
- Flatten dough balls slightly and place frozen filling ball in center of each.
- Pull dough around filling, pinching edges securely, and round into ball. Place seam side down on parchment-lined baking sheet.
- Transfer 3-4 buns at a time to simmering water. Cook 30 seconds, flip, and cook 30 seconds longer.
- Remove buns with skimmer or slotted spatula and return to pan. Repeat with remaining buns.
- Top all buns with Everything Bagel Topping.
- Bake 20 to 25 minutes until golden brown. Some filling may seep out, creating crispy melted cheese.
- Remove from oven and cool at least 30 minutes before serving.
- Store wrapped in refrigerator for up to a couple days; freeze for longer storage. Reheat before serving.
