## Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour (125g)
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, cold and cut into 12 pieces (85g)
- ¼ cup sour cream (60g)

## Directions

- Combine flour, sugar, and salt in a food processor and pulse to blend.
- Add butter pieces and pulse until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
- Pour in sour cream and pulse until the mixture starts to come together in clumps.
- Remove the blade, shape dough by hand into a ball, flatten into a disc, wrap, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
- Remove chilled dough and let it rest 5 minutes on a floured surface, then roll into a 12-inch circle.
- Transfer to a 9" or 10" pie dish, fold edges under, and crimp with a fork.
- For blind baking: freeze the crust, preheat oven to 350°F, line with foil, fill with sugar (or pie weights), and bake 45-55 minutes until cool.
