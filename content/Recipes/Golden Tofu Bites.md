Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram Reel. Prep time: 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 400g tofu
- 2 tbsp corn starch
- 1 tsp salt

**Glaze**
- 4 tbsp agave or maple syrup
- Juice of 1 small lemon
- 1 tsp each paprika, ground coriander, cumin
- ½ tsp each chili powder, garlic powder
- Salt to taste
- 1–2 tbsp water

## Directions

1. Pat dry the tofu and tear into bite-sized pieces.
2. Toss with the corn starch and salt.
3. Fry in oil until crispy.
4. Mix the glaze ingredients together and add to the tofu.
5. Stir and fry 2–3 minutes until nicely glazed.
