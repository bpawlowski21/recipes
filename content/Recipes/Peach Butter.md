## Ingredients

- 4 pounds (1.8 kilograms) peaches
- 1 cup (237 ml) water
- 2 cups (400 grams) granulated sugar
- Juice of one lemon

## Directions

1. **Peel (choose one method):** With a food mill, skip peeling — the mill removes skins later. Without a food mill, cut a small "x" in the bottom of each peach, dip into boiling water for 30 seconds, then into cold water for one minute; peels should slide off easily.
2. Halve peaches and remove pits, then cut each half into quarters (8 chunks per peach).
3. Place peach chunks and water in a large pot and bring to a boil. Simmer until peaches are tender, approximately 15 to 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. If using a food mill, run cooked peaches through it to puree and remove skins. Otherwise, puree using a food processor, blender, or immersion blender to desired smoothness.
5. Return peaches to the pot. Add sugar and lemon juice and bring to a strong simmer/gentle boil. Cook for 30 to 40 minutes, stirring occasionally at first, then more frequently as it thickens to prevent scorching.
6. **Test for doneness:** drizzle a ribbon of sauce across the surface — when it holds its shape before dissolving, it's done. (Alternatively: use a chilled plate and look for no water ring, or check that it stays rounded on a spoon for two minutes, or that a wooden spoon leaves a clear trail across the pot bottom.)
7. **Storage:** let cool; if not canning, keep in an airtight container in the refrigerator for at least two weeks.
8. **Canning:** sterilize jars by boiling submerged for 10 minutes (or wash and heat in a 200°F oven for 20 minutes). Divide hot peach butter between jars, leaving headspace. Wipe rims clean and cover with lids. Submerge jars in a large pot of boiling water for 10 minutes. Let cool completely on towels (may take overnight); properly canned jars last indefinitely at room temperature.
