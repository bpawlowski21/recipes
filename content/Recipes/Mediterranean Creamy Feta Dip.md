## Ingredients

- 6 oz. feta
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
- 4 oz cream cheese (½ of an 8 oz block), softened
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- Juice and zest of 1 lemon
- Kosher salt and pepper
- ¼ tsp chili flakes
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- Pinch crushed red pepper flakes
- 2 tbsp freshly chopped dill

**Toppings**
- ⅓ cup chopped cucumber
- ⅓ cup chopped red bell pepper
- ⅓ cup chopped cherry tomatoes
- ¼ cup chopped kalamata olives
- ¼ cup chopped red onion
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh dill

## Directions

1. Combine all the dip ingredients into a food processor and process until smooth. Check for seasoning.
2. Transfer dip to a serving bowl. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
3. Top with all the toppings and serve with pita chips, crackers, or vegetables.

## Notes

- "It's creamy and smooth and topped with loads of veggies and herbs" — described as going fast at parties.
