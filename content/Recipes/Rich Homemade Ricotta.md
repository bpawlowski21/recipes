## Ingredients

- 3 cups whole milk
- 1 cup heavy cream (or less)
- 1/2 teaspoon coarse sea salt
- 3 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice

## Directions

1. Pour milk, cream, and salt into a 3-quart nonreactive saucepan and attach a candy or deep-fry thermometer.
2. Heat the mixture to 190°F, stirring occasionally to prevent scorching on the bottom.
3. Remove from heat and add lemon juice, stirring gently and slowly once or twice.
4. Let the pot sit undisturbed for 5 minutes.
5. Line a colander with several layers of cheesecloth and place over a large bowl to catch whey.
6. Pour curds and whey into the colander and let strain for at least one hour (one hour yields tender, spreadable ricotta; two hours creates a firmer, cream cheese-like texture).
7. Discard whey (or save for other uses) and transfer ricotta to an airtight container or serve immediately.
