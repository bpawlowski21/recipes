## Ingredients

- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cardamom (optional)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 sticks (8 ounces, 1 cup) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1 1/4 cups light brown sugar, packed
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon orange zest, finely grated
- 1/2 teaspoon orange extract
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1 1/2 tablespoons molasses (not blackstrap)
- 2 3/4 cups old-fashioned oats, divided
- 2 cups butterscotch chips

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Combine flour, cinnamon, cardamom, baking soda, and salt in a bowl. Set aside.
- Beat butter until smooth using a stand mixer with paddle attachment or handheld mixer.
- Add both sugars and beat until light and fluffy (about 2 minutes), scraping bowl as needed.
- Mix in orange zest, orange extract, and vanilla extract until combined.
- Add eggs one at a time, scraping between additions. Beat in molasses.
- Gradually add flour mixture on low speed until just incorporated.
- Fold in 2 1/2 cups oats and all butterscotch chips until combined.
- Scoop 2-inch dough balls onto prepared sheets, spacing them 1 1/2 inches apart.
- Sprinkle remaining oats on cookie tops.
- Bake 12-13 minutes until golden at edges and slightly soft in center.
- Cool on baking sheet placed on wire rack for at least 10 minutes before serving.
