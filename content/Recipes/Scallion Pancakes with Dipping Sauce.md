## Ingredients

**Dough:**
- 2 1/2 cups (300g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1 teaspoon table salt
- 3/4 cup (170g) boiling water (212ºF)

**Dipping Sauce:**
- 4 large scallions (63g or 1/2 bunch), white parts only, finely chopped
- One 1" piece (8g) fresh ginger, peeled and finely grated
- 1 tablespoon white sesame seeds
- 2 tablespoons (28g) soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon water
- 2 teaspoons maple syrup
- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil

**Assembly:**
- 4 large scallions, green parts only, sliced into rings
- 3/4 cup (149g) vegetable oil or neutral oil, divided
- 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon (17g) toasted sesame oil
- Table salt for sprinkling

## Directions

- Mix flour and salt. Slowly add boiling water while stirring with wooden spoon or chopsticks until lumpy. Fold with bowl scraper until dough holds together.
- Knead dough on clean surface until smooth, about 5 minutes. Return to bowl, cover, and rest 30 minutes at room temperature.
- Combine all dipping sauce ingredients in small bowl. Set aside.
- Reserve scallion greens. In separate bowl, combine 1/4 cup neutral oil and sesame oil. Set aside.
- Divide dough into 8 equal portions (60g each). Roll each into smooth ball and cover with damp cloth.
- Line baking sheet with parchment. Roll one dough portion into thin 6"-7" round. Brush with thin oil layer, roll into log, then wind into coil with tucked end. Flatten slightly and cover with damp towel.
- Flatten coil, roll into 6"-7" round again. Brush with oil, sprinkle with 1 tablespoon scallion greens and pinch of salt. Roll into cylinder, wind into coil, tuck end.
- Cover with damp towel while working through remaining dough.
- Roll each filled coil one final time into 6"-7" round. Lightly brush with oil mixture, place on baking sheet, and cover. Separate pancakes with parchment layers to prevent sticking.
- Repeat with remaining coils, oiling and stacking as you progress. Keep stack covered with damp towel.
- Preheat 10" nonstick or cast iron skillet over medium heat until water droplets sizzle immediately. Add 1 tablespoon neutral oil. Place one pancake in pan and gently press onto hot surface. Cook about 2 minutes until top turns opaque and bubbles appear; bottom will be golden brown. Flip and cook 2 more minutes until deep golden and crispy.
- Transfer pancake to paper towel, then wire rack to cool slightly. Repeat with remaining pancakes, adding 1 tablespoon oil between batches.
- Cut into wedges and serve hot with dipping sauce. Best eaten immediately after cooking.
