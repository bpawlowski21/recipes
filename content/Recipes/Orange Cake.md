"This Orange Cake is so fluffy and soft, with a delicious Orange Syrup, and a tangy Orange Cream Cheese Glaze on top."

Prep Time: 40 minutes · Cook Time: 1 hour · Total Time: 1 hour 40 minutes · Calories: 320kcal

## Ingredients

**Orange Cake**
- 2 cups granulated sugar (400g)
- 4 tablespoons orange zest (about 2 oranges)
- 2⅔ cups all-purpose flour (335g)
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1¼ teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 1 cup unsalted butter (226g), plus more for greasing the pan
- 4 large eggs
- ½ cup sour cream (120g)
- ¼ cup orange juice (60mL)
- ½ cup milk (120mL)

**Orange Syrup**
- ½ cup orange juice (120mL)
- ½ cup sugar (100g)
- Peel of one orange

**Orange Glaze**
- ⅓ cup cream cheese (75g)
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter (14g)
- ¾ cup powdered sugar
- 3 tablespoons orange juice
- 2 tablespoons orange zest, to decorate

## Directions

**Orange Bundt Cake**
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (325°F if convection).
2. Grease a 12-cup bundt cake pan with softened butter, then flour it evenly, making sure to coat all the nooks and crannies.
3. Make the orange sugar: zest the oranges and rub the zest into the sugar with your fingers to release the oils. Set aside.
4. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, cornstarch, salt, baking powder, and baking soda. Set aside.
5. In a large bowl, beat the butter with an electric mixer on medium speed for 2 minutes.
6. Add the orange sugar and beat on medium-high until fluffy and the butter lightens in color, about 2 minutes.
7. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well until incorporated before adding the next.
8. Add the sour cream and orange juice and mix on low to combine.
9. Add half of the flour mixture to the bowl. Mix on low to combine.
10. Once just incorporated, add the milk all at once. Mix on low to combine.
11. Add the remaining dry ingredients and mix until incorporated. Finish mixing with a spatula to avoid overmixing (dry cake).
12. Pour the batter into the prepared bundt pan and spread evenly.
13. Bake for about 55-65 minutes. If browning too much before the inside is cooked, cover with aluminum foil.
14. The cake is done when it doesn't feel jiggly on top and a toothpick comes out clean.

**Orange Syrup**
15. While the cake bakes, place the sugar, orange juice, and orange peel in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, then turn off the heat as soon as the sugar dissolves. Set aside.
16. When the cake comes out of the oven, let it rest about 5 minutes, then prickle holes on top with a fork.
17. Remove the orange peel from the syrup, then pour the syrup all over the cake.
18. Let the cake cool 15 more minutes in the pan to absorb the syrup.
19. Flip the cake onto a cooling rack or plate. Let cool completely before slicing and serving.

**Orange Glaze**
20. Beat the cream cheese and butter with a mixer for 30 seconds.
21. Add the powdered sugar and beat for another 30 seconds.
22. Add the orange juice and mix to combine. Adjust consistency with more juice (thinner) or powdered sugar (thicker).
23. Drizzle the glaze over the cake, then sprinkle orange zest on top.

## Notes

- **Cornstarch** helps the cake have a fluffy, soft texture.
- **Milk, eggs, sour cream:** bring to room temperature (about 20-30 minutes out of the fridge) before using.
- **Butter:** also needs to be softened — ideally at 72°F, which can take longer than 20 minutes.
- **Orange syrup variation:** substitute half the orange juice for ¼ cup of orange liqueur if desired.
- You'll need about 4 medium oranges for the whole recipe.
- **Orange juice glaze alternative:** instead of the cream cheese glaze, mix 1 cup powdered sugar + 2-3 tablespoons orange juice.
- **Sour cream** can be substituted for full-fat Greek yogurt.
- Could also pulse the orange sugar in a food processor instead of rubbing by hand.
- **Pan size:** a 12-cup bundt pan was used; alternatively a 9x13" pan, two 8.5"x4.5" loaf pans, or two 9-inch round cake pans work for this amount of batter.
