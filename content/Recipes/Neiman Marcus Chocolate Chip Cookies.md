"The famous $250 cookie recipe everyone loves. Neiman Marcus chocolate chip cookies are loaded with oatmeal and walnuts."

Prep Time: 10 mins · Cook Time: 10 mins · Total Time: 20 mins · Calories: 235kcal

## Ingredients

- 1 cup salted butter, room temperature (8 ounces)
- 1 cup packed dark brown sugar (200g)
- 1 cup granulated sugar (200g)
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (4g)
- 2½ cups old fashioned oats (240g)
- 2 cups all-purpose flour (260g)
- ½ teaspoon salt (4g)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda (4g)
- 1 teaspoon baking powder (3g)
- 4 ounce milk chocolate Hershey bar
- 12 ounces semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 1½ cups chopped walnuts (150g)

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Using a hand mixer or stand mixer, cream together butter and sugars in a large bowl. Mix in eggs and vanilla, scraping the sides of the bowl as needed.
3. Grind the oats (old fashioned or quick oats both work since they're being ground) in a blender or food processor until powdery.
4. Combine the ground oats, flour, salt, baking powder, and soda in a medium bowl.
5. Slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients. Beat just until combined, but don't over mix.
6. Using a sharp knife on a cutting board, chop or grate the Hershey bar. Add the chocolate shavings, chocolate chips, and nuts to the batter. Mix to combine but do not over mix.
7. Using a cookie scoop, scoop the cookie dough onto the prepared baking sheets. Cookies should be at least 2 inches apart.
8. Press extra chocolate chips and a few nut pieces into the tops of the cookies to make them look pretty, if desired.
9. Bake for 10 minutes. You can use a round cookie cutter or a mason jar ring to swirl the cookies while still hot to get them perfectly round; this also thickens them.
10. Allow the cookies to cool on the pan for 5 minutes, then move to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Notes

- Line cookie sheets with parchment paper or nonstick silicone baking mats.
- Scoop the cookie dough balls as evenly as possible.
- There's no need to refrigerate this cookie dough — recommend NOT refrigerating it. Just mix and bake.
- Don't over bake the cookies if you want them soft and chewy.
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 4 days; freezes well too.
- Wendy's handwritten adaptation: add 1 tablespoon instant espresso powder (maybe start with 2 teaspoons) along with the dry ingredients in step 4.
