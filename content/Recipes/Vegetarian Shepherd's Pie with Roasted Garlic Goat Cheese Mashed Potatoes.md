## Ingredients

- 1 spaghetti squash
- 1 head of garlic
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 2 cups bread crumbs
- 1 cup milk
- ¼ cup roasted pine nuts
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 tablespoon fresh basil, chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme, chopped
- 1 tablespoon flat parsley, chopped
- 6 Yukon Gold potatoes, boiled but not peeled
- ¼ cup melted butter
- ½ cup smooth goat cheese
- 1 cup 35% cream
- 3 cups sliced mushrooms
- 6 cups wild mushrooms (or zucchini, substitute)
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 celery stalks, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup dried Du Puy lentils, cooked
- Fried shallots, for garnish
- Salt and pepper

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 300°F. Halve spaghetti squash, remove seeds, place on a baking sheet and bake 1½ hours (or microwave 20 minutes).
- Place garlic head on a tray, drizzle with olive oil, season, and roast 30 minutes until tender.
- Mix bread crumbs with milk for 5 minutes. Stir in pine nuts, eggs, and herbs.
- Mash potatoes with butter, goat cheese, roasted garlic puree, and cream. Season.
- Fork-shred the spaghetti squash flesh into a bowl, season.
- Increase oven to 450°F. Sauté onions, carrots, celery 5 minutes, add garlic and mushrooms and cook 10 minutes. Mix in lentils and bread mixture.
- Layer in an 8x11" dish: vegetable mixture, spaghetti squash, then potatoes. Bake 30 minutes until browned. Garnish with fried shallots.
