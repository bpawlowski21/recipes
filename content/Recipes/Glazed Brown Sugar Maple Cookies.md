## Ingredients

**For the cookies:**
- 1 1/2 sticks (3/4 cup) salted butter, room temperature
- 3/4 cup packed dark brown sugar
- 1/3 cup real maple syrup
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 egg, room temperature
- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (plus 2-4 tablespoons if needed)
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 cup very finely chopped pecans (optional)

**For the brown butter glaze:**
- 1 stick (1/2 cup) salted butter
- 1/3 cup real maple syrup
- 1 1/4-1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Cinnamon sugar, for decorating (optional)

## Directions

- Cream butter with brown sugar, then add maple syrup and vanilla; beat until light and fluffy (2-3 minutes). Mix in egg, then add flour, baking soda, and salt. Stir in pecans if using. Add extra flour if dough is too wet.
- Divide dough in half. Roll each portion 1/4 inch thick on floured parchment. Cut with a cookie cutter and transfer to parchment-lined baking sheet using a floured spatula. Freeze until firm (20-33 minutes). Repeat with dough scraps.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Bake for 12-14 minutes until lightly golden. Cool on sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.
- Make glaze by browning butter in a small pot over medium heat (2-3 minutes). Whisk in maple syrup, powdered sugar, vanilla, and salt.
- Drizzle glaze over cooled cookies and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar as desired. Store in airtight container up to 4 days.
