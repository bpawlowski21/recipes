## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, plus more for the baking dish
- 3 small heads cauliflower (about 3 pounds total), cored and cut into small pieces
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 cups whole milk
- 6 ounces extra-sharp Cheddar, grated (1 1/2 cups) — Wendy's substitution: 3/4 cup Cheddar + 3/4 cup Gruyère
- 1/8 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 cup panko bread crumbs — Wendy's substitution: 3/4 cup panko + 1/4 cup Parmesan
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Butter a shallow 2-quart baking dish. Fill a large pot with 1 inch of water and fit with a steamer basket; bring the water to a boil. Place the cauliflower in the basket, cover, and steam until very tender (Wendy notes 10 minutes, rather than the printed 12-15); drain well.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Sprinkle with the flour and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes. Gradually whisk in the milk and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, until thickened, 6 to 8 minutes.
3. Whisk in the cheese, nutmeg, 1 1/2 teaspoons salt, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper and cook, whisking, until the cheese is melted, 1 to 2 minutes. Remove from heat and gently fold in the cauliflower; transfer to the baking dish. Wendy adds an extra 1/4 cup Parmesan at this step.
4. In a bowl, toss together the bread crumbs, parsley, oil, and 1/4 teaspoon each salt and pepper. Sprinkle over the cauliflower and bake until golden brown and bubbling, 15 to 20 minutes.

## Notes

- Wendy's handwritten substitutions/tips are captured inline above (cheese blend, panko/Parmesan topping blend, shortened steaming time).
- Tip (Wendy's handwriting): can be prepared 1 day in advance. Cover and refrigerate; do not bake. Add 5-10 minutes to the baking time when baking from cold.
