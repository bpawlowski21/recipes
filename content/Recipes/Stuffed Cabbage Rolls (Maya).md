## Ingredients

**Filling**
- 1 onion
- 2 carrots
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 block (200g) firm or smoked tofu, crumbled
- 1 can (220g) lentils, drained
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste
- A handful breadcrumbs
- Parsley

**Wrapping & Sauce**
- 1 cabbage (post used savoy)
- 2 tbsp tomato sauce
- 1 tbsp flour
- 2 cups (500ml) water
- ⅓ cup (100ml) vegan cream
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp rosemary

## Directions

1. Fry the onion, carrots, and garlic in oil. Add the tofu, lentils, soy sauce, paprika, thyme, salt, pepper, breadcrumbs, and parsley. Fry/mix and slightly mash, then keep warm on low heat.
2. Remove the core of the cabbage and blanch it in water 8–10 minutes.
3. Take the leaves, cut off the white end, and add the filling. Roll like a burrito (use two leaves per roll to prevent breaking).
4. Press the rolls tightly with a kitchen towel and fry in some oil on both sides.
5. In the same pan, stir in the tomato sauce and flour on medium-high heat. Add the water, vegan cream, soy sauce, salt, pepper, paprika, and rosemary. Stir and bring to a simmer.
6. Place the rolls back in the sauce and cook on medium-low heat for 20 minutes.
