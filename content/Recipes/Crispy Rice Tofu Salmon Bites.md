## Ingredients

- 2 cups sushi rice
- 2¼ cups boiling water
- ¼ cup rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 200g tofu
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- Juice of one lemon
- Nori seaweed
- 1 tbsp light miso paste
- ½ cup water
- Vegan mayonnaise
- Sweet chili sauce
- Avocado
- Spring onion
- Sesame seeds

## Directions

1. Wash the rice until the water runs clear, transfer into a pot, and add the boiling water. Bring to a boil, cover with a lid, and cook about 15 minutes.
2. Add the soy sauce, lemon juice, nori, miso paste, and water to a pot and bring to a boil. Cut the tofu into very small cubes, place in a bowl, and cover with the sauce. Set aside to marinate.
3. Transfer the cooked rice to a bowl, let cool for a bit, then gently fold in the rice vinegar, sesame oil, maple syrup, and salt. Press down half the rice into a baking dish lined with plastic wrap. Add a layer of nori seaweed and press the remaining rice on top. Freeze.
4. Season the marinated tofu with vegan mayo and sweet chili sauce (or any other sauce).
5. Take the rice out of the baking dish and cut it into bite-size pieces. Fry a couple of minutes on medium-high heat until golden and crispy.
6. Assembly: top the crispy rice with a few slices of avocado, the tofu "salmon," spring onion, and sesame seeds. Enjoy!
