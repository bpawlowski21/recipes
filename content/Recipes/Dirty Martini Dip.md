## Ingredients

- 16 oz. cream cheese, softened
- 16 oz. sour cream
- 1/2 cup blue cheese, crumbled
- 1/2 cup olive brine
- 3/4 cup diced olives
- 2 oz. gin
- extra olives & olive oil for garnish
- toasted baguette for serving

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, beat cream cheese, sour cream, blue cheese, olives, brine, gin and seasonings.
2. Transfer to large martini glass, then garnish with more olives and a drizzle of olive oil.
3. Serve with toasted baguette and enjoy.

## Notes

- Dirty martini dip served the only acceptable way: in an oversized martini glass.
