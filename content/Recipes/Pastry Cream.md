## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cups (568g) whole milk
- 2/3 cup (132g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup (28g) cornstarch
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- 6 large egg yolks
- 2 teaspoons King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 8 tablespoons (113g) unsalted butter, cold, cut into 1" cubes

## Directions

- Set a fine mesh sieve over a medium bowl and set aside.
- Heat milk in a medium saucepan over medium heat until bubbles form around the edges.
- In a large bowl, whisk together sugar, cornstarch, and salt until lump-free. Add egg yolks and whisk until pale yellow and thick, about 1 minute.
- Gradually whisk about half the hot milk into the egg mixture, then pour the mixture back into the saucepan, whisking to combine.
- Whisking constantly, cook over medium heat until thickened with bubbles forming and bursting in the center; continue whisking 1 additional minute.
- Press the mixture through the sieve into the bowl. Add vanilla and stir. Add butter pieces gradually, stirring until melted and fully incorporated.
- Cover with plastic wrap or parchment pressed directly onto the surface. Refrigerate until completely cool, at least 4 hours.
- Before using, beat in a large bowl or mixer on medium-high speed until smooth and glossy, 30-60 seconds.
- Store covered in the refrigerator up to 5 days. Do not freeze.
