## Ingredients

- 7 1/2 cups (900g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 3/4 cup plus 3 tablespoons (100g) King Arthur Whole Wheat Flour or Golden Wheat Flour
- 3 1/2 cups (800g) tepid water (75-80°F)
- 1 tablespoon (20g) table salt
- 3 tablespoons (40g) sourdough starter, unfed/discard

## Directions

- Combine all ingredients in a large bowl or 6-quart dough rising bucket; mix by hand until flour is hydrated and dough forms a shaggy mass. Cover and rest 15 minutes.
- Perform three sets of folds over 45 minutes: grab dough from one side with a wet hand, lift, press to center; repeat on remaining sides. Rest 15 minutes between sets.
- Cover tightly after the final fold and leave at room temperature (~72°F) for about 12 hours.
- Turn dough onto a floured surface, divide into two equal pieces, form into rounds, cover, rest seam-side down for 10 minutes.
- Dust two lined brotforms or towel-lined bowls with whole wheat flour. Shape loaves into tight boules, place seam-side up in prepared forms, cover with plastic bag or shower cap.
- Refrigerate immediately if dough doubled during the 12-hour rise; otherwise rest at room temperature 30-60 minutes first. Refrigerate boules 8-12 hours.
- Preheat Dutch ovens on the lowest rack to 500°F for 45 minutes. Turn loaves onto parchment and slash with a lame or knife.
- Transfer loaves to preheated Dutch ovens using the parchment, cover with lids.
- Reduce temperature to 450°F immediately; bake covered 20 minutes. Remove lids, bake uncovered 20-25 minutes until deep golden brown.
- Cool completely on racks. Store loosely wrapped for several days or freeze for longer storage.
