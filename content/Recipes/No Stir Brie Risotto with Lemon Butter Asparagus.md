## Ingredients

- 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth (plus additional as needed)
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 5 tablespoons salted butter
- 3 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 2 cups Japanese sushi rice or arborio rice
- 1 cup dry white wine (Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc)
- Kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest, plus 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 8 ounces Brie, rind removed and cubed
- 1 bunch asparagus, chopped
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
- Black pepper

## Directions

1. Warm the broth until steaming.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil and 2 tablespoons butter in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add 2 cloves garlic and cook about 30 seconds. Stir in rice, cook until golden, 2-3 minutes. Add wine and 1 teaspoon salt, cook 3-4 minutes until absorbed. Stir in 5 cups warm broth, bring to boil, cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer 15 minutes.
3. Stir in remaining 1 cup broth, lemon juice, and parmesan, stirring 3-4 minutes until thickened. Add Brie chunks and stir. Remove from heat. Add more broth if needed for creaminess. Season with salt.
4. While risotto cooks, heat remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil and 3 tablespoons butter in a medium skillet. Add 1 clove garlic, lemon zest, and thyme; cook 1 minute. Add asparagus, season with salt and pepper, cook until tender, about 5 minutes.
5. Divide risotto into bowls and top with asparagus. Season with fresh pepper and serve.
