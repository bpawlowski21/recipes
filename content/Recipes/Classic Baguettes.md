## Ingredients

**Poolish:**
- 1/2 cup (113g) water, cool
- 1/16 teaspoon active dry yeast or instant yeast (alternative: 1 tablespoon/25g healthy sourdough starter)
- 1 cup (120g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour

**Dough:**
- 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons (255g) water, lukewarm
- All of the poolish
- 1 1/2 teaspoons active dry yeast or instant yeast
- 3 1/2 cups (420g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 2 teaspoons (12g) table salt

## Directions

- Mix poolish ingredients; cover, rest at room temperature ~14 hours overnight until bubbly.
- Combine dough ingredients and knead by hand, mixer, or bread machine until soft and somewhat smooth; dough should stick slightly at bowl bottom.
- Place in greased bowl, cover, rise 45 minutes. Gently deflate, fold edges to center, turn over, rise another 45 minutes until puffy.
- Turn onto lightly greased surface, deflate gently, divide into three equal pieces.
- Round each into a rough ball, cover with greased plastic, rest 15 minutes to 1 hour.
- Working one piece at a time, flatten slightly, fold nearly in half, seal edges with heel of hand. Turn 180° and repeat twice more.
- With seam side down, roll into a 16-inch log, tapering ends to a point.
- Place logs seam-side down on a greased sheet pan/parchment, or in floured cotton towel folds. Cover, rise 45 minutes to 1 hour until "marshmallow-y."
- Preheat oven to 450°F with cast iron pan on lowest rack (or baking stone on middle rack). Heat 1 1/2 cups water to boiling.
- Roll baguettes onto parchment seam-side down. Slash 3-5 times lengthwise at a 45° angle with a lame or sharp knife.
- Load into oven, pour boiling water into the cast iron pan, close door quickly to create steam.
- Bake 24-28 minutes until deep golden brown. Cool on a rack, or for crispiest results turn off oven, crack door 2 inches, and cool completely inside.
- Store leftovers in a paper bag overnight; freeze for longer storage. Thaw and reheat before serving.
