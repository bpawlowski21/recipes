## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, plus more for rubbing
- 1 large head cauliflower, cut into small florets (about 6 cups chopped)
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 2-3 chipotle peppers in adobo, finely chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 cup red enchilada sauce
- 8-12 corn tortillas, warmed
- 1/2 cup shredded pepper jack cheese
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- Salsa, shredded lettuce, and pickled onions for serving

### Honey Lime Avocado Crema:
- 1 large avocado, halved
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro
- 1-2 teaspoons honey
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- Juice from 2 limes
- Kosher or flaky salt

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 425°F. Heat olive oil in large skillet over high heat. Add cauliflower and onion, cooking until charred (about 5 minutes). Add chipotle peppers, enchilada sauce, oregano, salt, and 1/3 cup water. Reduce heat to medium and simmer 10 minutes until sauce thickens.
- Warm tortillas in microwave 30 seconds to 1 minute. Rub with olive oil on baking sheet. Layer each with cheese and cauliflower filling, fold in half. Bake 5-8 minutes, flip, and bake another 5 minutes until cheese melts and tortillas crisp.
- Meanwhile, combine all crema ingredients in blender and blend until smooth. Season with salt.
- Serve tacos topped with crema, lettuce, limes, and desired toppings.
