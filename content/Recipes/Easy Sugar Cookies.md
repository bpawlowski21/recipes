## Ingredients

### Sugar Cookies
- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 large egg
- 2 1/2 cups (315 g) all-purpose flour
- 3/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 3/4 teaspoon table salt

### Sugar Cookie Frosting
- 3 cups (375 g) powdered sugar, sifted
- 3-4 tablespoons milk
- 2 tablespoons light corn syrup
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Gel food coloring (optional)
- Decorative candies and sprinkles (optional)

## Directions

- Combine butter and sugar in a stand mixer bowl and beat until creamy and well-combined.
- Add egg and vanilla extract; beat until completely combined.
- In a separate medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt.
- Gradually stir dry ingredients into wet mixture until dough is smooth.
- Divide dough in half, wrap each portion in plastic wrap, flatten into disks, and refrigerate 2-3 hours (up to 5 days).
- Preheat oven to 350°F and line baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Dust work surface with flour and roll one dough disk to 1/8" (crispy) or 1/4" (soft) thickness.
- Cut shapes with cookie cutters and transfer to baking sheet, spacing 1" apart.
- Bake 8-10 minutes until edges begin turning light golden brown.
- Cool completely on cookie sheet before decorating.
- For frosting: combine powdered sugar, 2 tablespoons milk, corn syrup, and vanilla in a medium bowl; stir until combined. Adjust consistency with more milk (too thick) or powdered sugar (too thin).
- Divide into bowls and add gel food coloring as desired. Transfer to piping bags and decorate cooled cookies. Allow frosting to harden before stacking or storing.
