## Ingredients

- 14 tablespoons (196 g) salted butter
- 2 1/2 cups (300 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon flaky sea salt
- 1 cup (213 g) dark brown sugar, packed
- 1/2 cup (99 g) granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 cup milk chocolate chips
- 3/4 cup dark chocolate chips
- 1/2 cup bittersweet chocolate chips

## Directions

- Melt butter in a small saucepan over medium-high heat for about 2 minutes, stirring constantly until lightly browned. Transfer to a heatproof bowl and refrigerate until room temperature (about 1-1.5 hours).
- Preheat oven to 375°F. Line 2 large baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Whisk together flour, baking soda, and sea salt in a medium bowl; set aside.
- In a stand mixer, beat chilled brown butter and both sugars for 2 minutes until fluffy. Add vanilla and beat smooth. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
- Gently fold in the flour mixture with a wooden spoon until flour disappears. Fold in chocolate chips.
- Roll 3-tablespoon-sized dough balls and place on prepared sheets with spacing for spreading.
- Bake for 11 minutes until golden at edges but still soft in the middle.
- Cool on baking sheet for 10-12 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
