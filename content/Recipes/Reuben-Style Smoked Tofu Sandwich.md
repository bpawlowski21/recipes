## Ingredients

- 200g smoked tofu
- ½ cup beetroot juice
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp maple syrup (or other sweetener)
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder

**Russian dressing**
- 1 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
- Dash hot sauce
- Dash vegan Worcestershire sauce
- 1 finely chopped shallot
- 2 cornichon or ½ dill pickle, finely chopped
- Pinch paprika

**Else:** sourdough or rye bread, vegan cheese, sauerkraut

## Directions

1. Slice the smoked tofu into thin, "meaty" strips.
2. In a shallow dish, combine the beetroot juice, soy sauce, apple cider vinegar, smoked paprika, maple syrup, garlic powder, and onion powder. Submerge the tofu strips in the marinade and let it sit at least 30 minutes (up to 2 hours for deeper color and flavor).
3. Air fry at 200°C for about 10–12 minutes, flipping halfway.
4. Mix the Russian dressing ingredients — chunky or smooth, to taste.
5. Spread the Russian dressing on buttered bread. Add a layer of sauerkraut, the marinated tofu slices, and vegan cheese if using.
6. Butter the outsides of the sandwich and grill in a pan over medium heat about 3–5 minutes on each side, until golden and crisp. Optionally finish under the broiler for extra crispness.
