Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta") — despite the folder, this is not actually vegan (contains cheese and honey). An Instagram Reel.

## Ingredients

- 2 pears
- 50 g almonds
- 120 g gorgonzola
- 2 sprigs of thyme
- Honey

## Directions

1. Halve the pears and top with almonds, gorgonzola, and thyme.
2. Bake 15–20 minutes at 350°F / 180°C.
3. Drizzle with honey.
