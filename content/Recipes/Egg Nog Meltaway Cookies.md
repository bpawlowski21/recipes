Santa called, he wants Egg Nog Meltaway Cookies this Christmas. And I don't blame him! These pillowy, melt-in-your-mouth Christmas Cookies are flavored with Egg Nog, Vanilla, Cinnamon + Nutmeg and seriously taste like Christmas.

Prep Time: 30 minutes · Cook Time: 10 minutes · Servings: 50 small cookies · Calories: 81 kcal

Printed recipe with handwritten annotations in the margins from an actual bake — original scan (includes the handwritten notes transcribed below):

![[Egg Nog Meltaway Cookies (annotated).pdf]]

## Ingredients

- 1 cup salted butter
- 1¼ cup powdered sugar
- ¼ cup egg nog
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2½ cups cake flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt *(handwritten correction: use ¼ tsp instead)*
- ¼ tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp nutmeg

**Glaze**
- 2 tbsp salted butter
- ½ tbsp shortening
- ¾ cup powdered sugar *(handwritten correction: use ½ cup instead)*
- ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
- ⅛ cup milk (plus more if needed) *(handwritten note: substitute egg nog for the milk)*
- ½ tsp vanilla extract

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Line a cookie sheet with parchment paper and set aside.
2. In the bowl of a stand mixer or with an electric hand mixer, combine the butter, powdered sugar and egg nog until smooth and creamy.
3. Add the vanilla, mixing until evenly distributed.
4. Add the cake flour, baking powder, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg. Mix until a sticky dough forms. Allow the dough to rest for 5 or so minutes — it will be easier to handle that way. *(handwritten note: chill in fridge for 5-10 min)*
5. Portion out the dough into small, ½ tbsp portions. Roll into a ball and arrange on the cookie sheet. *(handwritten note: did not spread too much — put 24-30 per sheet)*
6. Repeat with remaining dough.
7. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 7-9 minutes, or until the cookies have spread slightly and look dry on the top.
8. While the cookies are baking, make the icing by combining the butter and vegetable shortening until smooth.
9. Add the powdered sugar, ground nutmeg and milk to bring the mixture together into a thick frosting. *(handwritten note: "egg nog!" — use egg nog here)*
10. Continue mixing while adding the vanilla extract and enough milk to thin out the icing.
11. Microwave the icing for about 10 seconds to thin and warm it, and as soon as the cookies come out of the oven, ~~dip, spread or brush the icing onto the cookie~~. *(handwritten correction: let cookies cool first, then dip)*
12. Allow the cookies to cool and enjoy!

## Notes

- Source credits "Cookies for Days" (Instagram: @cookiesfordaaays) for the base recipe.
- **Handwritten annotations from an actual bake, dated 12.20.24 ("made for cookie exchange"):**
  - Reduce salt in the dough from ½ tsp to ¼ tsp.
  - Reduce powdered sugar in the glaze from ¾ cup to ½ cup.
  - Use egg nog in place of the milk in the glaze (twice noted — both mixing it in and thinning it out).
  - Chill the dough in the fridge for 5-10 minutes before rolling into balls, rather than just resting it.
  - Dough didn't spread too much when baked — fits 24-30 cookies per sheet.
  - Let the cookies cool completely before dipping/glazing, rather than glazing straight out of the oven as originally written.
