## Ingredients

**Levain build:**
- 100g bread flour
- 20g whole wheat flour
- 120g water (26°C / 78°F)
- 20g mature starter

**Final dough:**
- 740g bread flour
- 130g whole wheat flour
- 620g water (initial mix)
- 30g water (added with salt)
- 20g salt
- 225g liquid levain

## Directions

- **Mix the levain (night before):** combine all levain ingredients, cover loosely, ferment overnight (10-12 hours) at 18-21°C (65-70°F) until doubled, bubbly, and mildly tangy.
- **Autolyse:** mix bread flour, whole wheat flour, and 620g water until combined; cover, rest 20-30 minutes.
- **Incorporate levain:** add 225g levain, mix by hand, cover, rest 30-60 minutes.
- **Add salt & water:** sprinkle 20g salt over dough, add remaining 30g water, pinch until fully incorporated.
- **Bulk fermentation** (3-3.5 hours at 26°C): stretch and folds at 60, 90, and 120 minutes. Dough should look light, airy, slightly domed, with visible bubbles.
- **Pre-shape & final shape:** divide into two ~880g portions, pre-shape into rounds, rest 30 minutes uncovered, shape into boules or batards, place seam-side up in floured bannetons.
- **Cold fermentation:** rest at room temperature 20 minutes, then refrigerate 12-18 hours at 3-5°C (38-41°F).
- **Preheat & bake:** preheat Dutch oven at 260°C (500°F) for 1 hour. Place dough on parchment, transfer to Dutch oven, lower to 245°C (470°F). Bake 35-40 minutes, removing lid after 20 minutes.
- **Cool** on a rack at least 1 hour before slicing.
