Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. "The perfect after dinner treat and sort of guilt free. Can be made your way using your favorite nut butters and toppings."

## Ingredients

- 12 soft medjool dates
- ½ cup melted almond butter
- ¼ cup chopped almonds
- ¾ cup melted chocolate chips (author used non-dairy)
- Handful of dried cranberries
- Sprinkle of flaky salt

## Directions

1. On a piece of parchment paper, place the pitted dates in a row (author did 3 across, 4 rows down). Press them down and together so they stick to each other.
2. Pour the melted nut butter over the dates and spread it over them. Add the chopped nuts for extra crunch.
3. Melt the chocolate in the microwave in increments of 30 seconds, mixing each time — takes about 2–3 rounds. Pour over and add the toppings on top.
4. Freeze for a minimum of 3 hours or overnight. Chop it up and enjoy. Keep frozen for best results.
