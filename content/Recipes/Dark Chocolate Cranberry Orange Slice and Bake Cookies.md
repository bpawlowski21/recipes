## Ingredients

- 4 ounces unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
- 1 teaspoon orange zest, finely grated
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup dried cranberries
- 8 ounces dark chocolate, chopped
- Sparkling sugar (optional)

## Directions

- Combine butter, orange zest, and sugar in a stand mixer with paddle attachment (or handheld mixer) and beat on medium-high until pale and fluffy. Add vanilla and egg, then mix in flour and cranberries until a stiff dough forms.
- Knead dough briefly on a floured surface. Divide in half and roll each portion into a 2-inch diameter log. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 3 hours (up to 5 days).
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper. Slice logs into 1/4-inch rounds and space 1/2 inch apart.
- Bake 10-12 minutes until lightly golden. Cool on pan for at least 15 minutes.
- Melt chocolate using a double boiler or microwave (in 15-second increments on low power). Dip each cookie halfway into chocolate. Optional: sprinkle with turbinado sugar and orange zest.
- Allow chocolate to set before serving.
