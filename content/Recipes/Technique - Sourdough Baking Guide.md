## Key Tips

- Sourdough refers to both the bread and the starter used to make it; flour mixed with liquid activates friendly bacteria and wild yeast, which "generate byproducts that cause bread to rise and give it complex, rich flavor."
- **How it rises:** wild yeast in the flour and environment produces carbon dioxide bubbles; trapped by gluten, these bubbles cause the bread to rise.
- **Flavor development:** sourdough's signature taste comes from friendly bacteria and yeast, which produce flavorful lactic and acetic acids during fermentation.
- **Philosophy:** there are as many ways to create, nurture, and bake with a starter as there are bakers in the world — no single "right" way.
