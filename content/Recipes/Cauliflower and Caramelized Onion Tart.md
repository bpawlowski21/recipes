## Ingredients

- 1 small head of cauliflower (about 1 pound), cut into 1-inch florets
- 3 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon truffle oil or truffle salt (optional)
- 1 refrigerated pie crust or homemade tart shell (see below)
- 1 large onion, halved lengthwise and thinly sliced
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 2 large eggs
- 1 (7 to 8-ounce) container mascarpone cheese
- 1/2 cup whipping cream
- 1/4 teaspoon ground white or black pepper
- Pinch of ground nutmeg
- 1 cup grated Gruyère cheese
- 1/3 cup Parmesan cheese
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Homemade Savory Tart Shell**
- 1 1/4 cups flour
- 1 tablespoon plus 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 6 tablespoons butter, diced
- 1 egg

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss cauliflower with 2 tablespoons olive oil, spread on a baking sheet, season with salt and pepper. Roast 15 minutes, turn florets over, and roast another 15-25 minutes until brown and tender.
2. Cool cauliflower, thinly slice, and drizzle with truffle oil or sprinkle with truffle salt if using. Reduce oven temperature to 350°F.
3. If using a store-bought crust, press into a 9-inch tart pan, line with foil and pie weights, and bake 20 minutes. Remove foil and weights and bake 5 more minutes until golden. Press down any bubbles and cool. (For the homemade shell: combine flour, cornstarch, and salt; cut in butter until it resembles tiny bits; add egg and mix with a fork until dough forms, kneading if necessary; roll to a 12-inch circle, press into a 9-inch tart pan, crimp edges, and refrigerate 30 minutes — no par-baking required.)
4. Heat remaining 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil in a skillet over medium-low heat. Add onion, season with salt and pepper, and cook until deep golden brown, stirring occasionally, about 30 minutes. Cool slightly.
5. Spread mustard over the crust bottom and sides. Layer onion, then cauliflower on top.
6. Place tart on a rimmed baking sheet. Whisk eggs, mascarpone, cream, pepper, and nutmeg together. Stir in Gruyère. Pour mixture over the filling and sprinkle with Parmesan.
7. Bake until golden and the center is set, about 40 minutes. Cool 15 minutes before serving.
