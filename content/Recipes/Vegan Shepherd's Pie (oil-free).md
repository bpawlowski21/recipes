Hearty, filling, delicious vegan shepherd's pie, great for the holidays or as a main course. It is oil-free, gluten-free, 100% plant-based, soy-free, and has no added sugar.

Prep Time: 15 minutes · Cook Time: 45 minutes · Total Time: 1 hour

## Ingredients

**Bottom Layer:**
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 6 oz can tomato paste
- 3 cups cooked french lentils (brown lentils — pre-cooked from Trader Joe's work well)
- 2 cups chopped carrots
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 2 cups chopped mini bella mushrooms (white button mushrooms work too)
- 1/2 cup red wine (organic vegan wine, such as Trader Joe's Charles Shaw brand)
- 1 1/4 cup veggie broth
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp dried rosemary
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1.5 tsp salt

**Mashed Potato Layer:**
- 6 medium yukon gold potatoes
- 1/4 cup nutritional yeast
- 1 cup almond milk
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F. Peel and cut up the potatoes and bring a large pot of water to a boil. Once boiling, add potatoes into pot and cook for about 20 minutes.
2. In a large non-stick pan or pot, saute onions, carrots, frozen peas, and mushrooms in 1/2 cup veggie broth until soft, completely cooked, and all liquid is cooked out.
3. Add lentils, tomato paste, red wine, rosemary, thyme, parsley, garlic, and salt to the pan along with the remainder of the veggie broth (3/4 cup). Stir and combine completely and saute for 5 minutes.
4. Add into a 9x12 casserole dish and evenly distribute. Bake for 15 minutes.
5. Drain potatoes and put them back into the pot. Add in nutritional yeast, almond milk, garlic, and water and mash until smooth.
6. Remove dish from oven and top with mashed potatoes — pipe them on using a large ziplock bag with a corner cut off, or simply add dollops of the potatoes and gently spread.
7. Bake for another 15 minutes then broil on high for another 10 minutes or until the top is golden brown.
8. Remove from oven and enjoy!
