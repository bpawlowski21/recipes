## Ingredients

### Chocolate Sheet Cake
- 3 ounces semi-sweet chocolate, finely chopped
- 1 stick (4 ounces) unsalted butter
- 1/3 cup melted coconut oil, canola oil, or vegetable oil
- 1 cup cocoa powder
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup light brown sugar, packed
- 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
- 1½ teaspoons baking soda
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 large eggs + 1 large egg yolk, at room temperature
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup whole milk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup freshly brewed coffee or freshly boiled water

### Chocolate Frosting
- 1 cup (8 ounces) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 2 cups confectioners' sugar
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 1½ tablespoons heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 6 ounces (170g) quality dark chocolate, melted and cooled for 10 minutes
- Chocolate sprinkles (optional)

## Directions

### Cake
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9x13-inch baking pan with non-stick spray.
- Microwave chocolate, butter, and oil in 30-second increments, stirring between each, until melted. Whisk smooth and set aside.
- Combine cocoa powder, both sugars, flour, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
- In another bowl, beat eggs, yolk, sour cream, milk, and vanilla until well combined.
- Pour chocolate mixture into wet ingredients and stir to combine. Add dry ingredients and mix until just combined.
- Add hot coffee and let sit 1 minute, then gently stir until fully combined. Batter will be thin.
- Pour batter into prepared pan and smooth the top. Bake 38-45 minutes until a cake tester comes out clean.
- Cool completely on a rack before frosting.

### Frosting
- Beat butter on medium speed until smooth. Gradually add confectioners' sugar on low speed.
- Add salt, cream, and vanilla, then beat smooth.
- Add melted chocolate and beat until smooth. Increase speed to medium-high for 1 minute.
- Spread frosting on cooled cake. Top with sprinkles if desired.
