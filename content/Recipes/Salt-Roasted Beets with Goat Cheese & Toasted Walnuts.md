Total time: 1 hour 15 minutes · Yield: 4 as a side dish · Diet: Vegetarian

"I've recently updated/simplified the recipe." Notes from the author: if you liked the original dressing that called for reducing fresh orange juice, there's a Saveur recipe for that instead — the recipe below simply calls for fresh orange juice, vinegar, shallots, and olive oil, with no reducing of the juice required. Because some people don't love wasting so much salt for the salt-roasted method, a steam-roasted method is included as well. To toast the walnuts, place them on a sheet pan and roast at 375°F (can do this after the beets are removed) for 8 minutes or until they begin to smell and look toasty.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 to 2 lbs. beets, washed, greens removed
- kosher salt, if doing the salt-roasted method
- a few sprigs thyme and rosemary, optional

**For the Dressing**
- 1/4 cup finely minced shallot
- 1/4 cup white balsamic vinegar
- juice of one orange, about 1/4 cup
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/3 to 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil

**For Assembly**
- goat cheese, about 4 ounces
- 1/2 cup walnuts, toasted (see notes above)
- greens, about 5 ounces
- chives, snipped with scissors or finely chopped
