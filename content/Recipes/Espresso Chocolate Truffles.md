## Ingredients

### Espresso Chocolate Truffles
- 2 2/3 cups (454g/16 oz) semi-sweet chocolate, finely chopped
- 1 cup (227g/8 oz) heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons espresso powder
- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, room temperature, cubed

### Chocolate Coating
- 2 2/3 cups (454g/16 oz) semi-sweet chocolate, finely chopped
- 72 espresso beans or coffee beans

## Directions

- Place chopped chocolate in a medium heatproof bowl.
- Heat cream in a small saucepan over medium heat until boiling. Remove from heat and whisk in espresso powder.
- Pour cream mixture over chocolate; let sit untouched for 2 minutes. Whisk until smooth, then stir in butter and whisk vigorously until shiny.
- Cool to room temperature, cover with plastic wrap, and refrigerate 2 hours until firm enough to scoop.
- Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Scoop approximately 3 teaspoons of filling and roll between hands to form balls. Transfer to baking sheet and refrigerate rolled truffles at least 20 minutes before dipping.
- For the coating: fill a medium pot one-third full with water and bring to a low simmer. Place a heatproof bowl on top without touching the water. Reduce heat to low and add two-thirds of the chocolate.
- Stir frequently with a silicone spatula, keeping temperature below 120°F.
- Remove bowl from heat; wipe bottom to remove condensation.
- Stir in remaining chocolate gradually, allowing each addition to fully melt.
- Cool chocolate to 82°F, then reheat over simmering water to 88-91°F. Remove from heat.
- Using a fork or candy dipper, dip each truffle in chocolate, allowing excess to drip off before returning to parchment. Top each with two espresso beans.
- Allow chocolate to set approximately 1 hour before serving.
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.
