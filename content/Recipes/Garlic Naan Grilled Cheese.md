## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons salted butter, at room temperature
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh herbs (parsley, cilantro, chives, and/or dill)
- 2 pieces naan (homemade or store-bought)
- 1 1/2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1/2 cup shredded havarti or fontina cheese
- Honey, for serving
- Crushed red pepper flakes for serving

## Directions

- Mix together the butter, garlic, and herbs in a small bowl.
- Spread one side of each naan piece with garlic butter. Flip and layer cheese on one half of the naan. Fold the other half over the cheese (like a taco). Repeat with the second piece.
- Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Place naan (buttered sides out) one at a time in the skillet and cook until golden on each side, about 3-4 minutes per side, until the cheese inside has melted.
- Serve topped with honey, chili flakes, and sea salt as desired.
