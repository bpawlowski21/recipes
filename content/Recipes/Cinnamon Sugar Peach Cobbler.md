## Ingredients

**Biscuits**
- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 1/2 sticks (12 tablespoons) cold salted butter, cubed, plus melted butter for brushing
- 3/4 cup cold buttermilk
- Cinnamon sugar for sprinkling

**Filling**
- 6 cups fresh or frozen peaches, sliced (about 3 pounds)
- 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup bourbon (optional)

**Crumble**
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons light brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 4 tablespoons salted butter, at room temperature

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Combine flour, baking powder, and butter in a food processor; pulse until pea-sized clumps form. Drizzle in buttermilk until dough becomes shaggy.
3. Turn dough onto a clean surface and pat into a 1-inch square. Cut into 4 pieces, stack, and flatten. Roll into a 1-inch rectangle and cut into 18-20 biscuits using a 1.5-2 inch cutter. Freeze on a parchment-lined sheet for 10 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, combine peaches, brown sugar, cornstarch, bourbon, and vanilla in a 10-12 inch baking dish.
5. Mix flour, brown sugar, and cinnamon in a small bowl. Add butter and combine by hand until a crumble forms. Sprinkle over peaches.
6. Remove biscuits from freezer, brush with melted butter, sprinkle with cinnamon sugar, and arrange on top of the filling. Place dish on a baking sheet.
7. Bake 50-55 minutes until golden. Cool 5 minutes. Serve warm or at room temperature with ice cream.
