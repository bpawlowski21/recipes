Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post ("Veganuary 7/25 — 25 recipes in 25mins: Tofu Döner"). "In honor of the national German 'Dönerstag' we have this beautiful Tofu Döner today!" Prep time: 20 minutes.

## Ingredients

**Tofu**
- 200g smoked or extra firm tofu, peeled into slices with a peeler
- 1 tsp each cumin, paprika, chicken-style spice
- ½ tsp each garlic powder, ground coriander
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- Salt to taste

**Quick salad**
- 1 cucumber
- A handful tomatoes
- ½ bunch parsley
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Salt to taste

**Garlic sauce**
- 200g unsweetened vegan yogurt
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- ½ tbsp white wine vinegar
- Salt to taste
- A pinch of sugar

**To assemble:** 2 döner flatbreads

## Directions

1. Add the tofu slices to a pan with hot oil. Season with the cumin, paprika, chicken-style spice, garlic powder, coriander, soy sauce, and salt. Fry a few minutes until golden.
2. Chop the cucumber, tomatoes, and parsley for the salad, add the lemon juice and salt, and mix.
3. Mix the garlic sauce ingredients together well.
4. Assemble the döner in the flatbreads and take a big bite.
