## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup diced yellow onion
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 can chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 1 cup diced zucchini
- 16 oz orzo
- 6 cups water
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 2 cups chopped spinach
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Vegan Parmesan

## Directions

1. In a large pot on medium heat, sauté the onion, garlic, tomatoes, zucchini, and chickpeas for a few minutes.
2. Pour in the orzo and water — start with 4 cups of water and add 2 cups more if the orzo still isn't tender.
3. Turn the stove to medium-high and stir every 30 seconds until all the liquid is absorbed. When it's almost done, add the peas and spinach.
4. This should take about 10 minutes. Taste the orzo once the liquid is absorbed and make sure it's tender to your liking.
5. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
6. Sprinkle on vegan Parmesan and enjoy!
