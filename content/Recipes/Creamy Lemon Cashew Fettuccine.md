## Ingredients

- 16 oz fettuccine
- 2 cups pasta water (save it!)
- 1 cup raw cashews, soaked in hot water or boiled
- 1.5 lemons, juiced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ onion & 3 cloves garlic, sautéed
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- ½ tsp pepper
- 1 tsp salt

## Directions

1. Boil the pasta, then save 2 cups of pasta water before straining.
2. Sauté the onion and garlic in 1 tbsp olive oil.
3. Add the rest of the ingredients to a blender along with the onion and garlic, and blend until smooth.
4. Pour the sauce over the pasta and garnish with vegan Parmesan and parsley.
5. Enjoy.
