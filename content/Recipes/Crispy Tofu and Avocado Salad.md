## Ingredients

**Tofu**
- 2 packages firm tofu (15.5oz/439g each)
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ⅓ cup bread crumbs
- Avocado oil spray

**Salad**
- 1 package prewashed microgreens (or arugula)
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 2 persian cucumbers

**Dressing** (mix all in a small bowl)
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon hot honey (or regular)
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 orange, juiced
- ½ teaspoon flaky salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper

**Garnish:** black sesame seeds

## Directions

1. Press tofu to release some water.
2. Slice the tofu into thick cubes, then cut each cube in half.
3. Mix tofu spices in a small bowl. Place the tofu on a baking sheet and season, making sure it's well coated. Spray with avocado oil.
4. Bake in a preheated convection oven at 450°F for 10-15 minutes, until golden and crispy.
5. On a plate, arrange microgreens and place avocado slices on either side.
6. Pour salad dressing over the greens and avocado.
7. Add the crispy tofu, garnish with black sesame seeds.
8. Enjoy!
