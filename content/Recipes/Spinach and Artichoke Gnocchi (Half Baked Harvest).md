## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 medium sweet onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon fennel seed
- 1/4-1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- Kosher salt and black pepper to taste
- 1 pound uncooked potato gnocchi
- 3 cups fresh baby spinach or roughly torn kale
- 1/3 cup dry white wine (Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc)
- 3/4 cup canned full fat coconut milk, heavy cream, or whole milk
- 1/3 cup basil pesto (homemade or store-bought)
- 1 jar (12 ounce) marinated, quartered artichokes, drained
- 1/2 cup shredded provolone or fontina cheese
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- Fresh basil for serving

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Add onion and cook until fragrant, about 5 minutes. Add garlic, oregano, fennel seed, and red pepper flakes; cook until lightly golden, 2-3 minutes. Stir in gnocchi, spinach/kale, wine, and 1 cup water. Season with salt and pepper. Bring to a boil and cook 3-5 minutes until gnocchi is soft. Add cream, pesto, and artichokes; cook 5 more minutes. Remove from heat and top with cheese.
3. Transfer to oven and bake uncovered for 10 minutes until cheese melts and sauce bubbles. Serve topped with lemon zest, juice, and fresh basil if desired.
