Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram comment screenshot. "This is a game changing condiment for your larder... goes in burgers, sandwiches, in rice bowls, noodle dishes... wherever you like."

## Ingredients

- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 red onion
- 1 block firm smoked tofu
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp sweet paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper
- 1½ tbsp maple syrup
- 1½ tbsp balsamic vinegar (post used an oak-smoked one)
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- Generous amount sea salt
- Drizzle olive oil

## Directions

1. Fry the onion and garlic in some oil until softened and fragrant.
2. Add the tofu and let it cook and char slightly, then add both paprikas and the cayenne and stir to combine.
3. Add the maple syrup, brown sugar, salt, and balsamic vinegar and bring down to a simmer. Stir and let it bubble away until thickened and sticky.
4. Pulse it only slightly in a blender, just to break up some of the larger bits of tofu. Add it back to the pan for a further 5 minutes, then transfer to a sterilized jar and refrigerate.
