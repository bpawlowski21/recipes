## Ingredients

- 3 pounds small red potatoes, scrubbed, washed and quartered (Wendy: cut into small, bite-sized pieces)
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar, plus a pinch for dressing
- 4 tbsp white wine vinegar, divided
- Kosher salt & ground black pepper
- ½ cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard
- ¼-½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes, to taste
- ½ cup fresh basil, gently packed & chopped (Wendy substituted basil for the original parsley)
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, gently packed & chopped (Wendy substituted cilantro for the original dill)
- 3 tbsp fresh cilantro, gently packed & chopped
- 4 scallions (green part only), thinly sliced (about ½ cup)
- 3 ribs celery, finely diced (about 1 cup)
- 1 small red onion, finely diced (about ¾ cup) — Wendy didn't add this
- Optional: 2 chopped hard-boiled eggs (Wendy's addition; not vegan if used)

## Directions

- Cook potatoes: Add 2½ quarts of COLD water to a large saucepan or pot. Add potatoes and bring the water to a gentle boil over high heat (do not continuously boil or they'll fall apart). Add in 2 tablespoons of salt, all of the sugar and 2 tablespoons of vinegar. Reduce heat to maintain a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are fork tender, about 10-12 minutes. Drain the potatoes in a colander and transfer to a large mixing bowl.
- While the potatoes are cooking, make the dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together the oil, garlic, remaining 2 tablespoons of vinegar, Dijon, crushed red pepper flakes and a generous pinch of sugar. Season with ¾ teaspoon of kosher salt and ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper. Whisk until fully combined. Add in the basil, cilantro (both amounts). Stir until combined. Set aside.
- Finish the potato salad: Drizzle half of the dressing over the warm potatoes and gently toss to combine. Add in the scallions, celery, and red onion (if using). Toss again. Drizzle in the remaining dressing to taste. Gently toss until everything is coated. Taste and adjust for seasoning with salt and pepper.
- Serve warm, at room temperature or chilled. If serving warm and right away, let it sit out on the counter for at least 10 minutes for the flavors to marry together.

## Notes

- Do NOT continuously boil the potatoes or they will be more susceptible to falling apart and becoming mushy.
- Make sure the potatoes are still warm when tossed with the dressing — warm potatoes soak up the dressing much better than cool potatoes.
- Can be kept in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
- Wendy's handwritten notes on the card: "excellent, most delicious recipe ever!" She swapped basil for the parsley and cilantro for the dill, didn't add the red onion, added 2 chopped hard-boiled eggs (noting this makes it not vegan), and clarified to cut the potatoes into small, bite-sized pieces.
