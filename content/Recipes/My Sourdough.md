## Ingredients

### Levain
- 30g ripe sourdough starter (100% hydration)
- 30g medium-protein bread flour
- 30g whole wheat flour
- 60g water

### Main Dough
- 822g medium-protein bread flour
- 64g whole wheat flour
- 745g water (650g Water 1 + 95g Water 2)
- 17g fine sea salt
- 151g ripe liquid levain

Yield: two 900g loaves (1,800g total dough weight).

## Directions

- Levain (9:00 AM): Mix levain ingredients and hold at 77-80°F for 5 hours.
- Autolyse (12:00 PM): Combine flour and 650g water until hydrated; rest 2 hours near the levain.
- Mix (2:00 PM): Add 95g water, salt, and levain to the dough. Perform in-bowl folds for 2-3 minutes until cohesive. Transfer to bulk fermentation container.
- Bulk Fermentation (2:15-6:15 PM): Perform six stretch-and-fold sets — first three at 15-minute intervals, final three at 30-minute intervals. Rest for the remainder of bulk after folds. Maintain 78°F dough temperature.
- Divide & Preshape (6:15 PM): Divide into two pieces; gently preshape into rounds. Rest 30 minutes uncovered.
- Shape (6:45 PM): Shape into batard or boule; place in floured bannetons.
- Proof (7:25 PM - 9:00 AM next day): Cover and refrigerate at 38°F overnight.
- Bake (9:00 AM): Preheat to 450°F. Score dough; bake 20 minutes with steam, then 30 minutes without. Cool 1-2 hours on a rack.
