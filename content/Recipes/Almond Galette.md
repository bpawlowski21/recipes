## Ingredients

### Crust
- 1 1/2 cups (180g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 16 tablespoons (227g) unsalted butter, cold
- 1/2 cup (113g) sour cream

### Filling
- 2/3 cup (170g) almond paste
- 6 tablespoons (85g) unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup (99g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 2 large egg yolks
- 1 teaspoon King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 1/3 cup (40g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1/2 cup (48g) almond flour or finely ground whole almonds

### Glaze
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 teaspoon cold water

## Directions

- Combine flour, salt, and baking powder; work in cold butter until crumbly with larger butter pieces intact.
- Mix in sour cream until dough is cohesive; knead briefly on a floured surface.
- Pat into a rough square and roll to an 8"x10" rectangle. Fold in thirds lengthwise, flip, turn 90°, and repeat the rolling and folding.
- Wrap in plastic and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
- Preheat oven to 400°F and prepare a baking sheet.
- Divide dough in half; roll one half to a 10" square and cut a 10" circle; place on the sheet.
- Beat almond paste, butter, sugar, and salt; add egg yolks and vanilla, then mix in the flours.
- Spread filling on the smaller circle, leaving a 1" rim.
- Roll the second dough half to an 11" square and cut an 11" circle.
- Brush egg glaze on the pastry rim; center the larger circle on top and crimp edges with a fork. Brush glaze over the surface.
- Decorate with knife patterns; poke a center vent and four small slits.
- Bake 30-35 minutes until golden; cool slightly on the sheet.
- Serve warm or at room temperature; store leftovers at room temperature or freeze.
