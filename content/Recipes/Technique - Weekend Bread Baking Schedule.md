## Key Tips

- The schedule spans the full week, with the refrigerator doing most of the work.
- Monday-Wednesday: the starter rests in the fridge.
- Thursday evening: remove the chilled starter and let it warm for a couple hours.
- Friday morning & evening: give two refreshments to reactivate the starter.
- Saturday morning: build the levain early, and refresh the starter again.
- Saturday afternoon/evening: mix the dough, do bulk fermentation, shape, and refrigerate overnight. Also prepare pancake batter using discard, and refresh the starter with maintenance ratios before refrigerating it.
- Sunday morning: make pancakes from the prepared batter while the oven preheats.
- Sunday afternoon: bake the bread that retarded overnight.
- Only three starter refreshments are needed (after removing from cold storage) before baking, making weekend baking manageable alongside a busy work schedule.
