## Ingredients

- 2 long slices of bread
- Enough vegan butter to butter 1 side of both bread slices
- 600g rinsed canned chickpeas
- 75g diced red bell peppers
- 15g diced spring onions
- 15g chopped fresh parsley
- ½ tsp chili flakes
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp celery salt
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 4 tbsp vegan mayo
- ½ tbsp virgin olive oil

**Garnish**
- Thinly sliced red onions
- Sliced tomatoes (post used heirloom)

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, crush the chickpeas with a fork, then mix in all the ingredients except the garnish and vegan butter.
2. Butter one side of both bread slices and toast the buttered side on a hot pan.
3. Once toasted, spread a generous amount of the chickpea salad on the non-toasted side of one slice; spread a thin layer on the second slice.
