## Ingredients

- 3 cups pineapple juice
- 3 cups carrot juice
- 1 cup orange juice
- ½ cup ginger juice
- ½ cup turmeric juice

## Directions

1. Combine all the juices.

## Notes

- Pro tip: if you don't have a juicer, throw the ingredients into a blender with water, then strain with a nut milk straining bag. Use gloves for the turmeric (it stains).
