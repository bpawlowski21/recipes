![[Sweet Potato Casserole With Pecan Crumble (handwritten).pdf]]

This sweet potato casserole is an especially festive, over-the-top take on the Thanksgiving classic, topped with a crisp pecan crumble and dotted with marshmallows. First appeared in Saveur's November 2011 issue.

## Ingredients

**For the filling:**
- 3 lbs. sweet potatoes
- 1/3 cup packed dark brown sugar
- 6 tbsp. unsalted butter, melted
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 1 tsp. ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp. freshly grated nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp. kosher salt
- 2 eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 1" piece ginger, finely grated
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

**For the crumble topping:**
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup packed dark brown sugar
- 1/4 cup finely chopped pecans
- 1/2 tsp. kosher salt
- 5 tbsp. unsalted butter, cubed and chilled
- 1/4 cup mini marshmallows

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Place sweet potatoes on a parchment paper-lined baking sheet and bake until soft, about 1 1/2 hours; let cool for 30 minutes, and then remove skins. Pass potatoes through a food mill into a large bowl; stir in sugar, butter, cream, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt, eggs, ginger, and pepper. Pour mixture into a 1 1/2-qt. baking dish and smooth top; set aside.
2. Reduce oven temperature to 350°F. Stir together flour, oats, sugar, pecans, and salt in a bowl; add butter and, using your fingers, rub butter into flour mixture until large crumbles form. Mound crumble mixture over filling, dot with marshmallows, and bake until filling is hot and crumble and marshmallows are browned, about 30 minutes. Let cool for 15 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- **Wendy's tested notes (11.27.14):** Top with 1/4 cup toasted pecans. Top with 2 cups mini marshmallows. Make the day before but don't bake — add toppings and bake the day of.
- **Wendy's tested notes (11.23.16):** Use about 4 large sweet potatoes (ish). Prick all over with a fork, place on foil, and bake at 425°F for about 1 hour 30 minutes total. Let cool, then peel. Can do this step a day in advance. Also add one 8 oz can crushed pineapple, drained.
- **Wendy's tested notes (11.26.25):** Use a whole bag of mini marshmallows instead of the crumble topping — put marshmallows on 15 minutes into baking. Make the day before and refrigerate; bake on Thanksgiving day.
