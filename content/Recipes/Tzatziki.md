Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta") — despite the folder, this uses real Greek yogurt, not a vegan substitute. "It starts with the cucumber — it needs to be peeled and properly strained. Use full-fat Greek yogurt, not 'Greek style.' Don't overload it with garlic, one clove is enough — it gets stronger as it sits. It needs acidity, vinegar is what brings it to life. And finish it with good Greek olive oil. That's it. Perfect for gyro, souvlaki, grilled meats, or your next BBQ." "Keep it simple, keep it real."

## Ingredients

- 500g full-fat Greek yogurt
- 1 English cucumber, peeled
- 1 small garlic clove, smashed
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar (for the cucumbers)
- 2 tbsp white wine vinegar (for the yogurt)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- ¼ to ½ bunch fresh dill, finely chopped
- Salt and pepper

## Directions

1. Grate the cucumber.
2. Add salt and 1 tbsp vinegar. Let it sit 20 to 30 minutes.
3. Squeeze out all the liquid — this step brings everything together.
4. Add to the yogurt. Mix in the garlic, remaining vinegar, olive oil, and dill. Season and chill.
5. Serve cold. Drizzle with olive oil. Add a sprig of thyme and an olive if you like (optional).
