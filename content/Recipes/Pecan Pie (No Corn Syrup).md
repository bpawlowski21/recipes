## Ingredients

- 1 all-butter pie crust (or favorite pie crust)
- 8 ounces pecan halves
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 cup (213g) dark brown sugar, packed
- 3/4 cup real maple syrup
- 3 tablespoons (42ml) bourbon
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 large eggs, room temperature, lightly beaten
- 1 large egg yolk, room temperature, lightly beaten
- 5 tablespoons (70g) unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 teaspoons flour

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F with a refrigerated 9-inch pastry-lined pie plate ready. Optionally sprinkle the crust bottom with 1 teaspoon flour and 1 teaspoon sugar to prevent sogginess.
- Spread pecans evenly in the unbaked crust and refrigerate while preparing the filling.
- In a large bowl, combine salt, cinnamon, and brown sugar, breaking up clumps. Whisk in maple syrup, bourbon, and vanilla, then incorporate lightly beaten eggs and egg yolk.
- In a small saucepan over medium-low heat, melt butter. Stir in flour, cooking while whisking for 30 seconds. Remove from heat and whisk this mixture into the maple syrup mixture until combined.
- Remove pie crust from fridge, pour filling over pecans, and bake approximately 45 minutes until edges set and center barely moves when pan is gently shaken.
- Cool on wire rack for 4 hours before serving.
