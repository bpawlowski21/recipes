## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 ounces amaretto liqueur
- 3/4 ounce bourbon
- 1 ounce freshly-squeezed lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon maple syrup or simple syrup (optional)
- 1 dash Angostura bitters (optional)
- 1/2 ounce egg white
- Ice
- Garnish: lemon twist and Luxardo cherry

## Directions

- Dry shake: combine amaretto, bourbon, lemon juice, maple syrup, bitters, and egg white in a cocktail shaker. Cover and shake vigorously (without ice) for 15 seconds.
- Add a large handful of ice to the shaker. Cover and shake vigorously for 15 more seconds until chilled.
- Strain into a cocktail glass over ice. Top with a lemon twist and a Luxardo cherry or two.
