## Ingredients

- 2 cup butter, cold, cut into small cubes
- 3.5 cup sugar
- 4 eggs
- 4 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 cup cocoa, high quality
- 4.5 cup flour
- 2 teaspoon baking powder
- 0.5 teaspoon salt
- 2 cup chocolate chunks
- 4-7 ounce bars Peppermint Bark or White Chocolate Candy Cane Bar, cut into chunks (may want more depending on preference; Ghirardelli or Hershey's)
- 6 candy canes, crushed

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 360 degrees.
- In a large mixing bowl, cream cold butter cubes and sugar for at least 4 minutes, scraping the bowl halfway through mixing.
- Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Add in vanilla.
- Stir in flour, cocoa, baking powder, and salt. Stir in chocolate chips.
- Drop large spoonfuls onto a cookie sheet lined with a Silpat or parchment paper.
- Place chunks of the peppermint bark bar throughout the cookie dough.
- Cook for 10-14 minutes.
- Sprinkle with crushed candy canes.
