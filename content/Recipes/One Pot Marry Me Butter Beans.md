## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 shallots
- 1 small jar sun-dried tomatoes
- 1 cup (230ml) cherry tomatoes
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp chili flakes
- ½ tsp salt & pepper
- 1 cup (230ml) coconut milk
- ½ cup (120ml) vegetable broth
- 2 cans butter beans
- 3 cups (7dl) spinach
- ¼ cup shredded parmesan cheese
- ½ cup (120ml) nutritional yeast (optional, gives a cheesy flavor)
- Fresh basil, for topping

## Directions

1. Fry the shallot for a minute.
2. Add the garlic and halved cherry tomatoes. Cook 5 minutes until the tomatoes start bursting.
3. Add the spices and sun-dried tomatoes, coconut milk, broth, nutritional yeast, parmesan, spinach, and beans. Simmer until thick and creamy.
4. Top with basil and enjoy!
