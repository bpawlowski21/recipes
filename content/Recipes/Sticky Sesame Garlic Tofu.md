## Ingredients

- 1 block firm tofu (400g)
- ¼ cup cornstarch/cornflour
- ¼ tsp sea salt
- ¼ tsp ground white pepper

**Sauce**
- 4–5 cloves garlic, grated or minced
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp tomato sauce/ketchup
- 2 packed tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- ¼ cup water
- 2 tsp cornstarch/cornflour
- 2 tsp sesame oil

**Garnish:** toasted sesame seeds, green onion

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Open the tofu and drain the excess liquid. Using clean hands, break it into bite-sized chunks. Add to a bowl with the cornstarch, salt, and pepper and toss to coat.
2. Spread the tofu on a lined baking tray so pieces don't touch or overlap. Spray generously with neutral oil and bake 20 minutes until golden and crispy (or air-fry in batches).
3. Meanwhile, fry the garlic in a splash of oil over medium heat. Add the soy sauce, tomato sauce, sugar, and vinegar. Stir well and cook 1 minute.
4. Mix the water and cornstarch together, then pour into the pan. Let it bubble, stirring frequently until thickened. Turn off heat and stir through the sesame oil.
5. Add the baked tofu and stir to coat in the sauce.
6. Transfer to a plate and garnish with sesame seeds and green onions. Enjoy immediately.

## Notes

- Not recommended to make ahead/reheat — you'll lose the crispy coating, though it's still tasty.
