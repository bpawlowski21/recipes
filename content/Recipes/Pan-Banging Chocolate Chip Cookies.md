## Ingredients

- 2 cups (284 g) all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- 2 sticks (227 g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1½ cups (297 g) granulated sugar
- ¼ cup (50 g) packed brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1½ teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons water
- 6 ounces (170 g) bittersweet chocolate, chopped into bite-size pieces

## Directions

- Adjust oven rack to middle position and preheat to 350°F. Line 3 baking sheets with aluminum foil, dull side up.
- Whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt in a small bowl.
- Beat butter on medium in stand mixer until creamy. Add sugars and beat until light and fluffy (2-3 minutes). Mix in egg, vanilla, and water on low. Add flour mixture and mix on low until combined. Fold in chocolate on low.
- Form dough into 3½-ounce (100g) balls. Place 4 balls equidistant on prepared pan and freeze for 15 minutes. After placing first sheet in oven, freeze second sheet.
- Bake for 10 minutes until slightly puffed. Lift pan side up about 4 inches and gently drop against oven rack. Repeat after cookies puff again in 2 minutes. Repeat several more times to create ridges. Bake 16-18 minutes total until edges are golden but centers remain light.
- Transfer baking sheet to wire rack and cool completely before removing cookies.
