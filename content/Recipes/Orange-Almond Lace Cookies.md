The egg makes these a little less delicate (and less likely to break) than traditional lace cookies. Arrange them in a decorative box or tin lined with festive tissue paper, then secure the package with a fancy bow.

## Ingredients

- 1 stick unsalted butter (originally called for 1 1/2 sticks — use just 1 stick)
- 1 1/2 cups finely chopped almonds (8 oz bag slivered almonds from Trader Joe's)
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour (originally 1 tablespoon)
- 2 teaspoons grated orange peel
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 large egg, beaten to blend

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Line large baking sheet with parchment paper. Stir butter in heavy medium saucepan over medium-low heat until melted. Remove from heat. Stir in nuts, sugar, flour, orange peel and salt; then stir in egg. Drop batter by small teaspoonfuls (not tablespoonfuls) onto prepared baking sheet, spacing 3 inches apart — the cookies expand a lot, so keep the spoonfuls small.
2. Bake cookies until lacy and golden brown, about 13 minutes. Gently slide parchment paper with cookies onto rack; cool completely. Repeat with remaining batter, lining cooled baking sheet with clean parchment for each batch. (Can be made ahead. Store between sheets of waxed paper in airtight container at room temperature up to 1 week or freeze up to 1 month.)
