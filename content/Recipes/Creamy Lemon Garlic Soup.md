## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 red onion
- ½ tsp paprika
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp chili flakes
- 1.2 L (5 cups) cooked butter beans
- 1 L (4 cups) vegetable broth
- Salt & black pepper, to taste
- Juice from 1 lemon + zest

## Directions

1. Heat the olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the onion, sauté about 1 minute, add the garlic, and sauté another minute.
2. Add the spices, butter beans, and broth.
3. Use an immersion blender and blend until smooth.
4. Let it simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Add the lemon juice and zest.
6. Add more salt and pepper to taste. Enjoy!
