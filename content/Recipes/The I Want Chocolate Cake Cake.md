## Ingredients

**Cake**
- 6 tablespoons (85 grams) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 3/4 cup (145 grams) firmly packed dark brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons (25 grams) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 large egg yolk
- 3/4 cup (175 ml) buttermilk
- 1 teaspoon (5 ml) vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup (41 grams) Dutch cocoa powder
- 1 cup (125 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon table or fine sea salt

**Frosting**
- 2 ounces (55 grams) unsweetened chocolate, melted and cooled
- 1 1/2 cups (180 grams) powdered sugar, sifted if lumpy
- 1/2 cup (4 ounces or 115 grams) unsalted butter, room temperature
- Pinch of fine sea salt (optional)
- 1 tablespoon cream or whole milk
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Directions

**Cake**
1. Heat oven to 350°F. Line bottom of 8-inch square cake pan with parchment; butter or spray interior.
2. Beat butter and sugars until fluffy. Add egg, yolk, and vanilla; beat until combined.
3. Mix in buttermilk.
4. Sift flour, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder, and salt over batter. Stir on low until just combined.
5. Scoop into prepared pan and smooth flat.
6. Bake 18-22 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
7. Cool 5-10 minutes in pan, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely.

**Frosting**
1. Place all frosting ingredients in a food processor.
2. Process to mix, scrape down bowl.
3. Process another 1-2 minutes until smooth and somewhat fluffy.
4. Spread onto cooled cake and swirl. Top with rainbow sprinkles.

Yield: one 8x8-inch cake, cut into 16 small squares.
