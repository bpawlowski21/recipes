## Ingredients

- 4 ounces (1/2 cup) bourbon
- 1 cup pomegranate juice
- Zest of 1 orange
- 1/2 cup freshly squeezed orange juice
- 1 (750 ml) bottle Prosecco or sparkling wine, chilled
- Arils from 1 pomegranate

## Directions

1. Combine bourbon, pomegranate juice, orange zest, and orange juice in a large pitcher. Refrigerate until serving time.
2. Just before serving, add ice to the punch bowl, pour in the Prosecco, and top with pomegranate arils.

## Notes

- Saved from Apple Notes folder "Cocktails". Label: "Bourbon, pomegranate, orange. Topped w prosecco."
- Serves 8, ~325 calories per serving.
- Optional festive ice cubes: freeze fresh cranberries, blood orange slices, and rosemary sprigs into ice cube trays to prevent dilution while adding visual appeal.
