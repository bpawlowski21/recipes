## Ingredients

- 1 large cucumber
- 1 cup (230 ml) cherry tomatoes
- ½ red onion
- 1 cup (230 ml) fresh parsley
- 1 mango
- 1 cup (230 ml) dry lentils

**Dressing**
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp water
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 1 garlic clove
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp agave syrup
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Directions

1. Cook the lentils according to package instructions.
2. Chop the cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, mango, and parsley.
3. Make the dressing by mixing all the dressing ingredients in a small bowl.
4. Assemble the salad and mix in the dressing.
