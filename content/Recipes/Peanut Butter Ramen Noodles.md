"It's time to level up your ramen to restaurant-quality levels! This peanut butter ramen recipe with smoky shredded tofu is absolutely delicious and will have everyone talking about wanting more!"

Prep Time: 15 mins · Cook Time: 30 mins · Total Time: 45 mins · Diet: Vegan, Vegetarian · Calories: 627kcal

## Ingredients

**Smoky Shredded Oven Tofu**
- 1 pound super-firm tofu (450g), pressed if using extra firm
- 3 tablespoons neutral oil (avocado or grapeseed)
- 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 teaspoons maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil, optional

**Tofu Spice Blend**
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon white pepper
- 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes, more or less to taste
- 1 teaspoon chili powder

**Noodle Spice Blend**
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon onion powder
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon ground coriander
- ½ teaspoon toasted sesame seeds
- ¼ teaspoon white pepper
- ¼ teaspoon ground ginger
- ¼ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes, more or less to taste
- ¼ teaspoon Chinese five-spice, optional

**Noodle Base**
- 1 tablespoon chili oil crunch
- 1 teaspoon neutral oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
- ¼ cup natural creamy peanut butter (60g)
- 1 tablespoon white or yellow miso
- 3 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce, divided
- 1 teaspoon maple syrup (up to 2 tsp)
- 1½ teaspoon rice vinegar

**Liquids**
- 3 cups low-sodium vegetable broth (720mL)
- 1 cup full-fat coconut milk (240mL)

**Add-Ins**
- 6 ounces uncooked ramen noodles (2 packs, seasoning discarded)
- 1-2 cups chopped bok choy or spinach

## Directions

**Bake the Smoky Shredded Tofu**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment.
2. Using the large holes of a box grater, shred the pressed tofu into a bowl. Add 3 tablespoons oil, 2 tablespoons each soy sauce and maple syrup, and if using, 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil. Toss to combine.
3. Add 2 teaspoons smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon each garlic powder, onion powder, ground coriander, white pepper, crushed red pepper flakes, and chili powder. Combine well, then spread the tofu in a thin, even layer on the baking sheet. Do not press down.
4. Bake 22-28 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until edges are darkened and slightly crispy. Set aside.

**Make the Noodle Spice Blend**
5. In a small bowl, combine 1 teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon each onion powder, smoked paprika, ground coriander, and toasted sesame seeds, ¼ teaspoon each white pepper, ground ginger, crushed red pepper flakes, and if using Chinese five-spice. Whisk together and set aside.

**Create the Creamy Broth**
6. In a large pot over medium heat, add 1 tablespoon chili oil crunch and 1 teaspoon neutral oil. Add 3 cloves minced garlic and 1 teaspoon fresh ginger. Cook 30-60 seconds until fragrant. Sprinkle in the noodle spice blend. Stir constantly for 30-45 seconds to bloom the spices in the oil. Add 1 teaspoon of the soy sauce directly into the hot oil and let it sizzle briefly to deepen the flavor.
7. Add ¼ cup natural peanut butter and 1 tablespoon miso to the pot. Stir until softened and incorporated. Add 3 tablespoons soy sauce, 1½ teaspoon rice vinegar, and 1 teaspoon maple syrup. Stir to combine.
8. Slowly pour in the vegetable broth while whisking to bring together. Stir in coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer for 5 minutes. The broth should look creamy and unified.
9. Add ramen noodles directly into the broth and cook 2-3 minutes until just tender. Add bok choy or spinach during the last minute to wilt, if desired.
10. Assemble the bowls and serve warmed.

## Notes

- Peanut butter: natural, unsweetened works best.
- For a lighter broth, substitute unsweetened soy milk for the coconut milk.
- Do not overcook the noodles — they continue softening in the hot broth.
- Tofu can be baked up to 2 days ahead and reheated briefly before serving.
- Nutritional facts are only estimates.
