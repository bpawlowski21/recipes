## Ingredients

**Gingerbread Syrup:**
- 1/4 cup honey
- 3 tablespoons molasses
- 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

**Cocktail:**
- 2 ounces (1/4 cup) vodka
- 1 1/2 ounces (3 tablespoons) Kahlúa
- 1-2 ounces (2-4 tablespoons) gingerbread syrup
- 1-2 ounces (2-4 tablespoons) heavy cream
- Cinnamon sticks and gingerbread cookies, for garnish

## Directions

**Gingerbread Syrup:** Combine 1 1/3 cups water, honey, molasses, ginger, and cinnamon in a medium pot. Bring to a boil over high heat and boil 3-5 minutes. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla. Cool. Store refrigerated up to 2 weeks.

**Cocktail:** Fill a glass with ice. Pour in vodka, Kahlúa, and gingerbread syrup. Stir gently. Add heavy cream and stir to combine. Garnish with cinnamon sticks and gingerbread cookies if desired.
