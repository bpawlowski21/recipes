Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta") — despite the folder, this recipe includes shrimp (swap for tofu to keep it vegan). "This Thai inspired chopped salad had been on repeat over here! I love this because it's fresh, easy and so delicious. Feel free to use whatever veggies you have on hand too — super versatile and forgiving." Best served cold. The sauce makes more than needed, so start small.

## Ingredients

- 1 romaine head
- 1 cup shredded cabbage
- 1 carrot, shredded
- 1 scallion, chopped
- 2 tbsp cilantro
- Shrimp
- 1 cup rice noodles
- Sesame seeds
- Lime

**Tahini dressing**
- ½ cup tahini/peanut butter
- 3 tbsp soy sauce/coconut aminos
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- ¼ cup water
- Pinch of grated ginger

## Directions

1. Cut the romaine and cabbage into thin slices and shred the carrot; set everything aside.
2. Cook the shrimp, either over the stove or by tossing in a pot of boiling water for about 4 minutes, until no longer translucent.
3. Add boiling water over the rice noodles and stir, letting them cook 3–4 minutes, then drain.
4. Add the tahini, soy sauce, rice vinegar, water, and ginger to a bowl and mix until well combined.
5. Add the veggies, rice noodles, and shrimp to a bowl and mix in the tahini sauce — start slow, you can always add more and might not need it all.
6. Top with lime juice and sesame seeds.

## Notes

- Would also be delicious with chopped nuts on top, like cashews or almonds.
