## Ingredients

**For the Cauliflower Al Pastor**
- 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo
- ½ cup pineapple juice
- ¼ cup apple cider or white vinegar
- 2 cups fresh pineapple chunks, plus additional for serving
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
- ½ yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and diced
- ½ cup fresh cilantro, chopped

**For Assembly**
- 4 whole wheat or regular flour tortillas
- ½ cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- ½ cup shredded pepper jack cheese
- Extra virgin olive oil for cooking

**For Lime Smashed Avocado**
- 2 avocados, mashed
- Juice and zest of 1 lime
- Maldon sea salt

## Directions

1. Blend garlic, chili powder, paprika, chipotle peppers, pineapple juice, vinegar, and 1 cup pineapple chunks until smooth.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add cauliflower, onions, salt, and pepper. Cook 12-15 minutes until tender and caramelized. Reduce heat to low, stir in sauce, remaining pineapple chunks, and jalapeño. Simmer 8-10 minutes until sauce reduces and coats cauliflower. Remove from heat and mix in cilantro.
3. Combine mashed avocado, lime juice, lime zest, and salt.
4. Lay tortillas flat. Layer cheeses and cauliflower on 2 tortillas, then top with remaining tortillas.
5. Heat olive oil in skillet over medium heat. Cook quesadillas until golden, about 4-5 minutes per side. Serve topped with avocado and grilled pineapple.
