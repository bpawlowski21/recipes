## Directions

1. In a large frying pan over medium-high heat, sauté spring onion and ginger until fragrant.
2. Add leek and shiitake mushroom, sauté until tender, and season with salt and pepper. Set aside to cool slightly.
3. In a large mixing bowl, crumble tofu with your hands until fine or creamy.
4. Transfer the spinach and sautéed leek/shiitake mushroom into the mixing bowl.
5. Season the filling with soy sauce, oyster sauce (or a vegan substitute), sesame oil, five spice, salt and pepper.
6. To wrap the wontons: prepare a bowl of clean cold water on the side. Scoop roughly 1 tbsp filling into the center of a wrapper. Wet all edges and fold into a sealed triangle, pressing out air bubbles. Wet one bottom side of the triangle and connect the two ends together.
7. Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook the wontons until they float, then let them stay afloat a few more seconds.
8. Transfer the cooked wontons to a serving bowl. Mix all the sauce ingredients together and pour over the wontons.
9. Garnish with spring onion, sesame seeds, and more chili oil.
