## Ingredients

**Cookies:**
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1 cup packed light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon molasses
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup old fashioned oats
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

**Vanilla Cream Filling:**
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 3 cups powdered sugar
- 2 tablespoons heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 325°F and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Using a stand mixer with whisk attachment, beat butter, brown sugar, and molasses until smooth (about 2 minutes).
- Scrape bowl, then add egg and vanilla; beat on medium-high until combined.
- Add flour, oats, baking soda, and salt; beat on low until just combined.
- Use a cookie scoop to portion dough onto baking sheets, spacing 2 inches apart.
- Bake 9-11 minutes until cookies puff and set at edges but remain soft in center. Cool on sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to cooling rack for 30 minutes.
- Once cooled, make filling by combining butter, powdered sugar, cream, vanilla, and salt in mixer; beat until creamy.
- Transfer filling to piping bag and pipe about 2 tablespoons onto one cookie's underside; sandwich with second cookie. Repeat with remaining cookies.
