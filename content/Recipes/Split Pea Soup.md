## Ingredients

### For the soup
- 2 tablespoons olive oil or 1 tablespoon olive oil and 1 tablespoon butter
- 3 leeks, halved and sliced into ribbons
- 1 carrot, chopped small
- 1 large rib celery, chopped
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 4 cloves garlic, sliced thin
- 1 pound dried green split peas, rinsed and picked over
- 2 quarts vegetable stock or broth
- 2 to 3 sprigs of thyme, leaves still on (optional)
- 1 bay leaf

### To finish
- Fresh herb sauce (garlic, parsley, lemon zest, salt, red pepper flakes, olive oil, blended until saucy)
- Sour cream
- Two slices of crumbled crisp bacon (optional, no longer vegetarian)
- Garlicky or gruyere croutons

## Directions

- On the stove: Heat a 4 to 5-quart heavy pot over medium. Add oil, or oil and butter, and once warm, add leeks, carrots, and celery. Season with salt and pepper and cook until softened and beginning to get slightly brown at edges, about 10 minutes. Add garlic and cook two minutes more. Add the dried peas and stir to coat with the vegetables, then add the vegetable stock or broth, thyme, if using, and bay leaf. Increase the heat to bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce to a low simmer and cook, partially covered, until peas have softened, 50 to 60 minutes. Remove thyme sprigs (most of the leaves will have fallen off) and bay leaf and season very well with salt and black pepper.
- In an InstantPot or electric pressure cooker: Set your pot to sauté and cook the vegetables as written above. Once you've added the stock, dried peas, and herbs, cook the mixture under high pressure for 15 minutes and then let it naturally release for at least 5 minutes, manually releasing it the rest of the way. Remove thyme sprigs and bay leaf and season very well with salt and black pepper.
- Both methods: This soup isn't pureed, but you can at this point with an immersion blender, either all or just halfway. Ladle soup into bowls and finish with garnishes of your choice.

## Notes

- If you miss the ham, you can add about 1 to 2 cups of diced cooked ham in the beginning and brown it up with vegetables.
- Quick herb sauce: blend 1 large or 2 small peeled garlic clove(s) and a couple handfuls of fresh flat-leaf parsley leaves (or a mix of herbs such as mint and/or cilantro) with the finely grated zest of half a lemon, salt, and red pepper flakes until well chopped, then drizzle in olive oil with the machine running until saucy. Season with more salt and pepper. Keeps in the fridge for a week; also great on roasted potatoes, squash, or fried eggs.
- Do ahead: Split pea soup keeps fantastically in the fridge or freezer, but it looks crazy thick once chilled. It should loosen as you rewarm it; add a splash of broth or water if needed.
