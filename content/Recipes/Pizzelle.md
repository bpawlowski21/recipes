Thin, crisp cookies that can be transformed into ice cream sandwiches or rolled into small cones.

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 3/4 cup sugar + 1 tablespoon
- 8 Tbs. (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted, plus more for brushing
- 2 tsp. vanilla extract
- 1 tsp. finely grated orange zest (originally lemon zest)
- splash of orange juice
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder

## Directions

1. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs and sugar until light yellow and thick ribbons fall from the whisk, 2 to 3 minutes. Add the melted butter, vanilla, orange zest, and splash of orange juice and whisk until blended.
2. Over a sheet of waxed paper, sift together the flour and baking powder. Fold the flour mixture into the egg mixture in two additions, folding each addition just until blended.
3. Heat a pizzelle maker according to the manufacturer's instructions. Brush lightly with butter and spoon about 1 Tbs. of the batter onto the pizzelle maker. If any batter oozes out, cut it off immediately. Cook according to the manufacturer's instructions until golden brown. Transfer the pizzelle to a wire rack and let cool. Repeat with the remaining batter.

Makes about 25 pizzelle.

## Notes

- Wendy's handwritten substitution (dated 12.24.17): used orange zest and a splash of orange juice instead of lemon zest.
- Wendy's handwritten addition: +1 tablespoon extra sugar beyond the printed 3/4 cup.
