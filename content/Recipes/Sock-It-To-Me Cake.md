Photographed from the side panel of a Duncan Hines Moist Deluxe Butter Recipe Golden Cake Mix box.

## Ingredients

**Streusel Filling**
- 2 tablespoons dry cake mix (from the box, set aside from the main mix)
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 cup chopped pecans

**Cake**
- 1 package Duncan Hines Moist Deluxe Butter Recipe Golden Cake Mix (remainder after filling is set aside)
- 4 large eggs
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/4 cup sugar

**Glaze**
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 to 2 tablespoons milk

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease and flour a 10-inch tube pan.
2. For streusel filling, combine 2 tablespoons dry cake mix, brown sugar, and cinnamon in a medium bowl. Stir in pecans; set aside.
3. For cake, combine remaining cake mix, eggs, sour cream, oil, water, and sugar in a large bowl. Beat at medium speed with an electric mixer for 2 minutes.
4. Pour 2/3 of the batter into the pan. Sprinkle with streusel filling. Spoon remaining batter evenly over the filling.
5. Bake at 375°F for 45 to 55 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
6. Cool in pan 25 minutes. Invert onto a heat-resistant serving plate. Cool completely.
7. For glaze, combine powdered sugar and milk in a small bowl. Stir until smooth. Drizzle over cake.
