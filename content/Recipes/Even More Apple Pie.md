## Ingredients

### Filling
- 1/2 cup (95g) light or dark brown sugar
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea or table salt
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- Freshly grated nutmeg to taste (approximately 1/4 teaspoon ground)
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves
- 4 1/4 to 4 1/2 pounds baking apples
- 3 tablespoons tapioca flour or starch

### Crust
- 1 recipe All Butter, Really Flaky Pie Dough
- 1 egg, lightly beaten (optional)
- Coarse or raw sugar for sprinkling (optional)

## Directions

1. Combine sugars, salt, and spices in a large bowl. Peel, halve, and core apples; cut into thin (scant 1/4-inch) slices and add to bowl. Toss to coat. Let sit at room temperature for 1 to 4 hours.
2. Prepare pie dough. Roll out first half to a 14-inch circle and transfer to a 9-inch pie plate. Trim overhang to 1 inch. Refrigerate.
3. Roll out second dough half to the same size, transfer to a parchment-lined sheet, and chill.
4. Preheat oven to 400°F.
5. Stir tapioca starch into apple filling. Pour into prepared crust; pack and heap apples as high as possible. Pour accumulated juices over apples.
6. Place second crust over filling or lattice the top. Trim to edge. Fold lower crust overhang over to form rim; crimp to seal. Brush with egg; sprinkle with sugar if desired. Cut vents if using a solid crust.
7. Bake on a parchment-lined sheet for 75 minutes, turning once or twice. If browning too quickly, cover with a foil dome. Pie is done when juices bubble through vents or internal temperature reaches 195°F.
8. Cool at room temperature for at least one hour before cutting. Filling fully thickens after chilling in the refrigerator for several hours.
