Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. A refined-sugar swap, tastes like caramel; keeps in the fridge up to two months. Use in oatmeal, smoothies, dressings, sauces.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb medjool dates
- 1.5 cups water, for soaking
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt

## Directions

1. Soak the dates in water for 3 hours to soften — this makes them easier to blend.
2. Strain and reserve the water, then add the dates to a blender with the vanilla and a pinch of salt.
3. Add water as needed to thin until you're able to blend — about a cup of the reserved water.
4. Store and enjoy.
