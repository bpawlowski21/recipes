## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons active dry yeast or instant yeast
- 7/8 to 1 1/8 cups (198g to 255g) lukewarm water
- 2 tablespoons (25g) olive oil
- 3 cups (360g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1 1/4 teaspoons (8g) table salt

## Directions

- If using active dry yeast, dissolve it with a pinch of sugar in 2 tablespoons lukewarm water and let sit 15 minutes until bubbly; skip this step for instant yeast.
- Combine yeast with remaining ingredients and knead by hand, mixer, or bread machine until soft and smooth (4-5 minutes in a stand mixer).
- For pizza within 24 hours, proceed to shaping. Otherwise, let dough rise covered in a greased bowl until very puffy (about 1 hour instant yeast / 90 minutes active dry).
- For pizza later, let dough rise covered 45 minutes at room temperature, then refrigerate 4-24 hours; remove 2-3 hours before serving.
- Choose a size/shape: two 14" rounds, two 12" rounds, one 13"x18" rectangle, one 9"x13" rectangle, or one 14" round.
- Divide dough in half for two pizzas or leave whole for one. Shape into a rough oval/circle without flattening; rest covered 15 minutes.
- Spray pan(s) lightly with vegetable oil and drizzle with olive oil. Press dough into pan(s), stretching toward the edges; rest 15 minutes and repeat stretching as needed.
- Let dough rise covered until noticeably puffy: 90 minutes if not refrigerated, or 2-2.5 hours if refrigerated. Preheat oven to 450°F near the end of rising.
- Bake on the lower oven rack until set on top and beginning to brown at the edges (8 minutes thin crust, 10-12 medium, 12-14 thick); reverse two pizzas midway through.
- To serve immediately: add toppings and return to the upper rack for 10-15 minutes until crust is browned and cheese melts.
- To serve later: cool the untopped crust completely, wrap, and store at room temperature up to 2 days; top and bake at 450°F when ready, adding a couple minutes.
- Transfer pizza to a rack to cool slightly before serving.
