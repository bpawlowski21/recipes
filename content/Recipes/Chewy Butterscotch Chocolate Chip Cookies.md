## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup unsalted butter, browned
- 3/4 cup brown sugar, packed
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 egg + 1 egg yolk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 3/4 cup all purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2/3 cup butterscotch chips
- 1/3 cup chocolate chips

## Directions

- Brown butter over medium heat while stirring constantly until golden and nutty-smelling; cool completely.
- Combine cooled brown butter with both sugars in a large bowl and beat together. Add egg, egg yolk, and vanilla; mix well.
- In a separate bowl, combine flour, salt, and baking soda. Mix half the dry ingredients into wet mixture, then gradually add remaining flour. Fold in chips without overmixing.
- Scoop dough into tablespoon-sized balls and refrigerate at least 30 minutes (or overnight).
- Preheat oven to 350°F with parchment-lined baking sheet. Space balls 2 inches apart and bake 11 minutes until edges set and centers remain gooey.
- Transfer to cooling rack and cool completely before serving.
