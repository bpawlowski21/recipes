## Ingredients

- 4 portions dried udon noodles or other noodles (does not have to be ramen) — originally 3 packages instant noodles
- 1.5 tablespoon soy sauce (triple sauce quantities per note below)
- 1.5 teaspoon dark soy sauce
- 1.5 teaspoon oyster sauce (optional for vegetarians)
- 0.75 teaspoon sugar
- 0.75 teaspoon sesame oil
- Fresh ground white pepper
- 3 tablespoons canola oil
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 0.38 cup sweet red bell peppers or Holland chilies, julienned (Wendy omits peppers, uses same amount of cremini mushrooms instead)
- 7.5 fresh shiitake mushrooms, sliced
- 1" ginger paste or grated ginger
- 1.5 medium carrot, julienned
- 2-3 small zucchini, sliced and halved
- 1 cup frozen (thawed) edamame beans
- 4 cups cabbage, shredded (originally 2.25 cups)
- 1.5 tablespoon Shaoxing wine, x3
- 1.5 cup snow peas, cut in half at an angle
- 1.5 cup fresh mung bean sprouts
- 5 scallions, split and cut into 2-inch pieces (originally listed as fewer)

## Directions

1. Open the instant ramen and discard the flavor packet (if using instant noodles; if using dried udon, cook per package instead). Bring 6 cups of water to a boil. Add the noodles and cook for about 45 seconds to a minute, using chopsticks or a fork to stir occasionally. You want to just rehydrate the ramen until it breaks up from its rectangular form — don't cook it fully in this step. Once rehydrated, immediately drain in a colander and rinse under cold water to stop cooking and remove excess starch.
2. Combine soy sauces, oyster sauce, sugar, sesame oil, and white pepper in a bowl and set aside. Heat your wok over high heat, and add oil and garlic. Immediately add the peppers (or cremini), mushrooms, carrots, zucchini, edamame, and cabbage, and stir-fry until the vegetable mix is sautéed down. Next, add the Shaoxing wine and stir-fry for another 15 seconds.
3. Add in the cooked noodles, pulling them apart if stuck together. Pour the soy sauce mixture evenly over the noodles. Stir-fry about 20 seconds using a scooping motion until the sauce is well distributed. Add in the snow peas, bean sprouts, and scallions. Mix well for another minute and serve.
4. Serve with a marinated ramen egg (recipe from tofbycooks.com uses an egg steamer, but Wendy boiled hers instead).
