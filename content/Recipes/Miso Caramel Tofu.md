Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post ("Tantalising Tofu S4E4"). "This tofu is sticky, sweet, salty and spicy... literally what more do you want?"

## Ingredients

**Tofu**
- 220g firm tofu, pressed and cubed (2–3cm)
- 1 tbsp cornstarch (or potato starch)
- 1½ tbsp neutral oil (sunflower, vegetable, etc.)

**Miso caramel sauce**
- 1½ tbsp white miso paste
- 1½ tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- ½ tsp grated ginger
- ½ clove garlic
- 2 tbsp water
- ½ tsp chilli flakes, chilli oil, or a few dashes hot sauce (adjust to taste)
- 1 tsp gochujang

**Garnishes:** sliced spring onions, toasted sesame seeds or togarashi, chilli crisp or extra chilli oil (optional)

## Directions

1. Cube the tofu, season with salt and pepper, and air fry 10–15 minutes, or until crisp.
2. In a pan, reduce heat to medium. Add a touch more oil if needed. Sauté the garlic and ginger 30 seconds.
3. Add the miso, sugar, syrup, soy sauce, vinegar, and water. Add the gochujang.
4. Stir constantly 3–5 minutes until smooth and bubbling.
5. Stir in the chili and optional sesame oil. Simmer another 1–2 minutes to thicken slightly.
6. Add the tofu and serve over rice.
