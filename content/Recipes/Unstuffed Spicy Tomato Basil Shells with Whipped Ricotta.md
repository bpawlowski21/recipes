## Ingredients

- 1 pound jumbo pasta shells
- 6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 pound ground spicy Italian chicken sausage
- 3 shallots, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano (or 1 tablespoon fresh)
- 2 teaspoons dried thyme leaves (or 1 tablespoon fresh)
- 1 pinch crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 (6 ounce) can tomato paste
- 1 (14 ounce) can San Marzano tomatoes, crushed
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 2 cups chopped greens (kale, spinach, or chard)
- 1 cup fresh basil, roughly chopped
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon (about 3 tablespoons juice)
- 1 cup whole milk ricotta cheese, whipped (optional)

## Directions

1. Boil salted water, cook pasta until al dente per package directions. Reserve 1½ cups cooking water before draining.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a skillet over high heat. Brown sausage 5-8 minutes. Reduce heat, add shallots, garlic, oregano, thyme, and pepper flakes. Cook until shallots caramelize (about 5 minutes). Stir in tomato paste, cook 2 minutes. Add crushed tomatoes and 1 cup pasta water; season with salt and pepper. Simmer 10-15 minutes until sauce thickens. Add greens until wilted.
3. Toss pasta shells and ½ cup basil into sauce. Thin with additional cooking water if needed.
4. Mix remaining ¼ cup oil with remaining ½ cup basil, lemon zest, lemon juice, and salt in a small bowl.
5. Spread ricotta in bowl bottoms. Top with shells and sauce. Drizzle basil-lemon mixture over top.
