## Ingredients

- 1⅔ cups (333 g) granulated sugar
- 1½ cups (188 g) all-purpose flour
- ⅔ cup (65 g) natural cocoa powder
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- 1¼ teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon table salt
- ½ cup (118 ml) avocado, canola, or vegetable oil
- 1 large egg + 1 large egg yolk (room temperature preferred)
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- ⅔ cup (157 ml) whole milk (room temperature preferred)
- 1 cup (236 ml) hot coffee or hot water
- 1 batch chocolate buttercream frosting (or preferred frosting)

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line two 12-count muffin tins with paper liners.
2. Whisk together sugar, flour, cocoa powder, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
3. Add oil, egg, egg yolk, and vanilla extract; stir until combined (mixture will be dry and crumbly).
4. Drizzle in milk while stirring until batter is uniform.
5. Gradually add hot coffee or water, stirring carefully until fully incorporated. Batter will be very thin — this is normal.
6. Fill cupcake liners between ½ and ⅔ full (approximately 3 tablespoons per liner). Do not overfill.
7. Bake one pan at 350°F for 17-19 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in center comes out with a few moist crumbs. Cool 10-15 minutes in pan, then transfer to cooling rack.
8. Repeat with second pan. Cool completely before frosting.
