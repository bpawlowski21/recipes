## Ingredients

**Roasted Butternut Squash:**
- 1 extra large butternut squash, cut into 1-inch chunks
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Caramelized Onions:**
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 large onions, halved and thinly sliced
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Flatbread:**
- 1 prepared pizza crust
- ½ batch caramelized onions (from above)
- ½ cup Fontina or Italian blend cheese, shredded
- ½ batch roasted butternut squash (from above)
- 2 teaspoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons sage leaves, cut into ribbons
- 2 tablespoons toasted walnuts, chopped (optional)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 400°F.
- Divide squash onto two rimmed baking sheets; drizzle each with 1 tablespoon olive oil, season with salt and pepper, toss to coat. Bake 45–50 minutes until tender. (Recipe makes a double batch of squash and onions — reserve half of each for another use.)
- Heat oil in a large skillet over low heat. Add onions and a pinch of salt; cook until completely caramelized, stirring occasionally then more frequently as they brown (30 minutes to over an hour). Season with pepper.
- Preheat oven per pizza crust package directions.
- Top crust with caramelized onions, cheese, and squash. Bake per package instructions until squash is heated through and cheese melts.
- While baking, heat oil in a small skillet over medium-high heat; sauté sage leaves, stirring constantly, about 3 minutes until crispy but still green.
- Top finished flatbread with crispy sage leaves and walnuts before serving.
