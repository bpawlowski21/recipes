## Ingredients

**Levain:**
- 40g bread flour
- 40g whole wheat flour
- 81g water
- 8g ripe sourdough starter

**Main Dough:**
- 766g bread flour
- 161g whole wheat flour
- 19g salt
- 685g water
- 169g ripe levain

## Directions

- Prepare levain (7:00 a.m.): Combine levain ingredients in a jar and allow to ferment at room temperature (72-75°F) for approximately 10 hours.
- Autolyse with levain (5:00 p.m.): Mix levain with bread flour, whole wheat flour, and 635g water in a bowl. Let rest covered for 20 minutes.
- Mix (5:20 p.m.): Add salt and remaining water to the dough. Blend thoroughly using wet hands, folding 5-10 times until smooth. Transfer to bulk fermentation container.
- Bulk fermentation (5:30-9:00 p.m.): Perform two sets of stretch and folds, each 30 minutes apart, then allow dough to rest.
- Divide and preshape (9:00 p.m.): Split dough in half and gently shape into rounds. Rest uncovered for 30 minutes.
- Shape (9:30 p.m.): Form each portion into a boule or batard and place in proofing baskets.
- Cold proof (overnight): Cover baskets and refrigerate overnight (9-12+ hours).
- Bake (next day): Preheat oven with Dutch oven to 450°F. Score dough, transfer to preheated vessel, and bake covered 20 minutes, then uncovered 30-35 minutes until internal temperature reaches 205-210°F. Cool 2 hours before slicing.
