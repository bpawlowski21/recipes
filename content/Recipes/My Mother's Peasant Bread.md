"My Mother's Peasant Bread: The Best Easiest Bread You Will Ever Make" — a sticky, no-knead dough baked in small oven-safe bowls (two 1-qt bowls, or ramekins for mini loaves).

Total time: 2 hours 27 minutes · Yield: 2 loaves

## Ingredients

- 4 cups (512 g) unbleached all-purpose or bread flour
- 2 teaspoons (10 g) kosher salt
- 2 cups (454 g) lukewarm water (made by mixing 1.5 cups cold water with 0.5 cup boiling water)
- 2 teaspoons (8 g) sugar
- 2 teaspoons (8 g) instant yeast (author recommends SAF Instant Yeast)
- room temperature butter, about 2 tablespoons

## Directions

1. **Mixing the dough:** In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, sugar, and instant yeast. Add the water. Mix until the flour is absorbed. (If using active dry yeast, proof it first.)
2. **Let it rise.** Cover bowl with a tea towel or plastic wrap and set aside in a warm spot to rise for at least an hour (up to two hours in a cool place). To create a warm spot: turn the oven on at any temperature (350°F or so) for one minute, then turn it off — don't let it get up to 300°F and hold there, just preheat for a total of 1 minute so it doesn't get above 100°F.
3. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Grease two 1-qt or 1.5-qt oven-safe bowls with about a tablespoon of butter each. Using two forks, punch down the dough, scraping it from the sides of the bowl and pulling it toward the center. Divide the dough into two equal portions with the two forks, working from the center out, then scoop each half into a prepared bowl. This part is messy — the dough is very wet. Small forks or flexible plastic dough scrapers make it easier.
4. Let the dough rise again for about 20 to 30 minutes on the countertop near the oven (or near a warm spot) until it has risen to just below or above the top of the bowls, depending on bowl size. (Don't use the warm-oven trick for the second rise — don't cover the bowls either, just set them in a warm spot; 20 minutes is usually enough.)
5. **Bake it.** Bake for 15 minutes. Reduce the heat to 375° and bake for 15 to 17 minutes longer. Remove from the oven and turn the loaves onto cooling racks — if the bowls were greased well, the loaves should fall right out. If the loaves look pale and soft when turned out, place them back in the oven (outside of their bowls) for about 5 minutes longer. Let cool for 10 minutes before cutting.
