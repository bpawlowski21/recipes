## Ingredients

- 3 small zucchinis, sliced into thin rings
- Enough olive oil to coat the pan
- 8 oz spaghetti or linguine
- ½ cup grated pecorino
- Pepper and salt
- Basil, for garnish

## Directions

1. In a large pan, heat olive oil over medium-high. Add zucchini slices in a single layer (work in batches). Fry on both sides until golden. Remove and drain on a paper-towel-lined tray. Repeat with remaining slices.
2. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water.
3. Drop the pasta into the sauté pan and add ½ cup of water. Crank in black pepper. Slowly stir in the cheese, continuing to stir and adding more pasta water, until the pasta is glossy and cooked perfectly. Garnish with basil.
