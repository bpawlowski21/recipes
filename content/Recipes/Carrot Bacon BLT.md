Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram comment ("the ultimate B.L.T made from plants").

## Ingredients

- Carrots (peeled into ribbons)
- 1 tbsp agave
- 2 tbsp coco aminos
- 2 tbsp liquid smoke
- 1 tbsp liquid aminos

## Directions

1. Make carrot ribbons with a peeler.
2. Mix the agave, coco aminos, liquid smoke, and liquid aminos into a marinade. Toss the carrot ribbons in it and let sit for 20 minutes.
3. Cook on a hot cast iron until you get crispy, caramelized faux bacon.

## Notes

- Assemble into a BLT with lettuce, tomato, and bread as usual — the post's focus is the carrot "bacon" itself.
