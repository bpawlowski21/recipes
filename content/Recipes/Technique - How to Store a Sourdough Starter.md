## Key Tips

### Short-term (1 day - 3 weeks), refrigerator method
- Create a stiffer mixture: 20g ripe starter, 100g flour, 80g water.
- Let it sit on the counter for about an hour before refrigerating.
- To revive: warm the jar, stir, then feed normally using your standard ratio. Do at least 2-3 regular feedings before using for baking.

### Long-term storage (months+)
- **Crumble method (preferred):** mix ripe starter into dry flour until pea-sized pieces form; air-dry for an hour, then seal in a jar away from light and moisture. To revive: add fresh flour and water to the crumbles without discarding any; let sit 12-24 hours at 78-80°F before feeding.
- **Shard method:** spread starter thinly on a lined baking sheet and dry completely; break into pieces and store airtight. To revive: soak shards in warm water for one hour, then add flour and water; keep warm during a 12-24 hour activation period.

### Critical tips
- "Moisture is the enemy" of dried starter storage.
- Maintain 78-80°F (25-26°C) during revival for faster activation.
- If struggling to revive: use whole grain rye flour for 50% of feedings and ensure warm conditions.
