"White Claumsopolitan." Prep Time: 4 minutes

**Equipment:** Cocktail shaker, jigger, strainer, martini glass or coupe glass

## Ingredients

- 1 oz vodka
- 0.5 oz triple sec
- 0.5 oz elderflower liqueur
- 2.5 oz white cranberry juice
- 0.5 oz lime juice
- 3 fresh cranberries, for garnish

## Directions

1. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice and add the vodka, triple sec, elderflower liqueur, white cranberry juice, and fresh lime juice.
2. Shake vigorously for 15-20 seconds until the mixture is well chilled.
3. Strain the cocktail into a chilled martini or coupe glass for an elegant presentation.
4. Garnish with three fresh cranberries, either floating in the drink or skewered on a cocktail pick for a festive touch.

## Notes

- Pro Tips: use white cranberry juice for a clear, snow-like appearance that contrasts beautifully with the cranberries. Chill your glassware in advance for a frosty and refreshing experience. Add a sugar rim to the glass for an extra festive and sweet finish.
