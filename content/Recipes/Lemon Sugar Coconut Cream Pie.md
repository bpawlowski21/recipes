## Ingredients

### Crust
- 1/2 cup shredded sweetened coconut
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon golden sugar (or granulated sugar)
- 6 tablespoons cold salted butter, cubed

### Coconut Filling
- 4 large egg yolks
- 1/4 cup cornstarch
- 2 (14 ounce) cans full fat coconut milk
- 1/2 cup plus 1 tablespoon golden sugar (or granulated sugar)
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons salted butter
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup shredded sweetened coconut
- 1 cup cold heavy cream
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Toasted coconut, for serving

## Directions

- Heat oven to 375°F. Grease an 8 or 9-inch tart pan or pie plate.
- Pulse coconut into fine crumbs in a food processor. Add flour, sugar, and butter; pulse until pea-sized balls form. Add cold water, 1 tablespoon at a time (up to 3 tablespoons total), until dough comes together. Press into pan and bake 12-14 minutes until golden.
- Whisk egg yolks and cornstarch together. Add sugar, coconut milk, and salt. Heat over medium-high, stirring continuously until thickened and pudding-like, about 8-10 minutes. Remove from heat; stir in vanilla, butter, and coconut. Pour into crust and refrigerate 3-4 hours.
- Before serving, whip heavy cream to soft peaks. Mix lemon zest with 1 tablespoon sugar. Top pie with whipped cream, lemon sugar, and toasted coconut. Slice and serve.
