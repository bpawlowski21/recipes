## Key Tips

- Feed the starter twice daily on a schedule aligned with your routine — morning at breakfast and evening before bed. "The key to coaxing out maximal fermentation with your starter is to be observant."
- Visual cues through the day: 2 hrs = slight activity, sweet aroma; 4 hrs = ~100% expansion with a doming top; 5-7 hrs = increased bubbling, aroma turns subtly sour; 8 hrs = ripe (flat top with holes, ready to use); 10+ hrs = overly fermented (collapsed center, strong vinegary smell).
- To adjust fermentation speed, control: water temperature, percentage of mature starter carried over, flour type (whole grains speed fermentation up), and ambient temperature.
- Example feeding ratio: 20g mature starter, 70g bread flour, 30g rye flour, 100g water at room temperature (75°F/23°C).
- Avoid letting the starter collapse excessively — extended fermentation creates unwanted sourness in the resulting bread.
