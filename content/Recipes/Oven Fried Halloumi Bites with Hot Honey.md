## Ingredients

- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 2 (8 ounce) blocks halloumi cheese, cubed
- 1 cup Panko bread crumbs
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- 3 tablespoons sesame seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- Black pepper (to taste)
- Extra virgin olive oil

**Hot Honey**
- 1/3 cup honey
- Red pepper flakes (to taste)

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Whisk eggs in a bowl, add halloumi, and toss to coat. In a separate bowl, combine panko, parmesan, sesame seeds, paprika, and black pepper.
3. Remove each halloumi piece from eggs and dredge through crumb mixture. Place on prepared baking sheet without crowding. Lightly drizzle with olive oil.
4. Bake for 10-12 minutes, flip, then bake another 10 minutes until golden and crisp.
5. Meanwhile, combine honey with a pinch of chili flakes and sea salt.
6. Serve halloumi warm, drizzled with hot honey and optional ketchup for dipping.
