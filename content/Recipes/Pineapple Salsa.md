Prep Time: 5 minutes + 1 hour chilling · Total Time: 1 hour 5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 3 cups (500 g) finely diced pineapple
- ⅔ cup (67 g) finely diced red onion
- ½ red pepper, finely chopped
- ½ cup orange pepper, finely chopped
- 1–3 tablespoons finely diced jalapeños (seeds removed)
- ⅔ cup (75 g) finely chopped ripe Roma tomatoes (seeds removed)
- ⅓ cup (16 g) finely chopped cilantro
- ¾ teaspoon finely ground sea salt, plus more to taste
- Freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
- ¼ teaspoon ground cumin (optional)
- 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- Salty tortilla chips, for serving

## Directions

1. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl and stir thoroughly.
2. Taste and adjust seasoning with additional salt, pepper, and lime juice as needed.
3. Refrigerate, covered, for at least one hour before serving to let the flavors meld.
4. Stir again before serving with tortilla chips.

## Notes

- Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
