## Ingredients

- 1 oz. Campari
- 1 oz. gin
- 1 oz. sweet vermouth
- Orange slice, for garnish

## Directions

1. In a tumbler filled with ice, stir together the Campari, gin, and vermouth.
2. Garnish with the orange slice.
