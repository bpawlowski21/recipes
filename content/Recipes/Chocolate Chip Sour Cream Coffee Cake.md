"There's no coffee in this cake — by 'coffee cake,' we mean something you'd serve with afternoon coffee — just lots and lots of sour cream. Yes I've made it with plain yogurt instead. I've even made it with low-fat yogurt. But mostly I make it as my grandmother and her sisters did, and am glad I did. Every time I make this, I'm convinced there's too much cinnamon-sugar and chocolate chips on each layer and each time it comes out of the oven and we try it, I am so glad I didn't touch the amounts. It's perfect." Source: a family recipe. First published November 21, 2006 on smittenkitchen.com.

Time: 1 hour

## Ingredients

**Cake**
- ½ cup unsalted butter (4 ounces or 115 grams) at room temperature
- 1½ cups (300 grams) granulated sugar
- 3 large eggs, separated
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 cups (16 ounces) sour cream
- 3 cups (390 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1½ teaspoons baking soda
- ¾ teaspoon fine sea or table salt

**Filling and Topping**
- 2 cups or 12 ounces semi- or bittersweet chocolate chips
- ½ cup (100 grams) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Butter a 9x13-inch baking pan and line the bottom with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, cream butter and 1½ cups granulated sugar. Beat in egg yolks and vanilla. Whisk flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt together into a separate bowl. Alternately mix in sour cream and then dry ingredients into butter mixture until both are used up and the batter is smooth and very thick. In a medium bowl with clean beaters, beat egg whites until stiff, then fold gently into batter.
3. In a small dish, combine the cinnamon and remaining ½ cup granulated sugar for filling and topping.
4. Spread half the cake batter in the bottom of prepared pan and spread smooth. Sprinkle with half of cinnamon-sugar mixture and 1 cup of chocolate chips. Dollop remaining cake batter over filling in spoonfuls. Use a rubber or offset spatula to gently spread it over the filling and smooth the top. Sprinkle batter with remaining cinnamon-sugar and remaining chocolate chips.
5. Bake for 40 to 50 minutes, rotating halfway through, until a tester inserted into the center of the cake comes out clean. Let cool in pan.

## Notes

- Do ahead: don't cover this when storing it at room temperature, so as not to lose the crackly top.
- 2018 update note from the author: didn't change the recipe, but now whips the egg whites first and sets them aside in a smaller bowl, uses the same mixing bowl for the rest of the batter, and folds the egg whites back in at the end as written. Sometimes beats the baking powder, baking soda, and salt right into the butter-sugar mixture at the start to save on dishes, adding the flour near the end.
