## Ingredients

### Donuts
- 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons fresh lime zest
- 2½ tablespoons coconut oil, melted
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- ½ cup coconut milk

### Key Lime Glaze
- 1 cup confectioners' sugar
- 1½ tablespoons key lime juice
- 1 teaspoon lime zest
- ⅛ teaspoon salt

### Optional Decoration
- ½ cup toasted shredded coconut
- 2 teaspoons lime zest

## Directions

- Heat oven to 350°F and grease a donut pan thoroughly.
- Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt; set aside.
- Mix sugar and lime zest with fingers until evenly distributed. Add melted coconut oil and whisk to combine. Beat in egg, then stir in coconut milk.
- Gently fold dry ingredients into wet mixture using a rubber spatula until just combined.
- Fill each donut mold two-thirds full. Bake 10-11 minutes until firm and lightly golden.
- Cool in pan for at least 10 minutes before transferring to cooling rack.
- Whisk glaze ingredients together, adjusting consistency as needed. Dip cooled donuts in glaze and let set 5 minutes. Top with toasted coconut and lime zest if desired.
