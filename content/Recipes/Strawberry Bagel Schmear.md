"Strawberry Bagel Schmear is a perfect combination of sweet strawberries and rich cream cheese. Top bagels, toast or even fresh bread with this schmear to dazzle your tastebuds."

Prep Time: 5 mins · Total Time: 5 mins

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces cream cheese, room temperature
- 4 tbsp strawberry jam, room temperature
- 4 tbsp powdered sugar

## Directions

1. Combine the cream cheese and jam in the bowl of a stand mixer. Using the whisk attachment, whip for 5 minutes.
2. Add the powdered sugar and whip for an additional 30 seconds. Store in the refrigerator for 3-4 days.
