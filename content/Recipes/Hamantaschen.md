## Ingredients

### Cookie Dough
- 12 tablespoons + 1 teaspoon (174g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1/3 cup + 1 tablespoon (78g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 1/4 cups + 3 tablespoons (291g) all-purpose flour

### Apricot Filling
- 1 1/4 cups (425g) apricot preserves
- 1/2 cup (39g) fresh breadcrumbs, packed

## Directions

- Beat butter and sugar until light and creamy using an electric mixer or by hand.
- Add egg, vanilla, and salt; mix until thoroughly combined.
- Gradually add flour, mixing until completely incorporated.
- Divide dough in half; press each into a circle or square, wrap in plastic, and refrigerate 45 minutes (or up to one week).
- Stir apricot preserves and breadcrumbs together for filling.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Remove one dough portion; roll to 1/8" thickness on floured surface.
- Cut circles using a 3" round cutter; place on prepared baking sheet.
- Place a rounded teaspoon of filling in center of each circle; brush edges with water.
- Fold three corners up to form a triangle, pinching corners and angling sides toward center.
- Bake 18-22 minutes until edges and bottoms turn golden brown.
- Cool cookies on pan; store wrapped at room temperature for several days or freeze for longer storage.
