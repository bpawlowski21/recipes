## Ingredients

### Cinnamon Rolls
- 1 cup (227ml) whole milk (full-fat)
- 4 tablespoons (57g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 3⅓ cups (400g) all-purpose flour, divided
- 1 (0.25 oz) package active dry yeast
- 3 tablespoons (42g) granulated sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1 large egg, room temperature

### Filling
- 1 cup (213g) light brown sugar
- ¼ cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon ground cloves
- ¾ cup (170g) unsalted butter, melted

### Glaze
- 3½ cups (392g) confectioners' sugar, sifted
- ½ cup (113ml) heavy cream
- ¼ teaspoon salt

## Directions

- Warm milk until bubbles form, add room-temperature butter, and let cool. Combine 2½ cups flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in stand mixer. Add egg and milk mixture, then gradually incorporate remaining flour. Beat for 5 minutes on medium speed until dough pulls away from bowl edges. Hand-knead for 1 minute.
- Form dough into a ball, place in greased bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and let rise in warm location until doubled (approximately 1 hour).
- Grease a 9×13-inch baking pan. Roll dough into a 12×18-inch rectangle. Combine melted butter with both sugars, cinnamon, and cloves; spread over dough leaving ¼-inch border. Roll tightly into an 18-inch log and slice into 12 rolls using dental floss.
- Arrange rolls in baking pan, cover, and refrigerate 8-12 hours (or allow 1 hour room-temperature rise).
- Remove from fridge and let rolls rise approximately 2 hours until nearly doubled. Preheat oven to 375°F and bake 23-25 minutes until golden.
- Combine all glaze ingredients and drizzle over warm rolls. Store leftovers wrapped in plastic wrap for up to 2 days.
