## Ingredients

- 2 cups cabbage, shredded
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup grapes, halved
- 8 oz farro, cooked and cooled
- ¼ red onion, diced
- 3 roasted baby bell peppers
- 1 avocado, chopped
- 1 can white beans, drained
- 2 persian cucumbers, chopped
- 1 handful fresh dill, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh mint, chopped
- 3 tbsp vegan yogurt
- 1 lemon, squeezed
- 1 tbsp date syrup
- Salt and pepper

## Directions

1. Cook the farro according to package directions, drain, rinse with cold water, and let drain well for the salad.
2. Roast the peppers by adding them whole to a sheet pan and cooking at 400°F for 15 minutes. Set aside to cool, then deseed and chop into small pieces.
3. Make the dressing by combining the yogurt, lemon, date syrup, a pinch of salt and pepper, and mixing well.
4. To a large mixing bowl, add the cabbage, tomatoes, grapes, onion, cucumbers, beans, farro, avocado, dill, mint, and peppers. Pour the dressing over the salad and mix well, making sure it's all coated.
5. Taste for salt and add if needed.
