## Ingredients

- 8 corn tortillas
- Vegetable oil, corn oil, or canola oil (for frying or brushing)
- Salt

## Directions

**Frying method:**
- Stack tortillas and cut into 6 wedges. Heat 1/4 to 1/2 inch oil to 350°F in a heavy skillet or deep fryer.
- Add a handful of tortilla pieces and fry until lightly golden and crisp, about 1-2 minutes. Avoid overcrowding the pan.
- Transfer to paper towels and season with salt while warm. Repeat with remaining tortillas.

**Baking method:**
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly brush tortillas with oil, stack, and cut into wedges.
- Arrange on a baking sheet in a single layer and sprinkle with salt.
- Bake 6 minutes, flip, then continue baking 6-10 minutes until crisp.
- Prep time: 5 minutes. Cook time: 10 minutes. Servings: 6.
