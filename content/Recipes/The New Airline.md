## Ingredients

- 2 (2-inch-thick) rounds of cucumber
- 2 thick slices of Granny Smith apple
- ¾-inch slice fresh ginger
- 1 oz. gin
- ⅓ oz. Cointreau
- ⅓ oz. elderflower liqueur
- ⅓ oz. fresh lime juice
- ¼ oz. apple juice
- 1 thin slice cucumber, for garnish

## Directions

1. Muddle the cucumber, apple, and ginger together in the bottom of a cocktail shaker.
2. Add the gin, Cointreau, elderflower liqueur, lime juice, apple juice, and plenty of ice.
3. Shake vigorously and strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
4. Garnish with the cucumber slice.
