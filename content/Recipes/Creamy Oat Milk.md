Prep Time: 5 minutes · Cook Time: 6 minutes · Total Time: 11 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup organic rolled oats
- ¼ cup cashews
- 4 cups water

## Directions

1. Add the oats, cashews, and water to a blender and blend for 30 seconds.
2. Pour into a nut milk bag or cheesecloth set over a bowl.
3. Gently twist the bag, and once most of the liquid has drained, squeeze out the remaining milk.
4. Pour into a Mason jar with a lid and store in the fridge for up to 4 days.

## Notes

- Nutrition (per serving): 121 kcal, 16g carbohydrates, 4g protein, 5g fat, 2g fiber, 1g sugar.
