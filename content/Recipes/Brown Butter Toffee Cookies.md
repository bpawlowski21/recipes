"My soft and chewy brown butter toffee cookies are packed with toffee pieces and flavored with vanilla, brown butter, and sprinkling of sea salt. Never browned butter before? I'll show you how!" A different recipe from the other saved [[Brown Butter Toffee Chocolate Chip Cookies]] (Sofi | Broma Bakery).

Prep Time: 30 minutes · Cook Time: 10 minutes · Total Time: 40 minutes · Calories: 157

## Ingredients

- 1 cup unsalted butter
- 200 g (1 cup) brown sugar (light or dark is fine, a blend of the two works well)
- 135 g (⅔ cup) white sugar
- 2 eggs, room temperature preferred
- 1.5 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 350 g (2¾ cups) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch (cornflour in UK)
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 0.75 teaspoon salt
- 170 g (1 cup) milk chocolate toffee pieces (like Heath Milk Chocolate Toffee Pieces)
- Sea salt, for sprinkling on top of the cookies

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C) and line cookie sheets with parchment paper. Set aside.
2. Cut the butter into tablespoon-sized pieces and place in a medium-sized saucepan. Melt over medium-low heat.
3. **To Brown the Butter:** once the butter has melted, increase heat to just above medium. Swirl and scrape the sides of the pan frequently with a wooden spoon or heatproof spatula — the butter will bubble and pop. Once the bubbling/popping slows, the butter will begin to turn brown.
4. Watch the butter very carefully at this point, and once it begins to brown and you smell the nutty aroma of browned butter, remove from heat quickly and pour into a large, heatproof bowl.
5. Allow butter to cool for 25-30 minutes before proceeding with the recipe.
6. **Cookie Dough:** add the sugars into the cooled browned butter, stir well.
7. Stir in the eggs, one at a time, stirring well after each addition.
8. Add the vanilla extract and stir.
9. In a separate, medium-sized bowl, whisk together the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
10. Gradually add the flour mixture to the butter mixture, stirring until ingredients are nearly completely combined.
11. Add the milk chocolate toffee bits and stir until ingredients are well-incorporated.
12. Drop by heaping, rounded 1½ Tbsp (45g) scoops onto prepared cookie sheets, placing at least 2" apart.
13. Bake on 350°F (177°C) for 9-10 minutes or until edges are just beginning to turn golden brown (cookies should still be quite soft when removed from the oven, and may even appear slightly underbaked in the center).
14. Sprinkle lightly with sea salt.
15. Allow cookies to cool on baking pan for about 3 minutes and then use a spatula to gently scoop beneath them and carefully wiggle them free from the parchment paper, as the toffee tends to stick.
16. Allow to cool several more minutes and then transfer to cooling rack to cool completely.

## Notes

- Storing: store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 4 days.
