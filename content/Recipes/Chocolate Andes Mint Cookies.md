Tawnie Graham of Kroll's Korner. "Calling all chocolate and mint lovers! These Chocolate Andes Mint Cookies taste like a warm, gooey brownie bursting with Andes mint flavor. They're incredibly soft, chewy, and fudgy, aka the perfect festive treat for the holidays."

Prep Time: 10 minutes · Cook Time: 10 minutes · Chill Time: 10 minutes · Total Time: 30 minutes · Calories: 187

## Ingredients

- 218¾ g all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled
- 21½ g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. cornstarch
- ½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. espresso powder, optional but recommended
- 8 Tbsp. unsalted butter
- 113⅔ g Andes Mints (about 24 mints), plus extra for placing on top of cookies
- 113⅔ g chocolate chips (milk, semi-sweet or dark)
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 165 g brown sugar, packed (light or dark)
- 50 g granulated white sugar
- Flaky sea salt, for garnish

## Directions

1. **Whisk dry ingredients:** in a medium-sized bowl, combine the flour, cocoa powder, cornstarch, baking powder, salt, and espresso powder. Whisk and set aside.
2. **Melt chocolate and butter:** in a small saucepan over medium-low heat, melt the chocolate chips, Andes mints, and butter. Stir just until ingredients are melted. Be careful to watch so the chocolate doesn't burn. Remove from heat, stir in vanilla extract, and let cool.
3. **Whisk eggs and sugars:** using a stand-mixer fitted with the whisk attachment, whisk the eggs and sugars for 3-4 minutes or until thick and pale in color.
4. **Add chocolate mixture:** using a rubber spatula, stir in the cooled chocolate mixture until well combined.
5. **Combine wet and dry ingredients:** add in the flour mixture and fold until combined. Be careful to avoid over-mixing. The dough will look shiny.
6. **Chill dough:** chill cookie dough in the fridge for at least 10 minutes so the batter can thicken and look more like cookie dough (this also helps the cookies bake up thicker & helps prevent spreading). Preheat oven to 350°F while dough chills.
7. **Scoop:** using a 2 Tbsp. cookie scoop, scoop the batter and place on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
8. **Bake:** bake for 8-10 minutes, or until edges are set and centers still look slightly soft. Immediately after baking, cookie-scoot into perfect circles using a round cutter.
9. **Let cool and top with Andes mint:** allow to cool on cookie sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely. Once cool, press an Andes mint in the center and sprinkle with flaky sea salt. Enjoy!

## Notes

- If you press the Andes mint on cookies while still warm, it will melt — if that's what you want, go for it! The author recommends allowing the cookies to cool and pressing the mint into the gooey center.
- Be sure to chill the batter in the fridge for at least 10 minutes to prevent spreading.
- Recipe inspired by Gimme Some Oven Chocolate Brownie Cookies.
- This recipe was updated in December 2025 to make 20 (2 Tbsp.) cookies. If you prefer the original version of making 10 larger cookies, you can still do that.
