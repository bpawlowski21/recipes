## Ingredients

- 8 avocados
- 1 finely chopped jalapeño pepper
- 1 medium red onion, chopped
- Juice of 2 limes
- 1 large tomato, seeded and finely chopped
- A fistful of cilantro, finely chopped (save a sprig for garnish)
- Tabasco sauce, to taste
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Directions

1. Mash up the avocados (a potato masher keeps it a little chunky).
2. Add the rest of the ingredients, and enjoy.
