## Ingredients

**Cake**
- 1 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar, divided
- 4 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 3 cups all purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- ¾ teaspoon baking soda
- 1⅓ cups sour cream or yogurt
- 1 tablespoon cinnamon

**Glaze**
- ½ cup cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup powdered sugar
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- 5 tablespoons milk
- Pinch of salt

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 9x13-inch sheet pan with parchment paper and grease sides.
2. Beat softened butter, granulated sugar, and ⅓ cup brown sugar until light and fluffy. Add eggs and vanilla extract.
3. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a separate bowl. Alternate folding flour mixture and sour cream into wet ingredients.
4. Mix remaining ⅔ cup brown sugar with cinnamon; set aside.
5. Pour half the batter into the pan, sprinkle with ⅓ cup cinnamon-sugar mixture. Add remaining batter, sprinkle remaining cinnamon-sugar on top. Create swirls with a knife. Bake 45 minutes until golden and a knife inserted in center comes out clean. Cool before removing.
6. For the glaze: beat softened cream cheese, powdered sugar, vanilla, salt, and milk until smooth.
7. Pour glaze over cooled cake and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar. Serve with coffee or milk.
