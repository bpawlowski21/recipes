## Ingredients

- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- Pinch of orange zest
- 3/4 cup high-quality extra virgin olive oil
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 cup + 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup almond flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 cups berries, fresh or frozen

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F and line an 8 or 9-inch round cake pan with parchment paper.
- Beat together sugar, orange zest, and eggs until pale yellow (about 1 minute).
- With mixer on medium-low speed, slowly stream in the olive oil to emulsify. Mix in vanilla extract.
- Add dry ingredients (flour, almond flour, baking powder, salt) and stir until just combined. Gently fold in the berries.
- Pour batter into prepared pan and bake 45-55 minutes until lightly golden brown and a knife inserted in the center comes out mostly clean. Dust with powdered sugar before serving.
