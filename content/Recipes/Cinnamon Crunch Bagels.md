## Ingredients

**Bagel Dough:**
- 2 cups warm water
- 2 packets instant yeast
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 3½ cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups white whole wheat flour or pastry flour
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt

**Cinnamon Crunch Layer:**
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 4 tablespoons salted butter, melted

## Directions

- Combine water, yeast, honey, flours, cinnamon, and salt in a stand mixer with dough hook. Knead until dough comes together (1-2 minutes). Cover and let rise in a warm place for 1-2 hours.
- Preheat oven to 425°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Punch down dough. Divide into 10-12 balls. Poke a hole through each ball's center and stretch until the hole is about 2 inches wide. Place on prepared baking sheets. Cover lightly with plastic wrap and let rise 15 minutes.
- Mix brown sugar, granulated sugar, and cinnamon together. Stir in 2 tablespoons melted butter.
- Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Lower 2-3 bagels into water. Boil 3 minutes, flip, then boil 1 additional minute. Remove with slotted spoon and transfer to parchment-lined baking sheet. Repeat for all bagels.
- Brush each bagel with remaining 2 tablespoons butter and spoon brown sugar mixture over tops. Bake 25-30 minutes until golden brown, rotating pans halfway through. Cool before serving.
