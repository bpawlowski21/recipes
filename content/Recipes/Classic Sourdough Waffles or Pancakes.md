## Ingredients

**Overnight Sponge:**
- 1 cup (227g) sourdough starter, unfed/discard
- 2 cups (454g) buttermilk
- 2 cups (240g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 2 tablespoons (28g) granulated sugar

**Batter:**
- All of the overnight sponge
- 2 large eggs
- 1/4 cup (50g) vegetable oil or 4 tablespoons (57g) butter, melted
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 3/4 teaspoon table salt

## Directions

- **Prepare overnight sponge:** mix unfed starter, buttermilk, flour, and sugar in a large bowl.
- **Let rest:** cover and let sit at cool room temperature (65-70°F) for ~12 hours overnight; mixture will expand and become bubbly.
- **Combine wet ingredients:** beat eggs and oil/butter together, then add to the sponge, stirring gently.
- **Add leavening:** stir in baking soda and salt; batter will expand and bubble slightly.
- **Cook:** pour batter by 1/4-cupful onto a preheated, lightly greased griddle (or waffle iron). Cook until bubbles form and pop on top, then flip and cook until underside browns.
- **Serve** immediately with preferred toppings, or keep warm in oven until ready.
- **Storage:** refrigerate leftovers 1-2 days or freeze for longer storage.
