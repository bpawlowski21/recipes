Prep Time: 5 minutes · Cook Time: 15 minutes · Total Time: 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (16 oz) French green beans (haricot verts), trimmed
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- ¼ heaping cup raw sliced almonds
- 2 medium shallots, finely diced
- 2 medium garlic cloves, finely minced
- Zest of one small lemon
- 2 teaspoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Directions

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and season it heavily with kosher salt — this seasons the beans and helps them keep their bright green color.
2. Blanch the green beans 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until crisp-tender and slightly squeaky between teeth. Don't overcook, or they'll break down during pan-searing.
3. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium-low heat until lightly bubbling. Add the sliced almonds and sauté 2–3 minutes, stirring frequently, until golden brown. Reduce heat to low and add the shallots and garlic, sautéing 1–2 minutes until fragrant and lightly caramelized.
4. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the blanched beans directly to the skillet. Toss gently until combined and the beans are tender. Add the lemon zest and juice, toss again, and season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately.
