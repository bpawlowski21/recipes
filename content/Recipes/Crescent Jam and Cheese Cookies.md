## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (1 cup, 8 ounces or 227 grams) unsalted butter, softened
- 7.5 ounces farmer cheese (or enough pot cheese to fill 1 cup after sieving)
- 2 tablespoons (1 ounce or 30 grams) sour cream
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 cups (8 3/4 ounces or 250 grams) all-purpose flour, plus additional for rolling
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Jam or preserves (raspberry suggested)
- Milk, for brushing
- Powdered sugar, for dusting

## Directions

1. Cream butter in a large bowl with an electric mixer until smooth. Force cheese through a sieve directly onto the creamed butter and stir to combine. Add sour cream and vanilla, mixing well.
2. Whisk or sift together flour and salt in a separate bowl. Gradually blend into the cheese mixture. Wrap dough in plastic and chill at least 3 hours.
3. Preheat oven to 400°F. Roll one-fourth of the dough very thinly on a lightly floured surface, keeping the remaining dough chilled. Cut into 3-inch squares and place about 1/2 teaspoon jam in the center of each.
4. Fold dough diagonally in half, pressing firmly to seal edges around the jam. Roll the triangle into a crescent shape, starting at the wide end.
5. Arrange crescents on a baking sheet about 1 inch apart. Brush lightly with milk. Bake 15 to 20 minutes until golden.
6. Transfer to a wire rack and dust with powdered sugar. Repeat with remaining dough.
