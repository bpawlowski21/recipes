## Ingredients

- 1 large or 2 small yellow onions
- 3 pounds (about 5 large) baking potatoes (Russet or Idaho), peeled
- 1/3 cup potato starch
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- Lots of freshly ground black pepper
- 3 large eggs
- 5 tablespoons olive oil, schmaltz, or another oil of choice

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F.
2. Chop onions finely and coarsely grate potatoes (by hand or using a food processor with grating blade).
3. Place onions and potatoes in a large bowl. Sprinkle salt, pepper, and starch evenly over mixture and toss together with two forks or your hands, coating strands thoroughly.
4. Break eggs on top and work them into the strands until evenly coated.
5. Heat 1/4 cup oil or fat in a 10-inch cast-iron skillet over high heat until very hot.
6. Pour potato mixture into pan carefully and spread evenly. Tousle top strands for texture. Drizzle with remaining tablespoon of oil.
7. Bake for 75 to 80 minutes until browned on top and tender in the middle. Cover with foil if top browns too quickly before center is baked.
8. Serve in squares from skillet or unmolded onto a platter.
