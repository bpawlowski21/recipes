Fully handwritten recipe (Wendy's), written below a printed Pumpkin Pie Smoothie recipe on the same page.

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup canned pumpkin
- 1/2 cup almond milk
- 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
- 2 tablespoons chia seeds
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 Tablespoon maple syrup

## Directions

1. Stir it up.
2. Let it sit overnight.
3. Enjoy for breakfast!
