## Ingredients

- 1 cup to 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons (227g to 255g) water, lukewarm
- 1/4 cup (50g) vegetable oil
- 1/4 cup (85g) honey, molasses, or maple syrup
- 3 1/2 cups (397g) King Arthur Golden Wheat Flour
- 2 1/2 teaspoons instant yeast, or 1 packet active dry yeast dissolved in 2 tablespoons of the water
- 1/4 cup (28g) King Arthur Baker's Special Dry Milk or nonfat dry milk
- 1 1/4 teaspoons (8g) table salt

## Directions

- Weigh flour or measure by gently spooning into a cup, then sweep off excess.
- Combine all ingredients in a large bowl or stand mixer, stirring until dough leaves bowl sides. Rest 20-30 minutes to allow flour absorption.
- Knead by hand for 6-8 minutes on a greased surface, or use stand mixer dough hook at low speed for 5-7 minutes until soft and smooth. Adjust consistency as needed.
- Transfer to greased bowl, cover, and let rise 1-2 hours until puffy.
- Deflate gently, shape into an 8" log, and place in greased 8½" x 4½" loaf pan. Cover loosely.
- Rise 1-2 hours until center crowns about 1" above pan rim. Preheat oven to 350°F toward end of rising.
- Bake 35-40 minutes, tenting with foil after 20 minutes. Internal temperature should reach 190°F.
- Turn out onto rack to cool. Optionally rub warm top with butter for soft crust.
- Store wrapped at room temperature several days, or freeze longer.
