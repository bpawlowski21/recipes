## Ingredients

**Crust**
- 1 1/4 cups (165 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 cup (8 tablespoons/113 grams) unsalted butter, cold and cubed
- 1/4 cup (60 grams) sour cream, plain yogurt, or ricotta
- 2 teaspoons (10 ml) fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) cold water

**Filling**
- 1 large or 2 small zucchinis (about 1 pound/455 grams), sliced into 1/4-inch rounds
- 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1 medium garlic clove, minced
- 1/2 cup (120 grams) ricotta cheese
- 1/2 cup (50 grams) grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup (50 grams) shredded mozzarella

**To Finish**
- 1 egg yolk beaten with 1 teaspoon water
- 1 tablespoon basil leaves, thinly sliced

## Directions

1. **Make crust:** Whisk together flour and salt. Sprinkle butter bits over and use a pastry blender to cut in until mixture resembles coarse meal with the largest pieces the size of tiny peas. Whisk together sour cream, lemon juice, and water; add to butter mixture. Mix with fingertips or a wooden spoon until large lumps form. Gather into a slightly flattened ball, wrap, and chill 1 to 2 hours.
2. **Make filling:** Spread zucchini slices on paper towels, sprinkle with 1 teaspoon salt, and drain 30 minutes. Blot dry. Whisk together olive oil and garlic. In a separate bowl, combine ricotta, Parmesan, mozzarella, and 1 teaspoon of the garlic oil; season with salt and pepper.
3. **Assemble:** Heat oven to 400°F. Roll dough into a 12-inch round on a floured surface. Transfer to an ungreased or parchment-lined baking sheet. Spread ricotta mixture over the bottom, leaving a 2-inch border. Shingle zucchini slices in overlapping concentric circles from outer edge toward center. Drizzle with remaining garlic oil and season zucchini with black pepper. Fold border over filling, pleating to fit; center remains open. Brush crust with egg yolk glaze.
4. **Bake:** until cheese puffs, zucchini wilts slightly, and the galette turns golden brown, 30 to 40 minutes. Sprinkle with basil, let stand 5 minutes, then cut into wedges and serve hot, warm, or at room temperature.
