## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup (170g) sourdough starter, unfed/discard
- 2 tablespoons (28g) water
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 2 scallions, white parts chopped finely and green parts sliced about 1/4" wide
- Large handful of fresh herbs, coarsely chopped (cilantro, parsley, dill, tarragon, chives, etc.)
- Vegetable oil, for frying

## Directions

- Combine all ingredients in a medium bowl, reserving the sliced green scallion parts. Stir until thoroughly combined.
- Heat a heavy-bottomed frying pan (cast iron preferred) over medium heat; add enough vegetable oil to coat the bottom 1/8" deep. Heat until ripples appear.
- Spoon a heaping 2 tablespoons of batter into the hot pan. Sprinkle reserved green scallions on top. Cook ~1 minute until golden brown on the bottom.
- Flip and cook 30-60 seconds on the second side until golden brown.
- Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate. Repeat with remaining batter, adding more oil as needed.
- Serve immediately, or reheat in a pan or toaster oven. Store leftovers wrapped in the refrigerator for one day, or freeze for longer storage.
