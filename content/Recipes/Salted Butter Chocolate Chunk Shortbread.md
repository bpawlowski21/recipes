## Ingredients

- 1 cup (8 ounces / 225 g) salted butter, cold, cut into small pieces
- 1/3 cup (65 g) granulated sugar
- 1/3 cup (70 g) light brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon (5 ml) vanilla extract
- 2 cups (260 g) all-purpose flour
- 5.5 to 6 ounces (155 to 170 g) semi- or bittersweet dark chocolate, chopped into chunks
- 1 large egg or 1 large egg white
- Turbinado sugar, for rolling
- Flaky sea salt, for sprinkling

## Directions

- Beat butter, both sugars, and vanilla with a stand mixer until light and fluffy, scraping the bowl as needed until no buttery chunks remain. Add flour and mix until combined and all flour is moistened. Fold in chocolate chunks until just incorporated.
- Transfer dough to parchment paper and form into a log approximately 2.25 to 2.5 inches in diameter. Chill until totally firm, about 2 hours.
- Heat oven to 350°F. Lightly beat the egg and brush over the log's sides. Roll the log in coarse sugar to coat thoroughly.
- Using a sharp serrated knife, cut logs into 1/2-inch thick rounds, gently sawing to prevent breakage. Arrange on prepared sheets one inch apart and sprinkle each with sea salt flakes. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until edges just begin turning golden brown.
- Let cool slightly before transferring to wire racks.
