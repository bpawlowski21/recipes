Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram comment screenshot. "This cookie dough bark is vegan, requires no baking, & takes like 5 mins to make!"

## Ingredients

- ½ cup of your favorite protein powder (post used a salted caramel flavor)
- ½ cup oat flour (can sub almond flour, but use closer to ¾ cup)
- ¼ cup melted coconut oil
- ¼ cup maple syrup
- ½ tsp vanilla + pinch of sea salt
- Handful of cacao nibs (can sub chocolate chips)
- 1 cup chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Mix together the cookie dough ingredients.
2. Spread onto a lined baking sheet.
3. Melt the chocolate. Pour over top. Sprinkle with sea salt.
4. Place in the freezer for 2–4 hours until completely firm, before breaking into pieces. Store in an airtight container in the freezer.
