## Ingredients

### Shortbread
- 2 sticks (16 tablespoons) salted butter, at room temperature
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/3 cup dark brown sugar
- 2 cups all-purpose flour

### Caramel
- 2 (14 ounce) cans sweetened condensed milk
- 1 stick (8 tablespoons) salted butter
- 1/4 cup real maple syrup
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract

### Chocolate Coating
- 24 ounces semi-sweet or milk chocolate, chopped
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- Flaky sea salt

## Directions

- Brown 1 stick butter in a saucepan over medium heat for 3-4 minutes. Transfer to a bowl and chill in freezer for 10-15 minutes.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 9x13 inch baking dish with parchment paper.
- Beat remaining butter, cooled browned butter, brown sugar, and vanilla until creamy. Mix in flour until combined. Press dough into prepared dish. Bake 20-22 minutes until lightly golden.
- Combine condensed milk, butter, and maple syrup in a pot. Boil over medium heat while whisking constantly for 15-20 minutes until golden brown and thickened, stirring often. Whisk in vanilla. Pour over shortbread immediately and spread evenly. Refrigerate at least 1 hour or freeze 30 minutes.
- Melt chocolate and coconut oil in microwave until smooth.
- Cut into 24 rectangles or 48 squares. Freeze 10 minutes. Dip each bar in chocolate. Freeze until set, about 10 minutes. Sprinkle with sea salt. Store in cool, dry place or refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
