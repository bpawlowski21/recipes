## Ingredients

- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- ¼ cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ cup (113 g) very cold unsalted butter
- ½ cup (120 ml) heavy cream
- ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
- ½ cup add-ins (optional): frozen berries, chocolate chips, nuts
- Additional heavy cream, for brushing
- Additional sugar, for sprinkling

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C); line baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in food processor; pulse to blend.
- Cut butter into tablespoon pieces, scatter over flour mixture, and pulse until no visible butter remains.
- Mix cream and vanilla extract; pour over flour mixture and pulse until dough clumps together.
- Transfer dough to floured surface. Add optional mix-ins and gently fold dough over itself, then rotate 90 degrees and repeat about 5 times total.
- Form dough into a smooth disk approximately 1 inch thick and 6 inches wide.
- Cut disk into 8 wedges using straight downward knife presses (avoid sawing).
- Transfer wedges to prepared baking sheet, spacing about 2 inches apart.
- Brush lightly with cream and sprinkle with sugar.
- Bake 14-16 minutes until edges turn golden brown. Cool on baking sheet before serving.
