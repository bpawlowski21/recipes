## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (8 ounces) unsalted butter, melted
- 1 cup light brown sugar, packed
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 large egg plus 2 egg yolks, at room temperature
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup cornstarch
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 3/4 cup toffee bits

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly spray a 9-inch pie plate with nonstick baking spray.
2. In a large bowl, combine melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar; mix well. Beat in the egg, egg yolks, and vanilla extract.
3. Add flour, cornstarch, and salt; mix until just combined without overmixing.
4. Fold in chocolate chips and toffee bits.
5. Spread mixture into prepared pie plate and bake for 35-40 minutes until the top is golden brown, edges are set, and the center shows slight jiggle.
6. Cool on a wire rack for at least one hour before slicing and serving.
