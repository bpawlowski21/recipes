## Ingredients

- 1 package crumbled high-protein tofu
- 1 diced bell pepper
- 2 cups diced shiitake mushrooms
- Garnish: chopped roasted peanuts, scallions, sesame seeds
- Bibb, romaine, or iceberg lettuce, for wraps

**Secret sauce**
- ½ cup hoisin sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 3 tbsp soy sauce (sub coconut aminos)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sriracha
- 1 tsp ginger
- ½ tsp garlic powder

## Directions

1. Whisk the sauce ingredients in a bowl.
2. Dice the veggies and crumble the tofu.
3. Sauté the veggies and tofu in a nonstick pan until the water cooks out, about 10 minutes.
4. Add the hoisin sauce and cook on medium-low, simmering 10–15 minutes until the sauce thickens.
5. Add the mixture to lettuce cups and top with garnishes.
6. Enjoy.
