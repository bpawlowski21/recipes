"This 10-ingredient Lentil Bolognese is what comfort food dreams are made of. The meaty, umami, and flavor-rich sauce simmers together wholesome, pantry-staple ingredients in one pot in less than 1 hour. An easy, yet gourmet Italian-inspired sauce for pasta night!"

Prep Time: 10 mins · Cook Time: 45 mins · Total Time: 55 mins · Diet: Vegan

## Ingredients

- 1½ tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme (or use more oregano)
- 1½ teaspoons kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 (5.3-ounce/150g) tube of tomato paste (tube preferred over canned)
- ½ cup (120mL) dry red wine (optional)
- 3 cups (720mL) vegetable broth
- 1 cup (185g) red lentils, soaked
- ¼ cup (32g) walnuts (or pecans), crushed finely
- 1 (14.5-ounce/410g) can crushed tomatoes or whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand
- 12-16 ounces (340-454g) long, wide pasta (tagliatelle, pappardelle, fettuccine, or tube pasta such as rigatoni/penne rigate, or gnocchi)
- 1 tablespoon high-quality balsamic vinegar
- Flat-leaf Italian parsley or fresh basil, chopped or slivered (optional)

## Directions

1. Soak the lentils in water for 30-60 minutes. Meanwhile, prep the other ingredients (chop onions, garlic, walnuts).
2. Heat a 12-inch deep sauté pan or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the olive oil, then the onions with a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally and deglazing with splashes of water as needed, until golden brown, 9-10 minutes.
3. Add the garlic, thyme, oregano, salt, and pepper. Stir frequently and cook 60-90 seconds.
4. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 2-3 minutes to caramelize, stirring frequently, until darker red.
5. Optional: if using red wine, pour it in and deglaze, scraping up browned bits. Cook 1-2 minutes until the alcohol smell burns off and the mixture is jammy.
6. Pour in the broth to deglaze the pan, stirring in browned bits. Add the lentils and walnuts, stir to incorporate. Bring to a boil, then reduce to medium-low and simmer rapidly for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer another 15-20 minutes, or until the lentils are tender but still al dente, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking/burning. Add water as needed if it gets too thick.
8. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta until just al dente. Reserve a ladle of pasta water. Drain but don't rinse.
9. Taste the bolognese for seasoning, adding more salt and pepper. Finish with the balsamic vinegar (or sugar, see notes), and stir to combine.
10. Add the hot cooked pasta to the bolognese and toss until well coated, adding pasta water as needed. Garnish with chopped parsley or basil.

## Notes

- If not serving all of the bolognese, transfer only the amount you want to eat to the pasta pot; store the rest of the sauce separately in the fridge.
- Best with tomato paste from a tube, not canned tomato paste.
- Dry red wines: malbec, chianti, tempranillo, sangiovese, merlot, cabernet sauvignon, pinot noir, syrah.
- For the best tomato flavor, use whole peeled tomatoes crushed by hand; hard to find in 14.5-ounce cans, so half of a 28-ounce can works.
- If you don't have high-quality balsamic vinegar, omit or substitute 1-2 teaspoons of sugar (brown, coconut, or cane; start with 1 teaspoon and add more to taste).
