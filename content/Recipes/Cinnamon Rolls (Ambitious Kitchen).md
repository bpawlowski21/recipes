## Ingredients

**Dough**
- ¾ cup (180g) milk (whole or 2%)
- 2¼ teaspoons quick-rise or active dry yeast
- ¼ cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 4 tablespoons (57g) salted butter, melted and cooled
- 1 large egg plus 1 large egg yolk, room temperature
- 3 cups (360g) bread flour, plus more as needed
- ¾ teaspoon kosher salt
- Extra-virgin olive oil for greasing

**Filling**
- 4 tablespoons (57g) salted butter, softened
- ⅔ cup (142g) packed dark brown sugar
- 1½ tablespoons ground cinnamon

**Cream cheese frosting**
- 4 oz (112g) cream cheese, softened
- ¾ cup (85g) powdered sugar
- 3 tablespoons salted butter, softened
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

## Directions

1. Warm milk to 115°F. In stand mixer bowl with paddle attachment, add milk and sprinkle yeast on top. Add sugar, melted butter, egg, and egg yolk. Mix on medium speed for 30 seconds.
2. Switch to dough hook. Mix in flour and salt on medium speed for 8-10 minutes until soft and slightly sticky. Add flour 1 tablespoon at a time if too sticky.
3. Transfer dough to greased bowl. Cover with plastic wrap and warm towel. Let rise until doubled, 1-1½ hours.
4. Dust surface with flour. Roll dough into a 10x14-inch rectangle.
5. Spread softened butter over dough, leaving a ¼-inch margin at the far short end. Mix brown sugar and cinnamon; sprinkle over butter and rub in gently.
6. Roll tightly from the short side without a margin. Seal edge by pinching. Cut ½ inch off ends. Cut into nine 1-inch slices using dental floss (not mint-flavored!) or a serrated knife.
7. Line a 9x9-inch pan with parchment paper. Place rolls in pan. Cover with plastic wrap and towel. Let rise 45 minutes to 1 hour until doubled — or, for the second rise, place the covered pan in the oven (unheated, no temperature set) with the oven light on for one hour.
8. Preheat oven to 350°F. Bake 20-25 minutes until golden brown at edges.
9. Cool for 10 minutes. Beat frosting ingredients (cream cheese, powdered sugar, butter, vanilla) on low then medium-high speed until smooth and fluffy. Spread over warm rolls.
