## Notes (as captured)

- Freezing: you can freeze unbaked dough balls for up to 3 months. Allow the cookie dough balls to come to room temperature prior to baking since they are so large. Smaller cookie dough balls can be baked from frozen. Baked cookies can be frozen for up to 3 months in a freezer safe baggie or container.
- Vanilla extract is optional because rumor has it Levain Bakery doesn't add it in theirs — however, you can add in 1 tsp vanilla; the author tested it and found it delicious.
- Feel free to make the cookies smaller in size — may need to bake for a minute or so less since they're smaller.
- The original recipe called for 3 cups of chocolate chips, but after several people made it, 3 seemed to be too much — 2 cups seems to be plenty for most people.
- No cake flour? A "homemade cake flour" recipe (14 Tbsp all-purpose flour + 2 Tbsp corn starch, sifted twice) worked just as well, even better than the 100% all-purpose version, per the author's testing. Makes ~1 cup flour; only ¾ cup needed for this recipe.
- Spoon and level flours by using a spoon to scoop the flour into the measuring cup and use the back of a knife to level off the top.
- If the cookies are coming out flat or spreading, may need to add a few tablespoons more flour.
- Gluten free: replace both the all-purpose flour and cake flour with Bob's Red Mill Gluten Free 1-to-1 Baking Flour — tested and found very similar in taste and appearance.
