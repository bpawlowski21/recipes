## Ingredients

- 1/3 cup dried mushrooms (porcini)
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 medium carrot, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 pound mushrooms (fresh)
- 1/2 cup farro, pearled barley, or spelt, rinsed
- 6 cups low sodium beef broth or stock (vegetable, mushroom, or chicken stock acceptable)
- 1/4 cup dry sherry
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon sherry vinegar

## Directions

1. Cover dried mushrooms with 1 cup boiling water; let sit 20 minutes while preparing remaining ingredients.
2. Trim and slice fresh mushrooms, then chop to desired texture.
3. Heat oil in a heavy pot. Sauté onions and carrots over medium heat until onions begin to color (10-15 minutes). Add garlic and cook 30 seconds. Add fresh mushrooms and cook until they release liquid (5-10 minutes).
4. Raise heat and add grain; sauté until beginning to color. Add broth and sherry with tomato paste.
5. Remove rehydrated porcinis from soaking liquid, chop finely, and strain soaking liquid through a fine-mesh strainer to remove grit. Add both liquid and chopped mushrooms to pot.
6. Season with salt and pepper; simmer approximately 40 minutes until grains are tender.
7. Stir in sherry vinegar; adjust seasonings and serve.
