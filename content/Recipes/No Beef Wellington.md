Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post by the same author as the other "Maya" recipes in this vault. "This 'no beef wellington' is definitely the perfect vegan Christmas dinner along with some roast potatoes and veggies. The hearty and meaty tofu 'beef' covered in crispy vegan puff pastry... just pour over some vegan gravy and it's ALL GOOD!" 285 cal / 7P / 22C / 18F per serving.

## Ingredients

- 1 onion
- 1 carrot
- 3 cloves garlic
- 200g tofu
- 2 tbsp starch
- 8 tbsp breadcrumbs
- ⅛ cup (30ml) red wine or veg stock
- 2 tsp mustard
- 1 small cooked beetroot
- ½ cup (80g) walnuts or sunflower seeds
- 1 tsp each rosemary, thyme, smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 200g mushrooms
- 1 vegan puff pastry sheet
- Plant milk, for brushing

## Directions

1. Sauté the onion, carrot, and garlic for a few minutes.
2. Blend the sautéed veggies with the tofu, starch, breadcrumbs, red wine/stock, mustard, beetroot, walnuts, rosemary, thyme, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Shape into a log and fry each side for a few minutes.
3. Sauté the mushrooms and season with salt and pepper.
4. Lay out the puff pastry, add the mushrooms, then the tofu "beef" log, and fold the pastry around it. Brush with plant milk.
5. Bake at 180°C/360°F for 50 minutes. Let cool down for a bit before slicing.
