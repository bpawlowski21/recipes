## Ingredients

- 4 Tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 Tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ cup (118 ml) buttermilk
- ½-1 cup (55-110 g) sharp shredded cheddar cheese, thickly shredded

**Topping**
- 2 Tablespoons salted butter
- ⅛ teaspoon garlic powder
- ¼ teaspoon dried parsley

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Melt butter and let cool.
2. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and garlic powder in a large bowl.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk cooled butter into buttermilk (some curdling is acceptable).
4. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
5. Gently stir cheese into batter.
6. Drop batter by heaping spoonfuls onto prepared baking sheet, spacing 2 inches apart.
7. Bake 10-12 minutes until light golden brown.
8. Whisk together topping ingredients and brush generously over warm biscuits.
9. Serve warm.
