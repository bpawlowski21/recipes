## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 cups (230 grams) all-purpose flour, plus more for the counter
- 6 tablespoons (75 grams) granulated sugar, divided
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 8 tablespoons (4 ounces or 115 grams) unsalted butter, cold, diced
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) half-and-half or 2 tablespoons each milk and heavy cream, cold
- 1 large egg
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon, divided

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Combine flour, 3 tablespoons sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl. Pinch butter into the dry mixture with fingers or a pastry blender until it resembles coarse cornmeal. Make a well in the center, pour in half-and-half, then the egg.
3. Use a fork to gently combine egg and cream in the center, then combine all ingredients into a rough mass. Dip hands in the bowl and knead the mixture several times into an even mass.
4. On a lightly floured counter, roll dough to approximately a 10-by-6-inch rectangle. Sprinkle 1 tablespoon remaining sugar and 1 teaspoon cinnamon over half; fold in half.
5. Roll out again into an 8-by-6-inch rectangle. Sprinkle another tablespoon of sugar and the remaining teaspoon of cinnamon over half; fold in half again.
6. Pat dough into a roughly 6-inch circle. Cut with a sharp knife into 6 wedges. Space evenly on the pan, sprinkle with the final tablespoon of sugar, and bake until slightly golden at the edges, 15 to 17 minutes.
