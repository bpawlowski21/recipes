## Ingredients

- 5 cups (600g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1 1/2 cups (340g) lukewarm water
- 1 cup (227g) ripe sourdough starter
- 2 1/2 teaspoons (15g) table salt
- 1 to 2 teaspoons instant yeast

## Directions

- Combine all ingredients and knead into a smooth dough.
- Let the dough rise in a covered bowl until doubled, approximately 90 minutes.
- Divide the dough in half on a floured surface. Preshape each piece by pulling the edges toward the center, then roll into a ball. Rest, covered, for 15 minutes.
- Elongate each ball by rolling it into a fat oval about 10-11 inches long.
- Place the loaves on a greased or parchment-lined baking sheet, cover, and let rise until puffy (about 1 hour). Preheat the oven to 425°F during the final rise.
- Spray the loaves with lukewarm water and dust lightly with flour.
- Score each loaf with two diagonal slashes using a serrated knife or bread lame.
- Bake 25-30 minutes until the crust is deep golden brown. Cool on a rack.
- Store loosely wrapped at room temperature for several days or freeze for extended storage.
