## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup (113g) semisweet or bittersweet chocolate wafers
- 3 large egg whites
- 1/4 cup plus 1 teaspoon (25g) natural cocoa powder
- 3/4 cup plus 1 tablespoon (159g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 4 cups (340g) shredded sweetened coconut

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Melt chocolate in a double boiler over medium heat; cool 10-15 minutes.
3. Combine egg whites, cocoa powder, sugar, salt, and vanilla in a mixing bowl; stir well.
4. Add melted chocolate and blend thoroughly.
5. Mix in coconut until evenly distributed.
6. Using a 3/4-ounce portioner, form mounds on parchment-lined baking sheets (approximately 1½ tablespoons each).
7. Bake 22-25 minutes until slightly crispy outside and soft inside.
8. Transfer to cooling rack and cool completely. Store in sealed container for up to one week or freeze for three months.
