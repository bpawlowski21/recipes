## Ingredients

- 2 ounces (4 tablespoons) whiskey (bourbon preferred)
- 1 ounce (2 tablespoons) fresh lemon juice
- 3/4 ounce (1 1/2 tablespoons) pure maple syrup (or simple syrup)
- 1 egg white (or 2 tablespoons aquafaba for vegan variation)
- Garnish: orange peel and cocktail cherry
- Ice, for serving (optional)

## Directions

- Combine whiskey, lemon juice, syrup, and egg white in a cocktail shaker without ice. Shake for 15 seconds (dry shake).
- Add ice to the shaker. Shake again for 30 seconds.
- Strain into a glass; foam will collect at the top. Serve with ice (optional), orange peel, and cocktail cherry.
