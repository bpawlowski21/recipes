## Ingredients

**Tofu**
- 1 block extra firm tofu, broken into craggy pieces
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp salt
- ¼ cup olive oil

**Special sauce**
- 1 cup vegan mayo
- 3 tbsp ketchup
- 2 tbsp mustard
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp white vinegar

**Extras**
- 1 shallot, julienned
- ½ head iceberg lettuce, finely sliced
- 1 tomato, thinly sliced
- Burger buns, toasted

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 450°F. Combine the tofu with the spices in a large bowl. Place on a tray and bake 35–40 minutes.
2. Mix all the special sauce ingredients together in a bowl until combined.
3. Layer: bottom bun, sauce, lettuce, tomato, shallot, tofu, special sauce, top bun. Dive in.
