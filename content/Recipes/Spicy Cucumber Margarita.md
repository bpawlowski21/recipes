## Ingredients

- 4-6 cucumber slices
- 4 fresh mint leaves (optional)
- 2 ounces silver tequila
- 1/2 ounce orange liqueur
- Juice from 1/2 lime
- 1-2 jalapeño slices
- 2 teaspoons honey or sugar
- Ginger beer, for topping (optional)

**Chili Salt Rim:**
- 1/4 cup pink sea salt or flaky sea salt
- 1 teaspoon chipotle chili powder
- 1 pinch granulated sugar
- Fresh thyme leaves (optional)

## Directions

1. Combine salt, chili powder, and sugar on a shallow plate. Rim glasses with this mixture, then fill with ice.
2. In a cocktail shaker or glass jar, add cucumbers, mint, tequila, orange liqueur, lime juice, and honey. Muddle together to release juices.
3. Add jalapeño slices, fill with ice, and shake for about 1 minute (longer shaking increases spiciness).
4. Strain into prepared glass. Top with ginger beer if desired. Garnish with cucumber slices and thyme as preferred.

## Notes

- Saved from Apple Notes folder "Cocktails".
- If no orange liqueur on hand, substitute 2 teaspoons orange zest, orange juice, or omit entirely.
