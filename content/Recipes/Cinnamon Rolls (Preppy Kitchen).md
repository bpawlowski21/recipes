## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 1 cup warm milk (110-120°F)
- 5 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 2¼ teaspoons active dry yeast
- 4⅔ cups all-purpose flour
- ⅓ cup packed light brown sugar
- ½ cup unsalted butter, melted
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- ½ teaspoon cinnamon

**Filling**
- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, room temperature
- ½ cup light brown sugar
- 1½ tablespoons cinnamon

**Glaze**
- 4 ounces cream cheese, room temperature
- 2 tablespoons butter, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- 2 cups powdered sugar
- 1-2 tablespoons milk

## Directions

**Prepare dough**
1. Mix warm milk, granulated sugar, and yeast in a stand mixer bowl; let foam for 5 minutes.
2. Add flour, brown sugar, melted butter, egg, vanilla, salt, and cinnamon; mix on low until combined, then increase to medium-low for 8-10 minutes until springy and tacky.
3. Transfer to oiled bowl, cover, and let rise in warm place (75°F) until doubled, about 1½-2 hours.
4. Grease a 9x13-inch baking pan.

**Shape and fill**
5. Turn dough onto floured surface and roll into a 12x24-inch rectangle.
6. Spread soft butter in a thin layer, leaving a ½-inch border on one long side.
7. Sprinkle cinnamon-sugar mixture evenly over butter.
8. Roll dough tightly from opposite long end; mark into 12 equal pieces and cut using a serrated knife or dental floss.
9. Place rolls in prepared pan, spacing evenly; cover loosely and let rise until nearly doubled, about 1 hour.

**Bake**
10. Preheat oven to 350°F.
11. Bake uncovered 20-25 minutes until golden brown.
12. Meanwhile, beat cream cheese, butter, vanilla, and salt until smooth; add powdered sugar and beat on low; thin with milk if desired.
13. Spread one-third of glaze on warm rolls immediately after removing from oven; let cool 5 minutes, then spread remaining glaze.
