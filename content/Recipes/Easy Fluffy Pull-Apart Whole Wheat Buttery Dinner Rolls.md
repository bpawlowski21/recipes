## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 cups whole wheat pastry flour (or white whole wheat flour)
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 cup very warm water, divided
- 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast
- 2 tablespoons honey, divided
- 1 egg
- 4 tablespoons butter, melted, divided

## Directions

- Combine 1/4 cup warm water, yeast, and 1 tablespoon honey in a small bowl. Stir and let sit 5-10 minutes until foamy.
- In a large bowl or stand mixer, combine whole wheat pastry flour, all-purpose flour, and salt.
- In another bowl, mix remaining 3/4 cup warm water, 1 tablespoon honey, egg, and 2 tablespoons melted butter.
- Add both water mixtures to the flour mixture. Knead on medium speed for about 5 minutes until dough forms a ball (it will be wet and sticky).
- Oil the bowl, return dough, cover, and place in a warm location for 25 minutes.
- Grease an 8x8 or 9x9 baking dish. Divide dough into 9 balls and arrange in 3 rows. Cover with a damp towel or sprayed plastic wrap.
- Preheat oven to 400°F.
- Allow rolls to rise 15-25 minutes until popping from the pan. Brush with remaining 2 tablespoons melted butter.
- Bake 15-20 minutes until lightly browned. Serve warm with butter.
