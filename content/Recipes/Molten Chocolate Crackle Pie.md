## Ingredients

### For the Pie:
- 1 pie crust round (homemade recipe below, or store-bought/Trader Joe's)
- 1 egg, beaten, for brushing
- Vanilla sugar or coarse sugar, for sprinkling (optional)
- 4 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 teaspoons instant coffee granules
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 10 tablespoons salted butter, melted
- 1 tablespoon hazelnut liquor (optional) — Wendy specifies Frangelico
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips or chunks

### For Hazelnut Whipped Cream:
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 tablespoon real maple syrup
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 tablespoon hazelnut liquor (optional) — Wendy specifies Frangelico

### All Butter Pie Crust (optional, if making homemade):
- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 sticks (1 cup) cold salted butter, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
- 2 tablespoons hazelnut liquor (optional)

## Directions

- Position rack in lower third of oven. Preheat to 375°F.
- Fit pie crust into 9-inch deep pie plate. Brush edge with beaten egg and sprinkle with vanilla sugar. Prick bottom lightly with fork. Line with parchment and fill with pie weights. Freeze 10-15 minutes.
- Bake until set, 20 minutes. Remove weights and bake until golden, 5 minutes. Remove from oven and reduce temperature to 350°F.
- Whisk together eggs and sugar until combined. Add cocoa powder, instant coffee, and salt. Whisk in melted butter, hazelnut liquor, and vanilla until smooth. Fold in chocolate chips and pour into baked crust.
- Bake 45-55 minutes until puffed on top but wiggly in center. Cool 20-30 minutes. Serve warm with whipped cream, or chill for later.
- For whipped cream: whip cream until soft peaks form. Add maple syrup, hazelnut liquor, and vanilla. Whip until fluffy.
- For homemade crust (optional): combine flour, salt, and butter in food processor. Pulse until pea-size balls form. Add hazelnut liquor, then add 1/2 cup cold water (1 tablespoon at a time) until dough comes together. Roll into 2 rounds and use as directed above.
