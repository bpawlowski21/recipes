## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (113g) coconut oil, solid
- 3/4 cup (159g) dark brown sugar, packed
- 1/2 cup (99g) granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons (40ml) unsulphured molasses
- 2 tablespoons (28ml) unsweetened coconut milk
- 1/3 cup pumpkin puree
- 2 1/4 cups (275g) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 1/2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
- 1 1/2 cups chocolate chips, divided
- Flaky sea salt (optional)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F. Line baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Beat coconut oil, both sugars, and vanilla on medium speed until smooth (about 2 minutes). Add molasses, coconut milk, and pumpkin puree; beat on low until combined.
- In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, salt, and pumpkin pie spice.
- Add dry ingredients to wet mixture on low speed. Fold in 1 1/4 cups chocolate chips.
- Scoop three-tablespoon mounds onto prepared sheet, leaving space between cookies.
- Bake 9-10 minutes until edges are golden and centers are set.
- Press remaining chocolate chips on warm cookies; sprinkle with sea salt if desired.
- Cool on baking sheet for 30 minutes before transferring to cooling rack.
