## Ingredients

- 2 cups basmati rice (unrinsed) or long-grain white rice (rinsed)
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt to taste
- 1 teaspoon olive oil or rice bran oil (optional)
- 2 tablespoons fresh minced cilantro
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice

## Directions

**Stovetop (pasta method):**
- Bring 8 cups water to a boil; add rice, bay leaf, oil, and salt (about 2 teaspoons). Stir and return to boil.
- Boil uncovered for 10-12 minutes until grains are tender.
- Remove bay leaf and drain rice using a fine mesh strainer; rinse with hot water.
- Transfer to a bowl and stir in cilantro, lime juice, and lemon juice. Adjust salt as needed (about 1/4 teaspoon more). Serve warm or at room temperature.
