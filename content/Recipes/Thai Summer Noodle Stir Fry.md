## Ingredients

- 8 ounces wide rice noodles
- 3 tablespoons sesame or extra virgin olive oil
- 1/4 cup, plus 1 tablespoon honey
- 1/2 cup raw peanuts
- 2 tablespoons raw sesame seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes
- 1 pound boneless skinless chicken thighs or breasts, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup low sodium soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 2-3 tablespoons Thai red curry paste
- 1 tablespoon ketchup
- 4 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1 inch fresh ginger, grated
- 1 zucchini or yellow summer squash, thinly sliced
- 2 red or orange bell peppers, thinly sliced
- 1 cup fresh Thai or regular basil, roughly torn
- 1/3 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
- Limes for serving

## Directions

1. Cook rice noodles per package directions and drain.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil, 1 tablespoon honey, peanuts, sesame seeds, and chili flakes in a large skillet over medium heat. Toast until lightly caramelized (3-4 minutes). Transfer to a parchment-lined plate and sprinkle with salt.
3. Combine remaining 1/4 cup honey, soy sauce, fish sauce, curry paste, and ketchup to make the sauce.
4. Wipe skillet clean. Heat oil over medium heat. Add chicken, season with pepper, and brown until crispy (about 5 minutes). Add garlic and ginger; cook 2-3 minutes. Pour in 1/3 of the sauce and boil until it coats chicken (2-3 minutes). Add zucchini and peppers; cook 5 minutes until softened.
5. Pour remaining sauce in and bring to a boil. Stir in noodles, basil, and cilantro. Toss to combine and cook 5 minutes until sauce coats noodles.
6. Serve topped with sesame peanuts, additional basil, and lime juice.
