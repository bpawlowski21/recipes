## Ingredients

- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg yolk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 cups + 2 tablespoons (265 g) all-purpose flour

*Optional for coating:*
- 1 cup (170 g) dark chocolate chips
- 1/2 teaspoon vegetable shortening
- Nonpareil sprinkles

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
- Beat butter and sugar together until creamy.
- Add egg yolk, vanilla, and salt; mix well.
- Gradually add flour on low speed, then increase speed until dough is smooth.
- Fill piping bag (fitted with Ateco 827 tip) with half the dough.
- Pipe swirl shapes onto ungreased baking sheet.
- Bake 10-12 minutes until edges turn golden brown.
- Cool 10 minutes on sheet, then transfer to rack.
- Once completely cooled, melt chocolate with shortening and dip cookies.
- Decorate with sprinkles and allow chocolate to harden (or refrigerate 10-15 minutes).
