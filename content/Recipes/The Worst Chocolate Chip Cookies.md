## Ingredients

- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- 1 1/2 cups (300 g) light brown sugar, firmly packed
- 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs, room temperature preferred
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) maple syrup
- 3 1/3 cups (415 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 cups (340 g) chocolate chips

## Directions

- Combine melted butter and sugars in a large bowl, stirring together.
- Add eggs one at a time, stirring until combined.
- Mix in vanilla extract and maple syrup.
- In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
- Gradually add dry ingredients to wet ingredients, stirring until combined.
- Stir in chocolate chips.
- Cover and chill for at least 30 minutes (up to 3 days).
- Preheat oven to 350°F and line cookie sheets with parchment paper.
- Scoop about 2 tablespoons of dough per cookie, rolling into slightly tall balls.
- Space cookies at least 2 inches apart on prepared sheets.
- Bake approximately 13 minutes until edges begin turning golden.
- Cool completely on baking sheet.

## Notes

- Saved from Apple Notes folder "COOKIES". Label notes: no stand mixer needed; try 3/4 cup milk, 3/4 cup bittersweet chocolate, and 1/2 cup butterscotch chips as a variation. Recipe is ironically named "The WORST Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe" by the author.
