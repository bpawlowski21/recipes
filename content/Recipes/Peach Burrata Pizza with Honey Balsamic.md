## Ingredients

**Pizza**
- 1/2 pound homemade or store-bought pizza dough
- 3-5 tablespoons basil pesto
- 4 ounces blue cheese, crumbled
- 3-4 ripe peaches, thinly sliced
- 1 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
- 8 ounces burrata cheese
- Olive oil for drizzling
- Sea salt and pepper to taste
- Handful of Italian microgreens
- Salami (6-8 pieces)

**Honey Balsamic Glaze**
- 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
- 1/4 cup honey
- Pinch of crushed red pepper flakes

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 450°F and grease a large baking sheet with olive oil.
2. On a floured surface, roll out dough into a thin 10-12 inch circle and transfer to prepared sheet.
3. Spread pesto over dough and top with blue cheese, sliced peaches, and half the basil. Drizzle lightly with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
4. Bake 10-15 minutes until crust is golden, cheese melts, and peaches caramelize.
5. Remove from oven and top with salami and burrata. Let burrata warm through for about 5 minutes.
6. For the glaze, whisk together balsamic vinegar, honey, and red pepper flakes until smooth.
7. Sprinkle remaining basil and microgreens on pizza, drizzle with honey balsamic glaze, slice, and serve.
