## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons salted butter, at room temperature, plus more for greasing
- 1 (1-pound) box short-cut pasta, such as macaroni
- 2 cups whole milk
- 1 (8-ounce) package cream cheese, cubed
- 3 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
- Ground cayenne pepper
- 2 cups packed fresh baby spinach, chopped
- 1 (8-ounce) jar marinated artichokes, drained and roughly chopped
- 1 1/2 cups crushed Ritz crackers (about 1 sleeve)
- 3/4 teaspoon garlic powder

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease a 9x13 inch baking dish.
- In a large saucepan, bring 4 cups salted water to a boil. Add pasta and cook, stirring occasionally, for 8 minutes. Stir in milk and cream cheese, cooking until cream cheese melts and pasta is al dente, about 5 minutes more.
- Remove from heat and stir in 2 cups cheddar and 3 tablespoons butter. Season with salt, pepper, and cayenne. Stir in spinach and artichokes. Add 1/4 cup milk or water if sauce is too thick.
- Transfer mixture to prepared baking dish. Top with remaining 1 cup cheddar.
- In a medium bowl, combine crackers, remaining 3 tablespoons butter, and garlic powder. Sprinkle over mac and cheese.
- Bake until sauce bubbles and crumbs are golden, about 20 minutes. Cool 5 minutes before serving. Store leftovers refrigerated up to 3 days.
