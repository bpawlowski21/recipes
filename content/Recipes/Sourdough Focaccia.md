## Ingredients

- 423g all-purpose flour
- 181g high-protein bread flour
- 12g extra virgin olive oil
- 459g water
- 11g salt
- 115g ripe sourdough starter (100% hydration)

## Directions

- **Mix:** Combine flours, water, salt, and starter in a stand mixer bowl with dough hook. Mix on speed 1 for 1-2 minutes until incorporated, then speed 2 for 5 minutes. Rest 10 minutes, then slowly drizzle in olive oil on speed 1 until absorbed, finishing with 1-2 minutes on speed 2.
- **Bulk fermentation (2 hours):** perform 4 sets of stretch and folds at 30-minute intervals, starting 30 minutes after mixing begins.
- **Proof (4 hours):** transfer dough to an oiled 9x13" pan. Every 30 minutes for the first hour, gently stretch dough to the pan edges with wet hands. Cover and continue proofing. Preheat oven to 450°F halfway through.
- **Top & bake:** dimple the dough surface with wet fingers. Drizzle with olive oil, add herbs and coarse sea salt. Bake 30 minutes until golden, rotating the pan halfway through.
- Overnight option: refrigerate after the first 2 hours of proofing.
