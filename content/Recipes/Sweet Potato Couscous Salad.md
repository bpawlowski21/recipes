## Ingredients

- 2 (600g) medium sweet potatoes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp salt and pepper
- ½ cup (120ml) uncooked couscous
- 2 cups (480ml) kale
- ½ cup (120ml) pomegranate seeds
- 1 cup (240 ml) edamame beans
- ½ cup (120ml) sun-dried tomatoes
- 1 red onion
- ¼ cup (60 ml) almonds

**Dressing**
- ½ cup (120ml) cashews
- 1 cup (240ml) plant-milk
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 cup (240ml) cilantro
- Salt and pepper

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C/400°F.
2. Wash and cut the sweet potato into small cubes. Transfer to a baking sheet and drizzle with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Bake 25–30 minutes.
3. Cook the couscous according to package instructions.
4. Finely chop the rest of the ingredients.
5. Add all the dressing ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth.
6. Assemble the salad and enjoy.
