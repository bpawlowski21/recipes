## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 cups (215 g) all-purpose flour, heat-treated
- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 1/4 cups (250 g) brown sugar, tightly packed
- 1/4 cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 1-2 tablespoons milk, as needed
- 1/2 cup (85 g) semisweet chocolate chips

Optional mix-ins: creamy peanut butter, colored sprinkles, broken Oreo pieces

## Directions

- Heat-treat flour by baking at 350°F for 5-7 minutes until it reaches 165°F (verify with thermometer at multiple points), then cool and sift to remove clumps.
- In a large bowl, cream together softened butter, both sugars, vanilla extract, and salt using an electric mixer for 1-3 minutes until very creamy and well-combined.
- Gradually stir in cooled, sifted flour until fully incorporated. Add milk one tablespoon at a time if the dough becomes too stiff.
- Fold in chocolate chips and any desired add-ins.
- Enjoy immediately, or scoop into balls and refrigerate in an airtight container for up to one week.
