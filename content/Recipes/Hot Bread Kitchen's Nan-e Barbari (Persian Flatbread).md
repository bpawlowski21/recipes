## Ingredients

**Dough:**
- 1 2/3 to 1 3/4 cups (379g to 397g) lukewarm water
- 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast or instant yeast
- 4 cups plus 3 tablespoons (504g) unbleached bread flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons (9g) table salt
- Semolina flour for dusting (optional)

**Glaze:**
- 2 teaspoons unbleached all-purpose or bread flour
- 1/2 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vegetable oil
- 1/3 cup (74g) cool water

**Topping:**
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
- 1 teaspoon nigella (black onion) seeds

## Directions

- Mix water, yeast, flour, and salt until combined. Knead 5-8 minutes by hand (or until smooth and soft dough forms).
- Place dough in greased bowl, cover, and let rise until nearly doubled, about 1 hour.
- Transfer to floured surface, deflate gently, and divide into two pieces.
- Shape each into a 9" log, cover loosely, and rest 30 minutes.
- Preheat oven to 450°F (set pizza stone on lowest rack if using).
- Combine glaze ingredients in saucepan, bring to bare boil, stirring constantly until thickened (under 1 minute). Set aside.
- Flatten one dough piece into 14" x 5" rectangle on semolina-dusted surface or parchment.
- Press five lengthwise grooves into dough using fingers or spoon handle (don't cut through bottom).
- Spread half the glaze over dough and sprinkle with half the seeds.
- Bake on stone or sheet for 15-18 minutes until golden brown.
- Repeat with second loaf while first bakes.
- Cool on rack. Serve warm or at room temperature with olives, cucumbers, and feta.
- Store wrapped at room temperature several days; freeze for longer storage.
