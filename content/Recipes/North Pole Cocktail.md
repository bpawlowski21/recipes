## Ingredients

- 4 ounces vodka
- 2 ounces Kahlúa
- 4 tablespoons chocolate syrup
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 teaspoons molasses
- 1/8 teaspoon ginger
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or whole milk
- Whipped cream, candy canes, and gingerbread cookies (optional garnish)

## Directions

1. Combine vodka, Kahlúa, chocolate syrup, vanilla, molasses, and ginger in a cocktail shaker. Shake until well combined.
2. Add ice and shake again. Strain into 4 glasses.
3. Top each glass with heavy cream. Dollop with whipped cream and garnish as desired.
