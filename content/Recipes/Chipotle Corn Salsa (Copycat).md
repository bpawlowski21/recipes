## Ingredients

- 1 large poblano pepper
- 24 ounces frozen corn
- 1/2 cup red onion, finely chopped
- 2 jalapeño peppers, stemmed, seeded, and finely chopped
- 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh cilantro
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- Salt to taste

## Directions

**Roast the poblano:**
- Turn a gas burner to high. Using tongs, roast the pepper directly over the flame, turning occasionally, until charred and blistered on all sides (about 2-3 minutes).
- Transfer to a bowl, cover tightly, and let steam for 10 minutes.
- Rub off the skins. Remove stems and seeds, then finely chop.

**Make the salsa:**
- Cook corn according to package directions. Spread on a baking sheet to cool.
- In a large bowl, combine cooled corn, red onion, jalapeño peppers, poblano pepper, cilantro, lemon juice, and lime juice. Season to taste with salt.
- Yield: 10 servings (1/2 cup each). Prep time: 20 minutes. Cook time: 15 minutes.
