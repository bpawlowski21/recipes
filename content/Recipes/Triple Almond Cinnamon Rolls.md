## Ingredients

### Dough
- 1 cup (226g) whole milk
- 4 tablespoons (57g) unsalted butter, cubed and very soft
- 1/2 teaspoon almond extract
- 3½ cups (406g) all-purpose flour, divided
- 1 package (0.25 oz) rapid rise yeast
- 3 tablespoons (35g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 large egg, room temperature

### Filling
- 1 cup (213g) light brown sugar
- 1/2 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 cup (113g) unsalted butter, very soft

### Topping
- 1 cup (114g) powdered sugar, plus more as needed
- 1 teaspoon almond extract
- 1-2 tablespoons milk
- 1/2 cup (50g) slivered almonds, toasted

## Directions

### Dough
- Heat milk in a small saucepan over medium-low until bubbles form around edges. Remove from heat; add butter and almond extract. Stir until melted; set aside to cool.
- In stand mixer bowl with dough hook, combine 2½ cups flour, yeast, sugar, and salt; mix well.
- Add egg and beat until incorporated; add milk mixture and mix thoroughly, scraping bowl as needed.
- Add remaining flour gradually (½ cup at a time), beating well after each addition.
- Beat on medium speed for 5 minutes after all flour is added.
- Transfer dough to lightly floured surface and gently knead into a ball (1-2 kneads). Place in large bowl, cover with plastic wrap or damp cloth, and let rise in warm space for 40 minutes.

### Filling
- Mix brown sugar, cinnamon, and butter in small bowl.

### Assembly
- Grease a 9x13-inch baking pan.
- Roll dough into large rectangle (approximately 16x10 inches). Spread filling over dough surface, leaving 2-inch border around edges.
- Tightly roll dough and pinch seams to seal.
- Cut into 12 equal pieces using unscented floss or knife.
- Place pieces cut-side up in prepared pan. Cover and let rise for 30 minutes.
- Preheat oven to 375°F.
- Bake for 20 minutes until lightly browned on tops and edges.
- Cool briefly, then top with icing and almonds.

### Topping
- Whisk together powdered sugar, almond extract, and milk. Drizzle over warm buns and sprinkle with toasted almonds.
