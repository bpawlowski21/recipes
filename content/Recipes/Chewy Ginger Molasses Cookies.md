## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups (340g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
- 1 cup (200g) granulated white sugar
- 1 cup (213g) packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (170g) unsulphured molasses
- 2 large eggs
- 4 1/2 cups (639g) all-purpose flour
- 4 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 tablespoon ground ginger
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 teaspoon fine sea salt
- Granulated sugar, for rolling

## Directions

- Whisk together flour, baking soda, cinnamon, cloves, ginger, and salt in a medium bowl; set aside.
- Using a mixer, cream softened butter and sugars on medium-high speed until light and fluffy (about 2 minutes). Mix in eggs one at a time, then molasses on medium-low speed until combined. Gradually add dry ingredients and beat until incorporated.
- Transfer dough to an airtight container and refrigerate for at least 2 hours until completely chilled.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Roll dough into 1-inch balls. Roll each ball in sugar until coated and place on prepared baking sheet.
- Bake for 8-10 minutes until cookies begin to slightly crack on top. Cool on baking sheet for 4-5 minutes, then transfer to wire racks to cool completely.
- Serve warm or store in a sealed container for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
