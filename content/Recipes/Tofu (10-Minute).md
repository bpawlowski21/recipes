Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post by the same author as the other "Maya" recipes in this vault. "This is my go to recipe for tofu and it's pretty much foolproof! Just make sure you use firm tofu and not silken or soft tofu — you can also bake it." Prep time: 10 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 400g firm tofu, pat dried
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- Salt to taste

**Sauce**
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp white wine or balsamic vinegar
- 2 tbsp agave or maple syrup
- Optional: hot sauce
- 1 tsp each garlic powder and ginger powder
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- ⅛ cup (30ml) water

## Directions

1. Tear the tofu into chunks and combine with cornstarch and salt.
2. Fry in oil until crispy, or bake at 180°C/360°F for 20 minutes.
3. Mix the sauce ingredients and add to the hot pan. Stir while heating until thickened, then stir in the tofu.
