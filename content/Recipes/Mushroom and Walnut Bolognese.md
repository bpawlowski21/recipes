Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. "This meat-free version will surprise you. It's been soooo popular that I just had to add it as episode 5 of my 'Easy Dinners You'll Actually Make' series!"

## Ingredients

- 200g mushrooms, quartered
- 100g walnuts
- 1 red onion, quartered
- 1 carrot, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- ¼ can of water
- 1 tbsp tamari (or soy sauce)
- 1 vegetable stock pot
- Handful fresh basil
- 400g spaghetti (author used gluten-free)

## Directions

1. Add the mushrooms, walnuts, onion, carrot, and garlic to a food processor and pulse (don't over-blend — you want texture).
2. Heat a pan with oil and add the "mince," along with the dried herbs, and fry a few minutes.
3. Add the chopped tomatoes, water, tamari, stock pot, a pinch of salt and pepper, and simmer with the lid on for 10 minutes. Meanwhile, boil the spaghetti.
4. Stir through the fresh basil and drained pasta, and it's ready to serve.
