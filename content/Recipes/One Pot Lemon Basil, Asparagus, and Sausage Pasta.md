## Ingredients

- 1 pound short-cut pasta
- 1/4 cup, plus 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1 cup fresh basil, chopped, plus more for serving
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh chives
- 1/2 cup green olives, chopped
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 pound ground spicy Italian chicken sausage
- 1 bunch asparagus, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1/4-1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1/4 cup dry white wine (Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc)
- 3/4 cup grated parmesan cheese, plus more for serving

## Directions

- Boil pasta in salted water until al dente per package directions; drain.
- While the pasta cooks, combine 1/4 cup olive oil, vinegar, lemon zest, lemon juice, basil, chives, and olives in a bowl or food processor. Season with salt and pepper.
- In the same pot, heat the remaining 2 tablespoons oil over medium-high heat. Brown the sausage for 5-8 minutes. Reduce heat to low; add asparagus, garlic, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Cook 5 minutes until asparagus is tender. Add wine and simmer 2-3 minutes. Add pasta and parmesan, tossing to coat.
- Stir in the basil-olive sauce. Remove from heat and serve topped with additional basil and parmesan.
