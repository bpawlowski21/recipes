## Ingredients

### Sponge
- 3/4 cup (170g) lukewarm water
- 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon instant yeast
- 1/2 cup (60g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour

### Dough
- All of the sponge
- 3/4 cup (170g) plain or vanilla yogurt, room temperature
- 8 tablespoons (113g) unsalted butter, softened
- 2 large eggs, room temperature, beaten
- 1/2 cup (99g) granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons (12g) table salt
- 2 teaspoons King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 5 cups (600g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour

### Filling
- 6 ounces (170g) cream cheese, softened
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup (57g) sour cream, room temperature
- 2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 cup (30g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 3/4 cup (203g) prepared lemon curd

### Topping
- 1 large egg beaten with 2 teaspoons water and a pinch of salt
- Swedish pearl sugar or coarse sparkling sugar

## Directions

- Make the sponge: mix sponge ingredients in a large bowl, cover loosely, and proof 10-15 minutes.
- Combine dough ingredients: add yogurt, butter, eggs, sugar, salt, and vanilla to the sponge; mix in 4 1/2 cups flour with the paddle attachment until shaggy.
- Knead with the dough hook on speed 2 for 5-6 minutes until smooth and soft, adding flour as needed.
- First rise: place dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise 1-1 1/2 hours until nearly doubled. Prepare the filling during this time by combining all filling ingredients except the lemon curd until smooth; reserve both filling and curd.
- Divide dough in half. Cover one half and roll the other into a 10"x15" rectangle on parchment.
- Press two lines lengthwise to divide into thirds. Spread half the cream cheese filling down the center section, top with half the lemon curd, leaving 1" bare at the ends.
- Shape the braid: cut and remove 1" rectangles from each corner, fold the resulting tab over the filling edge, then cut 1" crosswise strips down the outside sections (equal number on each side).
- Braid: lift the top left strip across the filling diagonally, then the top right strip, alternating down the entire length. Fold the final tab over the filling and crisscross the last two strips.
- Repeat rolling, filling, and braiding with the remaining dough. Cover both loaves and let rise 45-50 minutes until puffy.
- Preheat oven to 375°F. Brush loaves with beaten egg and sprinkle with pearl sugar or sparkling sugar.
- Bake 25-30 minutes until golden brown. Cool 15-20 minutes before serving.
- Wrap leftovers and refrigerate for a couple days; freeze for longer storage. Reheat briefly before serving.
