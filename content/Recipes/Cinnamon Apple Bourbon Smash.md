## Ingredients

**Spicy Cinnamon Syrup**
- 1/2 cup maple syrup
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper

**Apple Smash (single drink)**
- 1 ounce (2 tablespoons) spicy cinnamon syrup
- 1 1/2 ounces (3 tablespoons) bourbon
- 1 ounce (2 tablespoons) lemon or grapefruit juice
- 1 tablespoon apple butter
- 1-2 dashes orange bitters
- Ginger beer, for topping

## Directions

- Prepare the spicy cinnamon syrup: bring 1/2 cup water, maple syrup, cinnamon sticks, and ginger to a boil over high heat. Boil 2-3 minutes, remove from heat, and stir in cayenne pepper. Cool completely, then strain into a glass jar (yields about 6 drinks).
- In a cocktail shaker, combine 1-2 tablespoons cinnamon syrup, bourbon, lemon/grapefruit juice, apple butter, and orange bitters. Add ice and shake. Strain into a glass and top with ginger beer.
- Garnish with thin apple slices, pomegranate arils, and a cinnamon stick. Optionally light the cinnamon stick for a smoky effect.
- Mocktail: omit bourbon; substitute 1/4 cup apple cider plus 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract.
