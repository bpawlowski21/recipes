Saved from Apple Notes ("Holiday Prosecco drinks"), an Instagram Reel screenshot with 4 holiday mimosa variations, transcribed from the on-screen caption text.

## Ingredients

**Pomegranate Mimosa**
- 1 oz vanilla vodka
- 1.5 oz pomegranate juice
- Top with Prosecco
- Garnish with pomegranate seeds

**Midori Mimosa**
- 1 oz Midori
- 1.5 oz pineapple juice
- Top with Prosecco
- Red sugar rim

**Bourbon Apple Mimosa**
- 1 oz bourbon
- 1.5 oz apple cider
- Top with Prosecco
- Cinnamon stick, ground cinnamon rim

**Cranberry Vodka Mimosa**
- 1 oz vodka
- 1.5 oz cranberry juice
- Top with Prosecco
- Garnish with fresh cranberries

## Notes

- For each: combine the base spirit and juice/cider in a glass, then top with Prosecco, and finish with the listed garnish.
