## Ingredients

- 1 pound boneless skinless chicken thighs or breasts, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons corn starch or flour
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1-2 teaspoons black pepper
- 3 tablespoons sesame oil or extra virgin olive oil
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup low sodium soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 8 ounces rice noodles
- 2 medium shallots, sliced or chopped
- 2 small heads broccoli, chopped
- 1/2 cup raw cashews
- Zest and juice of 1 lime
- 1/2 cup fresh basil or cilantro, roughly chopped
- 3 tablespoons salted butter
- 3-4 cloves garlic, finely chopped or grated
- 1/3 cup canned coconut milk

## Directions

1. Combine chicken with cornstarch, turmeric, ginger, black pepper, and 1 tablespoon oil in a bowl.
2. Mix honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, red pepper flakes, and 1/4 cup water in a jar.
3. Cook rice noodles according to package directions.
4. Heat remaining 2 tablespoons oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add chicken and brown until crispy (about 5 minutes). Add shallots, broccoli, and cashews; cook 2-3 minutes. Pour in sauce mixture and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Cook until sauce coats chicken (about 5 minutes). Stir in lime zest, juice, and basil.
5. In another skillet over medium heat, melt butter with garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook until garlic browns (1-2 minutes). Stir in coconut milk and warm through (3-5 minutes). Toss noodles with sauce.
6. Divide noodles among bowls and top with chicken mixture. Serve with additional basil and limes.
