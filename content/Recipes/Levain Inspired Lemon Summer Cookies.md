## Ingredients

- 405 g all purpose flour (3 1/4 cups)
- 8g baking powder (2 tsp)
- 3g salt (1/2 tsp)
- 6g baking soda (1 tsp)
- 4g cornstarch (1/2 tsp)

**Wet**
- 227g cold butter (2 sticks) cubed
- 200g brown sugar (1 cup)
- 120g white sugar (3/4 cup)
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 tsp lemon extract
- Zest of 2 lemons

**Mix-ins**
- 12oz white chocolate chips or chopped white chocolate bark (or combo)

## Directions

1. In your mixing bowl, combine the sugars and lemon zest. Rub together with your fingers until fragrant. This helps release the oils from the zest and intensifies the lemon flavor.
2. Add the cold cubed butter and mix until just combined. Do not cream the butter.
3. Add the egg yolks, whole egg, vanilla, and lemon extract. Mix until combined.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cornstarch.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet and mix until just barely combined.
6. Fold in the white chocolate.
7. Portion the dough into large 6oz cookie dough tall ovals. You should get about 8.
8. Freeze the cookie dough for at least 2 hours or overnight. This step is key for getting that thick Levain-style texture.

**Baking**
- Bake at 375 for 18-20 minutes until the outside is golden brown but the centers still look lightly soft.
- Let the cookies rest for 20-30 minutes before eating. They continue baking internally as they cool, which gives you that perfect gooey center and outside crunch without falling apart.

## Notes

- Using cold butter instead of softened helps create a thicker cookie with less spread.
- The overnight freeze deepens the flavor and helps maintain that iconic bakery style height.
- 6oz makes GIANT cookies. You can definitely make these smaller, just keep an eye on them in the oven as they will bake faster!
