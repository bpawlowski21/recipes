## Ingredients

- 4 1/2 to 5 cups (540g to 600g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 1 tablespoon (11g) granulated sugar
- 2 1/4 teaspoons instant yeast
- 2 1/2 teaspoons (15g) table salt
- 1 2/3 cups (379g) water, lukewarm (90°F to 110°F)
- Yellow cornmeal, for coating the pan

## Directions

- Weigh flour or measure by gently spooning into a cup and sweeping off excess.
- Stir together all ingredients (except cornmeal) in a large bowl, starting with 4 1/2 cups flour, until a rough, shaggy dough forms.
- Hand-knead about 6 minutes until bouncy and smooth, or use a mixer with dough hook on medium speed for about 7 minutes, sprinkling in flour as needed for the dough to clear the bowl sides.
- Place in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise at room temperature until doubled, about 1-2 hours.
- Gently deflate and cut dough in half. Pat each half into a rough 6"x8" oval, fold like a business letter, press the seam closed, then pat and roll into a 10" log.
- Place loaves seam-side down on a parchment-lined baking sheet and sprinkle generously with cornmeal.
- Cover lightly and let rise 45 minutes until puffy (indentation from a poke should remain).
- Preheat oven to 450°F toward the end of rising. Place an empty cast-iron pan on the lowest oven rack and bring 1 cup water to a boil.
- Dust risen loaves with flour, make three to four 1/2" deep diagonal slashes in each, place in the oven, and pour the boiling water into the pan below; shut the door quickly.
- Bake 20-25 minutes until golden brown and hollow-sounding when tapped (interior at least 190°F).
- Turn off the oven, crack the door open, and leave the bread inside 5 additional minutes. Remove and cool on a rack before slicing.
- Store wrapped at room temperature for a couple days or freeze for longer storage.
