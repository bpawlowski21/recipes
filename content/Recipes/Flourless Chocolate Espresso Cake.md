## Ingredients

**For the Cake**
- 7 ounces dark chocolate (75%-85%)
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 5 large eggs, separated
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons espresso powder
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 ounces semi-sweet or milk chocolate chunks

**For the Whipped Ricotta**
- 2 cups whole milk ricotta cheese
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9-inch springform pan and line with parchment paper.
2. Melt dark chocolate and olive oil in microwave until smooth. Cool 5 minutes. Whisk in egg yolks, sugar, cocoa powder, espresso powder, salt, and vanilla until combined.
3. Whip egg whites on medium speed until stiff peaks form. Fold half into chocolate mixture, then remaining whites. Gently stir in chocolate chunks. Transfer to pan and bake 25-30 minutes until center is set. Rest 10 minutes.
4. For ricotta topping: combine ricotta, honey, and vanilla in a food processor. Pulse 2-3 minutes until smooth and creamy. Adjust sweetness as desired.
5. Serve cake warm or at room temperature, dusted with cocoa powder and topped with honey whipped ricotta. Store uncovered at room temperature up to one week.
