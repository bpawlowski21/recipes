## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons coconut oil (or any oil)
- ¼ cup chopped red onion
- 2 stalks celery, chopped
- 2 chopped carrots *(handwritten addition — not in the original printed ingredient list)*
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 15 oz. can of chickpeas, drained and thoroughly rinsed
- 1¾ cup freshly cooked brown lentils, or 15 oz. can, drained and thoroughly rinsed
- 2 teaspoons liquid smoke, up to 2 tablespoons *(handwritten: use 1 tablespoon)*
- 2 teaspoons vegan Worcestershire sauce (or more liquid smoke)
- 1¼ cups breadcrumbs (gluten-free if needed)
- ½ teaspoon sea salt
- ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 flax eggs *(handwritten alternative: or 2 regular eggs)*
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- ½ teaspoon thyme

**Tomato Glaze**
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 teaspoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup (or agave or liquid sweetener)
- ¼ teaspoon sea salt
- *(handwritten note: double the glaze)*

## Directions

**Tomato Glaze**
1. Mix together the tomato paste, apple cider vinegar, maple syrup, and sea salt in a small bowl and set it aside until you need it.

**Meatloaf**
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F/190°C. Prepare a loaf pan by lining with parchment paper so that it's hanging over the sides — this helps remove the loaf with ease.
2. In a skillet over medium heat, heat the oil. Add the garlic, red onion, celery, and carrots. Sauté until onions are translucent, celery has softened, and garlic is fragrant, about 5 minutes.
3. In a large bowl, add all of the ingredients (the cooked garlic, red onion, celery, and carrots above, as well as the chickpeas, lentils, liquid smoke, vegan Worcestershire, breadcrumbs, sea salt, black pepper, flax eggs, tomato paste, and thyme).
4. Mix together slightly with a wooden spoon. This helps distribute the liquid ingredients equally over the beans and breadcrumbs.
5. In a food processor, add all of your ingredients from the bowl (in increments if your processor is smaller than 10 cups). Pulse a few times — about 10 times — until everything begins to come together. Do NOT over pulse, or it will turn to mush and not firm up in the oven; you want to see chunks of celery, lentils, and other ingredients.
6. Pour/scoop the mixture into your parchment paper-lined loaf pan. Smooth down the top with a spatula. Pour over your glaze from earlier, smoothing it down with a spoon or spatula.
7. Bake for 45 minutes to 60 minutes — ready when a toothpick comes out mostly clean (about 55 minutes for the original author's loaf).
8. Remove from oven and allow to cool for 10 minutes. Remove from loaf pan, slice up, and serve. Enjoy!

## Notes

- If sensitive to liquid smoke, start with 2 teaspoons, taste, and add more if desired (everything is cooked so it's safe to taste). If lowering the amount, replace the rest with water so the recipe has the right amount of liquid (e.g. 1 tablespoon water + 1 teaspoon liquid smoke).
- How to store: keep in an airtight container in the fridge, sliced or whole; lasts at least a week and firms up further in the fridge.
- How to freeze: wrap in foil, then place in a freezer-friendly zipped bag; freezes for at least 2-3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, reheat in the microwave.
- **Handwritten make-ahead note (signed "MH"):** Either place in fridge covered with foil overnight and bake the next day, or bake, cool, store in fridge overnight covered with foil, and then reheat day of.
