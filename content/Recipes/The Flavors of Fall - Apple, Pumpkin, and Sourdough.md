## Overview

A roundup/technique article on incorporating sourdough discard into autumn baking. The author explains the general substitution principle before showcasing seven fall-themed recipes, each with modification instructions for using discard.

**Substitution rule:** To substitute discard sourdough starter in any recipe, replace the combined weight of some of the flour and liquid with an equal weight of starter.

## Recipes Featured

- Thanksgiving Muffins
- Sugar-Crusted Apple Cobbler
- Pumpkin Yeast Bread
- Apple Pancakes with Boiled Cider Syrup
- Pumpkin Chocolate Chip Cookie Bars
- Pumpkin Cheddar Biscuits
- Apple Pie (with sourdough crust)

## Key Tips

- Benefits of using discard: enhanced flavor depth, extended shelf life, improved rise, and practical use of otherwise-discarded starter.
- Baker's tip: using boiled cider with apples intensifies their flavor and cuts through excess sweetness.
- Flexibility emphasized: minor ingredient adjustments won't significantly impact results.
