## Ingredients

- 1 1/3 cup warm water (approximately 110°F)
- 2 teaspoons sugar or honey
- 1 (0.25 ounce) package active-dry yeast
- 3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- 2 teaspoons flaky sea salt, plus extra for sprinkling
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary

## Directions

- Combine warm water and sugar in a stand mixer bowl. Sprinkle yeast on top, stir briefly, and let sit 5-10 minutes until foamy.
- Add flour, olive oil, and salt gradually while mixing on low speed. Increase to medium-low and mix for 5 minutes until smooth.
- Shape dough into a ball, place in greased bowl, cover with damp towel, and let rise in a warm location for 45-60 minutes until doubled.
- Roll dough to approximately 1/2-inch thickness in a circle or rectangle shape. Cover and let rise another 20 minutes.
- Preheat oven to 400°F. Transfer to parchment-lined baking sheet. Poke deep indentations across the surface. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with rosemary and sea salt.
- Bake for 20 minutes until lightly golden and cooked through.
- Remove from oven, optionally drizzle with additional olive oil, slice, and serve warm.
