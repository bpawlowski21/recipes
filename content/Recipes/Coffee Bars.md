## Ingredients

- 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
- 2 cups crispy rice cereal
- 1/4 cup cocoa powder (or swap in 1 to 1 1/2 scoops chocolate-flavored protein powder)
- 1 tablespoon instant ground coffee or espresso powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup creamy almond butter (or nut butter of choice)
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4-1/2 cup mini semi-sweet or dark chocolate chips (optional)

## Directions

- Line an 8-by-8-inch baking pan with parchment paper, letting it hang over the sides for easy lifting later.
- In a mixing bowl, add the oats, cereal, cocoa powder, instant coffee, and salt, and whisk to combine. Set aside.
- In a separate large mixing bowl, stir together the nut butter, honey, and vanilla. (If the nut butter is too stiff, microwave about 20 seconds to soften before adding the honey and vanilla.)
- Add half the oat-cereal mixture into the nut butter bowl and blend until well-combined. Mix in the remaining oat-cereal mixture and continue to stir — you'll need to push and mash the nut butter chunks to incorporate all the cereal. The batter will be crumbly but moist.
- Add the batter crumbles into the lined baking pan and press down using your fingers and palms to compress and pack it tightly, evenly distributing it on the bottom of the pan. Press down with a wide flat spatula to ensure it's neatly packed. If adding mini chocolate chips, scatter over the top and press down with your fingers so they stick.
- Stash in the fridge to firm for at least 30 minutes. Lift the parchment paper out of the pan and place on a flat cutting board. Using a long, sharp knife, cut into 9 even squares (3 slices horizontally, 3 vertically).
- Store in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.

## Notes

- No-bake, gluten-free coffee bars — energy bars with a jolt of caffeine, best for mid-morning or afternoon (avoid closer to bedtime). Can omit the instant coffee/espresso for a kid-friendly, caffeine-free version.
- If nut butter oil has separated to the top of the jar, mix it back in before measuring. Misting measuring cups/spoons with nonstick oil spray helps the nut butter release cleanly.
- Note: the printed ingredient list calls for "creamy almond butter" but the directions refer to it as "peanut butter" throughout — an inconsistency in the original printed recipe itself, transcribed faithfully here rather than guessed at.
