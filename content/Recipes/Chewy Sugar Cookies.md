## Ingredients

- ½ cup (113 grams) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- ½ cup (99 grams) granulated sugar
- ¼ cup (52 grams) light brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 1½ cups (180 grams) all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- 1 cup turbinado sugar
- Zest of 1 lemon (handwritten addition)

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Line two large sheet pans with parchment paper.
2. In a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, or in a large bowl using a hand mixer, cream butter and sugars for 1 minute. Scrape sides of bowl. Continue beating for another minute.
3. Scrape bowl again. Add vanilla. Beat for 1 minute. **Handwritten addition: add lemon zest here, beat 1 more minute.**
4. Scrape sides of bowl. Add egg. Beat for 1 minute. Scrape sides of bowl. Add flour, salt, and baking soda. Beat 1 minute.
5. Scrape sides of bowl and beat for 1 minute, or until combined and no flour pockets remain.
6. Place turbinado sugar in a small, shallow bowl. Using a small cookie/ice cream scoop (about 1½ inches in diameter), scoop balls of dough and drop a few at a time in the coarse sugar and gently roll around.
7. Place balls of dough on parchment, leaving about 1½ inches of space around each. Do not press the balls down — this ensures a chewy middle.
8. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, turning pans halfway through baking, until cookies have spread into rounds and the edges look set. Resist the urge to bake longer, or they won't be chewy — tops don't get much color, but bottoms will be nicely golden. Place pans on cooling racks. When cool, store cookies in air-tight containers.

## Notes

- Handwritten margin note (red pen) adds lemon zest (1 lemon) to the dough, to be added right before the egg goes in and beaten in with the vanilla — see Directions step 3. This is a tested adaptation to the original Food52 contest-winning recipe, not part of the original printed text.
- Step 2 of the original printed directions was left blank on the clipping (numbering skips from 1 to 3) — transcribed as-is, nothing appears to be missing from the actual method.
