## Ingredients

- 7 eggs
- 2 sticks butter
- 1 Tbls vanilla
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 3 Tbls baking powder
- Dash of salt
- 6 cups flour

**Icing**
- 4 cups confectionary sugar
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1 Tbls vanilla, anise, or lemon extract

## Directions

1. Cream the butter, vanilla, sugar, baking powder, and salt with the eggs.
2. Add the flour and knead all together; refrigerate for at least one hour.
3. Roll into 3" strips about 1/2" thick and twist or twirl.
4. Bake in 350 preheated oven for 7 minutes.
5. For the icing: mix confectionary sugar, milk, and vanilla/anise/lemon extract, adding milk a little at a time till desired consistency.
