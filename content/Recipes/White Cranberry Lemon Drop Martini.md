"Mix up a seasonal treat with a White Cranberry Lemon Drop Martini. Perfect for holiday festivities and family gatherings."

Handwritten note observing the recipe's overlap with the Cosmopolitan (signed "MH"). Original scan:

![[White Cranberry Lemon Drop Martini (annotated).pdf]]

**Equipment:** Cocktail shaker, jigger, citrus juicer

## Ingredients

- 2 ounces vodka
- 1 ounce white cranberry juice
- 1 ounce lemon juice, freshly squeezed
- ½ ounce orange liqueur
- ½ ounce simple syrup
- Ice

## Directions

1. Dip the rim of a coupe or martini glass into simple syrup. Firmly but gently press it onto a small plate covered with granulated sugar. Place glass in the freezer to set.
2. Add vodka, white cranberry juice, fresh lemon juice, and simple syrup to a cocktail shaker. Add ice, then shake until chilled.
3. Strain into your coupe or martini glass, prepped with a sugar rim. Garnish with fresh cranberries and a rosemary sprig.

## Notes

- **Handwritten note:** same recipe as the [[White Cosmopolitan]], using fresh lime juice.
