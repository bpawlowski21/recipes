## Ingredients

- 2 sticks unsalted butter, cut into tablespoons
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons heavy cream, half-and-half, or whole milk
- 2 teaspoons Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ¾ cup packed dark brown sugar
- ¾ cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs, cold from the refrigerator
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 10 ounces semi-sweet chocolate bits, or 5 ounces bittersweet chocolate disks (half coarsely chopped) + 5 ounces milk chocolate disks (half coarsely chopped)

## Directions

1. Using a skillet, brown 1 stick of butter over medium heat.
2. Transfer the browned butter to a large bowl. Let cool for a few minutes, then add the remaining stick of butter. Whisk well until the mixture is smooth. Whisk in the cream.
3. Add both sugars and whisk until smooth. Then add the eggs and vanilla; whisk some more!
4. In a separate bowl, sift the flour and baking soda, then add the 2 teaspoons of salt.
5. Stir the dry ingredients, then once well combined, carefully add to the wet ingredients. Fold in the flour mixture, then whisk the batter until you don't see any bits of flour.
6. Fold in the chocolate chips. Chill the dough for 15-20 minutes. Then remove from the fridge and portion out the dough for a second round of chilling. Using an ice cream scooper, scoop the dough (1 scoop = 1 cookie). Place on a lined baking sheet. Cover with plastic wrap and place in the fridge for 12-24 hours.
7. Once you're ready to bake cookies, take another lined baking sheet and add 4-6 cookies per sheet.
8. Bake at 350°F until golden for about 18-20 minutes. Remove from the oven and let cool. First, let cool for 5 minutes on the pan — the cookies must set before they can be moved to a wire rack. Then after another 5 minutes on a wire rack, enjoy.
