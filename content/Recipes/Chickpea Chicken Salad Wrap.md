## Ingredients

- 1 can chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- ¼ cup sunflower seeds
- 1 cup diced celery
- 1 cup dill pickles
- 1 tsp dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp pickle brine
- 1 tsp dill
- ⅓ cup vegan mayo
- 1 tsp salt

## Directions

1. Pulse all ingredients together in a food processor until combined — do not turn into mush, it should still have texture.
2. Add to a whole grain or gluten-free wrap with some greens and dig in.
