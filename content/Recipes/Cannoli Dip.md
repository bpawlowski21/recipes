Tawnie Graham of Kroll's Korner. "This Cannoli Dip is a deconstructed version of your favorite Italian dessert, perfect for dipping cookies, fruit, or waffle cones. Ready in just 10 minutes, it's the ultimate party treat!"

Prep Time: 5 minutes · Cook Time: 5 minutes · Chill time: 30 minutes · Total Time: 40 minutes · Calories: 121

## Ingredients

- 2 cups whole milk, full-fat ricotta, drained* (a 15 or 16 oz. container is perfect)
- 8 oz. mascarpone cheese
- 1½-2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- ½ cup mini chocolate chips, plus more for garnishing on top
- For serving: broken waffle cones, broken cannoli shells, Italian pizzelle cookies, biscotti, chocolate chip cookies, strawberries, etc.

## Directions

1. Be sure to drain and discard any excess liquid from the ricotta cheese to ensure a creamy texture.
2. Add the ricotta, mascarpone, powdered sugar, and vanilla extract to a stand mixer and beat on medium-high speed for 4-5 minutes, until it is thick and fluffy.
3. Fold in ½ cup mini chocolate chips with a rubber spatula.
4. Pour into a serving bowl, and top with more mini chocolate chips. Chill for at least 30 minutes - 1 hour, or up to 2 days.
5. Serve with your favorite cookie for dipping, and enjoy!

## Notes

- Storage: keep in the refrigerator for up to 3-5 days. Give it a good stir before digging in.
- Variations: feel free to add in some chopped pistachios/almonds/hazelnuts, orange zest, a pinch of ground cinnamon or nutmeg, add in 1-2 Tbsp. cocoa powder for a cocoa cannoli dip flavor, or add in a pinch of espresso powder for a coffee-infused twist.
