## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 1½ cups lukewarm water, divided
- 1 packet active dry yeast (2¼ tsp or .25 oz)
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 large egg
- 3 large egg yolks
- ⅓ cup honey
- 2 tablespoons avocado oil
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 4½-6 cups all-purpose flour

**Egg wash**
- 1 large egg
- 1 tablespoon cold water
- ½ teaspoon salt

**Optional additions:** raisins or chocolate chips (1½ cups), sesame seeds, poppy seeds, or kosher salt for topping

## Directions

1. Proof yeast: mix ¼ cup lukewarm water with yeast and sugar; wait 10 minutes until foamy.
2. Combine wet ingredients: add remaining water, egg, yolks, honey, oil, and salt; whisk together.
3. Incorporate flour gradually, stirring then kneading until smooth and elastic.
4. First rise: oil bowl, place dough inside, cover with damp towel, and let rise 1 hour in a warm oven with boiling water.
5. Punch down dough; let rise another hour until doubled.
6. Remove and knead briefly on floured surface.
7. Braid dough (see "How to Braid Challah" note for shaping techniques).
8. Place braided challah on parchment-lined baking sheet.
9. Apply egg wash with pastry brush; add toppings if desired.
10. Let rise 30-45 minutes until indentations remain when pressed.
11. Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes; apply second egg wash coat and rotate pan.
12. Bake 20 minutes longer, tenting with foil if browning too quickly.
13. Cool before serving.
