## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 3 lemons
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp dried dill
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper
- 10 oz uncooked chickpea pasta
- 1 can chickpeas (1.5 cups), strained & rinsed
- 1 red bell pepper
- 1 orange bell pepper
- ½ cucumber
- ¼ cup black olives
- 6 cups chopped arugula

## Directions

1. Cook the chickpea (or other protein) pasta. Once cooked, run under cold water to chill.
2. Chop up the veggies, and strain and rinse the chickpeas.
3. Mix the spices, salt, and pepper together in a small bowl.
4. Assemble the salad jars: add 1 tbsp olive oil and the juice of 1 lemon to each jar, then divide the spice mix.
5. Add the chickpeas, bell peppers, cucumber, chickpea pasta, black olives, and arugula.
6. Close them up and store in the fridge for up to 5 days.
