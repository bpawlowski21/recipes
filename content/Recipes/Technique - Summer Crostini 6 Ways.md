## Ingredients

**1. Avocado & Corn** — crusty grilled sourdough, smashed avocado, charred corn, cherry tomatoes, fresh basil, crumbled feta, lemon zest, kosher salt, pepper

**2. Fig & Burrata** — crusty grilled sourdough, fig preserves, burrata cheese, prosciutto, fresh fig, fresh thyme, fresh basil, flaky salt, pepper, honey drizzle

**3. Goat Cheese & Cherry** — crusty grilled honey whole wheat bread, goat cheese, fresh cherries, fresh basil, aged balsamic vinegar, olive oil, flaky salt, pepper

**4. Cheddar & Blueberry** — crusty grilled honey whole wheat bread, melted cheddar cheese, blueberry jam, fresh thyme, honey, fresh blueberries

**5. Blue Cheese & Peach** — crusty grilled sourdough, blue cheese, grilled peaches, chipotle honey, toasted pumpkin seeds, flaky salt

**6. Brie & Strawberry** — crusty grilled sourdough or honey whole wheat bread, melted Brie, fresh strawberries, basil, aged balsamic vinegar, honeycomb or honey, flaky salt

## Directions

1. **Avocado & Corn:** Layer smashed avocado, charred corn, cherry tomatoes, and basil on grilled bread; top with feta, lemon zest, salt, and pepper.
2. **Fig & Burrata:** Spread fig preserves on bread, top with burrata, prosciutto, fresh fig, herbs, salt, pepper, and a honey drizzle.
3. **Goat Cheese & Cherry:** Spread goat cheese on bread, top with cherries and basil, drizzle with balsamic and oil, season with salt and pepper.
4. **Cheddar & Blueberry:** Top bread with melted cheddar, spread blueberry jam, garnish with thyme, honey, and fresh blueberries.
5. **Blue Cheese & Peach:** Layer blue cheese and grilled peaches on bread, drizzle with chipotle honey, top with pumpkin seeds and salt.
6. **Brie & Strawberry:** Top bread with melted Brie, add strawberries and basil, drizzle with balsamic and honey, finish with salt.
