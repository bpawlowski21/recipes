## Ingredients

- 3 Persian or Lebanese cucumbers
- 2 medium tomatoes
- ½ red onion
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 medium lemon, juiced (approximately 4-5 tablespoons) or traditionally Ab Ghooreh (unripe sour grape juice)
- Handful fresh mint leaves
- Salt to taste

## Directions

- Dice the cucumbers and tomatoes into small, uniform cubes; chop the mint leaves.
- Dice the red onion into smaller cubes. Optionally, soak in water for 10 minutes to reduce the harsh flavor.
- Combine the dressing ingredients (olive oil, lemon juice, salt) in a jar or small bowl and stir together.
- Add chopped vegetables and mint to a large bowl, pour the dressing over them, and stir. Allow it to sit for 10 minutes for the flavors to develop.
- Serve alongside any meal and enjoy.
