## Ingredients

- 12 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 12 ounces bittersweet chocolate, chopped into small pieces
- 6 large eggs, at room temperature, divided
- 1 tablespoon pure vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup natural, unsweetened cocoa powder
- 3/4 teaspoon salt (fine sea salt or kosher salt)
- 1 cup sugar, divided

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9" springform pan. Combine butter and chocolate in a small pan over medium-low heat, stirring constantly until melted. Whisk smooth, remove from heat, and cool for 10 minutes.
- Separate 4 eggs, placing whites and yolks in separate medium bowls. To the yolk bowl, add vanilla extract, cocoa powder, salt, the 2 remaining eggs, and half the sugar; whisk well. Slowly add cooled chocolate mixture; whisk and set aside.
- Using an electric mixer on medium-high speed, beat egg whites until frothy. Gradually add remaining sugar and beat until firm peaks form.
- Carefully fold egg whites into chocolate mixture until incorporated. Pour batter into prepared pan and smooth the top.
- Bake 35-40 minutes until the top is slightly cracked and center no longer moves. Cool completely before slicing. Optional toppings include cocoa powder, confectioners' sugar, or whipped cream with fresh berries.
