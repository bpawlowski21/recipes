## Ingredients

- 2-2⅓ cups all-purpose flour OR bread flour, divided (250-295g)
- 1 packet instant yeast (2¼ teaspoon)
- 1½ teaspoons sugar
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- ⅛-¼ teaspoon garlic powder and/or dried basil leaves (Wendy: yes, add ¼ teaspoon of each!)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus additional
- ¾ cup warm water (175ml)

## Directions

1. Combine 1 cup flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in a large bowl. Add optional seasonings if desired.
2. Add olive oil and warm water; stir well with a wooden spoon.
3. Gradually add another 1 cup flour, stirring until dough forms a cohesive, elastic ball pulling away from bowl sides. Add additional flour as needed (up to ⅓ cup more).
4. Brush a clean bowl generously with olive oil. Form dough into a ball and transfer to oiled bowl, rolling to coat.
5. Cover tightly with plastic wrap and let rise in a warm place for 30 minutes or longer, until doubled. Preheat oven to 425°F during this time.
6. Gently deflate dough and transfer to floured surface. Knead briefly (3-5 times) until smooth.
7. Using hands or rolling pin, stretch dough into a 12-inch circle.
8. Transfer to parchment-lined pizza pan. Pinch or fold edges to form crust. Let rest, then stretch again to fit the pan.
9. Brush entire surface with olive oil using a pastry brush.
10. Poke holes across center with a fork to prevent bubbling.
11. Add desired toppings and bake at 425°F for 13-15 minutes until golden brown.
