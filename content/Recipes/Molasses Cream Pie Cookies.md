## Ingredients

**For the molasses cookies:**
- 4 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon baking soda
- 2 tablespoons ground ginger
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon cardamom
- Pinch of finely ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 12 ounces unsalted butter, room temperature
- 2 1/4 cups light brown sugar, packed
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon finely grated orange zest
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 2/3 cup molasses (not blackstrap)
- 1/2 cup sparkling sugar, for rolling

**For the white chocolate buttercream:**
- 1 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 2 1/2 cups confectioners' sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons heavy cream
- 6 ounces quality white chocolate, melted and cooled

## Directions

**Cookies:**
- Whisk together flour, baking soda, ginger, cinnamon, cloves, allspice, cardamom, black pepper, and salt.
- Beat butter and brown sugar until fluffy. Add vanilla, orange zest, then eggs individually. Mix in molasses, then dry ingredients.
- Refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Roll dough into 1 1/2-tablespoon balls, coat with sparkling sugar, place on sheets with 2-inch spacing.
- Bake 8-10 minutes until puffed and slightly cracked. Cool 5 minutes on pan, then transfer to racks completely.

**Buttercream:**
- Beat butter until smooth. Gradually add powdered sugar on low speed until fluffy.
- Add salt and cream, then melted white chocolate; beat smooth.
- Spread frosting on one cookie, top with another, press lightly together.
