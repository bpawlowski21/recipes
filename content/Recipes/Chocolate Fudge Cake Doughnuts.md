## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (43g) Dutch-process cocoa
- 1 1/3 cups (160g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 3/4 cup (160g) light brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon espresso powder, optional
- 1/2 cup (85g) chocolate chips
- 2 large eggs
- 1/3 cup (76g) milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon vinegar (white or cider)
- 8 tablespoons (113g) unsalted butter, melted

### Icing
- 1 cup (170g) chocolate chips
- 1/4 cup (57g) milk or half-and-half

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F.
- Whisk together cocoa, flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, espresso powder, and chocolate chips.
- In a separate bowl, whisk eggs, milk, vanilla, and vinegar together.
- Add wet ingredients to dry and stir until combined; add melted butter.
- Grease two doughnut pans; fill wells half to two-thirds full (about 64-70g batter per well).
- Bake 12-15 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
- After 30 seconds, loosen edges with a spatula; invert the pan over a rack and tap to release doughnuts.
- For the icing, heat chocolate chips and milk until steaming, then stir until smooth; dip doughnut tops to coat.
- Garnish as desired and serve.
