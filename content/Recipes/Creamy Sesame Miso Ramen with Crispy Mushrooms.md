## Ingredients

- 6 cups low sodium vegetable broth
- 1/4 cup low sodium soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1/4 cup tahini (sesame seed paste)
- 3 tablespoons white miso paste
- 2 tablespoons hot chili oil
- 1/4 cup dried porcini mushrooms (optional, but recommended)
- 1 inch fresh ginger, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 2-4 squares ramen noodles
- 4 cups shredded kale or baby spinach
- 4 tablespoons salted butter, melted, or olive oil
- 2 pounds mixed mushrooms, roughly torn
- 2 small shallots, sliced
- 2 tablespoons raw sesame seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 4-6 soft or hard boiled eggs, for serving
- Sliced carrots and green onions, for serving

## Directions

**Stove-Top Method**
1. Combine broth, 2 cups water, soy sauce, vinegar, tahini, miso, and chili oil in a large soup pot. Add dried mushrooms, ginger, and garlic. Simmer over medium-low heat for 20 minutes until fragrant.
2. Remove mushrooms. Bring soup to a boil. Stir in noodles, kale or spinach, and cilantro. Let sit 5 minutes until noodles soften.
3. Preheat oven to 425°F. Combine butter, mushrooms, shallots, sesame seeds, chili flakes, and salt on a baking sheet. Roast 15 minutes, stirring halfway. Broil 1-2 minutes until crisp.
4. Ladle soup into bowls. Top with crispy mushrooms, eggs, carrots, green onions, and additional chili oil.

**Instant Pot Method**
1. Combine all broth ingredients in the pot. Cook on high pressure 8 minutes.
2. Release steam. Remove mushrooms. Set to sauté. Add noodles, greens, and cilantro. Simmer 5 minutes.
3. Complete mushroom preparation as directed above.

**Slow Cooker Method**
1. Combine all broth ingredients. Cook on high 3-4 hours or low 6-8 hours.
2. Switch to high. Remove mushrooms. Add noodles, greens, and cilantro. Let sit 10-15 minutes.
3. Complete mushroom preparation as directed above.
