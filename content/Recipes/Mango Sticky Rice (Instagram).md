## Ingredients

**Rice**
- 210g glutinous rice
- 100ml coconut cream
- ½ tsp salt
- 600ml water

**Sauce**
- 100ml coconut cream
- 2 tbsp water
- 2–3 tbsp sugar
- ½ tbsp cornstarch
- Pinch of salt

**For serving**
- 1 mango
- Toasted sesame seeds

## Directions

1. Rinse the glutinous rice a few times and drain. Add water and salt. Cook over medium heat on the stove (about 20 minutes) or use a rice cooker.
2. Meanwhile, prepare the sauce: in a small heatproof bowl, mix together all the sauce ingredients. Mix well and microwave 40–45 seconds. Remove, mix again, and set aside.
3. Once the glutinous rice is cooked, add the 100ml coconut cream. Mix to combine and let it sit until the milk is absorbed. Meanwhile, slice the mango.
4. Serve the sticky rice together with the mango and pour the sauce on top. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and enjoy.
