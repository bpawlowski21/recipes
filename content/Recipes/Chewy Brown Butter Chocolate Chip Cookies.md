"Rich, chewy and golden brown chocolate chip cookies with nutty flavors from delicious brown butter! They are thick and a bit gooey inside with insanely crisp edges. Everyone who makes these cookies absolutely loves them!"

Prep Time: 10 mins · Cook Time: 10 mins · Chill Time: 2 hrs

## Ingredients

- 10 tablespoon (140g) salted butter
- 1½ cups (215g) all-purpose flour (measure accurately or the cookies will spread too much)
- ¾ teaspoon baking soda
- ¼ teaspoon salt (increase to ½ teaspoon if using unsalted butter)
- ⅔ cup (145g) packed light brown sugar
- ⅓ cup (65g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) milk
- 1 teaspoon (5ml) pure vanilla extract
- 3oz (85g) dark chocolate, coarsely chopped into chunks, plus extra for topping
- ½ cup (85g) semisweet chocolate chips
- Flaky sea salt (optional, for topping)

For best results, use a kitchen scale to weigh the ingredients.

## Directions

1. First brown the butter in a 1-quart stainless steel saucepan (8-10 minutes). Immediately pour into a large mixing bowl, then let cool for 20-30 minutes until just warmer than room temperature (32-34°C / 90-93°F). It should not be hot, but not too cool either or it will stiffen up when mixed with the other ingredients.
2. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl and whisk well to blend evenly.
3. Combine cooled brown butter with both sugars in a large bowl and mix gently with a spatula to combine — it should look thick like wet sand. (Can also use an electric handheld mixer, or a stand mixer with the paddle attachment on medium speed for 20 seconds.)
4. Add the egg, milk, and vanilla and mix in until well incorporated and creamy-looking. Do not beat vigorously at this stage — if it's cold in your house, the butterfat will firm up and become stiff.
5. Add the flour mixture and fold it in, or mix on low just until mostly combined, then add the chocolate chunks and chips before all of the flour is incorporated and continue mixing until evenly distributed. Cover the bowl and refrigerate the dough for 1-4 hours (in a pinch, chill for just 1 hour, but minimum 2 hours is best).
6. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line two large cookie sheets with parchment paper.
7. Use a 1.35-oz cookie scoop to portion dough into 14 pieces (about 2oz/56g each) and roll into smooth balls. Place onto prepared baking sheets, spacing 3 inches apart. Press a few extra chunks of chocolate on top if desired. Do not flatten. Bake for 8-10 minutes until golden on top, browned around the edges, and soft in the middle. (Can also make slightly smaller cookies using a 1¼-oz cookie scoop.)
8. Let cookies cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes before carefully transferring to a wire rack to finish cooling. Top with flaky sea salt if desired.

## Notes

- Recipe by Christina Marsigliese, Founder & Author of Scientifically Sweet.
- Wendy's handwritten adaptations:
  - Cool the browned butter sufficiently before mixing — if not, the cookies will turn out greasy.
  - Not necessary to chill the dough more than needed; you want a gooey center.
  - Max chill time used: 4 hours.
