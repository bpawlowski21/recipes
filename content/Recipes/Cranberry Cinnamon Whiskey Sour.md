## Ingredients

- 2 cups fresh cranberries
- 2 cups water
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 3/4 cup bourbon or whiskey
- 1/2 cup lemon juice
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- Ice cubes

## Directions

1. Combine cranberries, water, sugar, and cinnamon sticks in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to a simmer. Cook about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until cranberries burst and the mixture is smooth.
2. Strain the syrup through a fine mesh strainer into a bowl, discarding solids. Cool for at least 10 minutes.
3. Add 3/4 cup of the cranberry-cinnamon simple syrup, whiskey, lemon juice, orange juice, lime juice, and a large handful of ice to a cocktail shaker. Shake vigorously for 30 seconds.
4. Divide into 4 glasses and serve immediately.
