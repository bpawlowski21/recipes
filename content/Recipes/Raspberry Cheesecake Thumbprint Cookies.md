## Ingredients

- 4 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 8 tablespoons salted butter, at room temperature
- 1/2 cup + 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1 cup + 3 tablespoons flour
- 1/4 cup raspberry preserves

## Directions

- Beat cream cheese and butter until light and fluffy using a stand mixer with paddle attachment, scraping down sides as needed.
- Add sugar and beat well.
- Incorporate flour gradually on low speed until just combined.
- Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 45 minutes, up to 2 hours.
- Preheat oven to 375°F approximately 30 minutes before baking. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Using a cookie scoop, form 1-tablespoon-sized dough balls, roll into rounds, and place on the sheet.
- Gently press your thumb into the center of each round, then fill each indent with 1/2 teaspoon jam.
- Bake 11-12 minutes until golden at edges (cookies will still be soft).
- Cool on baking sheet for 10 minutes, then transfer carefully to a cooling rack to cool completely.
- Optional: add extra jam to indents for fuller appearance before serving.
