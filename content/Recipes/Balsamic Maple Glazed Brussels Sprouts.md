## Ingredients

**Brussels Sprouts**
- 1 large stalk Brussels sprouts
- 3 tbls olive oil
- 2 tbls maple syrup
- 1 tbls balsamic vinegar
- Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper

**Maple Mustard Dipping Sauce**
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 3 Tbsp grainy mustard (German-style used)
- 4 tsp maple syrup
- ½ tsp apple cider vinegar
- Salt and fresh cracked black pepper

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400F.
2. Rinse and dry the stalk and place on a large baking sheet lined with parchment.
3. Combine the oil, maple syrup, and balsamic vinegar in a small sauce pot over medium-low heat and heat through, about 2-3 minutes.
4. Pour the mixture evenly over the Brussels sprouts stalk, turning well to coat each side. Season the stalk generously with salt and pepper.
5. Roast for about 30 minutes, turning the stalk on the baking sheet every 10 minutes. Brussels sprouts should be a dark golden brown and knife tender.
6. Serve with the dipping sauce.

**Maple Mustard Dipping Sauce:**
- Combine everything in a bowl and stir well to combine.

## Notes

- This recipe roasts the Brussels sprouts on the whole stalk rather than as individual florets.
