Emailed to Wendy Nash by Linda Nash (LN Marketing), December 17, 2005. "Probably the most popular hors d'oeuvres in the book."

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs ground meat
- 1 slightly beaten egg
- 1 large grated onion
- Salt to taste
- 1 twelve-oz. bottle chili sauce
- 1 ten-oz jar grape jelly
- Juice of 1 lemon

## Directions

1. Mix ground meat, egg, onion, and salt; shape into small balls.
2. Drop into a sauce of chili sauce, grape jelly, and lemon juice.
3. Simmer until brown. Refrigerate or freeze. To serve, bring to room temperature. Reheat in chafing dish and serve with cocktail picks.
