## Ingredients

**Cauliflower & Breading:**
- 1 medium head cauliflower
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup plain, unsweetened almond milk
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon onion powder
- 2 cups panko breadcrumbs
- Optional garnishes: sesame seeds, sliced green onion, cilantro

**Teriyaki Sauce:**
- ⅔ cup water
- ⅔ cup tamari (low sodium)
- ½ cup pure maple syrup
- ½ cup toasted sesame oil
- 1 large garlic clove, finely grated
- 1½ teaspoons finely grated ginger
- 2½ teaspoons cornstarch

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 450°F. Cut cauliflower into small to medium florets for maximum crispiness.
- In a large bowl, whisk flour, onion powder, garlic powder, and salt with almond milk. Coat cauliflower thoroughly.
- Add panko breadcrumbs and toss until fully coated.
- Arrange on a parchment-lined baking sheet, spacing pieces apart. Bake 20 minutes until crispy and browning.
- While baking, whisk sauce ingredients in a bowl, then simmer in a saucepan for 7–10 minutes until thickened.
- Toss baked cauliflower with about ¾ of the sauce. Return to oven for 10 minutes.
- Serve over rice if desired, drizzle with remaining sauce, and garnish with sesame seeds and green onions.
