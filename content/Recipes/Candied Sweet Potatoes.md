## Ingredients

- 3 pound large sweet potatoes, peeled and halved crosswise (Wendy's note: use 5 lbs sweets, cut into 1" cubes)
- 1 cup packed light brown sugar (Wendy's note: double the sauce)
- 1/2 stick unsalted butter
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 to 1/3 cup bourbon

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F.
- Cut each potato half lengthwise into fourths (Wendy's note: 16ths, if cut into cubes — no need to steam). Transfer to a buttered 3-quart shallow baking dish.
- Simmer brown sugar, butter, water, and salt, stirring occasionally, until sugar is dissolved and syrup is thickened, about 5 minutes. Stir in bourbon to taste. Drizzle syrup over potatoes and bake in middle of oven, basting occasionally, until syrup is thickened, about 1 1/4 hours.

## Notes

- Cooks' note: sweet potatoes may be made 1 day ahead and chilled, covered. Reheat before serving.
- Wendy's note: everyone's favorite each year.
- 11.27.2024 (Wendy's handwriting): used purple sweet potatoes — they shrunk a lot and the sauce was so thick it was almost like caramel. Make 3x the sauce for 5 lbs of purple sweet potatoes.
