## Ingredients

- 14-ounce package extra-firm tofu
- Neutral oil for roasting (safflower recommended)
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 3/4 to 1 pound eggplant
- Kosher salt
- 3 to 4 tablespoons unsalted butter or vegan butter
- 1 heaped cup thinly sliced shallots or 1 medium white or red onion, thinly sliced
- 5 garlic cloves, peeled and crushed
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped ginger
- 1/2 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon granulated or brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon crushed or very coarsely ground black pepper, plus more to taste
- Rice, for serving
- Chile-garlic sauce, crispy chili oil, or sriracha for serving (optional)

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Drain tofu and wrap in paper towels for 5 minutes. Drizzle 3 tablespoons oil on the largest baking sheet and place in oven to preheat.
2. Trim eggplant and cut into 1-inch pieces. Toss with 1 tablespoon oil and salt in a large bowl. Remove hot pan from oven and spread eggplant over half to 2/3 of the pan.
3. Cut tofu into 1-inch cubes. Toss gently with cornstarch and salt until coated. Spread on the empty part of the sheet.
4. Roast for 20 minutes. Flip tofu and eggplant using a thin spatula. Roast another 10 minutes until tofu is crisp and eggplant is tender (total 30 minutes; add 5 more if needed).
5. While roasting, heat a large heavy skillet over medium heat and add butter. Once melted, add shallots, garlic, and ginger. Reduce heat slightly and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender (11-14 minutes).
6. Add soy sauce, sugar, and black pepper. Simmer 3 minutes more.
7. Add roasted tofu and eggplant to the sauce. Stir to coat and cook 1-2 minutes together.
8. Serve over or with rice, with extra heat as desired.
