## Ingredients

**To begin your sourdough starter:**
- 50g (7 tablespoons) King Arthur Whole Wheat Flour or 50g (generous 1/2 cup) King Arthur Pumpernickel Flour
- 50g (scant 1/4 cup) water, cool

**To feed your sourdough starter:**
- 50g (about 6 tablespoons) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 50g (scant 1/4 cup) water

## Directions

- **Day 1:** combine flour and water in a non-reactive container (glass, crockery, stainless steel, or food-grade plastic). Stir until no dry patches remain. Cover loosely, let sit at warm room temperature (~70°F) for 24 hours.
- **Day 2:** transfer starter to a bowl. Measure 50g starter back into the empty container, discard the remainder. Add 50g water and 50g all-purpose flour, mix well, cover, rest 24 hours.
- **Day 3:** begin twice-daily feedings ~12 hours apart. Measure 50g starter into container, discard remainder, add 50g water and 50g flour, mix, cover, rest ~12 hours before repeating.
- **Days 4-5:** continue twice-daily feedings. By day five the starter should double within 6-8 hours with abundant bubbles and a tangy aroma, indicating it's ready to bake with.

## Notes

- Key tip: weighing ingredients on a scale is most accurate and consistent when working with sourdough starter.
