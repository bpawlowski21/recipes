## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp unsalted butter, 28 g
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 cup vegetable broth, 236 mL
- 4 oz cream cheese, 113 g
- 2 15-oz cans butter beans, 425 g each
- 1/2 cup shredded parmesan cheese, 40 g

## Directions

1. Flavor Base: In a large saute pan or medium pot, melt 2 Tbsp unsalted butter over medium heat. Add 4 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tsp Italian seasoning, 1/2 tsp ground black pepper, and 1/2 tsp smoked paprika. Cook until garlic is a bit soft and fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes.
2. Simmer: Stir in 1 cup vegetable broth and 4 oz cream cheese, stirring until cream cheese is fully melted in. Stir in 2 15-oz cans butter beans (drained). Let gently simmer uncovered for 10 to 15 minutes, or until the sauce thickens.
3. Serve: Stir in 1/2 cup shredded parmesan cheese and serve warm.

## Notes

- Prep 5 mins, cook 15 mins, total 20 mins. Gluten-free, vegetarian; works as a main or side dish.
- Spicier: add red pepper flakes or a pinch of cayenne pepper when sautéing the garlic and seasonings.
- Dried beans: soak and cook dried butter beans ahead of time — you'll need about 3 to 3 1/2 cups of cooked beans total.
- Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for 3-4 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop over low heat, adding a splash of vegetable broth or water if needed to make the sauce creamy.
- Dairy-free: swap butter and cream cheese for plant-based butter and vegan cream cheese (or canned coconut milk), and use vegan parmesan.
- Add greens: stir in fresh spinach or kale at the end.
