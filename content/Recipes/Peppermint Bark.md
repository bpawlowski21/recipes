## Ingredients

- 2 2/3 cups (454g) bittersweet chocolate, chopped
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract (or 3 to 5 drops peppermint oil)
- 2 cups (340g) white chocolate, chopped
- 1/4 cup (39g) peppermint crunch or crushed hard peppermint candies

## Directions

- Gently melt the dark chocolate on very low heat or over hot water until it barely melts.
- Stir in the peppermint extract, then spread into an 8" x 12" oval on parchment paper or foil.
- Allow chocolate to set, but not harden completely.
- Melt the white chocolate (tempering recommended for best results).
- Mix melted white chocolate with approximately half the peppermint crunch and spread over the dark chocolate layer.
- Sprinkle remaining peppermint crunch on top, pressing gently.
- Cool until hardened, then break or cut into chunks.
- Store airtight at room temperature for up to one week; freeze for extended storage.
