## Ingredients

**Levain:**
- 44g high-protein white flour
- 44g water
- 44g ripe sourdough starter, 100% hydration

**Main dough:**
- 324g high-protein white flour
- 92g unsalted butter
- 88g whole milk
- 51g egg, beaten
- 18g superfine sugar
- 88g water
- 7g fine sea salt

**Filling:**
- 110g granulated sugar
- 75g unsalted butter
- 70g Valrhona Caraibe 66% baking chocolate
- 25g Valrhona Dutch-processed cocoa powder

**Topping (egg wash):**
- 1 whole egg
- 1 tablespoon whole milk
- Pinch of salt

## Directions

- **Prepare levain** (9:00 a.m.): mix ingredients, ripen 3 hours at 74-76°F.
- **Mix dough** (12:00 p.m.): cut butter into pats to warm. Combine flour, water, milk, egg, sugar, salt, and levain in a stand mixer; mix on low 1-2 minutes, then medium 5-7 minutes. Rest 10 minutes, mix on low 2-4 minutes. Gradually add butter pats over 5-8 minutes. Transfer to bulk container.
- **Bulk fermentation** (12:30-4:30 p.m.): one set of slap-and-folds after one hour; continue fermenting ~4 hours total until puffy.
- **Chill overnight:** refrigerate at least 1 hour or overnight.
- **Shape** (9:00 a.m.): warm dough 30 minutes. Make filling: melt butter, add sugar and chocolate, stir until smooth, whisk in cocoa powder. Roll dough to 12x17 inches, spread filling on half, fold over. Cut into twelve 1-inch strips; cut each strip halfway lengthwise (leaving one end connected), twist, coil into a knot. Place on parchment-lined pans.
- **Proof** (3.5 hours): rise at 74-76°F until very soft and marshmallow-like.
- **Bake:** preheat oven to 375°F with racks in top and bottom thirds. Egg wash. Bake 20 minutes (rotating halfway), reduce to 350°F, bake 10-12 more minutes until light golden. Cool 10 minutes before serving.
