## Ingredients

- 4 salmon filets, skin on or off
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tablespoons salted butter
- 2 small shallots, sliced or smashed
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh sage
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 1/4 cups canned full-fat coconut milk or heavy cream
- 2 ounces cream cheese, cubed
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan or manchego cheese
- 4 cups fresh baby spinach
- 1 (12 ounce) jar marinated artichokes, roughly chopped
- Juice from 1/2 a lemon
- Flaky sea salt for sprinkling

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Season salmon with paprika, salt, and pepper.
- Optional: sear salmon in hot olive oil for 2-3 minutes per side. Remove from skillet.
- In same oven-safe skillet, add butter, shallots, and garlic. Cook 2 minutes until fragrant. Add sage and cook 2-3 more minutes until butter browns and garlic caramelizes. Stir in red pepper flakes, coconut milk, and cream cheese. Season with salt and pepper, stirring until smooth. Add parmesan, spinach, and artichokes; cook until spinach wilts (3-5 minutes). Stir in lemon juice. Remove from heat.
- Place salmon in sauce and transfer to oven. Bake 10-15 minutes until cooked through.
- Plate salmon and spoon sauce over top. Garnish with flaky sea salt.
