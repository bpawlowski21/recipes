## Ingredients

**Soup**
- ½ Kent pumpkin, roughly diced/peeled
- 2 celery sticks
- 1 potato, chopped/peeled
- 1 leek, sliced (washed well first)
- 1 cup red lentils
- 1 tbsp vegan stock powder
- 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 tsp miso paste
- Enough boiling water to just cover (add more as needed after cooking)

**Tofu**
- ½ block extra firm tofu, diced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp water
- 1 tsp each garlic powder and smoked paprika
- Pepper

## Directions

1. Add all the soup ingredients to a large pot. Simmer together for around 15–20 minutes until the veggies are soft, then blend until smooth.
2. For the tofu: mix together the soy sauce, maple syrup, water, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and pepper.
3. Fry the diced tofu until golden, then toss through the seasonings and cook, stirring, until all the liquid has absorbed.
