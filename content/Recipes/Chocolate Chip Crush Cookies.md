## Ingredients

- 1 cup cold butter, cut into small cubes
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 1/2 cups cake flour (can substitute all-purpose)
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 cups chocolate chips
- 2 cups walnuts, roughly chopped

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 410°F.
- Cream together cold cubed butter, brown sugar, and sugar for 4 minutes until creamy.
- Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
- Stir in flours, cornstarch, baking soda, and salt until just combined. Fold in chocolate chips and walnuts.
- Form dough into large balls (4 per baking sheet).
- Bake 9-12 minutes until golden brown.
- Let rest at least 10-15 minutes before serving.
