## Ingredients

- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, melted until browned and cooled to room temperature
- 2 1/3 cups all-purpose flour (plus more if needed, one tablespoon at a time)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 cup + 2 tablespoons dark brown sugar, packed
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 12 ounces semi-sweet chocolate, roughly chopped

## Directions

- Prepare the brown butter: melt butter in a small saucepan over medium-high heat, stirring constantly for 3-4 minutes until golden brown with a nutty aroma. Pour into a heatproof bowl, scraping all browned bits. Refrigerate for 1 hour until solid but soft enough to leave an indentation when pressed.
- Preheat oven to 375°F.
- Whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl.
- In a large bowl, cream both sugars, vanilla, and cooled brown butter on medium speed for 2 minutes until light and fluffy.
- Add eggs one at a time, beating 15 seconds after each addition.
- Gently fold in flour mixture using a wooden spoon until just combined.
- Fold in chocolate chunks.
- Roll 3-tablespoon dough portions into balls and place on baking sheets with adequate spacing.
- Bake 9-10 minutes until golden at edges but soft in center.
- Cool on baking sheet for 10 minutes before transferring to cooling rack.
