## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons salted butter
- 1 sweet onion, chopped
- 3 carrots, chopped
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 6-7 cups low sodium vegetable broth
- 3 cups fresh or frozen broccoli florets
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh sage
- 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese

## Directions

- Heat olive oil and butter in a large soup pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook 3-5 minutes. Add carrots and thyme; season with salt and pepper. Cook another 5 minutes.
- Pour in broth. Add broccoli, bay leaves, nutmeg, and cayenne. Bring to boil over high heat, cover, and cook 10-15 minutes until broccoli is tender.
- Meanwhile, whisk together flour, baking powder, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl. Add buttermilk, parmesan, and sage; mix until just combined.
- Remove bay leaves. Transfer 3/4 of soup to blender and blend until broccoli is finely chopped. Return to pot and bring to boil over medium-high heat.
- Drop heaping tablespoons of dough into soup (about 6 at a time). Cover and cook 8-10 minutes until dumplings are cooked through and puffy.
- Move dumplings aside and stir in milk and cheddar cheese until melted. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
- Ladle soup and dumplings into bowls and serve topped with pepper.
