## Ingredients

**Tofu**
- 300g firm tofu
- 1 tbsp cornflour

**Glaze**
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- ½ orange, juiced
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 thumb ginger, grated
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp cornflour
- 3 tbsp water

**For the bowl**
- Spring onion
- Sesame seeds
- Red chilli
- Sticky rice
- Tenderstem broccoli

## Directions

1. Tear the tofu into nugget-sized pieces, then transfer to a bowl with the cornflour, salt, and pepper. Coat well, then spread out evenly on a baking sheet, drizzle with oil, and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes, or air fry 10 minutes, until crispy.
2. While the tofu cooks, make the sauce by adding all the glaze ingredients to a small jar and shaking to combine.
3. When the tofu has about 10 minutes remaining, spread the broccoli onto a baking sheet, drizzle with oil and salt, and bake 10 minutes.
4. Pour the sauce into a pan over medium heat and wait until bubbling and starting to thicken. Add the crispy tofu, spring onion, sesame seeds, and chilli, and stir to coat.
5. Serve the sticky tofu with rice and roasted broccoli. Enjoy!
