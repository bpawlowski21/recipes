"You will love this quick teriyaki tofu with crispy tofu pieces, mouthwatering homemade teriyaki sauce, fresh crisp vegetables, and warm, sticky rice."

Prep Time: 5 mins · Cook Time: 25 mins · Total Time: 30 mins · Calories: 519

## Ingredients

- 16 oz block extra firm tofu, drained + pressed
- 1 tbsp corn starch
- 1 cup uncooked basmati rice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 12 oz stir fried vegetables (includes broccoli, carrots, and pea pods)
- 1 medium red pepper, sliced into strips

**Homemade Teriyaki Sauce**
- 1 tbsp corn starch + 2 tbsp water, mixed in a small bowl
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp water
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- ½ tsp ginger, minced

**Optional Toppings:** sesame seeds, green onion, diced

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Cut tofu into small 1 inch cubes.
3. In a large resealable plastic bag, add tofu cubes and cornstarch. Gently shake until combined.
4. On a large parchment lined baking sheet, evenly spread out tofu in a single layer. Bake for 24-28 minutes, flipping halfway through, until crisp with a light golden brown outside. (Alternatively, air fry instead of bake at 400°F for 12-16 minutes, shaking halfway, until crisp and lightly golden brown.)
5. While tofu is baking, make rice and vegetables. Cook rice to package instructions.
6. Stir fry vegetables in a large pan with 1 tbsp of olive oil (optional) over medium heat. Pan fry for a few minutes and then add in 1-2 tbsp of water, cover, and steam for a few minutes. Cook until veggies are crisp and not overcooked.

**Homemade Teriyaki Sauce**
1. In a small bowl whisk together cornstarch with 2 tbsp of water and let sit for 1 minute.
2. Meanwhile, in a small saucepan, add in soy sauce, maple syrup, water, rice vinegar, garlic, and ginger. Cook over medium heat until it begins to low boil.
3. Turn heat down to low and add in the cornstarch water mixture. Stir to combine.
4. Cook for a few minutes, stirring frequently, until it begins to thicken. Remove from the heat once it starts to thicken — it will continue to thicken even when taken off the heat.

**Arrange Stir Fry**
1. In a large bowl, gently mix tofu with teriyaki marinade.
2. Arrange stir fry bowl with rice, then veggies, and top with tofu. Sprinkle with green onion (sliced) and/or sesame seeds. Enjoy!

## Notes

- Don't overcook the homemade teriyaki sauce — once you add the cornstarch it will start to thicken within a minute; remove from heat and continue to stir for a couple minutes, it will continue to thicken even off heat. If overcooked it will become gloopy.
- To make crispy tofu, make sure to press the tofu long enough (at least 15 minutes), evenly coat in cornstarch, and cook on high heat.
- Using pre-cut stir fry veggies saves a lot of time.
- Store in an airtight container for up to 4 days — the tofu won't be very crispy after the first day due to moisture accumulation, but you can pan fry it again.
- Substitutions: soy sauce → coconut aminos or tamari for gluten-free; veggies → green beans, baby corn, bok choy, onions, and more; oil → sesame oil, avocado oil, or another type of oil.
