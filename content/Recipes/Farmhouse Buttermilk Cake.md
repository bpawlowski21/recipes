## Ingredients

**Cake:**
- 8 tablespoons (113g) unsalted butter, at room temperature, at least 65°F
- 2 cups (425g) light brown sugar, packed
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 2 cups (454g) buttermilk, at room temperature
- 2 teaspoons King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon table salt
- 3 cups (360g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour

**Topping:**
- 6 tablespoons (85g) butter, melted
- 1 cup (213g) light brown sugar, packed
- 1/4 cup (57g) milk
- 1/8 teaspoon table salt
- 2/3 to 1 cup (74g to 113g) pecans, diced

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F and lightly grease a 9" x 13" cake pan.
- Beat butter and brown sugar until smooth.
- Add eggs, beating until smooth.
- Stir in buttermilk and vanilla extract.
- Add baking soda, salt, and flour to wet ingredients; beat until thoroughly combined.
- Pour batter into prepared pan.
- Bake 30-35 minutes until top feels set and cake is golden brown.
- While baking, combine melted butter, sugar, milk, salt, and pecans for topping.
- Spread topping evenly over baked cake; return to oven for 10 minutes.
- Remove from oven. Serve warm with runny glaze, or cool at room temperature for a few hours until glaze sets.
