## Ingredients

- 2 butternut squash, cut in half lengthwise
- 1 head garlic, cut in half horizontally
- 1 jalapeño, seeded
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- ½ bunch cilantro
- 16 leaves mint
- 15 oz unsweetened coconut milk
- 2 cups water
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 1 tsp sugar or maple syrup
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- Optional garnish: red pepper flakes

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. On a sheet pan, place the halved squash. Drizzle with oil, season with a pinch of salt and smoked paprika. Nestle the halved garlic and halved jalapeño into the hollows of the squash. Bake 45 minutes until the squash is fork tender.
3. Let cool enough to handle, then scoop or cut the squash out of the skin and squeeze the garlic from its skin. Place both in a large pot with all the other ingredients.
4. Blend with an immersion blender, or place all ingredients in a high-speed blender (be careful blending hot ingredients — keep a hand on the cover to avoid the lid blowing off from steam buildup).
5. Once pureed, put on heat and warm through. Add lime juice, then taste for salt and add more if desired.
6. Enjoy for 4–5 days for best quality.
