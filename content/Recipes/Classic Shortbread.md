## Ingredients

- 1 cup (230 grams or 8 ounces) unsalted butter, cut into chunks
- 2/3 cup (80 grams) powdered sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
- Flavoring of choice (1 teaspoon vanilla bean paste or extract recommended)
- 2 1/4 cups (295 grams) all-purpose flour

## Directions

1. **Preparation:** Heat oven to 300°F. Line an 8-inch square or 9-inch round cake pan with parchment paper.
2. **Mixing:** Using a stand mixer, beat butter, sugar, and salt until soft. Add flavoring and flour, mixing until combined. Alternatively, use a food processor: combine sugar, salt, and flour; pulse several times. Add flavoring and butter; pulse to break down butter into small pieces, then run machine until fully incorporated (1-2 minutes).
3. **Shaping:** Press dough evenly into prepared pan. Smooth the top with an offset spatula.
4. **First bake:** Bake 20 minutes, then remove from oven (leave oven on).
5. **Scoring:** Using the back of a wooden skewer, indent surface lines. Cut along these lines with a sharp paring knife to the pan bottom. Dock cookies by poking holes about 1/3 deep.
6. **Final bake:** Return to oven for 25-35 minutes until edges are deep golden but tops remain mostly pale.
7. **Cooling:** Cool in pan, or remove after 10 minutes.
