## Ingredients

**Bread**
- 2 medium zucchini, grated (about 2 cups before squeezing out water)
- 1 stick (1/2 cup) melted coconut oil or melted butter
- 1/2 cup maple syrup
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 cups whole wheat pastry flour or all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 cup semi-sweet or dark chocolate chunks
- 1/2 cup creamy almond butter

**Maple Butter**
- 4 tablespoons salted butter, at room temperature
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5 inch bread pan.
2. Spread grated zucchini on a clean towel, sprinkle with 1/2 teaspoon salt, cover with another towel, and let sit 10 minutes. Squeeze out excess water.
3. In a large bowl, combine melted oil/butter, maple syrup, eggs, and vanilla. Mix in the squeezed zucchini. Add flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt, stirring until just combined. Fold in chocolate chunks. Dollop almond butter into batter in teaspoon-sized amounts and gently swirl without overmixing.
4. Pour batter into prepared pan. Bake 50-60 minutes until center is just set. Cover with foil if browning too quickly.
5. While baking, combine butter and maple syrup in a small bowl for the maple butter topping.
6. Cool for at least 30 minutes before cutting, or enjoy warm with maple butter spread on top.
