## Ingredients

**Soup**
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 cauliflower, diced
- 1L vegan chicken-style stock
- 1 cup water
- Pepper
- Pinch nutmeg
- 2 tins butter beans, drained and rinsed
- Juice of ½ lemon

**Tempeh Bacon**
- 300g block tempeh, diced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Dash liquid smoke
- Pepper

## Directions

1. Fry the diced onion and garlic until soft.
2. Add the cauliflower, stock, water, pepper, and nutmeg. Simmer with the lid on until the cauliflower is soft.
3. Add the drained butter beans and lemon juice, then blitz until smooth.
4. For the tempeh bacon: toss the diced tempeh with the soy sauce, maple syrup, smoked paprika, garlic powder, liquid smoke, and pepper.
5. Transfer to an air fryer or oven tray, reserving any leftover marinade. Bake at 200°C for 20 minutes or until crispy, then toss through the leftover marinade.
6. Serve the soup topped with the tempeh, pepper, and crusty bread.
