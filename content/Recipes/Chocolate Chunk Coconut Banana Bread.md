## Ingredients

- 4 medium overly ripe bananas, mashed (about 1 1/4 cup mashed)
- 1/4 cup melted coconut oil
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups whole wheat pastry flour or all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 cup semi-sweet or dark chocolate chunks

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5 inch bread pan.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine mashed bananas, coconut oil, honey, eggs, and vanilla until well mixed.
3. Add flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt; stir until just combined.
4. Fold in chocolate chunks.
5. Pour batter into prepared bread pan.
6. Bake for 50-60 minutes until center is just set.
7. Remove from oven and cool for at least 30 minutes before slicing (or enjoy warm with butter and honey).

Covered bread keeps 3-5 days.
