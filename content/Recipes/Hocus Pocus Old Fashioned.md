## Ingredients

**Honey Syrup**
- 1/2 cup honey
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 cinnamon sticks (or 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon)
- Star anise (optional)

**Old Fashioned**
- 2 ounces (1/4 cup) bourbon
- 1 ounce (2 tablespoons) lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon apple butter or pumpkin butter
- 1-2 dashes orange bitters
- 1-2 ounces (2-4 tablespoons) honey syrup
- Ginger beer, for topping (optional)

**Spiced Cinnamon Sugar Rim**
- 1 tablespoon coarse sugar
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper

## Directions

- Heat 1/2 cup water with honey, rosemary, cinnamon, and star anise to a boil for 2-3 minutes; cool completely.
- Combine coarse sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, and cayenne on a plate. Rub the glass rim with a lemon wedge and dip into the sugar mixture.
- Shake honey syrup, bourbon, lemon juice, apple/pumpkin butter, and bitters with ice; strain into the rimmed glass.
- Finish with cinnamon sticks. Top with ginger beer if desired.
- Mocktail: omit bourbon; substitute 1 tablespoon apple cider or sparkling apple cider plus 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract.
