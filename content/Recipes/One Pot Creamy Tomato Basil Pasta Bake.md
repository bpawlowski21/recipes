## Ingredients

- 1 pound dry fettuccine or other long cut pasta
- 1 tablespoon salted butter
- 1 cup basil pesto, homemade or store-bought
- 1 jar (28 ounce) marinara sauce
- 1/3 cup red enchilada sauce
- 1/2 cup oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, oil drained and chopped
- 4 ounces goat cheese, crumbled
- 1 cup cubed fontina cheese
- 1/2 cup canned full fat coconut milk or heavy cream
- Kosher salt and pepper to taste
- 6 ounces fresh mozzarella cheese, torn
- 2 cups fresh cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F.
- Boil a large pot of salted water and cook pasta until al dente per package directions. Drain and return pasta to the hot pot.
- Add butter, pesto, marinara, enchilada sauce, sun-dried tomatoes, goat cheese, fontina, and milk/cream. Season with salt and pepper. Toss gently until creamy, about 3-5 minutes. Transfer to a baking dish if pot isn't oven-safe. Top with mozzarella.
- Bake 10-15 minutes, then broil 1-2 minutes until cheese is golden and bubbly.
- Remove from oven and let rest 5 minutes. Top with fresh tomatoes and basil before serving.
