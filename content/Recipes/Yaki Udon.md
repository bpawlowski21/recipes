Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. "This Japanese noodle stir-fry is one of my favorite quick dinners!" Prep time: 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

- About 200g veggies of choice (post used cabbage, red pepper, and carrot)
- 1 small onion
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 serving udon noodles
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce or vegan oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp white wine vinegar
- Salt to taste
- A touch of water
- 65g cubed tofu

## Directions

1. Chop the veggies, onion, and garlic, and sauté 4–5 minutes.
2. Cook the udon noodles according to package instructions.
3. Mix the soy sauce, hoisin sauce, sugar, vinegar, salt, and water.
4. Fry the tofu a few minutes.
5. Add the noodles and sauce, toss, and serve.
