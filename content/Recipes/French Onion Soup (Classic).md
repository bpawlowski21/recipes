Saved from Apple Notes ("ONION SOUP"), photographed from an unidentified cookbook, p.10 ("Soups and Salads" chapter) — a second, different French Onion Soup recipe alongside the Josh Weissman version also saved in the same note.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons (¼ stick) unsalted butter
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
- 3½ pounds onions, thinly sliced
- 2 cups dry white wine
- 6 cups chicken stock or canned low-salt broth (handwritten note on the card: substitute vegetable stock/broth)
- 12 half-inch-thick French bread baguette slices, toasted
- 1 cup grated Gruyère cheese (about 4 ounces)

## Directions

1. Melt butter with oil in a heavy large pot over medium heat. Add onions, cover, and cook until lightly colored, stirring occasionally, about 45 minutes.
2. Add wine and bring to a boil, scraping up any browned bits. Cook 5 minutes.
3. Add stock and bring to a simmer. Simmer uncovered 1½ hours. Season to taste with salt and pepper. (Can be prepared 1 day ahead — cover and refrigerate, then bring to a simmer before continuing with the recipe.)
4. Preheat broiler. Ladle soup into broilerproof bowls. Top with slices of toasted French bread. Sprinkle with grated Gruyère cheese. Broil until cheese melts. Serve immediately.
