## Ingredients

- Sweet potatoes (one per person)
- 4 cups boiled chickpeas/garbanzo beans
- 1.5 cups halved cherry tomatoes
- 2 large handfuls spinach leaves
- 1–2 cloves garlic, finely diced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Olive oil
- Salt, pepper to taste

**Topping**
- Red onion, thinly sliced
- Lemon juice
- Salt

**Dressing**
- ¼ cup tahini
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Water, as needed

## Directions

1. Pierce the sweet potatoes with a fork. Bake in the oven at 200°C for 30–45 minutes, until sweet caramelized juices come out and the skin loosens. (Time depends on the oven and the size of the potatoes.)
2. For the filling, sauté garlic in olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the halved cherry tomatoes and roast together with the spices.
3. Add the spinach leaves and chickpeas. Sauté until the spinach is wilted.
4. For the tahini sauce, blend tahini with lemon juice and salt. Add just enough water to keep the blade going — add more water if too firm, more tahini if too liquidy.
5. For the pink onions, finely slice and marinate in salt and lemon juice.
6. Cut the baked sweet potatoes in half. Fill with the chickpea-spinach mixture. Top with red onion. Drizzle with tahini dressing.
