## Ingredients

**Filling**
- 2 spring onions, chopped
- 2 carrots, shredded
- ¼ white cabbage, shredded
- 7 oz mushrooms
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 1 block tofu (7oz)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp corn starch
- Rice paper wrappers

**Dipping Sauce**
- 2 tbsp chili flakes
- 1 tbsp Sichuan pepper
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 2 tbsp spring onion, chopped
- Hot oil (poured over the above)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar

## Directions

1. In a large pan, sauté the spring onions, carrots, cabbage, mushrooms, ginger, and tofu in some oil for a few minutes until the veggies start to soften.
2. Add the soy sauce, rice vinegar, and corn starch. Stir and set aside.
3. Meanwhile, prepare the dipping sauce: to a heatproof bowl add the chili flakes, Sichuan pepper, sesame seeds, garlic, and spring onion. Pour hot oil over it, then add the soy sauce and rice vinegar.
4. To prepare the dumplings, dip a rice paper wrapper in warm water for a few seconds, then place on a damp surface to fill and fold.
5. Add the dumpling onto another rice paper sheet and fold again (double-wrapped). Pan fry the dumplings on each side until golden brown and enjoy.
