## Ingredients

**Mashed Potatoes:**
- 3 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, partially peeled (thoroughly washed)
- 3–4 Tbsp vegan butter
- Sea salt and black pepper, to taste

**Filling:**
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste (optional)
- Sea salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1½ cups uncooked brown or green lentils, rinsed and drained
- 4 cups vegetable stock
- 2 tsp fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried thyme)
- 1 10-ounce bag frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, green beans, and corn)

## Directions

- Slice large potatoes in half, place in a pot with water until covered. Bring to a low boil on medium-high heat, salt generously, cover and cook 20–30 minutes until they slide off a knife easily.
- Drain potatoes, return to pot to evaporate remaining water, then transfer to a mixing bowl. Mash until smooth. Add vegan butter and season with salt and pepper. Cover loosely and set aside.
- Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C) and lightly grease a 2-quart baking dish.
- In a large saucepan over medium heat, sauté onions and garlic in olive oil until lightly browned, about 5 minutes.
- Add tomato paste and a pinch each of salt and pepper. Add lentils, stock, and thyme; stir. Bring to a low boil, cover, reduce heat to simmer. Cook until lentils are tender, 35–40 minutes. Remove lid and continue simmering uncovered, stirring frequently, to evaporate excess liquid.
- In the last 10 minutes of cooking, add frozen vegetables, stir, and cover to meld flavors.
- Optionally thicken the mixture by stirring in 2–3 Tbsp mashed potatoes, or whisk half the mixture with 2 Tbsp cornstarch/arrowroot and return to the pan.
- Taste and adjust seasonings. Transfer to the baking dish and carefully top with mashed potatoes. Smooth with a spoon or fork, season with pepper and salt.
- Place on a baking sheet to catch overflow. Bake 10–15 minutes until potatoes are lightly browned on top.
- Let cool briefly before serving. Store covered in the fridge for up to several days; reheats well in the microwave.
