## Ingredients

- 600g extra firm tofu
- 3 cups broccoli, diced
- 4 cups cooked rice
- 1 spring onion, diced
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1.5 cups orange juice
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tbsp tamari (sodium reduced)
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1.5 tbsp tapioca starch + 3 tbsp water

## Directions

1. Bake the tofu in the air fryer at 180°C for 15 minutes (or 20 minutes at 200°C in the oven).
2. Bake the broccoli for 8 minutes at 180°C (15 minutes in the oven).
3. Combine the orange juice, ginger, garlic, tamari, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and maple syrup.
4. Combine the tapioca starch and water to make a slurry.
5. In a pan on medium heat, add the sauce and slurry. Cook until it starts to thicken.
6. Stir in the tofu and broccoli.
7. Serve with rice, spring onions, and sesame seeds.
