## Ingredients

### Chocolate Cookie Crust
- 1 regular full-size package Oreo cookies, crushed into fine crumbs (36 sandwich cookies)
- 8 tablespoons (4 ounces) unsalted butter, melted

### Chocolate Cheesecake Filling
- 12 ounces bittersweet chocolate, coarsely chopped
- 1.5 pounds (24 ounces) full-fat cream cheese, room temperature
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup light brown sugar, packed
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened natural cocoa powder
- 4 large eggs plus 2 large egg yolks, room temperature
- 1 cup heavy cream, room temperature
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

### Chocolate Ganache (optional)
- 16 ounces semi-sweet chocolate, finely chopped
- 1.25 cups heavy cream

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 9x13-inch pan with foil, allowing 2-inch overhangs, and spray with nonstick spray.
- Combine cookie crumbs with melted butter; press firmly into pan bottom and slightly up sides.
- Bake crust 10 minutes. Remove and cool slightly. Reduce oven to 325°F.
- Melt chopped chocolate over a double boiler; set aside.
- In a food processor, blend cream cheese until smooth. Add both sugars and cocoa powder; beat until smooth.
- Add eggs, yolks, and heavy cream; mix on low for 20 seconds. Fold in melted chocolate and vanilla.
- Pour filling over crust and bake 35 minutes until center is set but slightly jiggly.
- Cool completely, then refrigerate at least 4 hours before removing from pan.
- For ganache: pour warm cream over chopped chocolate, let sit 1 minute, whisk until smooth. Pour over cooled bars.
- Slice using a sharp knife, wiping between cuts. Store refrigerated up to 5 days.
