## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp vegan butter (post used Olivani)
- 1.5 kg onions (red and brown)
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 Tbsp rosemary (fresh or dried)
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1 Tbsp Italian red wine vinegar
- 3 cups chopped pumpkin
- 1½ cups brown lentils, well rinsed
- 5 cups vegetable stock
- 2–3 bay leaves
- 2 Tbsp vegan Worcestershire sauce
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce
- Salt, pepper to taste

**Mashed potatoes**
- 1.5 kg potatoes
- ⅓ cup vegan butter
- ½ cup plant-based milk
- ~½ tsp salt (or more)

## Directions

1. Prepare all the ingredients. Slice the onions into half-moons, mince the garlic, chop the pumpkin into 0.5cm cubes.
2. Heat a large saucepan to medium, melt the vegan butter. Add onion. Stir and fry 4–5 min on medium-high until golden brown. Lower heat to medium-low and let the onions cook, adding water if needed — about 45 min total.
3. Add garlic, rosemary, and thyme to the center of the pan and fry until fragrant, about 2–3 min. Add tomato paste and mix well.
4. Add pumpkin, mix well, and fry 3 min on medium heat. Add lentils, vegetable stock, and bay leaves. Stir until combined. Bring to a boil. Simmer 25 min, until the lentils and pumpkin are almost done.
5. Meanwhile, cook the potatoes in salty water.
6. Once the lentils are almost fully cooked, add Worcestershire sauce, red wine vinegar, and soy sauce, salt and pepper to taste. Simmer to let the flavors combine.
7. Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare mashed potatoes: drain the cooked potatoes and in the same pot add vegan butter, plant-based milk, salt and pepper. Mash until smooth.
8. In an oven dish, evenly spread the lentil mix and cover it with the mashed potatoes.
9. Bake for 30 minutes.
10. Enjoy.
