## Ingredients

**For the Crust**
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon active dry yeast

**For the Sauce**
- 1 28-ounce can crushed tomatoes
- 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 tablespoon dried basil
- 1 teaspoon finely minced garlic
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 1/2 teaspoon fresh cracked black pepper

**For the Toppings**
- 1/4 cup shredded parmesan cheese
- 8 slices pepperoni (optional)
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 cup shredded brick cheese (or mozzarella)
- Pinch of dried oregano
- Pinch of sea salt
- 1 cup warm pizza sauce

## Directions

- Combine water, salt, flour, and yeast in stand mixer bowl. Mix with dough hook on low speed for 4 minutes until dough pulls from bowl sides.
- Grease bowl, add dough, cover with plastic wrap, and rest 15 minutes.
- Line 8×8-inch pan with parchment; grease lightly. Spread dough to corners using oiled fingertips.
- Cover pan and let rise 1 hour until visibly puffed.
- While dough rises, combine tomatoes, sugar, oregano, basil, garlic, salt, and pepper in saucepan. Simmer 10-15 minutes until thickened. Blend with immersion blender if desired.
- Preheat oven to 450°F.
- Sprinkle parmesan around pan edges. Add pepperoni in two rows if desired.
- Top with mozzarella and brick cheese; sprinkle with oregano and salt.
- Bake 15 minutes until cheese bubbles and edges caramelize.
- Loosen crust sides with spatula; slide onto cooling rack. For crispier bottom, bake directly on oven rack 5 minutes more.
- Top with warm sauce in two rows. Serve warm.
