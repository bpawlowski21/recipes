## Ingredients

- 3 to 4 (1 1/4 to 1 1/2 pounds) Yukon Gold potatoes
- 1 small onion
- 1 cup olive oil
- 6 extra-large or 7 large eggs
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Peel potatoes and onion and slice very thin using a mandoline, food processor, or by hand. If either is large, cut lengthwise first so slices form half-moons.
2. Heat oil in an 8- to 10-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat for about 3 minutes until very hot. Add potatoes and onions in even layers and reduce heat to medium-low. Cook for 15 minutes, flipping and stirring to ensure even cooking. Potatoes are done when tender when pierced with a knife tip — they should not brown or fall apart.
3. Transfer potatoes and onion to a colander set over a bowl and drain. Season with salt and pepper, let cool about 5 minutes.
4. Lightly beat eggs in a large bowl with salt and pepper. Stir in drained potatoes and onions. Let soak together for 10 minutes if time permits.
5. Add 2 tablespoons drained cooking oil to the skillet over medium-high heat. Pour potato mixture into skillet and flatten with a spatula. Reduce heat to medium-low. Cook, moving the skillet and nudging the egg to allow it to run underneath, until the top is wet but not runny and golden underneath.
6. Loosen tortilla with a spatula, slide onto a large dinner plate. Invert the skillet over the plate and flip the tortilla back into the skillet. Tuck edges under if needed.
7. Return to the stove and cook another 2 to 3 minutes for a slightly loose center, or 3 to 4 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out dry.
8. Slice into wedges and serve hot, cold, or at room temperature, plain or dusted with smoked paprika and/or aioli.
