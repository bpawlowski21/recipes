## Ingredients

### Apples
- 1 pound apples (3 medium or 2 large), peeled if desired, cored, cut into 1/2-inch wedges
- Juice of half a lemon
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar

### Crumbs
- 1/2 cup (4 ounces or 115 grams) unsalted butter, melted
- 1/3 cup (65 grams) light or dark brown sugar
- 1/3 cup (65 grams) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 1/3 cups (175 grams) all-purpose flour

### Cake
- 6 tablespoons (85 grams) unsalted butter, softened
- 1/2 cup (100 grams) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1/3 cup (80 grams) sour cream
- 1 teaspoon (5 ml) vanilla extract
- 1 cup (130 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt

### Finish
- Powdered sugar, for dusting

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 325°F (165°C). Lightly coat an 8-inch square or 9-inch cake pan with butter or nonstick spray. Line with parchment paper for extra security.
2. Toss apples with lemon juice, then cinnamon and sugar. Set aside.
3. Make crumbs: whisk butter, sugars, cinnamon, and salt until evenly mixed. Add flour and mix until it disappears (mixture will be very thick). Set aside.
4. Make cake: beat softened butter with sugar until lightened and fluffy. Add egg, sour cream, and vanilla; beat until combined. Sprinkle baking powder and salt over batter surface and beat well. Add flour and mix only until it disappears.
5. Scrape batter into prepared pan and smooth flat. Arrange apple wedges on cake, slightly overlapped (reserve 2 wedges as "cook's snacks"). Pour any cinnamon-apple juices from the bowl over apples. Sprinkle crumbs over apple slices.
6. Bake until a toothpick inserted into the apples shows no crisp spots and juices bubble around some apples, approximately 50 to 55 minutes.
7. Cool to room temperature before cutting. Dust generously with powdered sugar.
