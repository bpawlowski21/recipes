## Ingredients

**Salad**
- 4 cups cubed watermelon
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 Persian cucumbers, diced
- 1 cup kalamata olives, pitted
- 8 ounces feta cheese, cubed

**Basil Vinaigrette**
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- Juice from 1 lemon
- 2 teaspoons honey
- 1 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 2 tablespoons fresh chopped chives
- Flaky salt and black pepper, to taste

## Directions

- In a salad bowl, combine the watermelon, tomatoes, cucumbers, olives, and feta cheese.
- Prepare the basil vinaigrette by whisking together the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, and lemon juice in a glass jar. Stir in the honey, basil, and chives, then season with salt and pepper.
- Pour the vinaigrette over the salad, sprinkle with additional salt and pepper, and serve.
