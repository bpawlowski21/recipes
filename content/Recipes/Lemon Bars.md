The handwritten card calls for pouring the lemon filling over a pre-baked shortbread crust but doesn't include the crust recipe itself. Matched 2026-07-29 against a shortbread crust already saved in Apple Notes (`RECIPES | COOKING`), which linked to Sally's Baking Addiction — fits the handwritten card exactly (glass 9×13, cuts cleanly into the 24 pieces the card specifies).

## Ingredients

**Shortbread Crust**
- 1 cup (16 Tbsp; 226g) unsalted butter, melted
- ½ cup (100g) granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 cups + 2 Tablespoons (265g) all-purpose flour, spooned & leveled

**Lemon Filling**
- 4 large eggs
- 1½ cups granulated sugar
- ¾ cup fresh lemon juice
- ⅓ cup flour
- 3 tbsp. confectioners' sugar, for dusting

## Directions

**Crust**
1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Use a 9×13-inch glass baking pan — avoid metal, which can impart a metallic flavor.
2. Mix melted butter, sugar, vanilla extract, and salt together in a medium bowl.
3. Add flour and stir until completely combined — the dough will be thick.
4. Press firmly and evenly into the pan.
5. Bake for 20-22 minutes until edges are lightly browned.
6. Remove from oven and, using a fork, poke holes all over the top of the warm crust (not all the way through) — this helps the filling stick and holds the crust in place.

**Filling** (handwritten card)
1. Raise oven temp to 350°F.
2. In a bowl, whisk together eggs and granulated sugar until well combined, and stir in lemon juice and flour.
3. Pour lemon mixture over the hot shortbread crust.
4. Reduce oven temp to 300°F and bake in the middle of the oven until set, about 30 minutes.
5. Cool completely in pan — cut into 24 pieces.
6. Cover, chill up to 3 days.
7. Sift confectioners' sugar over bars before serving.

## Notes

- The crust recipe (ingredients, bake temp/time, pan size) came from Sally's Baking Addiction, matched via a link saved in Apple Notes — not part of the original handwritten card. Everything under "Filling" above is transcribed as written on the card.
- The handwritten card's own oven temp (350°F) doesn't match the crust's bake temp (325°F) — that's expected, since the crust bakes first at its own temperature and the oven gets adjusted for the filling stage afterward. Don't preheat to 350°F until the crust step is done.
