## Ingredients

- 2 cups dark or semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 1/4 cup creamy or crunchy almond butter (handwritten correction — printed recipe said "peanut butter", or any other nut butter)
- 2 cups crispy brown rice cereal (or any whole-grain cereal you have in the house)
- Coarse sea salt or kosher salt, for sprinkling

## Directions

1. Mist a standard muffin tin with nonstick oil spray; set aside.
2. Melt the chocolate chips using a double boiler, or microwave until velvety smooth. Mix in the nut butter and stir until it's fully incorporated into the chocolate. Add in the cereal and mix until everything is well-combined. Divide between 12 muffin compartments. Press down with wet fingers to firm into circular shapes. Sprinkle on coarse sea salt or kosher salt. Place in the fridge for at least an hour to firm.
3. Store in the fridge or freeze in an air-tight container for up to 2 months.

## Notes

- Chef notes: a melt, mix, and muffin situation using only three ingredients — chocolate, nut butter, and a crispy whole-grain cereal. Joy Bauer normally uses just 1/4 cup peanut butter for a subtle nut flavor, but notes more can be used if you want the nut taste to shine.
- Handwritten correction on the physical page: "ALMOND!" is written above "peanut" in the peanut butter ingredient line, indicating almond butter was used instead of peanut butter for this batch. Original scan attached above.
