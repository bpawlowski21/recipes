## Ingredients

- 50-100g active sourdough starter
- 10g kosher salt
- 430-440g water (room temperature)
- 512g bread flour
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil (divided)
- Flaky sea salt (such as Maldon)

## Directions

- Combine starter, salt, and water in a large bowl, stirring to blend. Add flour and mix until fully incorporated.
- After 30 minutes, perform one fold by pulling dough up toward center 8-10 times as you rotate the bowl.
- Coat with olive oil, cover with a towel, and let bulk ferment at room temperature 4-18 hours until doubled (timing varies by season/temperature).
- Place 2 tablespoons olive oil in a 9x13-inch pan. Drizzle dough with 1 tablespoon oil, deflate gently, transfer to pan. Fold envelope-style into a rough rectangle, then flip seam-side down.
- Rub top with oil and leave uncovered 4-6 hours until puffy and nearly doubled.
- Heat oven to 425°F. Oil your hands and use all ten fingers to gently dimple and stretch dough across the pan.
- Sprinkle generously with sea salt, bake ~25 minutes until golden.
- Cool on a rack at least 20 minutes before slicing.
