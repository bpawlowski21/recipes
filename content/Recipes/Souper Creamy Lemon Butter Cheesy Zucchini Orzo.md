## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons salted butter
- 3-4 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1 pound dry orzo pasta (use gluten-free, if needed)
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
- 2 medium zucchini and/or yellow summer squash, grated
- 1/2 bunch kale, finely shredded
- 3 cups low sodium chicken or vegetable broth
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1/2 cup whole milk or canned full fat coconut milk
- 3/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley (or 2 teaspoons dried)

## Directions

1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add butter and garlic, cooking until fragrant (about 1 minute). Stir in orzo and thyme, toasting for 2-3 minutes. Add zucchini and kale, cooking another 2-3 minutes until wilted. Add 1/2 cup broth to deglaze. Stir in lemon zest, lemon juice, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Slowly pour in remaining 2 1/2 cups broth and bring to a boil. Simmer 8-10 minutes, stirring often, until orzo reaches al dente and becomes creamy.
2. Stir in milk, parmesan, and parsley. Cook a few more minutes until warmed through. Add additional broth if needed to maintain a creamy consistency.
3. Divide among bowls and top with fresh thyme, additional butter, and parmesan as desired. Serve immediately.
