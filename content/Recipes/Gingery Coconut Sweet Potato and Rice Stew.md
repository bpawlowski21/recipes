## Ingredients

### For the Stew:
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 2 inches fresh ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 2 sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (to taste)
- 1/3 cup fresh cilantro, chopped (plus more for serving)
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 (14 ounce) can coconut milk
- 3 cups chopped kale
- 2 cups cooked basmati rice

### For the Spiced Chili Oil:
- 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, lightly smashed
- 1 tablespoon raw sesame seeds
- 2-3 teaspoons crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin seed
- 1 teaspoon paprika

## Directions

- Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add chopped onion and cook 5 minutes until softened. Add ginger, garlic, and sweet potato cubes; cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes. Stir in garam masala, turmeric, and cayenne; cook another minute.
- Add 3 cups water and season generously with salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer 15-20 minutes until sweet potatoes are tender.
- Stir in coconut milk and kale; cook 5 minutes. Remove from heat and add cilantro.
- While stew simmers, prepare chili oil: heat olive oil and garlic in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until fragrant (about 5 minutes). Add sesame seeds, chili flakes, and cumin; cook 30 seconds. Remove from heat; stir in paprika. Season with salt and let cool.
- Divide cooked rice among bowls, ladle stew over top, and drizzle with chili oil. Garnish with cilantro and serve with naan bread.
- Instant Pot method: sauté onion in oil 5 minutes, add ginger/garlic/sweet potato and cook 2 minutes, stir in spices and cook 1 minute, add 3 cups water and salt, pressure cook on high 10 minutes, quick release, then sauté with coconut milk and kale 5 minutes and stir in cilantro. Finish with chili oil and rice as above.
