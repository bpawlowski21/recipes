## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 2 teaspoons muscovado sugar or brown sugar
- ½ cup raw walnut pieces (Wendy: buy candied walnuts from Trader Joe's instead)
- 1 medium ripe pear
- ⅓ cup crumbled Gorgonzola cheese
- 4 cups packed frisée lettuce
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Directions

- In a medium skillet, melt butter and whisk in sugar over medium low heat. Add walnuts and let cook. Stir often, until sugar has caramelized on the walnuts, about 3 to 4 minutes. Remove from skillet and set aside.
- Slice pear, ⅛ to ¼ inch thick. Whisk together olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt and pepper. Toss dressing with lettuce.
- To assemble salad, place lettuce in a bowl and top with walnuts, pears, and Gorgonzola cheese.

## Notes

- Frisée and other greens in the endive family stand up well in pre-dressed salads — the lettuce maintains its crunch without wilting. Dress immediately before serving if you prefer.
- Wendy's handwritten note: buy candied walnuts from Trader Joe's rather than making them fresh.
