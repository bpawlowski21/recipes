## Ingredients

**Dough:**
- 2 cups (232g) King Arthur '00' Pizza Flour
- 1/2 teaspoon instant yeast
- 1 teaspoon table salt
- 1 tablespoon (12g) extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 cup plus 2 to 3 tablespoons (141g to 155g) lukewarm water

**Filling:**
- One 14.5-ounce can whole tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled (about 1 teaspoon minced)
- 1 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 1/8 teaspoon table salt
- Pinch (1/16 teaspoon) black pepper
- Handful fresh basil leaves, torn or chopped
- 6 to 8 ounces (170g to 227g) cacciocavallo, aged provolone, or Romano cheese, thinly sliced or shaved

## Directions

- Combine dough ingredients in a stand mixer with dough hook; mix and knead at medium speed until smooth and elastic, approximately 15 minutes (dough should pass the windowpane test).
- Form into a ball, place in covered bowl, and let rise 1½ to 2 hours at 76°F to 78°F.
- Purée tomatoes, olive oil, garlic, sugar, salt, and pepper using a blender or food processor.
- Transfer mixture to saucepan over medium-high heat; add basil.
- Simmer, stirring regularly, until thickened like tomato sauce (10-15 minutes). Should yield about 1 cup.
- Preheat oven to 450°F and oil a 9" x 5" loaf pan.
- Roll dough on greased surface into thin 18" x 24" rectangle (thin enough to see through in places).
- Lightly score into 18" x 6" quarters without cutting through.
- Spread ½ cup sauce over center two quarters; sprinkle half the cheese atop.
- Fold uncovered side sections into center, overlapping slightly, creating an 18" x 12" rectangle.
- Score into four 4½" x 12" sections; spread scant ⅓ cup sauce over center two sections and three-quarters of remaining cheese.
- Fold uncovered sections into center, overlapping slightly, creating a 9" x 12" rectangle.
- Score into three 9" x 4" sections; spread remaining sauce over left two sections and remaining cheese.
- Fold right section over center, then left section over both (like folding a letter).
- Transfer seam-side up to prepared pan; pierce top with fork about a dozen times.
- Bake 50-60 minutes until top is dark and blistered; internal temperature should reach 212°F.
- Remove from oven and turn out onto rack.
- Cool about 10 minutes before slicing. Serve warm or at room temperature.
- Store wrapped at room temperature 2-3 days; freeze for longer storage.
