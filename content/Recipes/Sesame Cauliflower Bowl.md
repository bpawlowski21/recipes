## Ingredients

- 1 small head of cauliflower
- 1 cup flour + 1 cup water, mixed (batter)
- 2 cups plain bread crumbs

**Sauce**
- ⅔ cup soy sauce or tamari
- ⅓ cup maple syrup
- ¼ cup rice vinegar
- 1½ tsp sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- Optional: 1–2 tsp sriracha for heat
- ¼ cup water
- 2 tsp cornstarch

**Other**
- White rice
- Edamame
- Carrots, in ribbons
- Sesame seeds
- Green onion

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 430°F. Cut the cauliflower into bite-size pieces. Mix the water and flour in a medium bowl to make a batter. Pour the breadcrumbs into another bowl.
2. Cover each piece of cauliflower in the batter, then dip in the breadcrumbs and coat using your hands or a fork. Place on a lined baking sheet. Bake 20 minutes, flipping halfway through.
3. While the cauliflower bakes, prepare the sauce: heat the sesame oil on medium-high and add the garlic. Cook 1–2 minutes until fragrant. Whisk in the soy sauce, maple syrup, sriracha, and rice vinegar. In a separate bowl, mix the cornstarch and water together, then pour into the sauce and whisk a few minutes until dark and thick. Turn off heat and set aside.
4. Pour the baked cauliflower into the sauce and mix well. Serve over rice with edamame and carrots. Top with sesame seeds and green onion. Enjoy!
