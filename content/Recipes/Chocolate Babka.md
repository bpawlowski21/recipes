## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 4 1/4 cups (530 grams) all-purpose flour, plus extra for dusting
- 1/2 cup (100 grams) granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast
- Grated zest of 1 small lemon or half an orange
- 3 large eggs
- 1/2 cup water (cold is fine), and up to 1-2 tablespoons extra if needed
- 3/4 teaspoon fine sea or table salt
- 2/3 cup unsalted butter (150 grams or 5.3 ounces), at room temperature
- Sunflower or other neutral oil, for greasing

**Filling**
- 4 1/2 ounces (130 grams) dark chocolate (or approximately 3/4 cup chocolate chips)
- 1/2 cup (120 grams) unsalted butter, cold is fine
- Scant 1/2 cup (50 grams) powdered sugar
- 1/3 cup (30 grams) cocoa powder
- 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon (optional)

**Syrup**
- 1/3 cup water
- 6 tablespoons (75 grams) granulated sugar

## Directions

1. **Make the dough:** Combine flour, sugar, yeast, and zest in a stand mixer bowl. Add eggs and 1/2 cup water, mixing with the dough hook until combined. If too dry, add extra water one tablespoon at a time until dough forms a mass. With the mixer on low, add salt, then butter a spoonful at a time, mixing until incorporated. Mix on medium speed for 10 minutes until completely smooth, scraping the bowl several times; dough should begin pulling away from the bowl sides (add 1 tablespoon flour if not). Coat a large bowl with oil, place dough inside, cover with plastic, and refrigerate at least half a day, preferably overnight (dough will not fully double).
2. **Make filling:** Melt butter and chocolate together until smooth. Stir in powdered sugar and cocoa; mixture should form a spreadable paste. Add cinnamon if desired.
3. **Assemble loaves:** Coat two 9-by-4-inch loaf pans with oil or butter, line bottoms with parchment. Take half the dough from the fridge (keep the rest chilled). Roll on a floured counter to about 10 inches wide and 10-12 inches long. Spread half the chocolate mixture evenly over the dough, leaving a 1/2-inch border. Brush the far end with water. Roll tightly into a long cigar shape and seal the dampened end. Transfer to a lightly floured tray and freeze 10-15 minutes for cleaner cutting. Repeat with the second dough.
4. Trim 1/2 inch from each log end. Cut each log in half lengthwise and lay cut sides up. Pinch top ends together. Lift one side over the other, forming a twist with cut sides facing out. Transfer to the prepared loaf pan. Cover with a damp tea towel and let rise 1 to 1 1/2 hours at room temperature. Repeat with the second loaf.
5. **Bake:** Heat oven to 375°F. Bake each loaf on the middle rack for 30 minutes (check doneness at 25 minutes); a skewer inserted should feel almost no resistance.
6. While babkas bake, make the syrup: bring sugar and water to a simmer until the sugar dissolves; remove from heat and cool somewhat.
7. As soon as babkas leave the oven, brush syrup all over each — it will seem like too much but tastes just right. Cool halfway in the pan, then transfer to a cooling rack.
