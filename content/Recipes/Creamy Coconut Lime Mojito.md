## Ingredients

**For the Drink:**
- 1 lime, quartered
- 8 fresh mint leaves
- 1-2 teaspoons granulated sugar (optional)
- 1 ounce (2 tablespoons) white rum
- 1/2 ounce (1 tablespoon) coconut rum
- 1/4 cup canned full fat coconut milk, well stirred
- Ginger beer for topping

**For the Lime Sugar Rim:**
- Zest of 1 lime
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- Flaky sea salt

## Directions

1. Combine lime zest, sugar, and a pinch of salt on a shallow plate. Rim your glasses in this mixture, then fill with ice.
2. In a cocktail shaker or glass jar, muddle the lime, mint, and sugar (if using) together to release juices. Add white rum and coconut rum. Fill with ice and shake until combined. Add coconut milk and shake.
3. Strain into your prepared glass. Slowly top with ginger beer, then gently stir to combine.
4. *Mocktail option:* Replace rums with 1½ ounces apple cider or apple juice.

## Notes

- Saved from Apple Notes folder "Cocktails".
- Foam will form when adding ginger beer; gently stir to subside.
- Coconut milk may separate as drink sits; stir to recombine.
- Garnish with mint sprigs, lime sugar rim, and optional coconut flakes.
