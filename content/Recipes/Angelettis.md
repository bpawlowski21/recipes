## Ingredients

- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 6 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla or anise extract
- 1 box (16 ounces) confectioners' sugar
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- Zest of 1 lemon (Wendy's addition)
- Coarse sanding sugar, for decorating

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. In a medium bowl, sift together flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
2. In the bowl of an electric mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, cream butter and granulated sugar until light and fluffy. On medium speed, add eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Add vanilla; beat until incorporated. With mixer on, gradually add flour mixture; beat until dough comes together.
3. Scoop dough into tablespoon-size balls onto a baking sheet lined with parchment or a nonstick baking mat. Bake until golden brown, about 10 minutes. Transfer to a wire rack to cool.
4. Meanwhile, stir together confectioners' sugar, lemon juice, and 2 tablespoons water. Place wire rack over a rimmed baking sheet. Once cookies have cooled, top with icing. Sprinkle with sugar, and let stand until icing is firm.

## Notes

- Wendy's handwritten note at top: "spreads too much!" — dough is very sticky; if possible, roll into balls (1 teaspoon size) before chilling — may not always be able to.
- Wendy's note: they puff up a lot, but do not spread. Add zest of 1 lemon to 1/2 of the recipe and 1/4 teaspoon lemon extract to the dough.
- Wendy's dated notes (12/16/24): 2 cups flour, 1 tablespoon baking powder, 1 stick butter, 1/2 cup sugar, 3 eggs, 1 teaspoon lemon zest (beat into sugar before adding butter) — this appears to be a half-batch conversion.
- Wendy's dated notes (12/21/22): half recipe makes about 36 cookies — small! Dough is very sticky.
- Wendy's dated notes (12/23/17): they puff up a lot — drop small spoonfuls, size of a quarter, no bigger. Make a full batch: 4 rows x 5 per tray.
