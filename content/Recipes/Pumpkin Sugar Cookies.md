## Ingredients

- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 1/2 cups (300 g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 1/2 cups (315 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- Orange gel food coloring
- Green gel food coloring

## Directions

- Beat butter, sugar, egg, and vanilla until creamy, fluffy, and well-combined using an electric mixer.
- Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a separate bowl.
- Gradually blend dry ingredients into wet mixture on low speed, scraping bowl occasionally.
- Divide dough: remove 1/4 for orange coloring, 1 tablespoon for green coloring, leaving the remainder plain.
- Tint the larger portion orange and the small portion green, mixing thoroughly.
- Roll orange dough into two thin logs (2/3" tall x 1" wide) and indent the tops with a skewer.
- Roll green dough into strips and press into the indents. Freeze 15 minutes to 1 hour.
- Wrap each pumpkin log with plain dough, form smooth logs, wrap in plastic, and refrigerate on a kitchen towel for at least 4 hours.
- Preheat oven to 375°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Slice dough into 1/4" pieces, space 2 inches apart, and bake 10 minutes until edges turn light golden brown.
- Cool completely on baking sheet before serving.
