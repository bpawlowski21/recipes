## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 2½ tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- 12 ounces semi-sweet chocolate, roughly chopped
- 1 cup unsalted butter, cubed
- 3 teaspoons instant espresso powder
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 5 eggs, room temperature, lightly beaten
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 2 cups chocolate chunks

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 9x13-inch pan with parchment paper and spray with non-stick coating; set aside.
2. Combine flour, salt, and cocoa powder in a medium bowl; whisk and set aside.
3. Place semi-sweet chocolate, butter, and espresso powder in a large bowl over simmering water, stirring occasionally until melted and smooth.
4. Turn off heat, keeping bowl over water. Add sugar and whisk until combined, then remove from pan. Mixture should be room temperature.
5. Add beaten eggs and whisk until just combined. Add vanilla. Sprinkle flour mixture over chocolate and fold together using a rubber spatula until just combined. Fold in chocolate chunks.
6. Pour batter into prepared pan and smooth top. Bake 30-35 minutes, rotating halfway through, until a toothpick inserted in center comes out with a few moist crumbs.
7. Cool completely in pan (approximately 2 hours) before removing with parchment paper and cutting into squares.
