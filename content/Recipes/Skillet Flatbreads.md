## Ingredients

- 3 cups (360g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons table salt
- 3 tablespoons (35g) olive oil or vegetable oil
- 1 cup (227g) ice water
- 2 to 3 tablespoons (25g to 35g) vegetable oil, additional for frying

## Directions

- Combine flour, baking powder, and salt in a large mixing bowl, stirring to blend.
- Add oil and ice water; mix until a soft, cohesive dough forms. Adjust consistency as needed with additional flour or water. The dough should be moist yet not sticky. Cover with plastic wrap and rest for 10 minutes.
- Heat a heavy-bottomed skillet on the stovetop. Add 1 tablespoon oil and warm until it shimmers.
- Divide dough into 10 to 12 equal portions (approximately 1 1/2 to 2 ounces each). Coat each piece in flour, then roll into a rough circle or oval about 1/4" thick, or hand-shape by flattening between your palms.
- Working in batches, fry flatbreads in hot oil for 2-3 minutes until golden. Flip and cook the other side for approximately 2 minutes. Transfer to a cooling rack. Replenish oil between batches as needed.
