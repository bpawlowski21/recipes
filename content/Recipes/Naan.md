## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup hot water
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 3/4 teaspoon active dry yeast (Wendy uses instant yeast, since that's what she had on hand)
- 3/4 cup warm milk
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Melted butter for brushing (may use olive oil)
- Fresh cilantro or other herbs for topping

## Directions

- Combine water, sugar, and yeast in a medium bowl. Stir until dissolved and let sit 10 minutes until foamy and bread-scented.
- Add milk and yogurt to the yeast mixture. In a separate large bowl, combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt (add these dry ingredients to a large bowl). Pour the yeast mixture into the flour and mix with a wooden spoon until combined, then finish with your hands. The dough should be sticky but form a smooth ball.
- Cover with a damp towel or plastic wrap and let rest in a warm place for 1 hour (or refrigerate overnight). This dough can also be made earlier in the day and left out on the counter all day until ready to make.
- Divide dough into 8 equal balls. Using a rolling pin, shape each into an oval approximately 6-8 inches long and 1/4-inch thick.
- Heat a cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Brush naan with melted butter and optional spices such as cumin and garlic. Place on skillet, cover, and cook 1 minute until bubbles form. Flip and cook 1-2 minutes until toasted spots appear underneath.
- Brush with additional butter and top with salt, cilantro, or herbs. Transfer to a tea towel-lined dish. Repeat with remaining dough and serve warm. Best eaten fresh, but will keep in a ziplock bag for a few days or in the freezer.

## Notes

- Saved from Apple Notes folder "Half Baked Harvest". This is the basic step-by-step naan base recipe, distinct from the Cheesy Herb Stuffed Naan recipe.
- Wendy's handwritten notes, dated 1.4.21 (but made several times since, including during lockdown): uses instant yeast in place of active dry yeast since that's what she had; groups the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together to add to a large bowl; and notes the dough can be made earlier in the day and left out on the counter all day until ready to cook.
