"These crispy Italian breadcrumbs, known as 'pangrattato', are a traditional topping for pasta, vegetables and other dishes from southern Italy. Flavored with garlic, fresh herbs and fried in extra virgin olive oil, this is a condiment that's hard to beat!"

Prep Time: 10 mins · Cook Time: 15 mins · Total Time: 25 mins

**Equipment:** 1 small food processor, 1 skillet or frying pan

## Ingredients

- 7 ounces (200 grams) stale bread
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 teaspoon fresh rosemary, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, finely chopped
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon fine salt

## Directions

1. Tear the bread into small chunks and then use a food processor to reduce to coarse breadcrumbs.
2. Mince or grate the garlic.
3. Combine oil and garlic in a frying pan. Place over low heat just until the oil begins to warm and the garlic is fragrant. The garlic shouldn't fry.
4. Add the breadcrumbs, chopped fresh rosemary, oregano, and salt. Stir well to coat in the garlicky oil.
5. Increase the heat to medium-high. Stir all the time, not letting the breadcrumbs burn.
6. After 5 minutes add the parsley. Continue to stir over medium-high heat until golden brown. It'll take between 10-15 minutes in total.
7. Remove from the heat and immediately tip onto a baking sheet to cool.

## Notes

- **Tips for Success:** use stale, but not oven-dried, bread — a few days-old sourdough works perfectly. Avoid over-processing the bread; a mix of larger crumbs and fine crumbs creates great texture and crunch. Fry the crumbs over medium heat until deeply golden brown, keep stirring constantly to prevent burning.
- **Variations:** Parmigiano Reggiano or Parmesan cheese — stir in a few tablespoons of finely grated cheese after the breadcrumbs have cooled. Spicy — add a pinch of red pepper or chili flakes while cooking. Anchovies — melt one or two anchovy fillets with the oil and garlic for an extra hit of umami flavor. Black pepper — add ¼ teaspoon of freshly ground black pepper for a subtle, aromatic bite.
