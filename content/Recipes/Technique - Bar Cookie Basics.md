General method plus five bar cookie variations, all built on the same basic technique.

## Ingredients

**Bar Cookie Basics (technique, applies to all variations below):**
- Line a 9-by-13-inch baking dish with foil, leaving a 2-inch overhang on two sides; coat the foil with cooking spray.
- Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.
- Prepare your batter or dough. Spread in the prepared pan or press in using damp or oiled fingers.
- Bake as directed, then transfer to a rack and let cool completely in the pan.

**Marble Brownies:**
- 12 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 2 sticks butter
- 4 ounces semisweet chocolate, chopped
- 2 cups sugar
- 4 eggs
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

**Classic Blondies:**
- 2 sticks butter
- 2 cups light brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tablespoon vanilla
- 2 1/2 cups flour
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 cups butterscotch chips

**Cheesecake Bars:**
- 3 cups finely ground graham crackers
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 sticks butter, melted
- 24 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

**Key Lime Cheesecake Bars:**
- Crust for Cheesecake Bars (above)
- 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 2 eggs
- 1 14-ounce can sweetened condensed milk
- 2/3 cup Key lime juice

**Lemon Bars:**
- 2 cups flour
- 3/4 cup confectioners' sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 2 sticks butter, softened
- 5 eggs
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 1 cup lemon juice
- 1/3 cup flour
- Confectioners' sugar, for dusting

**Orange Bars (Wendy's handwritten variation):**
- Make Lemon Bars using only 2 Tbsp lemon juice, then add 1 cup orange juice and 2 tsp vanilla with the egg mixture.

## Directions

**Marble Brownies:**
1. Beat the cream cheese, 1/3 cup sugar, and 1 egg until smooth.
2. Make the brownie batter: melt the butter with the chopped chocolate; let cool slightly. Stir in 2 cups sugar and 4 eggs. Whisk in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt.
3. Swirl the cream cheese mixture into the batter in the pan. Bake 35 to 40 minutes.

**Classic Blondies:**
1. Melt the butter; let cool slightly. Whisk in the brown sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
2. Stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt. Fold in the butterscotch chips.
3. Bake until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, 30 to 35 minutes.

**Cheesecake Bars:**
1. Mix the graham crackers with the sugar and melted butter. Press into the prepared pan; bake 10 minutes. Let cool completely.
2. Beat the cream cheese with the sugar until smooth. Beat in the eggs, sour cream, and vanilla.
3. Spread over the crust; bake until the edges are set but the center is still loose, about 30 minutes. Let cool, then chill until set.

**Key Lime Cheesecake Bars:**
1. Bake the crust as for Cheesecake Bars.
2. For the filling, beat the cream cheese with the eggs until smooth. Beat in the sweetened condensed milk and Key lime juice.
3. Bake only 25 minutes.

**Lemon Bars:**
1. Mix the flour with the confectioners' sugar and a pinch of salt; work in the softened butter with your fingers until large clumps form.
2. Press into the prepared pan; bake until lightly browned, 20 to 25 minutes. Let cool 5 minutes.
3. Whisk the eggs with the sugar, lemon juice, and flour until smooth; pour over the crust.
4. Bake until set, 20 to 25 minutes. Dust the cooled bars with confectioners' sugar.

**Orange Bars:**
1. Make Lemon Bars as above, but use only 2 tablespoons lemon juice in the egg mixture, and add 1 cup orange juice and 2 teaspoons vanilla along with the egg mixture.

## Notes
