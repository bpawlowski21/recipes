Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post by the same author as the other "Maya" recipes in this vault. "These tacos were absolutely delicious." Prep time: 60 minutes.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (125ml) orange juice
- 6 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp chili paste
- A handful chopped cilantro
- 2 tsp each cumin and oregano
- 2 limes, juiced
- ¼ cup (60ml) olive oil
- 450g mushrooms (author used king oyster mushrooms)
- Soft taco shells

**"Salsa verde"**
- A bunch cilantro or parsley
- 2 cloves garlic
- 4 tbsp vegan yogurt
- 4 tbsp vegan mayo
- ½ lemon
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp agave
- Salt to taste

## Directions

1. Combine the orange juice, soy sauce, chili paste, cilantro, cumin, oregano, lime juice, and olive oil. Marinate the mushrooms in it for at least 30 minutes.
2. For the salsa verde: blend the cilantro/parsley, garlic, vegan yogurt, vegan mayo, lemon, cumin, agave, and salt until smooth.
3. Cook the marinated mushrooms and serve in soft taco shells with the salsa verde.
