## Ingredients

**Coconut Dates:**
- 20 medjool dates, pitted
- ½ cup coconut butter
- ¼ cup desiccated coconut
- 20 raw almonds (or pre-roasted, unsalted)

**Chocolate Coating:**
- 1¼ cup chopped vegan dark chocolate (60-70% cacao)
- 2 tsp coconut oil

**Optional Toppings:**
- Maldon sea salt
- Toasted or raw desiccated coconut

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F (176°C) if using raw almonds; place on baking sheet
- Pit dates and create a well in each center by pinching ends together
- Adjust coconut butter consistency (microwave briefly if too firm, refrigerate if too liquid)
- Toast almonds for 8 minutes; cool for 5-10 minutes
- Mix desiccated coconut with coconut butter; scoop ~1 tsp into each date cavity
- Place one toasted almond in the center of each date
- Freeze dates for ~10 minutes until firm
- Melt chocolate and coconut oil using a double boiler or microwave
- Cool chocolate slightly, then dip each date using a fork; tap off excess
- Add optional toppings if desired
- Freeze for another 10 minutes until chocolate hardens completely
- Store in refrigerator (2 weeks) or freezer (2 months)
