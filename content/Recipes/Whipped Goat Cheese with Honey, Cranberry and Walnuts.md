"Indulge in this creamy, sweet & savory whipped goat cheese dip! Topped with a warm honey, cranberry, and walnut crunch."

Prep: 10 mins

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup butter
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp orange zest
- ½ cup dried cranberries
- ¾ cup chopped walnuts
- 8 oz goat cheese
- 4 oz cream cheese
- 1 tbsp honey
- ½ tsp salt
- Orange zest, to top

## Directions

1. To the pan, add butter, brown sugar, 2 tbsp honey and orange zest.
2. Whisk together until combined, and sugar has dissolved (about 2-3 mins).
3. To the pan, add dried cranberries and walnuts and stir to combine.
4. In a separate bowl, combine softened goat cheese, cream cheese and 1 tbsp honey and season with salt.
5. Scoop the cheese mixture into a serving bowl and top with the walnut mixture! Serve with crackers or bread.
