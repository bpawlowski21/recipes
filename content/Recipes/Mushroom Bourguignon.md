## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons butter, softened
- 2 pounds portobello mushrooms, sliced 1/4-inch thick (stems reserved for other use)
- 1/2 carrot, finely diced
- 1 small yellow onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup full-bodied red wine
- 2 cups beef or vegetable broth
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves (or 1/2 teaspoon dried)
- 1 1/2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 cup pearl onions, peeled (thawed if frozen)
- Egg noodles for serving
- Sour cream and chopped chives or parsley for garnish (optional)

## Directions

1. Heat one tablespoon each of oil and butter in a medium Dutch oven over high heat. Sear mushrooms until darkened but before releasing liquid (3-4 minutes). Remove from pan.
2. Lower heat to medium, add remaining oil. Cook carrots, onions, thyme, salt, and pepper for about 10 minutes until onions brown lightly. Add garlic, cook 1 minute more.
3. Pour in wine, scraping up browned bits, then increase heat and reduce by half.
4. Stir in tomato paste and broth. Return mushrooms with collected juices. Simmer 20 minutes until mushrooms are tender.
5. Add pearl onions, simmer 5 minutes more.
6. Combine remaining butter with flour; stir into stew. Simmer 10 additional minutes. Reduce sauce if needed. Season to taste.
7. Serve over egg noodles, topped with sour cream and herbs if desired.
