## Ingredients

**Dressing**
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp water
- 4 tbsp hummus
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp basil
- ½ tsp salt and pepper

**Veggies**
- 3 carrots
- 1 red onion
- ½ head broccoli
- ½ head cauliflower
- 80g arugula

**Other**
- 200g vegan feta
- ½ cup dry lentils
- ¼ cup walnuts

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C and cook the lentils according to package instructions.
2. Wash, peel, and chop the veggies into small cubes. Bake until tender, 20–30 minutes.
3. Mix all the dressing ingredients in a small bowl.
4. Toast the walnuts in a skillet over medium-high heat until crunchy.
5. Assemble the salad, top with dressing, and enjoy.
