## Ingredients

- 100g whole-grain rye flour
- 300g all-purpose flour
- 400g water (total, distributed across 7 days)

## Directions

- **Day 1:** Mix 100g rye flour + 125g warm water. Rest 24 hours in a warm location (80-85°F).
- **Day 2:** Transfer 75g of the mixture to a clean jar. Add 50g rye flour, 50g all-purpose flour, 115g water. Rest 24 hours.
- **Day 3:** Repeat the Day 2 process: 75g carried mixture + fresh flour and water mixture. Rest 24 hours.
- **Day 4:** Begin twice-daily feedings. Morning: same ratios as Day 3, rest 12 hours. Evening: repeat feeding, rest overnight.
- **Days 5-6:** Continue twice-daily feedings with identical ratios, using the same jar; discard and refresh.
- **Day 7+:** Morning feeding uses 20g starter + 30g rye + 70g all-purpose + 100g water. Repeat evening feeding. Continue indefinitely.
- Keep the jar in a warm environment (80°F/26°C optimal).
- Use chlorine-free water (let tap water sit overnight or use bottled).
- Starter is ready when it shows a "consistent rise and fall" with visible bubbles and sour aroma.
- A fermentation surge on Day 2 is normal; activity may dip before stabilizing.
