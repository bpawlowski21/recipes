## Ingredients

**For the Starter:**
- About 1 tablespoon sourdough starter (100% hydration with 50/50 all purpose and whole wheat flour)
- 100 grams lukewarm water (~80°F)
- 50 grams all purpose flour
- 50 grams whole wheat flour

**For the Bread:**
- 100 grams active starter
- 350 grams warm water (~80°F), plus more for hands
- 450 grams all purpose flour
- 50 grams whole wheat or spelt flour
- 10 grams kosher salt
- Rice flour for dusting

## Directions

- Activate starter: Mix starter with 100g lukewarm water, then add flours. Let sit in warm spot 4-6 hours until doubled. Test readiness by floating a spoonful in cold water.
- Mix dough: Combine active starter with 350g warm water. Add flours and mix until dry bits disappear. Rest 25-40 minutes covered.
- Incorporate salt: Sprinkle salt on dough, wet hand, dribble water over salt. Perform stretch-and-folds by grabbing quarter of dough, pulling up and folding over. Rotate bowl 90°, repeat 4 times. Continue pinching and folding until salt dissolves.
- Bulk fermentation: Cover, set timer 30 minutes. Perform 4 more stretch-and-folds every 30 minutes for 3 hours total. Dough should be relaxed; wet finger indent should nearly disappear.
- Pre-shape: Transfer dough to floured surface. Stretch corners up and fold over 4 times creating taut surface. Dust with flour, flip. Gently tuck sides under forming half dome. Rest 20-30 minutes. Dust banneton with rice flour.
- Final shape: Lightly flour top, flip upside down. Apply another set of stretch-and-folds, flip again. Slide on work surface until taut. Transfer upside down into banneton using bench scraper. Cover (shower cap works) and refrigerate 12-14 hours.
- Bake: Preheat Dutch oven or baking stone with stock pot to 475°F. Remove banneton, invert dough onto parchment paper. Dust with rice flour if desired. Score with lame or razor blade. Transfer to preheated vessel, cover, reduce heat to 450°F. Bake 20 minutes covered, then 20-25 minutes uncovered until nicely browned. Cool at least 1 hour before slicing.
