## Key Tips

- **Things that won't kill your starter:** contact with metal spoons/bowls (stainless steel is fine); missing a feeding or not timing feedings exactly 12 hours apart; incorrect flour/water ratios (correctable); brief freezing of a mature starter (though extended freezing damages the yeast).
- **Things that will kill your starter:** excessive heat (yeast dies at 140°F; keep starter around 70°F); severe/extended neglect, which allows mold or bad bacteria to develop.
- **Signs your starter has gone bad:** visible mold (fuzzy, various colors); a pink or orange tint/streak (discard immediately); dark hooch (liquid layer) is a normal sign of hunger, not danger.
- Key takeaway: "Well-maintained mature sourdough starters are extremely hardy and resistant to invaders. It's pretty darn hard to kill them."
