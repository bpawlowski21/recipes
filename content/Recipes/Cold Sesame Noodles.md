## Ingredients

- 1 pound noodles (ramen, soba, and spaghetti work best)
- 1/2 cup Chinese sesame paste or tahini
- 6 cloves fresh minced garlic
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons toasted sesame oil
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup
- 2 tablespoons chili garlic sauce or sambal oelek
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 small cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
- 1 teaspoon chili flakes, optional
- Toasted sesame seeds, for garnish, optional

## Directions

1. Cook the noodles according to the package instructions.
2. Drain and rinse under cold water and set aside.
3. In large mixing bowl, whisk together the sesame paste, minced garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, maple syrup, chili garlic sauce, and rice vinegar until smooth – add 1 tablespoon of water as needed to reach a thick, but pourable consistency.
4. Add the noodles to the bowl and toss until they are coated in the sauce.
5. Taste and adjust seasonings, if necessary.
6. In a separate bowl, gently mix the cucumber, salt, and chili flakes until combined.
7. Serve immediately and garnish cucumbers and toasted sesame seeds, and enjoy!
