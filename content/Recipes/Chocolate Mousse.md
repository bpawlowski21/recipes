## Ingredients

- 1 can full fat coconut milk or cream (only the thick cream from the top of the tin)
- 2 tbsp cacao powder
- 1 tbsp sweetener of choice, like maple syrup or coconut sugar (author used xylitol for sugar-free)

## Directions

1. Put the coconut milk can or cream in the fridge overnight.
2. Scoop out the thick layer inside the coconut can and add it to a mixing bowl.
3. Add the cacao powder and sweetener and stir well.
4. Eat as is, or let it sit in the refrigerator for an hour (or more) for a better texture. Enjoy!
