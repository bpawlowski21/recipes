## Ingredients

- 6 rice paper sheets
- 2 large iceberg or romaine lettuce leaves
- 1 cucumber
- 1 carrot
- 1 red bell pepper
- 1 avocado
- Small bunch cilantro
- 4 tofu tenders

**Sauce**
- ⅓ cup peanut butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp chili oil
- Juice from 1 large lime

## Directions

1. Mix the peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, chili oil, and lime juice in a bowl until smooth. Set aside.
2. Julienne the cucumber and carrot. Thinly slice the bell pepper, avocado, and tofu tenders.
3. Dip three rice paper sheets in cold water until just pliable, about 5–10 seconds. Lay them overlapping on a work surface.
4. Place a lettuce leaf in the center. Layer on the veggies and tofu.
5. Fold up the bottom, tuck in the sides, and roll it up tightly like a burrito. If needed, wrap with an extra rice paper sheet for reinforcement.
6. Repeat for the second roll. Serve immediately with the peanut sauce.
