## Ingredients

- 3 pounds red potatoes, rinsed and quartered
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ¾ cup sour cream
- 1 tablespoon dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon dried dill
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, chopped (optional)
- ½ cup finely chopped celery
- 3 green onions, sliced

## Directions

- Place potatoes in a large saucepan covered with water by 1 inch. Salt the water and bring to a boil over high heat. Simmer approximately 15-20 minutes until potatoes are fork-tender, then drain and cool.
- While potatoes cool, combine mayonnaise, sour cream, dijon mustard, salt, dill, and pepper in a mixing bowl, whisking until blended.
- Add the cooled potatoes, eggs, celery, and green onions to the dressing mixture. Stir until everything is evenly coated.
- Cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours before serving.
