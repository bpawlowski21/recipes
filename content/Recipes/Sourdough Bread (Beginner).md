## Ingredients

### Levain
- 38g ripe sourdough starter (100% hydration)
- 38g stoneground whole wheat flour
- 38g bread flour
- 76g water

### Main Dough
- 773g bread flour
- 114g whole wheat flour
- 51g whole grain rye flour
- 653g water
- 18g fine sea salt

## Directions

- Levain (8:00 a.m.): Mix levain ingredients; maintain at 74-76°F (23-24°C) for 5-6 hours until expanded and bubbly.
- Autolyse (12:00 p.m.): Combine flours and 603g water in a bowl; mix until no dry bits remain. Cover; rest 1 hour.
- Mix (1:00 p.m.): Add salt, ripe levain, and the reserved 50g water to the autolyse. Mix by hand until incorporated; transfer to bulk fermentation container.
- Bulk Fermentation (1:10-5:10 p.m.): Perform 3 sets of stretch-and-folds at 30-minute intervals; rest between sets.
- Divide and Preshape (5:10 p.m.): Divide dough in half; shape into rounds. Rest uncovered 25 minutes.
- Shape (5:35 p.m.): Shape into boules; place in proofing baskets seam-side-up.
- Rest and Proof (5:40 p.m. to next day 9:30 a.m.): Cover baskets; rest 20 minutes on counter, then refrigerate 16 hours overnight.
- Bake (next morning): Preheat oven with Dutch oven to 450°F. Score dough; bake covered 20 minutes, then uncovered 30 minutes. Internal temperature should reach 208°F.
