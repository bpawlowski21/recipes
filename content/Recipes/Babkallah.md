## Ingredients

### Dough
- 1/2 cup (113g) whole milk, lukewarm (105°F)
- 2 1/4 teaspoons instant or active dry yeast
- 1/3 cup (66g) granulated sugar
- 4 large egg yolks
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 1/4 cups (390g) all-purpose flour, plus more for kneading
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 8 tablespoons (113g) unsalted butter, room temperature, cut into 1/2" pieces, plus more for the bowl

### Filling
- 1 cup (170g) finely chopped semisweet chocolate
- 1/3 cup (71g) light brown sugar, packed
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons (28g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled

### Topping
- 1 large egg, beaten
- Demerara or sparkling sugar for sprinkling

## Directions

- Make the dough: mix milk, yeast, sugar, egg yolks, and vanilla together. Add flour, salt, and butter; mix until shaggy.
- Knead by hand 8-10 minutes or in a stand mixer 5-8 minutes until smooth and supple. Place in a buttered bowl, cover, and let rise 1 1/2 to 2 1/2 hours until puffy.
- Prepare the filling: combine chocolate, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt in a small bowl.
- Divide dough into three equal portions (about 250g each). Roll each into a 12" rope, then flatten and roll into 12"x6" rectangles.
- Brush each with melted butter, sprinkle filling evenly, leaving a 1/2" border on one long edge. Roll from the filling side into logs; pinch seams to seal.
- Place seam-side down, pinch ends together, and braid loosely. Tuck ends underneath. Cover and proof 1-2 hours until 1 1/2 times original size.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with Demerara sugar.
- Bake 35-45 minutes until deeply browned (internal temperature 190°F). Cool on a rack.
