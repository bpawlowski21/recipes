## Ingredients

- 1 heaped cup frozen strawberries
- 2/3 cup ice cubes
- 5 tablespoons light rum
- 1 to 2 tablespoons lime juice, to taste
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar

## Directions

1. Combine all ingredients in a blender, starting with 1 tablespoon of lime juice.
2. Blend until smooth, scraping down the sides several times as needed.
3. Taste and adjust with more lime juice, ice, sugar, or rum as desired.
4. Pour into two glasses and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Saved from Apple Notes folder "Cocktails".
- Yields 2 one-cup servings, ~5 minutes to prepare. Frozen strawberries from the freezer section work well; adjust proportions since strawberry intensity varies between bags. Scales easily for a crowd.
