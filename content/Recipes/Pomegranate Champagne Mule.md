## Ingredients

- 1/3 cup pomegranate juice
- Juice from 1/2 lime
- Juice from 1/4 small grapefruit
- 1 1/2 ounces vodka
- 1 teaspoon fresh grated ginger (optional)
- 2 ounces chilled ginger beer
- Champagne, chilled, for topping
- Pomegranate arils and sugared rosemary, for garnish
- Grapefruit slices, for serving

## Directions

1. Combine pomegranate juice, lime juice, grapefruit juice, vodka, and ginger in a cocktail shaker or mason jar. Fill with ice and shake for 1 minute to infuse.
2. Strain into a Moscow Mule mug, champagne glass, or wine glass filled with ice.
3. Add ginger beer, then top with champagne.
4. Serve with a sugared rosemary sprig, grapefruit wedge, and pomegranate arils.

**Sugared Rosemary Garnish:** Dampen fresh rosemary sprigs with water, coat thoroughly with granulated sugar, and dry at room temperature for 1 hour. Can be made 1 day ahead.
