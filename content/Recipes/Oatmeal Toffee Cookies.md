Brown sugar and butter underscore the tawny sweetness of the almond toffee bits. December 2008, Cooking Light, p. 161.

## Ingredients

- 3.4 ounces all-purpose flour (about 3/4 cup)
- 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/4 cup butter, softened
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 large egg
- 1/3 cup almond toffee bits
- Cooking spray

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°.
2. Weigh or lightly spoon flour into dry measuring cups; level with a knife. Combine flour, oats, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl; stir with a whisk. Place sugar and butter in a large bowl; beat with a mixer at medium speed until well blended (about 5 minutes). Add vanilla and egg; beat well. Add flour mixture; beat just until combined. Stir in toffee bits.
3. Drop dough by tablespoonfuls 2 inches apart on 2 baking sheets coated with cooking spray. Bake at 350° for 12 minutes or until lightly browned (9-10 minutes if using convection). Cool on pans 1 minute. Remove cookies from pans; cool completely on wire racks. Yield: 2 dozen (serving size: 1 cookie).
