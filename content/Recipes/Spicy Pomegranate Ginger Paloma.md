## Ingredients

**Spicy Ginger Syrup**
- 1/2 cup honey
- 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 8 leaves fresh mint

**Paloma (single drink)**
- 1/4 cup pomegranate juice
- 1/4 cup grapefruit juice
- Juice from 1/2 a lime
- 2-4 tablespoons Spicy Ginger Syrup
- 2 ounces tequila
- Ginger beer, for topping

**Spicy Sweet Rim (optional)**
- 1 tablespoon coarse sugar
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- Cayenne to taste

## Directions

- Bring 1/2 cup water, honey, ginger, and cayenne to a boil over high heat; simmer 1-2 minutes, then remove from heat. Add mint, cover, and let sit 10 minutes. Strain out solids; store in a glass jar in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.
- On a small plate, mix coarse sugar, sugar, and a pinch of cayenne. Run a lime wedge around the rim of the glass and dip into the sugar mix.
- Fill glass with ice. Add pomegranate juice, grapefruit juice, lime juice, spicy ginger syrup, and tequila. Stir to combine and top with ginger beer.
- For a pitcher (serves 8): combine 2 cups pomegranate juice, 2 cups grapefruit juice, 1/2 cup lime juice, 1/2 cup Spicy Ginger Syrup, and 1 1/2-2 cups tequila in a large pitcher; chill. Rim glasses as above. Just before serving, add ice to the pitcher and top with 2-3 bottles ginger beer.
