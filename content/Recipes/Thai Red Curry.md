Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. Prep time: 20 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1 can (400ml) coconut milk
- 3 tbsp red curry paste (post used @koro_de brand)
- ½ head broccoli
- 2 carrots
- 1 bell pepper
- (or other veggies of choice — the post also used bamboo shoots)
- 5 lime leaves, optional
- Salt, to taste
- A pinch of sugar
- A splash of water
- Cooked rice, for serving

## Directions

1. Heat up a few tablespoons of the coconut milk. Fry the curry paste 4–5 minutes.
2. Add the broccoli, carrots, and bell pepper (or your chosen veggies). Stir-fry 3–4 minutes.
3. Add the rest of the coconut milk, lime leaves, salt, sugar, and a splash of water. Stir it in.
4. Let it simmer until the veggies are tender.
5. Serve with rice.
