## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil or rice bran oil
- 2 medium yellow onions, chopped
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 pound dried black beans, rinsed, sorted, and soaked overnight
- 2 chipotle chilies plus 2 teaspoons adobo sauce
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 2 bay leaves
- 6 cups water, plus more as needed
- Lemon juice to taste
- Lime juice to taste
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Directions

- Heat oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add chopped onion and cook until softened (about 5 minutes). Stir in minced garlic until fragrant (about 30 seconds).
- Add soaked beans, chipotle peppers and adobo sauce, cumin, oregano, and bay leaves. Pour in enough water to cover the beans and stir to combine.
- Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce heat and simmer until beans are tender (about 1 1/2 to 2 hours). Add additional hot water as needed to keep beans covered.
- Remove from heat. Take out chipotle chilies (if desired) and bay leaves. Stir in lemon and lime juices, then season with salt and pepper to taste.
