## Ingredients

- 3 sprigs fresh tarragon, plus a few leaves for garnish
- 2 oz. gin
- 3 oz. Ruby Red grapefruit juice
- 1/2 oz. elderflower liqueur (such as St. Germain)
- 1 oz. simple syrup
- Club soda
- Grapefruit slices, for garnish

## Directions

1. Muddle the tarragon in a cocktail shaker.
2. Fill the shaker with ice and add gin, grapefruit juice, elderflower liqueur, and simple syrup. Shake vigorously.
3. Strain into a Collins glass filled with ice. Top with club soda.
4. Garnish with grapefruit and tarragon.
