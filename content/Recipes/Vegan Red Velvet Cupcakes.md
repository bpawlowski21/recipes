## Ingredients

**Vegan Buttermilk**
- 1 cup soy milk
- 2 teaspoons white distilled vinegar

**Dry Ingredients**
- 1 1/3 cups all purpose flour
- 1/4 cup cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons natural cocoa powder
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

**Wet Ingredients**
- 1/2 cup canola oil
- 1/4 cup applesauce
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- 1-3 tablespoons vegan red food coloring

**Frosting**
- 1/2 cup vegan butter, softened
- 3 cups powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 1-2 tablespoons soy milk, as needed

## Directions

- Preheat the oven to 350°F and line a 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners.
- In a measuring cup, combine the soy milk and vinegar. Let sit a few minutes until it curdles (this is the vegan buttermilk).
- In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, cornstarch, cocoa powder, sugar, baking powder, and salt until well combined.
- To the bowl with the dry ingredients, add the oil, applesauce, vanilla, red food coloring, and the milk/vinegar mixture. Stir well with a large spoon or spatula until smooth and well combined.
- Fill the liners 3/4 full. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted comes out clean. Cool completely before frosting.
- Make the frosting: using a hand mixer, beat the softened vegan butter for 1-2 minutes until creamy, then add the powdered sugar, apple cider vinegar, and vanilla and beat until smooth and creamy, adding a tablespoon of non-dairy milk at a time as needed. If it gets runny, add more powdered sugar.
- Once the cupcakes are cooled completely, frost as desired and serve. If piping a lot of frosting on each cupcake, consider doubling the frosting.

## Notes

- Course: Dessert. Cuisine: American. Servings: 14 cupcakes. Calories: 340kcal.
- May substitute another non-dairy milk for the soy milk (almond, oat, coconut, or cashew).
- The flour + cornstarch mimics cake flour; could use all cake flour instead for similar results.
- Could likely use another oil instead of canola (refined coconut oil, avocado oil, maybe even olive oil).
- If vegan-friendly red food coloring isn't available, a few tablespoons of beetroot powder can substitute, though the cupcakes won't be as bright.
