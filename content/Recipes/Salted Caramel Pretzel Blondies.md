## Ingredients

**Caramel**
- 1/3 cup (65 grams) granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons (30 grams) unsalted butter
- Flaky sea salt
- 2 tablespoons (30 ml) heavy cream

**Blondies**
- 8 tablespoons (4 ounces or 115 grams) unsalted butter
- 3/4 cup (145 grams) light or dark brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon (5 ml) vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon flaky sea salt, or 1/4 teaspoon coarse sea salt
- 1 cup (130 grams) all-purpose flour
- 2/3 cup (115 grams) dark chocolate chips or chunks
- 1/2 cup thin salted pretzels, lightly crumbled

## Directions

1. **Make caramel:** Line a medium plate or tray with parchment paper and butter lightly. Melt sugar in a dry saucepan over medium heat until deep copper colored (about 4 minutes). Whisk in butter until melted, then add salt and cream. Return to stove over medium-high heat and cook without stirring for 3 minutes more, until reaching 245-250°F. Pour onto parchment and freeze 10-15 minutes until firm enough to cut into squares.
2. **Make blondies:** Heat oven to 350°F. Butter an 8x8-inch pan or line with parchment. Melt butter partially in a large bowl, then stir to finish melting. Add brown sugar and whisk to combine, then egg, vanilla, and sea salt. Stir in flour just until it disappears. Scatter chocolate chips and pretzels over batter. Cut caramel into 1-inch squares and scatter most into bowl. Fold ingredients gently together and spread in pan, then top with remaining caramel squares.
3. Bake 25-30 minutes until deeply golden at edges and mostly dry on top. Cool in pan for 10 minutes, then lift out using parchment and cool further on a rack. Cut into small squares.
