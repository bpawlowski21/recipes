## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup (100 g) light brown sugar, firmly packed
- 1/4 cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) light corn syrup
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup (31 g) all-purpose flour
- 2/3 cup (90 g) finely chopped almonds (or 2/3 cup whole oats for nut-free)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F and line baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Combine butter, sugars, and corn syrup in a medium saucepan over medium heat, stirring constantly until sugar melts and mixture begins to boil.
- Remove from heat immediately and stir in vanilla extract, salt, flour, and finely chopped almonds until fully combined.
- Allow batter to cool for 5 minutes to thicken slightly.
- Drop by 1 1/2 teaspoon onto prepared baking sheet, spacing cookies several inches apart (they spread significantly).
- Bake at 350°F for 9 minutes, rotating pan halfway through. Cookies are done when they stop bubbling and turn light golden brown.
- Cool completely on baking sheet — they won't crisp until fully cooled.

## Notes

- Saved from Apple Notes folder "COOKIES". Yield: 30 cookies. Store airtight, cool and dry, up to 1 week, separated with parchment paper.
