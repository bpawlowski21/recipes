Saved from Apple Notes as "Cooking Instructions for Michael Angelo's Vegetable Lasagna." This is heating instructions for a store-bought frozen entrée, not a from-scratch recipe — kept as-is since it was deliberately saved.

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Remove entrée from carton.
3. Place tray on a cookie sheet on the center rack of the oven (6–8" from the heating element).
4. Cook with film on for 55 minutes.
5. Carefully remove entrée from oven. Remove film. Let stand for 2 minutes before serving.
