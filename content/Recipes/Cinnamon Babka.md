## Ingredients

### Dough
- 3 cups (360g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 2 tablespoons (14g) Baker's Special Dry Milk
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast (SAF Gold preferred)
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 1 1/4 teaspoons (8g) table salt
- 2/3 cup (149g) lukewarm water
- 1 large egg
- 5 tablespoons (71g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1 1/2 teaspoons King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract

### Filling
- 3/4 cup (160g) packed light or dark brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon plus 2 teaspoons (17g) ground cinnamon
- 1 1/2 tablespoons (11g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 4 tablespoons (57g) unsalted butter, melted
- 2 teaspoons water
- 1/2 cup (57g) diced toasted pecans or walnuts, optional

### Topping
- 2 tablespoons (14g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1 tablespoon packed light or dark brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Pinch of table salt
- 1 tablespoon (14g) unsalted butter, cold
- 1 large egg beaten with salt (egg wash)

## Directions

- Weigh flour or measure by spooning gently into a cup and sweeping off excess.
- Combine all dough ingredients in the order listed in a large bowl or stand mixer, mixing until moistened (30-60 seconds). Cover and rest 20 minutes, then knead until soft and smooth, about 2 minutes.
- Place dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise 1.5-2 hours until puffy.
- For the filling, combine brown sugar, cinnamon, and flour; stir in melted butter and water until evenly incorporated. Set aside.
- For the topping, combine flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt; work in cold butter with fingertips until coarse crumbs form. Set aside.
- Roll or pat dough into a 9" x 18" rectangle, about 1/4" thick.
- Spread filling evenly over dough, leaving a 1" bare border. Scatter nuts if using and press lightly to adhere.
- Starting with the short end, roll the dough gently into a log, sealing the seam and ends. Cut the log in half lengthwise to create two 10" pieces.
- Place cut sides up, form an "X" with the pieces, twist together, then pinch the ends to seal and tuck under. Place in a lightly greased 9"x4" Extra Tall Loaf Pan or 9"x5" loaf pan.
- Brush loaf with egg wash and sprinkle crumb topping over the top.
- Cover and let rise until the crest reaches 3/4"-1" below the rim (Extra Tall pan) or 1" above the rim (standard pan), 1.5-2 hours. Preheat oven to 350°F.
- Bake 40-50 minutes, tenting with foil during the final 15-20 minutes, until deep golden brown and internal temperature reaches about 195°F.
- Loosen edges with a spatula, cool 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely.
- Slice and serve at room temperature, or reheat slices briefly in a toaster.
- Store wrapped at room temperature for several days; freeze for longer storage.
