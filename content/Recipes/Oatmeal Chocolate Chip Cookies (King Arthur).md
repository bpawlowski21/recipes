## Ingredients

- 16 tablespoons (227g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1 cup (213g) light brown sugar, packed
- 1/2 cup (99g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 large egg yolk, room temperature
- 1 tablespoon (14g) King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 2 cups (240g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1 cup (89g) rolled oats (old-fashioned or quick-cooking)
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt (or 3/4 teaspoon table salt)
- 3 cups (510g) semisweet chocolate chips

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 325°F. Line baking sheets with parchment or nonstick spray.
- Beat butter and sugars until smooth.
- Mix in egg, egg yolk, and vanilla.
- Whisk flour, oats, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together; add to butter mixture.
- Mix until thoroughly incorporated, scraping bowl as needed.
- Stir in chocolate chips.
- Scoop dough onto prepared sheets (spacing 1.5-2 inches apart). Use a 4-tablespoon scoop for 20 large cookies, tablespoon scoop for 50 medium, or teaspoon scoop for 100 small cookies.
- Bake 12-17 minutes until light golden brown with slightly darker edges.
- Transfer to cooling racks once set.
- Serve warm or at room temperature; store in airtight container.
