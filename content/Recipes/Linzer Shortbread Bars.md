## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup almond flour (replaces the oats in the base dough)
- 2 sticks butter
- 1 1/4 cups seedless jam
- Remaining base dough ingredients (flour, sugar, etc.) not captured — see Notes

## Directions

- Make Oatmeal Shortbread Bars (No. 17) dough [base recipe not captured in this scan — see Notes], substituting 3/4 cup almond flour for the oats and using only 2 sticks butter.
- Press two-thirds of the dough into the prepared pan.
- Spread with 1 1/4 cups seedless jam.
- Crumble the remaining dough over the top.
- Bake until lightly browned, 40 to 45 minutes.
