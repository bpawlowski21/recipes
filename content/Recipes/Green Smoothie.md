## Ingredients

- 1 cup chopped kale, large ribs removed
- 4 ounces sliced apple (about ½ medium apple)
- 3 fresh mint leaves
- ¼ cup frozen pineapple chunks
- ½ frozen banana
- ½ cup water
- ½ tablespoon lemon juice
- ½ tablespoon flax seed oil
- 1 teaspoon agave nectar
- ¾ teaspoon refrigerated pureed ginger (or fresh minced)
- 4–6 ice cubes

## Directions

- Add all ingredients to a high-power blender and blend until smooth.
- Serve immediately and enjoy as a breakfast or healthy snack.
