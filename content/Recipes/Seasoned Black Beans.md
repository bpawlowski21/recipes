Saved from Apple Notes as "Tex Mex Seasoned Black Beans."

Prep Time: 5 minutes · Cook Time: 25 minutes · Total Time: 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 (15-ounce) can black beans, undrained
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon hot sauce
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon chili powder
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika

## Directions

1. Heat a small saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the undrained black beans, hot sauce, and all the spices to the pan.
3. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer at least 15 minutes but no more than 30.
4. The beans are ready once simmering is done.

## Notes

- A quick, easy tex-mex bean dish, good for tacos, salads, burritos, and bowls.
- Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
