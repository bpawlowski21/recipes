## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp avocado or olive oil (or ¼ cup water)
- 1 cup chopped white or yellow onion
- 2 medium cloves garlic, minced
- 1½ tsp finely minced fresh ginger
- 1 cup yellow split peas
- 1 cup water
- 2½ cups vegetable broth
- ¾ tsp ground turmeric
- 1 pinch ground black pepper
- ⅓ cup full-fat canned coconut milk
- Sea salt, to taste
- Optional garnish: coconut milk, freshly chopped cilantro

## Directions

**Instant Pot method:**
- Activate the sauté function on normal setting. Heat oil/water, add onion, sauté 2–3 minutes until translucent. Add ginger and garlic, sauté until fragrant.
- Press Cancel. Add split peas, water, broth, turmeric, and black pepper; stir.
- Seal lid, pressure cook on HIGH for 15 minutes. Allow natural steam release (~15 minutes) or quick release if needed.
- Add coconut milk and stir. Optionally purée with an immersion blender for creamier texture. Adjust seasonings as needed.
- Serve fresh, garnished with cilantro and a coconut milk swirl if desired. Store leftovers refrigerated up to 3–4 days or frozen up to 1 month.

**Stovetop alternative:**
- Sauté aromatics, add remaining ingredients, bring to a gentle boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer 30–45 minutes until split peas are tender. Proceed with coconut milk and optional blending.
