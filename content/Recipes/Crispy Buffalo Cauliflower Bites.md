## Ingredients

- ⅔ cup brown rice flour
- 2 tablespoons almond flour
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon dried parsley
- ⅔ cup water
- 1 head cauliflower, cut into 2-inch florets
- ⅓ cup Frank's hot sauce or barbecue sauce
- Spinach Ranch Dip (optional)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 450°F and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Combine brown rice flour, almond flour, tomato paste, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, parsley, and water in a blender. Puree until smooth and thick, then transfer to a bowl.
- Add cauliflower florets to the batter and toss thoroughly to coat.
- Arrange coated florets in a single layer on the prepared baking sheets, ensuring they don't touch.
- Bake for 20–25 minutes until crispy on the edges.
- Remove from oven and let stand 3 minutes to crisp further.
- Transfer to a bowl, drizzle with sauce, and serve immediately.
