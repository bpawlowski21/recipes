![[Speedy Spinach Quiche (handwritten).pdf]]

Serve this quickly prepared dish with a tossed green salad and chilled melon for a summer brunch or light supper.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 10-ounce package frozen chopped spinach, thawed, drained well
- 1 9-inch refrigerated ready piecrust (1/2 box)
- 1 teaspoon all purpose flour
- 1/2 cup (about 2 ounces) grated Monterey Jack
- 1/2 cup (about 2 ounces) grated Parmesan
- 4 eggs
- 1/2 cup lowfat cottage cheese
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 1/8 teaspoon ground nutmeg

## Directions

- Melt butter in heavy medium skillet over medium-high heat. Add onion and sauté until translucent, about 8 minutes. Add spinach and stir until spinach is dry, about 3 minutes. Cool slightly.
- Preheat oven to 375°F. Dust 1 side of crust with flour. Transfer to 9-inch-diameter quiche dish or pie pan, floured side down. Press into pan, sealing any cracks. Trim edges. Sprinkle both cheeses over bottom of crust. Top with spinach mixture. Beat eggs, cottage cheese, salt, pepper, and nutmeg in large bowl to blend. Pour over spinach. Bake until filling is set, about 50 minutes. Cool slightly. Cut into wedges and serve.

## Notes

- Printed headnote already suggests: use 5 eggs, use Cheddar cheese, add mushrooms to the sauté pan after onions and before spinach, and there's no need to layer ingredients — just stir together.
- Wendy's handwritten doubled version (dated 2/7/13 and 3/7/13): 6 eggs, 1/2 cup real eggs (liquid), 1 cup grated cheese, 1 cup Parmesan, a handful of feta, spinach, 1 package mushrooms, onion, 1 cup chopped artichoke hearts. Order of operations noted: sauté onions, add mushrooms, add spinach, mix in artichokes.
- Wendy's follow-up note: "(2) 16oz bags [of spinach] is too much for 2 — triple or use only 1."
