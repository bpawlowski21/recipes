Saved from Apple Notes under "PASTA" as "Quick Spaghetti Carbonara," with a note to top with a jammy egg.

Total Time: 15 minutes (5 min prep, 10 min cook)

## Ingredients

- 1 pound spaghetti
- Salt
- ¾ cup milk, plus more if needed
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons butter
- ⅔ cup freshly grated Pecorino Romano cheese, plus more for serving (Parmesan works as a substitute)
- Pepper, to taste

## Directions

1. Cook spaghetti in generously salted water.
2. While the pasta cooks, whisk together the milk and egg in a small bowl.
3. Drain the pasta when cooked al dente.
4. Return the pasta to the pot over low heat. Add the milk/egg mixture and butter, stirring until it thickens and coats the noodles, about 1 minute.
5. Remove from heat. Stir in the cheese. Season with salt and pepper. Thin with additional milk if needed.
6. Serve immediately with more Pecorino Romano at the table.
