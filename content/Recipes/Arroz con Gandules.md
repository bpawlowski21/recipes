## Ingredients

- 2 cups medium grain white rice, rinsed
- 2 cups water
- 1½ cups chicken stock
- 1 teaspoon sazón (Goya brand)
- ⅓ cup recaito
- 1 small onion, diced
- ¼ cup thick-cut bacon, diced
- 15.5 oz gandules (pigeon peas), 1 can, drained and rinsed
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
- Salt to taste

## Directions

- Heat olive oil in a 6-quart pot over medium heat. Sauté onions and bacon for 5 minutes until soft, then add gandules and stir for about 3 minutes.
- Add sazón, broth, water, and rice. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium and cover.
- Cook covered for 20–25 minutes until rice is fluffy and liquid is absorbed.
- Fluff rice, garnish with parsley, and adjust salt to taste before serving.
