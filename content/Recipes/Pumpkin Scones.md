## Ingredients

- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup (50 g) sugar
- 1/4 cup (50 g) light brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 2 teaspoons pumpkin spice
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, cold/frozen, cut into 16 pieces
- 1/4 cup (59 ml) heavy cream, plus additional for brushing
- 1/3 cup (85 g) pumpkin puree, blotted
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Coarse sugar, for sprinkling

**Glaze:**
- 1/2 cup (65 g) powdered sugar
- 2-3 teaspoons milk
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Directions

- Combine flour, sugars, baking powder, pumpkin spice, cinnamon, and salt in food processor; pulse briefly.
- Sprinkle butter pieces over mixture and pulse until it resembles fine crumbs.
- Whisk heavy cream, pumpkin puree, and vanilla together.
- Pour cream mixture over dry ingredients; pulse until mixture begins clumping.
- Turn dough onto floured surface. Fold in half over itself and gently flatten. Rotate 90 degrees and repeat folding 5 times without overworking.
- Form into a 6-inch disk.
- Cut into 8 wedges using a large knife (press straight down; don't saw).
- Place scones 2 inches apart on parchment paper. Brush with cream and sprinkle with coarse sugar.
- Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 17 minutes.
- Cool, then drizzle with glaze made by whisking sugar, milk, and vanilla together.
