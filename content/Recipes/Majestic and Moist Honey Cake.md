## Directions

- Bake in a stack of two baking sheets (for angel/tube pans), which lets the bottom bake faster than the interior and top.
- Bake until the cake springs back when gently touched in the center: 60 to 75 minutes for angel or tube cake pans, 45 to 55 minutes for loaf cakes, 40 to 45 minutes for sheet-style cakes.
- Let cake stand fifteen minutes before removing from pan.
