## Ingredients

- 1 small head of cauliflower
- 1 can (220g) chickpeas
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp chili powder
- Salt to taste

**Salad**
- 2 tomatoes
- ½ cucumber
- A bunch parsley

**Sauce**
- 4 tbsp tahini
- 1 tsp chili sauce
- 2 tsp maple syrup
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Salt to taste
- Water, to thin

- Pitas, to serve

## Directions

1. Mix the cauliflower, chickpeas, oil, paprika, cumin, garlic powder, chili powder, and salt. Roast at 180°C/360°F for 20–25 minutes.
2. Finely chop the tomatoes, cucumber, and parsley for the salad and mix together.
3. Mix the sauce ingredients together.
4. Serve the roasted veggies with the salad and the sauce on pitas.
