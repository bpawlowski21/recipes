## Ingredients

- 200g firm tofu
- 1 cup cornstarch
- 1 cup breadcrumbs + seasoning of choice
- 1 cup vegan milk + salt + garlic powder
- ¼ cup tahini
- 1 tbsp harissa (optional)
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- Salt & pepper
- ¼ cup water
- Lettuce
- Tomato
- Avocado
- Pickled onions
- Bread, for toasting

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 220°C/440°F.
2. Cut the tofu into 1-inch thick slices. Coat with cornstarch, then the milk mixture, then the breadcrumbs. Place on a baking sheet, spray with oil, and bake about 15 minutes until crispy.
3. Combine the tahini, harissa, maple syrup, salt, pepper, and water. Add more water if too thick.
4. Prepare the veggies and toast the bread.
5. Assembly: spread a thick layer of tahini sauce, topped with the crispy tofu, lettuce, tomato, avocado, and pickled onions. Enjoy!
