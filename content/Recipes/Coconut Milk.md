## Ingredients

- 2 cups shredded unsweetened coconut
- 3–4 cups water (adjust for desired thickness)
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 whole date or 1 Tbsp maple syrup (optional, for sweetness)
- ½ tsp vanilla extract (optional)
- 2 Tbsp cocoa/cacao powder or ¼ cup fresh berries (optional, for flavored versions)

## Directions

- Combine coconut, 3 cups water, salt, and any optional add-ins in a high-speed blender. Cover with a towel and blend for about 2 minutes until well combined.
- Taste a small sample and adjust sweetness, salt, or vanilla as needed. Add the remaining 1 cup water if the mixture is too thick.
- Pour the blended mixture through a nut milk bag, thin towel, or clean t-shirt into a large bowl or pitcher to strain. Save the pulp for baking or smoothies.
- Transfer strained milk to a sealed container and refrigerate. Keeps up to 5 days. Shake before serving, as separation is normal. Use in smoothies, with granola, or in recipes like golden milk or matcha lattes.
