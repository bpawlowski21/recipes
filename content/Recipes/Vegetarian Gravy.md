## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup vegetable oil (Wendy's amendment to the printed 1/2 cup)
- 1/3 cup chopped onion
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour (Wendy's amendment to the printed 1/2 cup)
- 4 teaspoons nutritional yeast
- 1 cup chopped mushrooms (Wendy's addition)
- 4 tablespoons light soy sauce
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1/2 teaspoon dried sage
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Heat oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Sauté onion, garlic, and mushrooms until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes. Stir in flour, nutritional yeast, and soy sauce to form a smooth paste. Gradually whisk in the broth. Season with sage, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat, and simmer, stirring constantly, for 8 to 10 minutes, or until thickened.
