Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post by the same author as the other "Maya" recipes in this vault (Thai Red Curry, Lentil Shepherd's Pie, Stuffed Cabbage Rolls). "This burrito was pretty much freestyled but tastes SO GOOD! The combination of the lentils with the fresh veggie salsa and the creamy sauce, all wrapped in a tortilla and grilled until slightly crispy."

Prep time: 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

**Lentils**
- 1 onion
- 1 can (220g) lentils, rinsed
- 1 tsp each cumin, smoked paprika, salt
- ½ tsp chili flakes and cinnamon
- 1 tbsp tomato paste

**Veggie salsa**
- 1 bell pepper, finely chopped
- 1 can (200g) corn
- 1 tbsp vinegar or lemon juice
- 1 chili, finely chopped
- A handful fresh parsley
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Sauce**
- ⅓ cup (80g) vegan cream cheese or yogurt (post used homemade cashew cream cheese)
- 1 tbsp each hot sauce, maple syrup, vinegar
- A bit of water, to thin

**Else**
- 3 tortillas
- Lettuce

## Directions

1. Fry the onion, lentils, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, chili flakes, cinnamon, and tomato paste in a bit of oil for 5–7 minutes.
2. Mix the veggie salsa ingredients together.
3. Mix the sauce ingredients together.
4. Fill your tortilla with lentils, salsa, sauce, and lettuce. Roll it up and grill each side for 2–3 minutes.
