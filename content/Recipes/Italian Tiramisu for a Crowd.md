## Ingredients

**Mascarpone Mixture**
- 9 large egg yolks (160g)
- ½ cup (99g) granulated sugar
- ⅓ cup (74ml) Amaretto liqueur
- 16 oz (460g) mascarpone, room temperature
- 2¼ cups (511ml) cold heavy whipping cream
- 2 Tbsp (22g) confectioners' sugar

**Coffee Mixture & Assembly**
- 2 cups (454ml) freshly brewed strong coffee
- 1 oz (28g) dark chocolate
- ⅓ cup (28g) dutch-process cocoa powder, plus more for dusting
- ¼ cup (68ml) Kahlua liqueur
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 48 hard ladyfingers

## Directions

1. Set up a double boiler with a heatproof bowl over simmering water, ensuring the bowl doesn't touch the water.
2. Whisk egg yolks and sugar in the bowl until very thick and nearly doubled, about 10 minutes. Stir in amaretto.
3. Remove from heat and incorporate mascarpone gradually until smooth. Set aside.
4. In a chilled bowl, whip heavy cream and confectioners' sugar until medium-soft peaks form. Fold into mascarpone mixture.
5. In a saucepan, combine hot coffee, chocolate, and cocoa powder; whisk until melted and combined. Stir in Kahlua and vanilla; cool 5 minutes.
6. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into coffee mixture and arrange in a single layer in a baking dish.
7. Spread half the mascarpone cream over ladyfingers.
8. Repeat with a second layer of dipped ladyfingers and remaining cream.
9. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 8 hours (up to 24 hours). Dust with cocoa powder before serving.
