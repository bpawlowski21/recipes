## Ingredients

**Chocolate Fudge Filling**
- 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2/3 cup whole milk or heavy cream
- 1 1/2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips, divided
- 2 tablespoons salted butter, cubed
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

**Dough**
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 2 sticks (1 cup) cold salted butter, cut into 1/2 inch pieces
- 1 egg, beaten, for brushing

**Frosting**
- 2 cups powdered sugar
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 2 ounces dark or milk chocolate, melted
- Coarse salt or sugar, for sprinkling

## Directions

- Combine cocoa powder, milk, and 3/4 cup chocolate chips in a saucepan. Heat over medium for 5-8 minutes until melted and thickened. Remove from heat; stir in remaining chocolate chips, butter, and vanilla. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
- Mix flour, cocoa powder, sugar, and cold butter in a food processor until pea-sized. Add 1/2 cup cold water gradually until dough forms. Add more water as needed if dry.
- Roll dough to 1/8-inch thickness on a floured surface. Cut into 4 x 3-inch rectangles. Spread 1 tablespoon fudge on half the rectangles, leaving a 1/4-inch border. Brush edges with egg, top with remaining rectangles, and seal with fork tines.
- Place on parchment-lined sheets. Refrigerate 1 hour or freeze 20 minutes.
- Preheat oven to 400°F. Bake 15-20 minutes. Cool completely.
- Whisk powdered sugar, cocoa powder, vanilla, and 3 tablespoons water until smooth. Stir in melted chocolate. Thin with water if needed.
- Frost cooled pop-tarts. Let set 10 minutes, then sprinkle with coarse sugar or salt. Allow 2 hours to harden. Store in an airtight container up to 3 days.
