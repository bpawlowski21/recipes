"Soft, chewy, sweet sugar cookie bars are all the yum with a fraction of the work! No need to roll and shape these - just bake, cut and enjoy."

Prep Time: 20 mins · Cook Time: 20 mins · Total Time: 35 mins

## Ingredients

**Sugar Cookie Bar**
- 1½ cups salted butter, softened
- 1½ cups granulated sugar
- 3 eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 3¾ cups all-purpose flour
- 3 tsp baking powder

**Frosting**
- ¾ cup salted butter, softened
- 4½ cups powdered sugar
- 2 tsp clear vanilla extract
- Up to ¼ cup heavy cream or milk
- Food coloring

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a 9x13 pan with parchment paper or spray with non-stick spray and set aside.
2. In the bowl of a stand mixer or with an electric hand mixer, cream together the butter and granulated sugar until light in color and fluffy in texture.
3. Scrape the sides of the bowl and add in the eggs and vanilla extract.
4. Once mixed thoroughly, add in the flour and baking powder.
5. Press into the bottom of the prepared pan.
6. Bake at 350°F for 15-20 minutes or until the cookies have puffed up, no longer look glossy in the center, and the edges are just beginning to turn a golden color.
7. Allow the cookies to cool completely before frosting.
8. Once the cookies are cool, make the frosting by creaming the butter until smooth.
9. Add the powdered sugar 1 cup at a time, mixing until well incorporated before adding more. Add a splash of heavy cream or milk to help bring the frosting together as needed. Repeat until all the powdered sugar has been added.
10. Mix in the vanilla extract and food coloring. Thin the frosting by adding a bit more milk or heavy cream if needed.
11. Spread onto the cooled sugar cookie bars and top with sprinkles, if desired.
12. Cut and enjoy!
