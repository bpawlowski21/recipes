"Savory and crisp pan-fried tofu and veggies drizzled with a mouthwatering peanut sauce - yum! This is an easy one pan weeknight dinner that pairs perfectly with rice or noodles."

Prep Time: 10 mins · Cook Time: 20 mins · Total Time: 30 mins · Calories: 399

## Ingredients

- 14 oz block extra firm tofu, drained and pressed for 20 minutes
- 1-2 tbsp sesame oil (or coconut oil)
- 1 large head of broccoli, chopped into small florets
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 cup snap peas

**Peanut Sauce**
- ½ cup unsalted creamy peanut butter (should be drippy, not the clumpy kind at the bottom of the jar)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- ~1 tbsp ginger paste (or minced ginger)
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1-2 teaspoon sriracha, to taste
- 2 teaspoon brown sugar (or maple syrup)
- ¼-⅓ cup warm water, add more as needed for desired thickness

**Optional Sides & Toppings:** basmati rice, noodles of choice (such as udon or rice noodles), crushed peanuts for topping, chopped cilantro for topping

## Directions

1. **Tofu:** cut the drained and pressed tofu into 1 inch cubes or triangles. In a large non-stick pan over medium-high heat, begin heating some sesame oil. Once the oil is warm, add the tofu evenly spaced in the pan with no overlap. Cook for 5-7 minutes per side, or until golden brown and crispy to your liking!
2. **Sauce:** while tofu cooks, prepare the sauce. In a bowl or blender, combine the peanut sauce ingredients and stir or blend until well combined. Add more water if needed for desired consistency.
3. **Stir fry veggies:** remove the tofu from the pan and set aside in a bowl. Add the stir fry veggies to the pan and cook over medium-high for 8-10 minutes, or until crisp and cooked to your liking.
4. **Combine:** add the tofu back to the pan of veggies and drizzle in ¾ of the sauce. Gently toss until combined. (Other option: coat the tofu separately and just drizzle extra sauce on top of the veggies rather than tossing all together.)
5. **Serve:** serve either by itself or with rice or noodles. Drizzle on extra sauce over individual bowls and top with cilantro and/or crushed peanuts. Enjoy!

## Notes

- Cut the tofu into triangles for quicker pan-frying (vs a cube has 4 sides to cook).
- Use a creamy, drippy peanut butter — the thick, bottom-of-the-jar kind is too thick for the sauce.
- Instead of sriracha, use chili paste or red pepper flakes.
- For a healthier version, consider air frying or baking the tofu and eliminating the sesame oil for an oil-free option; use low-sodium soy sauce.
- Choose whatever stir fry veggies you like: any other colored pepper, zucchini, squash, cauliflower, mini corn, and more.
