## Ingredients

**Pate Brisee (Pie Crust)**
- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon sugar
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, chilled and cut into small pieces
- 2 to 3 tablespoons ice water

**Quiche Filling**
- 3 to 4 leeks, white part only, sliced
- 1/2 cup water
- Salt
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 5 to 6 large white mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tablespoon port
- 3 eggs
- 1 1/2 cups whipping cream (or whole milk as substitute)
- An 8-inch partially-cooked pastry shell on a baking sheet
- 1/4 cup grated Swiss cheese
- 1 tablespoon butter cut into pea-sized dots

## Directions

**Crust**
1. Combine flour, salt, and sugar in food processor. Add chilled butter and process until mixture resembles coarse meal, about 8-10 seconds.
2. With machine running, add ice water slowly through feed tube. Pulse until dough holds together without being wet or sticky; avoid processing beyond 30 seconds. Test by squeezing a small amount — add more water if crumbly.
3. Form dough into a ball, flatten into a disk, wrap in plastic, and refrigerate at least 1 hour.
4. Roll out chilled dough quickly on a lightly-floured surface until 2 inches larger than pan. Transfer to mold by rolling onto pin or folding into quarters.
5. Press dough lightly into pan bottom, lifting edges gently. Trim excess by rolling pin over top.
6. Push dough 1/8-inch above pan edge with thumbs to create even, rounded rim. Press decorative edge with dull knife. Prick bottom at 1/2-inch intervals.
7. Line with foil, fill with pie weights, uncooked rice, or beans. Bake at 400°F for 8-9 minutes. Remove foil and bake 2-3 minutes more until just starting to color.

**Quiche assembly and baking**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Boil leeks in covered, heavy-bottomed saucepan over moderately high heat with water, two tablespoons butter, and one teaspoon salt until liquid has almost evaporated. Lower heat and stew gently for 20-30 minutes until leeks are very tender. Set aside.
3. Add one tablespoon butter to pan with sliced mushrooms, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and port. Cover and cook over moderately low heat for 8 minutes. Uncover, raise heat, and boil several minutes until liquid completely evaporates and mushrooms begin sautéing in butter. Stir cooked mushrooms into leek mixture.
4. Beat eggs, cream or milk, and seasoning (salt and pepper) in a large mixing bowl. Gradually stir in leek-mushroom mixture. Check seasoning. Pour into pastry shell. Sprinkle cheese and distribute butter dots. Bake in upper third of oven for 25-30 minutes until puffed and browned.
