## Ingredients

**Crust**
- 3 cups all-purpose flour (360g), divided: 2½ cups for crust, ½ cup for filling
- ⅔ cup powdered sugar (66g), for the shortbread, plus more for dusting
- ½ tsp salt
- 12 tbsp unsalted butter, melted (170g)

**Filling**
- 3 tbsp lemon zest (zest of three lemons)
- 3 cups sugar (600g)
- 8 eggs, large, room temperature
- 1 cup lemon juice, fresh (240mL)

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F and line a 9x13 baking dish with parchment paper.
2. Whisk together 2½ cups of flour (300g) with the powdered sugar and salt, then pour in the melted butter and mix until combined. (A dash of vanilla can be added with the butter at this step, if desired.)
3. Sprinkle the dough into the lined pan and press down into a flat layer. Bake at 350°F for about 20 minutes or until a light golden color.
4. While the crust is baking, add the sugar to a food processor with the zest of three lemons. Pulse until the zest is fully incorporated and the sugar is light yellow. (If you don't have a processor, mince the zest and mix with the sugar in a bag or bowl.)
5. Add the sugar mixture and remaining half cup of flour to a large bowl and mix well.
6. Pour in the lemon juice and add the eggs, then mix very well until completely combined.
7. Pour the filling onto the warm crust, then transfer to the oven and bake for about 25 minutes, turning halfway through. Remove from oven and allow to cool for an hour before chilling in the refrigerator for two hours.
8. Dust with powdered sugar and cut into bars using a clean, sharp, damp knife. Clean and re-wet the knife after each cut.

## Notes

- ALWAYS use fresh lemon juice — don't substitute concentrate or bottled juice.
- Line the baking dish; parchment paper makes it easy to remove from the pan (foil works in a pinch).
- Bars will seem underdone straight from the oven but continue to set as they cool.
- Great make-ahead dessert: cut, freeze individually wrapped on a tray, and store up to three months. Thaw and dust with powdered sugar before serving.
