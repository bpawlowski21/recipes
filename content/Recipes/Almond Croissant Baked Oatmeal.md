Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. "This baked oatmeal will leave you feeling satisfied and energized for the day."

## Ingredients

- 1 medium ripe banana
- 2 cups non-dairy milk
- ¼ cup maple syrup
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ¼ tsp almond extract
- 2½ cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- ½ tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp salt

**Almond topping**
- ⅓ cup almond butter
- ½ tsp almond extract
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- ⅓ cup sliced almonds
- Powdered sugar, for dusting

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease an 8×8" baking dish if not non-stick.
2. In a large bowl, mash the banana until smooth (a few small lumps are okay). Whisk in the milk, maple syrup, vanilla, and almond extract. Add the oats, brown sugar, baking powder, and salt. Fold to combine. Pour into the baking dish.
3. To make the topping, whisk together the almond butter, almond extract, and maple syrup. Drizzle over the oats. Sprinkle over the sliced almonds.
4. Bake 30 minutes until the top is golden brown and the oatmeal is fully set. Let cool 5 minutes before serving. Sprinkle with powdered sugar and enjoy!
