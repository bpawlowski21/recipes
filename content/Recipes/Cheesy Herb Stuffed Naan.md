## Ingredients

**For the Dough:**
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 3/4 teaspoon active dry yeast (optional)
- 3/4 cup warm whole milk
- 1 cup full fat plain Greek yogurt
- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt

**For the Filling:**
- 8 ounces frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
- 1 1/2 cups shredded dry mozzarella cheese
- 8 ounces feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 cup tender mixed herbs (basil, cilantro, chives, dill)
- 1-2 cloves garlic, grated
- 1 pinch crushed red pepper flakes

**For Cooking & Serving:**
- 6 tablespoons salted butter, melted (divided 4+2)
- Honey for serving (optional)

## Directions

- Combine water, honey, and yeast in stand mixer bowl; let sit 5-10 minutes until bubbling. Add milk, yogurt, flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Mix with dough hook until incorporated (2-4 minutes). Dough should be sticky. Knead lightly, cover with plastic wrap, and let rise in warm place for 1 hour until doubled. Alternatively, refrigerate overnight.
- Make filling by combining spinach, herbs, both cheeses, garlic, and pepper flakes in a bowl.
- Divide dough into 8 equal balls. Flatten each ball, add approximately 3 tablespoons filling to center, fold edges up to seal, then roll into a large oval (5-6 inches long, 1/2-inch thick).
- Heat cast iron skillet over medium-high heat until very hot. Brush both sides of naan with 4 tablespoons melted butter. Lightly oil skillet, wipe with paper towel, then place naan on hot surface. Cover with lid and cook 1 minute until bubbles form. Flip and cook uncovered 1-2 minutes until toasted spots appear. Wrap in kitchen towel and repeat with remaining dough.
- Brush warm naan with remaining 2 tablespoons melted butter and serve. Store leftovers in airtight container for 3-4 days or freeze for 3 months.
