## Ingredients

- 1 (16-ounce) block extra-firm tofu, pressed and torn into bite-sized chunks
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch, divided
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 3/4 cup (6 fl oz) fresh squeezed orange juice
- 3 tablespoons (45 ml) seasoned rice vinegar
- 3 tablespoons (45 ml) low-sodium soy sauce
- 3 tablespoon (18g) coconut sugar
- 1 tablespoon (14g) neutral oil, such as avocado or canola, plus more for air-frying the tofu
- 1 bunch green onions, thinly sliced, white and light green parts separated from dark green parts
- 1 large jalapeño, deseeded and diced
- 8 cloves fresh minced garlic
- 1 tablespoon fresh minced ginger
- Toasted sesame seeds, for garnish, optional
- Cooked white or brown rice, for serving, optional

## Directions

1. Preheat your air-fryer to 400°F.
2. While that's preheating, place the tofu pieces in a large bowl with 2 tablespoons cornstarch, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Toss to combine until the tofu is thoroughly coated.
3. Arrange the tofu in a single layer in your air-fryer basket, making sure not to overcrowd it (you may need to do this in batches depending on the size of your air-fryer). Spray or drizzle with a bit of oil and air fry at 400°F for 15 to 20 minutes, tossing or shaking the basket halfway through, until the tofu is golden brown and crisp around the edges. Set aside.
4. In a small bowl, whisk the remaining tablespoon of cornstarch with 2 tablespoons of water. Add the orange juice, seasoned rice vinegar, low-sodium soy sauce and coconut sugar, and whisk once more to combine. Set aside.
5. Heat the oil in a large sauté pan or wok over medium heat. Once the oil is hot, add the white and light green parts of the green onion, jalapeño, garlic and ginger. Cook, stirring often, until fragrant, 2 to 3 minutes.
6. Pour in the orange sauce and bring to a simmer.
7. Reduce the heat to medium-low and continue to simmer, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, 5 to 7 minutes.
8. Add the crispy tofu and toss to coat in the sauce.
9. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds and the remaining dark green parts of the green onion. Serve with cooked rice and enjoy!
