## Ingredients

- 2 13-oz cans coconut milk, 760 mL
- 2 to 4 Tbsp Thai red curry paste, 30 to 60 g
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce, 30 mL
- 1 Tbsp chili garlic sauce, 15 g
- 1 Tbsp grated ginger, I like to use a tube of ginger paste
- 1 8-oz package sliced mushrooms, 226 g
- 1 8-oz can water chestnuts, drained, 226 g
- 1 cup shelled edamame
- 20 to 25 vegetarian frozen dumplings
- Garnish with chopped green onions, chili crisp, sesame seeds
- Serve with rice

## Directions

1. Prep: Preheat oven to 400°F (204°C). If serving with rice, get that going now.
2. Sauce: Into a large (9x13 inch or similar) casserole dish, dump 2 13-oz cans coconut milk, 2 to 4 Tbsp Thai red curry paste, 2 Tbsp soy sauce, 1 Tbsp chili garlic sauce, and 1 Tbsp grated ginger. Whisk to combine.
3. Fillings: Stir in 1 8-oz package sliced mushrooms, 1 8-oz can water chestnuts (drained), and 1 cup shelled edamame.
   Place 20 to 25 vegetarian frozen dumplings on top of the mixture in a single layer so they aren't overlapping. Spoon some sauce over each dumpling.
4. Bake: Tightly cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes, or until sauce is bubbling.
5. Uncover: Remove foil and return to the oven for 10 to 15 minutes to allow the tops of the dumplings to become a bit crispy and golden brown.
6. Serve: Spoon into bowls with rice, topping with chopped green onions, chili crisp, and sesame seeds.

## Notes

- Start with less curry paste and stir in more after combining the sauce to suit your spicy level preference.
- Mushrooms can be omitted if you are not a fan. They can also be replaced with cubes of silken tofu or a can of chickpeas.
- Dumplings: We use most of a 24 oz bag. Bibigo Vegetable Potstickers are our preferred brand.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container for up to 4 days in the fridge. Reheat gently in the microwave, adding a splash of water or broth if needed. Leftovers will not freeze well.
- Can I make it ahead of time? This makes a great prep-ahead casserole! Assemble everything up until the point where you would add the dumplings (don't add them). Tightly cover and store in the fridge for up to 2 days. When ready to bake, add the dumplings and bake as instructed.
- Can I swap the mushrooms? Yes. Cubes of silken tofu make a softer, protein-rich substitute that absorbs the curry sauce beautifully. A drained can of chickpeas adds protein and a slightly firmer texture. Both options work well if you're not a mushroom fan.
