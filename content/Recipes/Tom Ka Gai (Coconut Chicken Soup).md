Saved from an old (circa 2010) Allrecipes.com page, "the quintessential Thai soup." 5,738 people had saved it and it had 128 reviews at the time it was saved.

Prep Time: 15 min · Cook Time: 20 min · Ready In: 35 min

## Ingredients

- ¾ pound boneless, skinless chicken meat
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 (14 ounce) cans coconut milk
- 2 cups water
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh ginger root
- 4 tablespoons fish sauce
- ¼ cup fresh lime juice
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper
- ½ teaspoon ground turmeric
- 2 tablespoons thinly sliced green onion
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro

## Directions

1. Cut chicken into thin strips and sauté in oil for 2 to 3 minutes until the chicken turns white.
2. In a pot, bring the coconut milk and water to a boil. Reduce heat.
3. Add ginger, fish sauce, lime juice, cayenne, and turmeric. Simmer until the chicken is done, 10 to 15 minutes.
4. Sprinkle with scallions and cilantro, and serve steaming hot.

## Notes

- Nutrition (per serving): Calories 433, Total Fat 41.1g, Cholesterol 38mg.
- Multiple reviewers note this is a "mock"/simplified version of true tom kha gai — true galangal ("kah") rather than ginger is more traditional. One reviewer suggests keeping the soup at a lower temperature to prevent the coconut milk from curdling.
