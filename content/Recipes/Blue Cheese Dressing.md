## Ingredients

- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- ¼ cup sour cream
- 2 ounces (60g) crumbled blue cheese
- 1 tablespoon minced parsley
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice (optional)
- Pinch fine sea salt
- Pinch fresh ground pepper
- Milk, buttermilk, or water to thin (optional)

## Directions

- Combine sour cream, mayonnaise, blue cheese, parsley, lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a small bowl; stir until well blended and creamy.
- Use the back of a spoon to press some blue cheese crumbles into the sauce for enhanced flavor. The mixture will be thick, ideal for dipping.
- For a pourable salad dressing consistency, stir in one to two tablespoons of milk, buttermilk, or water. Taste and adjust seasonings with additional lemon juice, salt, and pepper as needed.
- Store tightly covered in the refrigerator for up to one week.
