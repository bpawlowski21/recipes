## Ingredients

**Preferment:**
- 1 1/4 cups (150g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 1 teaspoon (6g) table salt
- 1/2 teaspoon instant yeast
- 1/2 cup (113g) cool water

**Dough:**
- All of the preferment
- 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons to 1 1/4 cups (255g to 284g) lukewarm water
- 1 teaspoon (6g) table salt
- 3 1/2 cups (420g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 1 tablespoon King Arthur Pizza Dough Flavor (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon instant yeast

**Filling:**
- 2 1/2 cups (283g) grated Gruyère cheese (or sharp cheddar, or provolone/mozzarella blend)
- 1 tablespoon garlic oil (optional)
- 1 tablespoon King Arthur Pizza Seasoning (optional)

## Directions

- Mix flour, salt, yeast, and water for preferment; cover and rest overnight at room temperature.
- Combine risen preferment with water, salt, flour, flavor, and yeast; knead until smooth.
- Place in greased bowl, cover, and let rise 1.5 to 2 hours until nearly doubled.
- Pat dough into 3/4"-thick rectangle (9" x 12"); spritz with water and sprinkle cheese and seasoning.
- Roll into log from long side, sealing edges and cheese pockets.
- Place seam-side down on floured surface or parchment-lined baking sheet.
- Cut into four crosswise slices for mini-loaves or halve for two standard loaves.
- Arrange on baking sheets, cut-side up.
- Cover and let rise 1 to 1.5 hours until puffy. Preheat oven to 425°F midway through.
- Spread loaves open slightly; spritz with warm water.
- Bake 25-35 minutes for mini-loaves or 35-40 minutes for full-sized loaves until golden and cheese melts. Rotate pans halfway if baking four loaves.
- Cool on pans; serve warm.
