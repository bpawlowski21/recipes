## Ingredients

**Cake:**
- 1 1/2 cups (180g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1 cup (198g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup (21g) unsweetened cocoa (Dutch-process or natural)
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 1/2 teaspoon espresso powder (optional)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 cup (227g) water
- 1/3 cup (66g) vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon (14g) cider vinegar or white vinegar
- 1 teaspoon King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract

**Icing:**
- 1 1/2 cups (255g) semisweet chocolate chips
- 1/2 cup (113g) half-and-half (or 1/3 cup cold brewed coffee/water for non-dairy option)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease an 8" square or 9" round pan (at least 2" deep).
- Whisk together all dry ingredients in a medium bowl.
- In a separate bowl, whisk water, oil, vinegar, and vanilla. Combine wet and dry ingredients, stirring until blended. Pour batter into prepared pan.
- Bake 30-35 minutes until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean or with moist crumbs.
- For icing: Combine chocolate chips and half-and-half in a heatproof bowl. Heat in microwave in 30-second increments, stirring between each, until melted. Stir until smooth and pour/spread over cake. Allow icing to set before slicing.
- Store covered at room temperature for several days; freeze for longer storage.
