## Ingredients

- 1 cup plant milk
- 1 tsp vinegar
- 2½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp salt
- 8oz tofu of choice, cut into ½-inch patties
- Oil of choice, for frying
- Salt to finish
- Sandwich buns
- Sandwich pickles

## Directions

1. Warm the oil to 350°F in a pot or saucepan.
2. In a shallow bowl, combine 2 cups flour, baking powder, garlic powder, onion powder, 1 tsp salt and pepper.
3. In a separate shallow bowl, combine the nondairy milk, vinegar, ½ cup flour, paprika, 1 tsp salt and pepper.
4. Using a spoon, pour small amounts of the wet batter into the dry batter (about 3 tbsp of wet batter).
5. Coat the tofu in the wet batter first, then carefully move it to the dry batter. Repeat so it's been coated twice in wet and dry.
6. Set on a wire rack or plate until the oil is ready.
7. When the oil reaches 350°F, place the coated pieces in the oil. Cook 6–8 minutes, flipping at the halfway mark.
8. Once golden brown and crispy, remove and transfer to a wire rack or towel-lined dish. Sprinkle with salt.
9. Assemble on sandwich buns with pickles.
