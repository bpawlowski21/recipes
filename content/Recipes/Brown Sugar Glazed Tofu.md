## Ingredients

**Tofu Marinade**
- 1 block extra firm tofu, pressed and drained
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- Juice of half a lemon
- 1 tsp garlic chili sauce
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 3 cloves minced garlic
- ¼ small onion, minced
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 30–35 whole cloves (the spice)

**Brown Sugar Glaze**
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp spicy brown mustard

## Directions

1. Press and drain the tofu really well. Place on a clean surface and, using a toothpick or needle, poke holes all over the surface. Cut a shallow crosshatch pattern into both sides.
2. Mix the marinade ingredients in a bowl. Place the tofu in a resealable bag and pour the marinade over it. Seal, gently shake to coat, and refrigerate to marinate overnight (up to 24 hours for max flavor).
3. Preheat oven to 375°F. Remove the tofu from the marinade and place on a lined baking sheet. Place whole cloves into each crosshatch intersection. Bake 25 minutes.
4. While baking, prepare the glaze by combining the brown sugar and mustard in a mug. Microwave 15 seconds, then stir.
5. Carefully baste the tofu with a portion of the glaze until covered, and bake 10 more minutes.
6. Remove from the oven, remove the cloves with a fork, and brush with more glaze. Bake an additional 15–20 minutes, until the edges appear crisp.
7. Remove from the oven and let rest. Cut into desired slabs and enjoy.
