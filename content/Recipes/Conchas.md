## Ingredients

**Starter**
- 3/4 cup (170g) water
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 cup (120g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour

**Dough**
- 1 1/4 teaspoons (8g) table salt
- 2 3/4 cups (330g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 1/2 cup (99g) granulated sugar
- 3 tablespoons (43g) unsalted butter, softened
- 2 large eggs plus 1 egg yolk, white reserved
- 2 teaspoons Mexican vanilla extract

**Topping**
- 1/2 cup (99g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons Mexican vanilla extract
- 5 tablespoons (71g) unsalted butter, soft
- 2/3 cup (80g) King Arthur Unbleached Bread Flour
- 1 large egg white (reserved)
- Food coloring (optional)

## Directions

- Combine flour, yeast, and water for starter; cover and rest 1 hour until bubbly.
- Add dough ingredients to starter; mix and knead until sticky and elastic.
- Let dough rise covered 60-90 minutes until doubled.
- Prepare topping by mixing sugar, salt, vanilla, and butter until smooth; stir in flour; set aside.
- Divide dough into 10 pieces; roll into balls on greased surface.
- Place balls on greased or parchment-lined baking sheet.
- Divide topping into portions; flatten each into 3-4" disks between parchment sheets.
- Brush roll tops with beaten egg white; add topping disk; flatten slightly.
- Let rise covered 30-45 minutes until puffy.
- Preheat oven to 350°F.
- Using sharp knife, score designs into topping without cutting dough.
- Nest baking sheet into second pan to prevent over-browning bottoms.
- Bake 18-24 minutes until golden brown; cool on pan before serving.
- Store wrapped at room temperature for three days or freeze up to one month.
