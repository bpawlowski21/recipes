Total Time: 30 minutes

## Ingredients

**Roasted Carrots**
- 1 bag (32 ounces) rainbow carrots, peeled and sliced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- ¼ teaspoon each garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, and smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper

**Yogurt Sauce**
- ½ cup whole milk Greek yogurt
- ½ tablespoon olive oil
- ½ clove garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper

**Additional Toppings**
- Honey (handwritten: use 1–2 Tbsp)
- ¼ cup cilantro, roughly chopped
- ¼ cup pistachios, roughly chopped

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees. Spread the carrots out onto a large baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil. Combine the spices, salt, and pepper in a small dish and sprinkle over the carrots. Gently toss the carrots in the oil and seasoning. Roast for 20 minutes until tender and the edges are slightly charred.
2. Whisk together the yogurt, olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper.
3. Spoon the yogurt sauce over the carrots. Finish with a drizzle of honey and a sprinkle of cilantro and pistachios.
