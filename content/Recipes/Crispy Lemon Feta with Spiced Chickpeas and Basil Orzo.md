## Ingredients

**Basil Orzo**
- 1 cup dry orzo pasta
- 1 tablespoon salted butter
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped, plus more for serving

**Lemon Feta and Chickpeas**
- 1 (8 ounce) block feta cheese, patted dry, sliced into 1/4 inch pieces
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons plus 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 (16 ounce) cans chickpeas, drained and patted dry
- 3 cloves sliced garlic and 1 clove grated garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 pinch crushed red pepper flakes
- Kosher salt
- 2 zucchini or yellow summer squash, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme (or 2 teaspoons dried)
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tablespoon raw sesame seeds
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup mixed tender herbs (mint, dill, cilantro)

## Directions

- Cook orzo per package directions until al dente. Drain and toss with butter, basil, and salt.
- Coat feta slices with cornstarch. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Arrange feta in a single layer and cook until golden, about 1 minute per side. Remove to a plate.
- Add remaining 1/4 cup oil, chickpeas, 3 cloves sliced garlic, paprika, red pepper flakes, and salt to the skillet. Cook 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until chickpeas crisp. Add zucchini, thyme, lemon zest, and sesame seeds. Cook another 5 minutes until zucchini crisps on the edges. Remove from heat.
- Combine yogurt, lemon juice, 1 grated garlic clove, and salt in a small bowl.
- Spread yogurt in the base of a shallow bowl. Top with orzo, crispy feta, and the chickpea mixture. Garnish with fresh herbs.
