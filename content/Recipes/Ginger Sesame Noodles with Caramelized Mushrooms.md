## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons low sodium soy sauce or tamari
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 3 tablespoons creamy peanut butter or tahini
- 1 tablespoon molasses or pomegranate molasses (optional)
- 2 tablespoons fresh grated ginger
- 3 cloves garlic, grated
- 8 ounces Chinese style egg noodles or rice noodles
- 2 1/2 cups low sodium vegetable or chicken broth
- 2 cups fresh baby spinach, roughly chopped
- 2 tablespoons sesame or extra virgin olive oil
- 3 cups shiitake or cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 2 shallots, chopped
- Black pepper
- 2 tablespoons raw sesame seeds
- 2 green onions, chopped, for serving

## Directions

- Combine 1/2 cup soy sauce, honey, balsamic vinegar, rice vinegar, peanut butter/tahini, molasses, 1 tablespoon ginger, 1 clove garlic, and 1/3 cup water in a bowl to make the sauce.
- Cook noodles per package directions and drain. Add broth and half the soy sauce mixture to the noodle pot. Simmer over medium heat and stir in spinach. Keep warm.
- Heat sesame oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms and cook undisturbed 5 minutes until golden. Add shallots, 1 tablespoon ginger, and 2 cloves garlic; cook 4-5 minutes until fragrant. Add 2 tablespoons soy sauce and black pepper; cook 2-3 minutes until caramelized. Add sesame seeds and cook 2 minutes. Remove mushrooms to a plate.
- Add remaining soy sauce mixture to the skillet and simmer over medium heat. Add noodles and toss to combine; cook 2-3 minutes until sauce coats noodles. Remove from heat.
- Ladle broth into bowls, add noodles and toss. Top with mushrooms, green onions, and chili oil. Serve.
