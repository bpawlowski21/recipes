## Ingredients

- 2 2/3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar, packed
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 stick (4 ounces) unsalted butter, very cold and cut into tiny pieces
- 1 large egg
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 12-ounce bag chocolate chips
- 1 cup pecans, chopped

**For egg wash:**
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon milk or water
- 2 tablespoons confectioners' sugar, for sprinkling

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 400°F. Line baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Combine flour, salt, baking powder, brown sugar, and cinnamon in a large bowl.
- Cut cold butter into small cubes and work into mixture until it resembles a coarse meal.
- Whisk together egg, cream, and vanilla in a measuring cup. Add to flour mixture along with chocolate chips and pecans, stirring until just moistened.
- Knead dough on floured surface, then shape into an 8-inch disc. Cut into 8 wedges and transfer to prepared sheet, spacing 2 inches apart.
- Brush each scone with egg wash.
- Bake 18-20 minutes until light golden brown. Cool 10 minutes, then sprinkle with confectioners' sugar.
