## Ingredients

### Peach Cobbler Filling
- 3 pounds peaches, peeled and sliced into ¼" pieces
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar, firmly packed
- 1½ tablespoons cornstarch
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 1½ tablespoons lemon juice

### Peach Cobbler Topping
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 8 tablespoons unsalted butter, cold, cut into pieces
- ½ cup heavy cream, cold
- ⅓ cup milk, cold

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F and lightly butter a 9×13 baking dish.
- Combine sugars, cornstarch, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl. Add peaches and lemon juice, tossing until coated. Set aside.
- Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a food processor and pulse until combined.
- Scatter cold butter pieces over the flour mixture and pulse until resembling coarse crumbs.
- Add heavy cream and milk, pulsing briefly until the mixture forms a soft dough.
- Pour peaches and juices into prepared baking dish and distribute evenly.
- Drop cobbler batter over peaches, distributing fairly evenly.
- Bake for 45-55 minutes until the topping is golden brown and cooked through.
- Cool several minutes, then serve warm with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.
