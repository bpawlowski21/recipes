## Ingredients

- 2½ cups (20 ounces) milk, divided
- ½ cup (4 ounces) Baileys Irish Cream
- ½ cup (3.5 ounces) granulated sugar
- 8 ounces semisweet chocolate, coarsely chopped
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon espresso powder
- ⅓ cup cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract

## Directions

- In a heavy-bottomed medium saucepan, combine 2¼ cups milk, Baileys, sugar, chocolate, salt, cinnamon, and espresso powder. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until chocolate melts (6-8 minutes).
- In a small bowl, whisk together remaining milk and cornstarch until smooth. Slowly add this mixture to the saucepan.
- Increase heat to medium and whisk constantly while bringing the mixture to a simmer. Reduce heat and cook for 2-3 minutes until thickened.
- Remove from heat and stir in vanilla.
- Pour pudding into a large bowl or individual cups. Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface to prevent skin formation.
- Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or up to 3 days before serving.
