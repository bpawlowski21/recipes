Culinary school practical formula (baker's-scale weights). Total: 454 grams / 1 pound.

## Ingredients

- 135 grams water (100°F)
- 17 grams sugar (granulated)
- 17 grams DMS (dry milk solids)
- 5 grams salt
- 4 grams instant yeast
- 17 grams whole eggs
- 243 grams bread flour
- 17 grams shortening or butter

## Directions

1. Calculate final dough temperature for 80°F.
2. Place water into mixing bowl. Combine the sugar, DMS, and salt.
3. Dissolve the sugar, DMS, and salt into the water using a wire whisk.
4. Add the whole eggs and mix well.
5. Add the yeast and dissolve well.
6. Add the bread flour and mix just until a dough begins to form.
7. Add the shortening/butter and continue mixing, on second speed, until final clear, about 12 to 15 minutes.
8. Place the dough onto a lightly floured surface, cover with plastic sheet, and proof for 60 to 90 minutes.
9. Place rounded dough ball into a floured proofing box and allow to proof for an additional 30 to 45 minutes.
10. Cut into 8 pieces to bench rest for 15 minutes, then shape into desired shapes.
11. Place finished shapes onto parchment-lined sheet tray or combi oven tray. Egg wash, seed, and final proof at 95°F and 75% humidity until almost double in size (estimated time 30 minutes).
12. Bake at 400°F for 12 to 15 minutes (combi oven 5-6 minutes @ 380).
