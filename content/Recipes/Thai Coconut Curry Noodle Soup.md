## Ingredients

- ½ package rice noodles
- 2 cups broccoli florets, cut small
- ¾ package tofu, drained and gently pressed with towels to remove water
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 cups sliced mushrooms
- 2 tsp oil
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp ginger paste (or sub fresh grated ginger or ½ tsp ginger powder)
- 1½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp pepper (optional)
- ¼ cup red curry paste
- 1 can full-fat coconut milk
- Green onion and lime juice, for serving

## Directions

1. Cut the tofu into cubes and add to a large pan with some oil, seasoning with a generous dash of salt and garlic powder. Brown on a few sides and remove from the pan (set aside).
2. Cook the rice noodles according to package directions, drain, rinse, and sprinkle with a touch of oil, toss.
3. Separately, steam the broccoli florets until just crisp-tender and let cool.
4. Meanwhile, in the large pan you cooked the tofu in, add oil, onions, and mushrooms. After 7 minutes, add the rest of the ingredients down to the coconut milk. Stir well, let simmer gently for 5 minutes.
5. Add the tofu, noodles, and broccoli. Stir and top with green onion and a squeeze of lime juice.
