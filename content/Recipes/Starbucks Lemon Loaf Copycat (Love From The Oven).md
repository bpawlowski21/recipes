## Ingredients

**Cake**
- ½ cup butter, softened
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- ⅓ cup sour cream
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon lemon extract
- 3 large eggs, room temperature
- 2 tablespoons lemon zest
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1½ cups all-purpose flour
- ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt

**Glaze**
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon lemon extract
- Pinch of salt (optional)

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. In a mixer bowl, combine butter and sugar, beating well. Add eggs and mix until combined. Incorporate sour cream, vanilla extract, lemon extract, lemon zest, and lemon juice.
2. In a separate bowl, combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. Add dry ingredients to wet ingredients on low speed, avoiding overmixing.
4. Pour batter into a 9x5 loaf pan sprayed with non-stick cooking spray.
5. Place in oven and immediately reduce temperature to 350°F. Bake approximately 45 minutes until a cake tester inserted in the center comes out clean.
6. Remove from oven and allow to cool.
7. Combine glaze ingredients in a bowl and mix until combined. Adjust consistency as desired.
8. Remove loaf from pan and pour or spoon glaze over the top. Allow to set for 20+ minutes before slicing.
