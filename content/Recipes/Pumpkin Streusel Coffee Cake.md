## Ingredients

### Topping
- 2/3 cup (131g) granulated sugar
- Pinch of table salt
- 2/3 cup (80g) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 cup (28g) chopped pecans, optional
- 4 tablespoons (57g) butter, melted

### Filling
- 1/3 cup (71g) light or dark brown sugar, packed
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon unsweetened cocoa powder, optional

### Cake
- 1/3 cup (67g) vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 cup (198g) granulated sugar
- 1 cup (213g) pumpkin purée
- 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
- 1 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 1/2 cups (180g) all-purpose flour

## Directions

- Heat oven to 350°F and grease an 8" square or 9" round pan.
- Mix topping ingredients and set aside. Combine filling ingredients and set aside.
- Beat oil, eggs, sugar, pumpkin, spices, salt, and baking powder until smooth. Stir in flour until smooth.
- Spread half the batter into the prepared pan and sprinkle the filling evenly over it.
- Spread remaining batter atop the filling and gently swirl with a knife.
- Sprinkle topping over the batter.
- Bake 40-45 minutes until light brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
- Cool 20 minutes before serving from the pan.
