## Ingredients

**Peaches**
- 4 tablespoons salted cultured butter
- 5-6 ripe but firm peaches, thinly sliced (about 6 cups sliced)
- 1 cup packed light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon bourbon (optional)
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

**Topping**
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup finely ground pecans
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 stick (8 tablespoons) salted cultured butter, melted
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- Cinnamon sugar for dusting

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. In a 12-inch oven-safe skillet over medium heat, brown 4 tablespoons butter until golden and fragrant (about 3-4 minutes). Remove from heat and add peaches, 1/2 cup brown sugar, bourbon if using, and vanilla. Toss to combine and transfer to oven for 10-15 minutes. Reduce heat to 375°F.
2. In a bowl, whisk together flour, ground pecans, baking powder, cinnamon, and remaining 1/2 cup brown sugar.
3. Add melted butter and milk to dry ingredients; mix until just combined.
4. Remove peaches from oven and dollop batter over them. Dust with cinnamon sugar.
5. Bake 25-30 minutes until golden on top. Cool 5 minutes. Serve warm or at room temperature with ice cream.
