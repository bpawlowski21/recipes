## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup coconut milk
- 2 envelopes Knox gelatin
- 3/4 cup cream of coconut
- 1 cup Malibu coconut rum
- Food coloring (black, brown, blue, green)

**Equipment:** 2 trays 1.5-inch diameter sphere molds, small food-grade painting brush, large round piping tip (optional)

## Directions

- To a pot, add coconut milk and gelatin.
- Mix together then wait 5 minutes to allow the gelatin to bloom.
- Turn on the heat and warm until all gelatin has dissolved.
- Remove from heat then add cream of coconut and Malibu coconut rum, mix together and set aside.
- Pour the liquid into your molds, making sure to reserve 1/2 cup of the liquid (for the irises and pupils).
- Refrigerate a minimum of 5 hours until set.
- Once set, soak the bottom in hot water for 30 seconds to help loosen.
- Gently pull the jelled eyeballs away from the sides of the mold, then push out from the bottom.
- Warm your 1/2 cup of reserved liquid gently in a pot until just melted.
- Pour into bowls along with food coloring of your choice (black, green, brown, and blue).
- Paint on the irises — use a piping tip as a guide, brushing the color inwards towards the center.
- Add a black dot in the center for the pupils (a metal straw works as a guide).
- Refrigerate until ready to serve. Can be made up to 3 days ahead of time.

**Non-coconut version:** bloom gelatin in 3/4 cup water for 5 minutes, then add 1/2 cup cream cheese and 1/2 cup marshmallow fluff and melt; remove from heat and stir in 3/4 cup milk.
