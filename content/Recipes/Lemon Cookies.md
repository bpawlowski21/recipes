## Ingredients

- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 1/2 cups (300 g) granulated sugar
- 2 1/2 tablespoons lemon zest
- 1 large egg + 1 egg yolk, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon lemon extract
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 cups (375 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cream of tartar
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup (70 g) powdered sugar, for rolling

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F and line baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Using an electric mixer, combine butter, sugar, and lemon zest until creamy.
- Mix in egg, egg yolk, lemon extract, and vanilla extract until incorporated.
- In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, cornstarch, cream of tartar, baking soda, and salt.
- Gradually add dry ingredients to wet mixture on low speed, scraping bowl sides thoroughly.
- Scoop dough into 1 1/2-tablespoon balls, roll in powdered sugar, and space 2 inches apart on baking sheet.
- Bake 9-11 minutes at 350°F. Allow cookies to cool completely on sheet before serving.
