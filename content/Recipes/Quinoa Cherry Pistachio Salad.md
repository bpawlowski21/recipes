Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram comment screenshot. "When cherry season happens this pretty is always in my fridge. It can really be made with any grain, but I love it with quinoa."

## Ingredients

- 1 cup uncooked quinoa, rinsed
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 2 cups pitted and halved fresh cherries
- 1 cup Kirby cucumbers, sliced
- ½ cup chopped scallions
- ⅓ cup pistachios
- 2 large handfuls arugula
- ¼ cup chopped parsley
- ¼ cup chopped mint

**Vinaigrette**
- ½ cup olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp orange juice
- 3 tbsp pure maple syrup
- Zest of one orange
- 1 garlic clove, chopped
- ¼ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper

## Directions

1. Add all vinaigrette ingredients to a jar and shake well to combine.
2. Add the quinoa and broth to a pot and bring to a boil. Stir, cover, reduce to a simmer, and let simmer 15 minutes, or until the broth is absorbed. Remove from heat and let sit covered 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
3. When the quinoa has cooled, add all the components to a large bowl, drizzle with the vinaigrette, and toss to combine.
