## Ingredients

- 2 cups warm water
- 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast (1 packet)
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 5 - 5 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 15-20 drops red food coloring
- 6 tablespoons salted butter
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1/4 cup baking soda for boiling
- Pretzel salt or flaky sea salt for sprinkling

### Garlic Butter (optional)
- 6 tablespoons salted butter
- 2-3 cloves garlic, finely chopped or grated
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves

## Directions

- Combine water, yeast, and honey in stand mixer bowl with dough hook. Let sit 5-10 minutes until bubbly. Add flour, salt, and 2 tablespoons butter. Knead 1-2 minutes, adding extra flour as needed.
- Divide dough in half. Cover one half. Add red food coloring to other half and knead until uniform color. Cover both and let rise 1-2 hours until doubled.
- Preheat oven to 425°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment.
- Punch down dough. Divide each half into 16 pieces. Roll into 8-inch ropes. Twist one white and one red rope together, shape into candy cane, place on baking sheet. Repeat with remaining ropes. Cover with damp towel and let rise 15-30 minutes.
- Boil water with baking soda. Lower 2-3 bagels at a time into water. Boil 3 minutes, flip, boil 1-2 minutes more. Remove with slotted spoon to parchment-lined sheet. Repeat with all bagels.
- Reshape into candy canes if needed. Brush with beaten egg. Bake 10 minutes. Remove, brush with 4 tablespoons melted butter, sprinkle generously with salt. Bake 10-15 minutes until golden brown.
- For garlic butter (optional, for savory naan-style version): heat butter and garlic in skillet over medium heat 2-3 minutes until light brown and fragrant. Add thyme. Drizzle over bagels. Serve warm.
