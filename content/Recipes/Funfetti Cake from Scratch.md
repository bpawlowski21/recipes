## Ingredients

### Cake Layers
- 9 tablespoons (127 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3 cups (600 g) granulated sugar
- 1 cup (236 ml) neutral cooking oil
- 4 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 4 cups + 2 tablespoons (516 g) all-purpose flour
- 4½ teaspoons baking powder
- 1½ teaspoons table salt
- 1½ cups (355 ml) milk
- 9 large egg whites, room temperature
- ½ cup (80 g) sprinkles (jimmies or sequin style)

### Buttercream Frosting
- 1 lb (453 g) unsalted butter, softened
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 6 cups (750 g) powdered sugar
- 6 tablespoons (89 ml) heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons LorAnn Princess emulsion or vanilla extract

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F and prepare three 8-inch round pans with greasing, flouring, and parchment paper.
- Beat softened butter on medium-low speed until creamy.
- Add sugar and oil; beat until combined.
- Stir in vanilla extract.
- Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a separate bowl.
- Alternately add flour mixture and milk to butter mixture, starting and ending with flour. Mix until nearly combined between additions.
- In a clean, grease-free bowl, beat egg whites until stiff peaks form.
- Gently fold egg whites and sprinkles into batter using a spatula, being careful not to deflate.
- Divide batter evenly among prepared pans.
- Bake at 350°F for 35-40 minutes until a toothpick inserted in centers comes out clean or with few crumbs. Rotate pans halfway through.
- Cool 15 minutes, then invert onto cooling racks and cool completely.
- Beat softened butter until creamy, add salt.
- Gradually add powdered sugar, about one cup at a time.
- Add heavy cream one tablespoon at a time.
- Add flavoring and mix for 30 seconds.
- Layer cooled cakes with frosting between layers; apply crumb coat and freeze 10-15 minutes, then frost completely.
