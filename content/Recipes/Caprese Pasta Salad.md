"This Caprese Pasta Salad is our favorite side dish recipe for the summer. It's ideal to make in advance and takes less than 30 minutes. With juicy cherry tomatoes, mozzarella pearls, and a balsamic vinaigrette, everyone will love this recipe."

Prep Time: 15 mins · Cook Time: 10 mins · Total Time: 25 mins · Calories: 594

**Equipment:** Large pot, large serving bowl

## Ingredients

**Pasta Salad**
- 1 pound short pasta (such as gemelli, farfalle, orecchiette, or cavatappi)
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 cups mozzarella pearls
- ½ cup torn fresh basil
- ⅓ cup freshly grated Pecorino Romano cheese
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

**Homemade Balsamic Vinaigrette**
- ½ cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon honey
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to package directions until al dente.
2. Drain the pasta and rinse briefly under cold water to stop the cooking. Transfer the pasta to a large serving bowl and toss with 1 tablespoon olive oil to prevent sticking.
3. Add the tomatoes, mozzarella pearls, basil, and Pecorino Romano to the bowl.
4. Add the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, garlic, Dijon mustard, honey, salt, and pepper to a mason jar. Seal tightly and shake until combined.
5. Pour the vinaigrette over the pasta salad and toss gently to combine. Season with additional salt and pepper to taste.
6. Serve immediately or refrigerate until chilled before serving.

## Notes

- No time to make the vinaigrette? Use your favorite store-bought balsamic vinaigrette instead — choose one made with olive oil for the best flavor.
- Mozzarella swap: freshly chopped mozzarella (like a ball of Fior di Latte) works just as well if you don't have pearls on hand.
- To prevent the pasta from sticking while cooling, rinse briefly under cool water after draining and toss with a small drizzle of olive oil.
- Want to add protein? Grilled chicken or grilled Italian sausage make great additions.
- Make it ahead: prep a few hours in advance; if making ahead, reserve a bit of the dressing and toss it in right before serving to keep everything fresh and vibrant.
- Storage: keep leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Not recommended for freezing.
