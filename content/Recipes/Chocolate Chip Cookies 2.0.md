## Ingredients

- 2 cups (284 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 9 tablespoons (126 g) unsalted butter at room temperature
- ¾ cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- ¾ cup (150 g) brown sugar
- ¾ teaspoon fine salt
- 1 large egg
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tablespoon pure vanilla extract
- 5 oz (142 g) Ghirardelli 52% Cacao Dark Chocolate Chips
- 2 oz (57 g) Ghirardelli 72% Cacao Dark Chocolate Chips, chopped
- Flaky salt for sprinkling (optional)

## Directions

- Adjust oven rack to middle position and preheat to 400°F (200°C). Line two sheet pans with parchment paper.
- In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and baking soda.
- In a stand mixer with paddle attachment, beat butter on medium speed for about 1 minute until creamy. Add both sugars and salt, beating for 2-3 minutes until light and fluffy. Scrape bowl, add egg, yolk, and vanilla, mixing until smooth. Add flour mixture on low speed until combined. Mix in chocolate on low speed, then finish with a rubber spatula.
- Form dough into 2-tablespoon balls (50 g each) and place 8 cookies per sheet pan. Sprinkle with flaky salt if desired.
- Bake one pan at a time, rotating halfway through, for 10-11 minutes until tops turn golden and cookies slightly puff and crinkle. Remove from oven and gently press cookie tops with a spatula to flatten.
- Let rest on pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack to cool completely. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to three days.
