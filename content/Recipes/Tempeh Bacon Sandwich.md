## Ingredients

**Tempeh Bacon**
- 1 block tempeh, cut into strips
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 2 tsp maple syrup
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar

**Other sandwich ingredients**
- Toasted sourdough (or favorite bread)
- Avocado
- Sliced tomato
- Mustard
- Pickles
- Lettuce (post used arugula)

## Directions

1. Add the tempeh to a container and pour over the soy sauce, apple cider vinegar, and maple syrup. Seal and shake to fully coat. Let marinate at least 1 hour, up to 24.
2. Preheat oven to 350°F. Add the tempeh to a parchment-lined sheet pan. Bake 25–30 minutes.
3. Top toasted bread with avocado, tomato, mustard, lettuce, pickles, and the tempeh bacon. Dig in.
