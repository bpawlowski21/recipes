## Ingredients

- Puff pastry, cut into squares
- Fig jam
- Goat cheese (cream cheese works if you're not a goat cheese fan)
- Walnut pieces
- Mini muffin pan

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Spray a mini muffin tin with non-stick spray and place one puff pastry square in each muffin cup, pushing it down.
3. Add a teaspoon of goat cheese and top with fig jam.
4. Sprinkle walnut pieces on top.
5. Bake for 15 minutes. Serve and enjoy.
