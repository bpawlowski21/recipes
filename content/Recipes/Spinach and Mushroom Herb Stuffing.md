## Ingredients

- 2 large onions, diced
- 4 stalks celery, chopped
- 1 lb sliced mushrooms (Wendy's amendment to the printed 8oz; she used Baby Bella)
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil + 2 tablespoons, divided (or melted butter)
- 2 tablespoons chopped sage
- ~~2 tablespoons chopped rosemary~~ (crossed out)
- ~~2 tablespoons chopped thyme~~ (crossed out)
- ~~1/3 cup chopped parsley~~ (crossed out) — replaced with chopped chestnuts
- 1-5oz bag baby spinach
- 8 cups toasted bread cubes
- 1 1/2-2 cups vegetable broth
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- Lots of freshly ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Grease a 13x9x2-inch or 3 qt baking dish.
2. Place 2 tablespoons oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add mushrooms and a pinch of salt and pepper. Sauté until mushrooms are nice and browned, about 8 minutes. Transfer to a small bowl.
3. Place 1/4 cup oil in same skillet. Add onions and celery plus a pinch of salt. Sauté until tender, about 12 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, preheat oven to 350°F. If your bread needs toasting, place cubes on lined baking sheet and bake until bread is crusty, about 10-15 minutes. Transfer to large bowl to cool.
5. Once onions are tender, add herbs and sauté for another minute. Add spinach and cook for a few minutes more, or until wilted.
6. In a small bowl or liquid measuring cup, whisk broth (start with 1 1/2 cups), eggs, salt, and pepper. Add egg mixture and vegetable mixture to bowl with bread, tossing to combine. Add another 1/2 cup of broth if the bread is crunchy — use less for softer bread. It should be nice and moist but not at all soupy. Spoon mixture into baking dish.
7. Cover with foil and bake for 40 minutes. Remove foil and bake for another 15 minutes. Garnish with fresh parsley.

## Notes

- Wendy's handwritten substitutions on the scan: 1 lb mushrooms (not 8oz); rosemary, thyme, and parsley crossed out and replaced with chopped chestnuts.
