Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post (comments section, 2 slides). "Simple, satisfying and super delicious! Perfect with a drizzle of good olive oil and crusty bread to dunk with."

## Ingredients

- 1 leek, chopped
- 2 (15 oz) cans (850g) butter beans
- 2 ribs celery, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 cups spinach, packed and chopped
- ⅓ cup uncooked farro
- 5 cups vegetable broth
- 2 tbsp (30g) vegan butter
- 1½ tsp (7.5g) thyme
- ½ tsp (2.5g) tarragon
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 tsp (10g) miso paste
- Juice from ½ lemon
- Salt & pepper to taste
- Olive oil for topping

## Directions

1. Heat the butter over medium-high heat in a large pot. Sauté the leeks and celery until they begin to soften, about 3–5 minutes. Add the farro and stir well.
2. Add the beans, thyme, tarragon, bay leaf, garlic, salt & pepper. Continue cooking another 4–6 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent burning.
3. Add the vegetable broth and miso paste and mix well. Bring to a soft boil, then lower the heat, cover, and simmer 20–25 minutes.
4. Add the chopped spinach and lemon juice, and continue to simmer until the spinach is wilted and the farro is cooked through (soft but still with a bite).
5. Check for seasoning, and serve with a drizzle of good olive oil and slices of crusty bread.

## Notes

- For a creamier soup, add 2 tbsp of flour toward the end of step 2.
