## Ingredients

### Funfetti Cake Layers
- 2⅔ cups cake flour (330g)
- 1 tablespoon baking powder (13g)
- ½ teaspoon baking soda (2g)
- ¾ teaspoon salt (3g)
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened (8 oz)
- 1½ cups granulated sugar (298g)
- 6 large egg whites, room temperature, beaten until frothy (201g)
- ¾ cup milk (170g)
- ¾ cup sour cream (170g)
- ¼ cup oil (canola or vegetable)
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract (15g)
- ½ cup rainbow sprinkles (85g), plus more for decoration

### Cream Cheese Frosting
- 3 cups cream cheese, very soft (706g)
- ¾ cup unsalted butter, very soft (170g)
- 4 cups confectioners' sugar, sifted (454g)
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons milk (or more if needed)
- Nonpareil sprinkles for decorating (optional)

## Directions

### Cake
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and line two 9-inch cake pans with parchment paper.
- Sift together cake flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
- Beat softened butter on medium-high speed until smooth, about 1 minute.
- Gradually add sugar and beat on high for 2 minutes.
- Add egg whites gradually on low speed, mixing well between additions and scraping bowl frequently.
- Combine milk, sour cream, oil, and vanilla in a measuring cup.
- Alternate adding flour mixture and liquid mixture in three additions, beginning and ending with flour. Mix until just combined.
- Fold in sprinkles.
- Divide batter evenly between pans (approximately 765g per pan).
- Bake 25-30 minutes until lightly browned and a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.
- Cool in pans on wire racks for 15 minutes. Invert onto cooling racks and cool completely.

### Frosting
- Beat cream cheese and butter on medium-high speed until smooth, about 2 minutes.
- Reduce speed to low and gradually add sifted confectioners' sugar.
- Add salt and milk and mix until combined.
- Increase speed to high and beat 1-2 minutes until fluffy.
- Place ½ cup frosting in piping bag with star tip; reserve remainder.

### Assembly
- Using a serrated knife, slice each cake layer horizontally in half to create 4 equal layers.
- Place one layer on cake stand. Spread ⅔ cup frosting on top.
- Top with next layer and repeat frosting and layering process.
- Cover entire cake with remaining frosting. Pipe decorative stars and swirls.
- Press sprinkles around sides of cake.
- Allow cake to set 20 minutes before slicing.
