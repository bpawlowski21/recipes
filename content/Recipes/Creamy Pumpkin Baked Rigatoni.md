## Ingredients

- 8 ounces rigatoni pasta
- 4 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 1 cup finely diced onion (about ½ large)
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- 2½ cups whole milk
- 1 cup pumpkin puree
- 1 bay leaf
- ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Directions

- Boil pasta until al dente per package directions. Drain, rinse with cool water, and set aside.
- Preheat oven to 375°F.
- Heat 3 tablespoons olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and cook, stirring frequently, until transparent, about 4 minutes.
- Add flour, salt, and pepper. Stir constantly for three minutes.
- Mix in milk and pumpkin. Add bay leaf.
- Whisk constantly for about 10 minutes until sauce thickens.
- Add nutmeg and taste for seasonings. Remove bay leaf.
- Pour sauce over cooked pasta and toss gently to coat.
- Transfer to a 5"x8" baking dish. Sprinkle with Parmesan and drizzle with remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil.
- Bake 25–30 minutes until bubbly and golden brown. Serve.
