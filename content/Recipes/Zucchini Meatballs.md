## Ingredients

- 1 lb (500g) zucchini, cut into small cubes
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup (80g) grated Parmigiano Reggiano
- 1½ cups (200g) plain breadcrumbs
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- Fresh parsley, chopped
- Fresh basil
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Extra virgin olive oil

## Directions

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the zucchini and sauté for 8-10 minutes until soft. Let cool slightly.
2. Transfer the zucchini to a large bowl. Add the eggs, Parmigiano Reggiano, breadcrumbs, garlic, parsley, salt, and pepper.
3. Mix until well combined. If the mixture is too wet, add a little more breadcrumbs.
4. Lightly wet your hands with water and roll the mixture into golf ball-sized meatballs.
5. Heat a generous layer of extra virgin olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
6. Fry the meatballs for a few minutes until golden on all sides.
7. Finish with freshly grated Parmigiano Reggiano and fresh basil.
