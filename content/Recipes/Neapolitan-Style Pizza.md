## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons instant yeast or active dry yeast
- 7/8 to 1 cup (198g to 227g) water, lukewarm
- 2 tablespoons (25g) olive oil
- 3 cups (360g) King Arthur '00' Pizza Flour
- 1 1/4 teaspoons (8g) table salt

## Directions

- Weigh flour or measure by spooning into a cup and sweeping off excess. Mix all dough ingredients together until achieving a soft, smooth dough. Using a stand mixer at second speed takes 4-5 minutes; dough should barely clean bowl sides.
- Place dough in lightly greased bowl, cover, and allow to rise until very puffy, approximately 1 to 1½ hours.
- Divide risen dough in half, shape each into a ball, and place in lightly greased bowls. Cover and let rise 45 minutes to an hour while preheating oven.
- Position oven rack in center with baking stone; preheat to 500°F.
- Transfer one dough ball to floured or semolina-dusted surface and sprinkle top with flour. Use fingertips to gently depress interior while leaving a slightly thicker ½-inch rim. Stretch into a 10" to 12" circle.
- Top crust with sauce and cheese as desired, then bake on preheated stone for 10 to 12 minutes until golden and toppings bubble.
- Remove pizza and add any final toppings before serving.
- Store leftovers refrigerated for about a day or freeze for longer storage.
