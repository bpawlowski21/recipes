## Ingredients

**Ginger Syrup:**
- 1/2 cup honey
- 1 inch fresh ginger, chopped
- 8 leaves mint

**Cocktail:**
- Crushed ice
- 1/2 cup pomegranate juice
- 1/3 cup grapefruit juice
- Juice of 1/2 lime
- 2-4 tablespoons ginger syrup
- 2-3 ounces tequila
- Ginger beer or sparkling water, for topping

## Directions

1. Bring 1/2 cup water, honey, and ginger to a boil. Simmer 1-2 minutes, then remove from heat. Add mint, cover, and steep 10-15 minutes. Strain and store in a glass jar for up to 2 weeks.
2. Fill glass halfway with ice. Add pomegranate juice, grapefruit juice, lime juice, syrup, and tequila. Stir, then top with ginger beer. Garnish with mint.
