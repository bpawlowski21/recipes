"Enjoy the Pomegranate Holiday Hugo Spritz, a refreshing twist on the classic drink perfect for your holiday celebrations."

Handwritten variation note (signed "MH"). Original scan:

![[Pomegranate Hugo Spritz (annotated).pdf]]

**Equipment:** Wine glass, jigger, muddler

## Ingredients

- 2 ounces elderflower liqueur
- 2 ounces pomegranate juice *(handwritten alternative: or white cranberry, for another version of a holiday Hugo spritz)*
- 1 ounce club soda
- 3 ounces Prosecco
- Fresh mint leaves
- Fresh mint sprig, for garnish
- Pomegranate arils, for garnish
- Ice

## Directions

1. Add fresh mint leaves and elderflower liqueur to a wine glass. Gently muddle. Add ice cubes to your glass.
2. Add pomegranate juice and Prosecco. Top with club soda. Stir to combine.
3. Garnish with pomegranate arils (pomegranate seeds) and a fresh sprig of mint.
