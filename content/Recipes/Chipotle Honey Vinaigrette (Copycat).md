## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup red wine vinegar
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1 tablespoon adobo sauce
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 1/2 cups olive oil or rice bran oil
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Directions

- Combine red wine vinegar, honey, adobo sauce, water, cumin, garlic powder, and oregano in a food processor or blender. Pulse until mixed together.
- While the motor runs, gradually pour in the oil until the mixture emulsifies and comes together as a cohesive dressing.
- Adjust seasonings with salt and pepper (about 1 tablespoon salt and 1 1/2 teaspoons pepper are recommended).
- Yield: about 2 cups (16 servings of 2 tablespoons each). Refrigerate in a covered container up to 4 days; shake or stir before serving.
