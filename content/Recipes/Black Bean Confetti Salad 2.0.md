"This is a zhuzh-ed up edition of my 2007 Black Bean Confetti Salad — with added avocado, radishes, cilantro, more lime, and more seasoning. Look for avocados that feel like a firm rubber ball; they hold up well. We add them last so they don't get very banged up. We scoop it up with tortilla chips."

Time: 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 (15-ounce) cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- 3 bell peppers, a mix of colors, diced
- 1 to 2 jalapeños, chopped small, to taste
- ¼ large or half a medium white onion, chopped small
- A few radishes, chopped small (shown with a watermelon radish)
- 4 tablespoons (60 ml) olive oil
- Juice of 1 to 2 limes, to taste
- Kosher salt
- Chili powder or tajín (a blend of chili powder, lime, and salt)
- About ½ cup (a big handful) fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 firm-ripe avocados, peeled and diced
- Tortilla chips for serving (if you wish)

## Directions

1. Combine black beans, bell peppers, jalapeños, onion, and radishes in a large bowl. Dress with olive oil, the juice of one lime, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and chili powder or tajín to taste. Add cilantro and avocado and mix gently.
2. Taste the mixture — usually better with more lime (up to 1 lime more of juice) and salt (about a half-teaspoon more). Eat right away, scooping it up with tortilla chips. Leftovers keep a few days in the fridge.
