## Ingredients

- 4 ripe peaches
- 1/3 cup (55 grams) whole almonds
- 1/4 cup (50 grams) sugar
- 3 tablespoons (45 grams) cold unsalted butter, cubed

Optional additions:
- 1/4 cup (20 grams) rolled oats
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Pinch of sea salt

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F.
2. Halve peaches and remove pits. Place fruit cut side up in a baking dish.
3. In a food processor, grind almonds and sugar together until coarsely ground (with a few pebbles remaining).
4. Add oats and other flavorings, if using, then butter, pulsing the machine until ingredients are just blended.
5. Spoon the almond mixture into the center of each peach half, then press it flat, as if icing the tops of the peaches with it.
6. Bake for 45 minutes to 1 hour (baking time varies with peach size), until the top is brown and crisp and you can easily slice through the fruit with a fork or spoon.
7. Serve warm or at room temperature, with crème fraîche, lightly sweetened whipped cream, plain yogurt, or vanilla ice cream.

Alternative method without a food processor: use an equivalent weight of almond meal or finely chopped almonds, stir in sugar and any other ingredients, then the same volume of melted butter. Texture will be less clumpy but bakes the same.
