## Ingredients

**Filling**
- 1 cup (8 ounces) cream cheese, room temperature preferred
- 1/3 cup (65 grams) granulated sugar
- Two pinches of fine sea salt
- 1 teaspoon (5 ml) vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon (5 ml) lemon juice
- 1 large egg yolk

**Dough**
- 1/3 cup (40 grams) powdered sugar
- 1/3 cup (65 grams) granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon fine sea or table salt
- 2 cups (265 grams) all-purpose flour
- 12 tablespoons (6 ounces or 170 grams) unsalted butter, cut into cubes
- 1 large egg
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/3 cup (45 grams) additional all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup (30 grams) cocoa powder

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. **Make filling:** Mash cream cheese and sugar with a fork until smooth. Add salt, vanilla, lemon juice, and egg yolk; blend until smooth. Refrigerate until needed.
3. **Make dough (food processor method):** Combine sugars, salt, and 2 cups flour in work bowl. Add butter and pulse until it disappears, then continue running until mixture clumps. Add egg and vanilla, beat until combined into one blob, scraping bowl as needed. Keep running machine until dough is smooth — may take up to one minute longer.
4. **Make dough (stand/hand mixer method):** Beat butter, sugars, and salt until creamy (several minutes if starting with cold butter). Add egg and vanilla, beat until combined. Add 2 cups flour and beat until it disappears, continuing to mix until dough is smooth and forms one ball; this may take up to one minute longer.
5. **All methods:** Divide dough in half. Add remaining 1/3 cup flour to one half and 1/3 cup cocoa powder to the second half, mixing until blended. Run mixer or machine until dough is no longer crumbly and forms one mound (30-60 seconds longer).
6. **Marble the dough:** Place parchment paper on counter. Spoon alternating dollops of chocolate and vanilla doughs across surface. Use offset spatula or bench scraper to smoosh pieces together, creating blended and unblended areas.
7. **Roll out:** Cover with second parchment sheet and roll doughs into an even 1/8-inch thick slab. Peel off top parchment (loosens it), flip slab, remove top parchment entirely.
8. **Form shapes:** Cut dough into 3-inch rounds. Place a measured 1-teaspoon dollop of cream cheese filling in the center of each. Fold up edges in 3 sections and pinch corners closed, continuing to pinch until only a marble-to-quarter-sized opening remains. Transfer to parchment-lined baking sheet (no spreading needed, so place 1-2 inches apart).
9. **Reroll scraps:** Pile scraps in center of parchment, top with second sheet, and repeat rolling, cooling, and cutting process until all dough and about 2/3 filling is used.
10. **Bake** for 20 to 25 minutes, until pale dough parts are golden brown. Transfer to cooling rack.
11. **Store** in refrigerator for up to one week.
