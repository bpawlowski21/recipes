## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 pounds medium white button mushrooms, quartered
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 6 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 8 ounces cream cheese, cut into pieces
- 1 cup grated gruyere, comte, or cheddar
- 2 cups panko-style breadcrumbs
- 1 cup grated parmesan
- 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a large 12-inch frying pan over medium-high heat, warm half the olive oil. Once very hot, add half the mushrooms without stirring for 1-2 minutes until browned underneath.
3. Add salt, pepper, and half the garlic, then stir until mushrooms brown on multiple sides and release liquid. Cook until liquid mostly reduces, leaving a thin puddle. Transfer to a large bowl.
4. Repeat with remaining oil, mushrooms, garlic, and seasonings. Add to first batch.
5. Fold in cream cheese until combined, then mix in half the panko, all gruyere, half the parmesan, and most parsley.
6. Transfer mixture to a 2-quart baking dish.
7. Combine remaining butter, panko, and parmesan. Sprinkle over mushrooms.
8. Bake 25-30 minutes until breadcrumbs turn light golden brown. Top with reserved parsley and serve immediately.
