## Directions

- **My Best Sourdough Waffles** — a lengthy fermentation creates "light, crunchy, and slightly tangy" waffles. The batter ferments for several hours, producing an aerated texture with a noticeable sweet/savory tang. Full recipe is linked on the source site.
- **Sourdough Starter Pancakes** — quick to prepare with minimal advance planning; versatile, with seasonal variations like pumpkin puree, fresh blueberries, or ricotta. Use a very hot griddle for best results. Full recipe is linked on the source site.
- **Baker's Banana Bread** — uses overripe (black, mushy) bananas; flexible, commonly available ingredients. Can substitute grated, pressed zucchini for bananas. Topping variations include split bananas and slivered almonds. Full recipe is linked on the source site.
