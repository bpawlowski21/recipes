## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (4 ounces or 115 grams) unsalted butter, plus extra for the pan
- 1 10-ounce (285-gram) bag marshmallows
- Heaping 1/4 teaspoon coarse sea salt
- 6 cups (160 grams) crispy rice cereal (about half a 12-ounce box)

## Directions

- Butter (or coat with non-stick spray) an 8-inch square cake pan with 2-inch sides.
- In a large pot, melt butter over medium-low heat. It will melt, then foam, then turn clear golden and finally start to turn brown and smell nutty. Stir frequently, scraping up any bits from the bottom. Don't take your eyes off the pot — the period between when the butter starts to color and when it burns is often less than a minute.
- As soon as the butter takes on a nutty color, turn off the heat and stir in the marshmallows. Residual heat should melt them; if not, turn the heat back on low until smooth.
- Remove the pot from the stove and stir in the salt and cereal. Quickly spread into the prepared pan, pressing firmly and evenly into the edges and corners (a piece of oiled wax or parchment paper works well, or a silicone spatula).
- Let cool, cut into squares, and get ready to make new friends.

## Notes

- Makes 16 2-inch squares or 32 1x2-inch small bars.
- Twist on classic rice krispie treats: double the butter and brown it first for a nutty, salted flavor.
