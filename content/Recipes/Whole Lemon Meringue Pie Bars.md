## Ingredients

**Crust:**
- 9 whole graham cracker sheets (1 sleeve), broken into pieces, or 1 1/2 cups (150 g) crumbs
- 1/4 cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- Large pinch of salt
- 5 tablespoons unsalted butter, cubed

**Filling:**
- 1 whole (preferably organic) lemon, scrubbed
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 4 large egg yolks
- 8 tablespoons (4 oz / 115 g) unsalted butter, cubed
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract (optional)
- Pinch of salt

**Meringue:**
- 4 large egg whites
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

## Directions

**Crust:** Heat oven to 350°F. Line an 8x8-inch pan with parchment. Process graham crackers, sugar, and salt until fine crumbs form. Pulse in cold butter until mixture resembles wet sand. Transfer to pan, press in (including sides), and bake 10 minutes until golden. Cool.

**Filling:** Trim lemon stem end and slice thinly; remove seeds. Blend lemon slices, lemon juice, egg yolks, butter, sugar, vanilla, and salt until very smooth. Pour over crust and bake 30 minutes until bubbling and browning at edges. Cool on rack; run knife around edges after 10 minutes. Chill completely, then remove parchment and transfer to baking sheet.

**Meringue:** Combine egg whites, sugar, vanilla, and salt in mixer bowl over simmering water. Whisk constantly until sugar dissolves and mixture reaches 160°F. Transfer to stand mixer and beat on high speed 2-3 minutes until glossy and stiff. Spread over bars; create patterns with fork or spatula.

**Finish:** Brown meringue with kitchen torch or broiler. Refrigerate until serving. Cut into 12-16 squares using knife dipped in hot water.
