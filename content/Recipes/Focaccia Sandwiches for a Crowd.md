## Ingredients

- 4 cups (520 grams) all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon instant yeast
- 2 cups lukewarm water (made by mixing 1/2 cup boiling water with 1 1/2 cups cold water)
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- Flaky sea salt
- Optional: 1 tablespoon chopped or minced fresh rosemary for traditional focaccia flavor

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, salt, and instant yeast. Add water and mix with a rubber spatula until water is absorbed and ingredients form a loose, sticky dough. Cover and choose a rising method:
   - Quickest rise: 1 to 1 1/2 hours in a warm spot until doubled.
   - Overnight in fridge: 8 to 10 hours refrigerated.
   - Overnight at room temperature: 10 hours, using only cold water.
2. Heat oven to 425°F. Pour 3 tablespoons oil onto a rimmed sheet pan.
3. Using two forks, deflate dough by releasing from bowl sides and pulling toward center. Rotate bowl in quarter turns to form a rough ball. Transfer dough to oiled pan and roll to coat with oil.
4. Let dough rest 20 minutes (for quickest/room temperature rises) or 1 hour (if refrigerated).
5. Drizzle remaining 1 tablespoon oil over dough. Using fingertips, stretch and press dough to edges, creating intentional dimples. Rest and repeat if dough resists stretching.
6. Sprinkle with flaky sea salt. Bake 20 to 25 minutes until lightly puffed, golden, and crisp underneath.
7. Cool completely before assembling sandwiches. Cut horizontally through the middle to create two layers, or trim edges first if desired.
