## Ingredients

- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 cup (150 g) sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 large egg yolk
- 2 1/4 cups (295 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 cup (170 g) dark chocolate melting wafers
- 1 cup (170 g) white chocolate melting wafers
- Nonpareils, for decoration

## Directions

- Combine butter and sugar in a stand mixer bowl and beat until well-creamed.
- Stir in vanilla extract and salt until well-combined, scraping down bowl sides.
- Add egg yolk and beat until incorporated.
- Gradually add flour on medium-low speed, scraping down sides and bottom several times until well-incorporated.
- Transfer dough to a clean surface and form into a cohesive ball by hand.
- Flatten into a 1-inch thick disk, cover with plastic wrap, and chill for 15 minutes.
- Preheat oven to 350°F and line cookie sheets with parchment paper.
- Roll out chilled dough to 1/4-inch thickness on a floured surface using a floured rolling pin.
- Cut shapes with lightly floured cookie cutters and transfer to ungreased cookie sheets.
- Recombine scraps, re-roll, and cut additional shapes to maximize yield.
- Bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes until edges turn lightly golden brown. Cool completely before dipping.
- Melt dark and white chocolate melting wafers separately according to package directions.
- Line a cookie sheet with wax paper. Holding each cookie's base firmly, dip the surface into chocolate.
- Place dipped cookie on wax paper and immediately sprinkle with nonpareils.
- Alternate between white and dark chocolate until all cookies are covered.
- Allow chocolate to harden before serving.
