## Ingredients

- 1 cup (113g) Golden Whole Wheat Flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup (227g) sourdough starter (unfed/discard)
- 4 tablespoons (57g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- Oil for brushing
- Coarse salt for topping
- Optional add-ins: seeds, dried herbs, cheese, or Pizza Dough Flavor

## Directions

- Mix flour, salt, sourdough starter, and butter until smooth and cohesive (not sticky). Incorporate optional add-ins if desired.
- Divide dough in half (or into quarters for easier handling) and shape into rectangular slabs. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate 30 minutes to 2 hours until firm.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly flour parchment paper and rolling pin.
- Roll one dough piece to approximately 1/16" thickness on parchment. (Thinner dough yields crispier crackers; 1/8" thickness produces sturdier versions.)
- Brush dough lightly with oil and sprinkle with salt or preferred seasonings.
- Cut into 1¼" squares using a pizza wheel. Prick each square with fork tines if desired to keep flat.
- Transfer parchment with crackers to baking sheet. Bake 20-25 minutes, rotating sheets midway through. Thicker crackers need 30-35 minutes.
- Cool on rack. Store airtight at room temperature for up to one week or freeze for extended storage.
