## Ingredients

**Oreo crust**
- 35 Oreo cookies
- 5 Tablespoons (75 g) unsalted butter, melted

**Cheesecake**
- 15 Oreo cookies
- 32 oz (907 g) cream cheese, softened (full-fat brick-style)
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- ¾ cup (180 g) sour cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 4 large eggs, lightly beaten (room temperature preferred)

**Ganache (optional)**
- 3 oz (85 g) semisweet chocolate chips or baking bar
- ½ cup (118 ml) heavy cream

## Directions

**Oreo crust**
1. Preheat oven to 325°F (160°C).
2. Twist apart 35 Oreos, scrape out filling, discard it, and place cookies in a food processor.
3. Pulse until fine crumbs form.
4. Combine crumbs with melted butter; press into bottom and sides of a 9" springform pan, pressing high up the sides.

**Cheesecake filling**
5. Place 15 Oreos in a bag and crush into coarse pieces with a rolling pin.
6. Beat cream cheese and sugar together until smooth.
7. Add sour cream and vanilla on low speed until combined.
8. Add eggs one at a time, stirring gently after each addition.
9. Fold in crushed Oreo pieces.
10. Pour into prepared pan on a foil-lined baking sheet.
11. Bake 50-60 minutes until center is set but slightly jiggles.
12. Loosen edges with a sharp knife; cool 1 hour at room temperature.
13. Refrigerate at least 6 hours (preferably overnight).

**Ganache**
14. Heat cream until simmering; pour over chocolate and let sit 5 minutes.
15. Whisk until smooth; drizzle over chilled cheesecake.
16. Refrigerate 20 minutes before serving with whipped cream.
