## Ingredients

### Traditional Version
- 1 cup + 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar, packed
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 3/4 teaspoon pumpkin spice
- 1/4 cup pumpkin pie puree
- 2 1/2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 1/2 cup milk

### Cinnamon Sugar Coating
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 tablespoons cinnamon

### Vegan Version
- 1 cup + 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar, packed
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 3/4 teaspoon pumpkin spice
- 1/4 cup pumpkin pie puree
- 2 1/2 tablespoons coconut oil, melted
- 1/2 cup almond milk

### Vegan Cinnamon Sugar Coating
- 3 tablespoons coconut oil, melted
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 tablespoons cinnamon

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a donut pan.
- Whisk dry ingredients together; separately combine pumpkin puree, melted butter (or coconut oil), and milk (or almond milk).
- Gently fold wet mixture into dry mixture without overmixing.
- Spoon into donut pan and bake 10-11 minutes until donuts spring back when pressed.
- Cool briefly in pan, then transfer to wire rack.
- Melt butter (or coconut oil) for coating and mix sugar with cinnamon.
- Once cool enough, dip each donut in melted butter/oil then roll in cinnamon sugar mixture.
- Serve the same day.
