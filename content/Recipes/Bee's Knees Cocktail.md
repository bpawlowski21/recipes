## Ingredients

**For the honey syrup:**
- 1 cup honey
- 1/3 cup hot water

**For the cocktail:**
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice
- 3/4 oz honey syrup
- 2 oz gin, preferably Beefeater

## Directions

1. Make the honey syrup: combine honey and hot water and stir until completely mixed. Use immediately or store refrigerated for up to 5 days.
2. In a shaker tin, combine fresh lemon juice, honey syrup, gin, and ice.
3. Shake vigorously and strain into a coupe glass.

## Notes
