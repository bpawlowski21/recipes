"Diane said she turns to green beans anytime she's making food for a crowd because they're easy, delicious and nearly impossible to mess up. Plus, they can be prepped the day before, they don't need to be piping hot to taste good and they look beautiful in a big bowl or platter. When I am serving a lot of people with a buffet, this is always my 'fall-back' veggie."

Substantial handwritten additions on the card — the printed recipe alone doesn't specify vinaigrette proportions ("pour as much as you want"), but the handwriting fills in exact amounts plus a make-ahead plan and an olive addition. Original scan:

![[Steamed Haricot Verts in a Lemon Thyme Vinaigrette (annotated).pdf]]

## Ingredients

- Haricots verts (green beans)
- 1 part extra-virgin olive oil to 1 part fresh lemon juice for the vinaigrette *(handwritten: ⅓ cup lemon juice, ⅓ cup olive oil)*
- Salt, pepper, and fresh thyme leaves
- Lemon slices and lemon zest, for garnish
- *(handwritten addition: can add niçoise or kalamata olives)*

## Directions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the beans and boil for 3 minutes only!
2. Drain and add to a cold water bath, or simply run them under cold water to stop the cooking process.
3. Make a lemon vinaigrette by combining one part extra-virgin olive oil with one part fresh lemon juice. Season with salt, pepper, and fresh thyme leaves. Shake well in a jar and pour as much as you want over the beans.
4. Check for seasoning and garnish with lemon slices and lemon zest.

## Notes

- They can be prepped the day before, don't need to be piping hot to taste good, and look beautiful in a big bowl or platter — a reliable do-ahead dish for a party.
- **Handwritten make-ahead plan:** make the beans and vinaigrette the day before; store separately; combine and set on a platter; serve at room temperature.
