## Ingredients

- 240 grams all-purpose flour (2 cups)
- 36 grams rice flour (1/4 cup)
- 62 grams sugar (1/4 cup), more as needed
- 2 grams fine sea salt (1/2 teaspoon)
- 16 tablespoons unsalted butter (1 cup), melted and cooled
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350 degrees. Line an 8-inch square pan with parchment paper.
2. In a bowl, whisk together the flours, sugar, and salt. Stir in the butter and vanilla. Press dough evenly into the bottom of the prepared pan. Bake until golden brown, 40 to 45 minutes. Sprinkle evenly with turbinado sugar while warm and slice; cool completely.

## Notes

- Wendy's handwritten additions: added 1 teaspoon vanilla to the dough (not in the original printed ingredient list); substituted turbinado sugar for the plain sugar sprinkled on top.
- Wendy's note: slice cookies while still warm.
- Wendy's alternate version: rub the sugar with lemon zest before adding it to the flour mixture.
