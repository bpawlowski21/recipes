## Ingredients

**For the Sauce:**
- 1 (14-ounce) package silken tofu (no need to drain)
- 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice (about 2-3 medium lemons)
- 1/4 cup nutritional yeast
- 1 head roasted garlic (or 4 cloves fresh minced garlic)
- 1 teaspoon vegan chick'n bouillon paste or powder
- 1/2 teaspoon fresh ground black pepper, plus more to taste
- Salt, to taste

**For the Breadcrumbs:**
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 cup panko bread crumbs
- Zest of 1 small lemon
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon fresh ground black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt

**Pasta:**
- 1 pound noodles of choice (I like bucatini or fettuccine)
- Vegan Parmesan, for serving, optional

## Directions

1. First, place all the sauce ingredients besides salt in a high-powered blender and blend until completely smooth, 30 seconds to 1 minute.
2. Taste and adjust seasonings, if necessary. Set aside.
3. To prepare the breadcrumbs, heat the oil in a small sauté pan over medium-low heat. Once the oil is hot, add the all the breadcrumb ingredients to the pan, mixing gently with a spatula to combine.
4. Cook the breadcrumbs over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until they turn golden-brown and crispy, 5 to 8 minutes. Remove from the heat and set aside.
5. Meanwhile, prepare the pasta according to package directions. Reserve 1 cup of the starchy water before draining.
6. Drain but don't rinse. Set aside.
7. Toss the cooked noodles with the creamy lemon sauce. If the pasta sauce seems too thick, add 1/4 cup of the reserved water at a time, until you've reached your desired consistency (I usually end up adding about 1/2 a cup). Taste and adjust seasonings, if necessary.
8. To serve, divide the pasta evenly among 4 plates. Garnish each serving with 1/4 cup breadcrumbs, 1 tablespoon vegan parmesan if using, a pinch of lemon zest, parsley, and red pepper flakes! ENJOY!

## Notes

- If you're preparing the pasta for less than 4 people, do not toss the noodles with the sauce. This ensures freshness. Store the sauce and noodles separately in airtight containers.
