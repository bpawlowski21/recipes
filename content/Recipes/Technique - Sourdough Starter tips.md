## Directions

Addresses the common problem of excessive sourdough discard by maintaining a smaller starter (~60g, ~2 oz) instead of the traditional 12 oz. Tools: digital scale, 8-ounce wide-mouth jars, mini-spatula.

**Basic maintenance feeding (60g starter):** keep equal weight ratios — reserve 20g starter, add 20g water, add 20g flour = 60g total starter.

**Building starter for baking (3-feeding method):**
- Feeding 1 (Thursday night): 20g starter + 20g water + 20g flour = 60g starter (40g discarded).
- Feeding 2 (Friday morning): combine all 60g with 60g water and 60g flour = 180g (no discard).
- Feeding 3 (Friday evening): combine all 180g with 180g water and 180g flour = 540g (minimal discard).

**Quick 2-feeding build:**
- Friday morning: 60g + 60g water + 60g flour = 180g.
- Friday evening: 180g + 180g water + 180g flour = 540g.

**Important tips:**
- Allow at least 4 hours at room temperature after feeding and before refrigeration for proper fermentation.
- Refrigeration does not permanently harm starters; smaller starters respond faster to temperature changes.
- More room-temperature feedings increase starter vitality and bread rise.
- This method integrates revival feedings with building, generating minimal waste.
