## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups unsalted butter, softened
- 3 cups granulated sugar
- 5 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon lemon extract
- Zest of one whole lime
- Zest of one whole lemon
- 3 cups cake flour
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 1 cup lemon-lime soda pop

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 325°F.
- Cream butter and sugar together using a mixer; add eggs one at a time to create an emulsion.
- Mix in citrus zests and extracts.
- Add cake flour in three increments, mixing between each addition.
- Incorporate salt and sour cream; slowly pour in the lemon-lime soda while mixing.
- Line a Bundt pan with shortening and flour; pour batter inside.
- Bake for one hour on center rack.
- Test with a knife for doneness (should come out clean).
- Cool for 10 minutes before removing from pan.
