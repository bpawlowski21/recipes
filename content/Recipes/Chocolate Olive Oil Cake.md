## Ingredients

**Cake**
- 1 1/2 cups (195 grams) all-purpose flour
- 3/4 cup (60 grams) unsweetened cocoa powder, sifted if lumpy
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 3/4 cup (150 grams) granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup (145 grams) dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups (355 ml) water or coffee
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) cider vinegar or white vinegar

**Glaze**
- 3/4 cup (135 grams) semisweet chocolate chips
- 2 tablespoons (10 grams) cocoa powder
- 3 tablespoons (45 ml) olive oil
- 1 tablespoon (20 grams) light corn syrup
- A pinch or two of flaky sea salt

## Directions

**Cake**
1. Heat oven to 350°F. Line a 9-inch round cake pan bottom with parchment paper and coat with nonstick spray.
2. Whisk together flour, cocoa, baking soda, salt, and granulated sugar in a large bowl. Add brown sugar and olive oil, whisking to combine. Add water and vinegar, whisking until smooth.
3. Pour into prepared pan. Bake 30-35 minutes until top is springy and a tester comes out with just a few sticky crumbs.
4. Cool in pan 10 minutes, loosen edges, flip onto a cooling rack.

**Glaze**
1. Combine chocolate, cocoa powder, olive oil, corn syrup, and salt in a bowl. Microwave in 15-30 second increments, stirring between each, until melted. Whisk smooth.
2. Pour over completely cooled cake, nudging gently down the sides.
