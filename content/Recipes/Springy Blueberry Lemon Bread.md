## Ingredients

- ½ cup butter, room temperature
- 1 cup white sugar
- Juice and zest of one lemon
- 2 eggs
- 1½ cups flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ cup milk
- 1 cup blueberries (frozen without thawing recommended)
- Spreadable butter for topping

## Directions

1. **Prep:** Preheat oven to 350°F. Line loaf pan with parchment paper.
2. **Mix part one:** Cream together butter and sugar until fluffy. Incorporate lemon juice, lemon zest, and eggs.
3. **Mix part two:** Stir in flour, baking powder, salt, and milk until combined. Gently fold in blueberries.
4. **Bake:** Pour batter into prepared pan. Bake approximately one hour until golden brown and top springs back when touched.
5. **Serve:** Allow to cool. Slice and top with spreadable butter before serving.

Total time: 1 hour 15 minutes. Yield: 8-10 slices.
