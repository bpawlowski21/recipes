## Ingredients

- 2 cups blended almonds (dried almond pulp, left over from making almond milk)
- 120g sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp amaretto liqueur or almond essence
- 80g acquaba (aquafaba)
- ½ tsp lemon juice

## Directions

1. Bake at 160°C for 17 minutes.

## Notes

- Dry the almond pulp overnight (or in the oven on a low setting) before using.
- When you blend the dry almond pulp, it will release oil — don't worry if it feels moist to the touch.
- Store the finished amaretti in a jar for up to 2 weeks.
