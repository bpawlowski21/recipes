## Ingredients

- 2 small bundles dried glass noodles (mung bean vermicelli)
- 1½ pounds Taiwanese cabbage (680g)
- 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
- ½ teaspoon dark soy sauce
- ½ teaspoon Chinese black vinegar
- ½ teaspoon white pepper
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- ¼ teaspoon sugar
- ½ cup water
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 5 slices ginger, julienned
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 3-4 dried red chilies, roughly chopped (optional)
- Salt, to taste
- 2 scallions, chopped

## Directions

- Soak the dried glass noodles in cold water for 5 minutes until slightly softened. Drain and cut in half lengthwise. Set aside.
- Julienne the cabbage thinly. Prepare the stir-fry sauce by mixing the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, black vinegar, white pepper, sesame oil, sugar, and water.
- Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a wok over medium heat. Cook the ginger and garlic until the garlic browns lightly. Add the cabbage and turn the heat to the highest setting. Stir-fry 1-2 minutes until the cabbage is slightly wilted. Add the dried chilies if using.
- Add the prepared sauce and the glass noodles. Stir and mix well, turning down the heat a bit if the dish is getting too dry. Salt to taste and add the scallions. Stir to combine once more, and serve.
