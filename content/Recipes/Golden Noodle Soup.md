## Ingredients

- ½ red onion
- 1 red bell pepper
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp finely chopped ginger
- 1 tbsp red curry paste
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 vegetable broth cube
- 1 tbsp almond butter
- 1 can coconut milk
- 2 cups water

**Tofu**
- 1 block tofu
- 2 tbsp soy sauce

**Add-ins**
- 140g buckwheat noodles
- 1 carrot (as noodles)
- ½ green chili
- Fresh cilantro

## Directions

1. Start by frying the red onion and bell pepper for a couple of minutes.
2. Add the finely chopped ginger and garlic, sauté another minute.
3. Add the red curry paste, turmeric, onion powder, the vegetable broth cube, almond butter, coconut milk, and water. Bring to a boil and simmer a few minutes.
4. Cook the buckwheat noodles.
5. Use a peeler to make carrot noodles.
6. Fry the tofu with soy sauce until golden.
7. Assemble and enjoy!
