## Ingredients

**For the rice:**
- 1 cup of dry basmati rice (Wendy's note: noodles instead of rice if you prefer)

**For the broth:**
- 2 cups veggie broth
- 1 can 15 oz lite Coconut Milk (full fat also works)
- 2 Tablespoons natural creamy Peanut Butter
- 2 tablespoons red curry paste
- 1 lime, juiced
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce

**For the tofu "meat":**
- 15 oz Super-firm tofu (Extra-firm tofu also works great!)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, divided
- 1 teaspoon vegetarian beef bouillon
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/2 tablespoon maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated (or 1/2 teaspoon of fried ginger)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste

**For serving:**
- Green onions
- Cucumber
- Cilantro
- Chili crunch
- Sesame seeds

## Directions

1. Cook the rice according to package directions.
2. Make the broth combining the broth, coconut milk, peanut butter, red curry paste, lime juice, and soy sauce in a medium pot. Bring to a simmer and cook for 5 minutes.
3. Using the large side of a box grater, shred the block of tofu. Some large chunks will fall off, so I take those and very gently push them through the grater. You can also finely crumble the tofu with your hands as desired.
4. Meanwhile, cook the tofu. In a large heat 1/2 tablespoon of olive oil. Once hot, add in the shredded tofu and cook, stirring every couple of minutes, for about 10 minutes or until the tofu turns a golden brown color and most of the moisture has cooked out.
5. Make the sauce by whisking together 1/2 tablespoon of olive oil, bouillon, soy sauce, water, maple syrup, ginger, and tomato paste. Once the tofu has turned golden, add the sauce, stir, and cook for 2 more minutes.
6. Assemble the bowls by dividing the broth, rice, and tofu "meat" into 4 bowls. Layer on desired toppings and enjoy!

## Notes

- For the beef bouillon - Better than Bouillon makes a great vegetarian broth base but a plain veggie broth will also work well.
- Store leftovers separately with the broth in one container and the tofu + rice in a separate container. The rice and tofu soak up the broth as they sit so it's best to store them separately.
- Wendy's handwritten note: you can use noodles instead of rice if you prefer.
