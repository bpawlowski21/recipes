## Ingredients

- 1 cup cold butter, cut into cubes
- 1 cup + 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups flour
- 1/2 cup cake flour
- 1 1/2 cups rolled oats
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 1/2 cups raisins
- 1 cup walnuts, chopped (optional)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 410°F.
- Cream together cold butter and sugars until light and fluffy, about 4 minutes. Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
- Add cake flour, all-purpose flour, oats, cornstarch, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon; stir until combined. Fold in raisins and optional walnuts.
- Chill dough for 15 minutes.
- Form dough into large balls (4.5-6 ounces each) on a baking sheet; gently press to smooth. Fit 4-6 cookies per sheet.
- Bake 9-11 minutes until golden brown on top. Allow to rest 10-15 minutes to set before serving.
