## Ingredients

**For the Pretzels:**
- ½ cup warm water
- 2 tablespoons light brown sugar
- 2¼ teaspoons active dry yeast
- 1 cup pumpkin beer (or any beer)
- ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted
- 1½ teaspoons kosher salt
- 4½ cups all-purpose flour
- ¼ cup baking soda (for boiling)
- 1 egg, beaten (for brushing)
- Coarse sea salt or pretzel salt

**For the Chipotle Queso:**
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 4 ounces cream cheese, room temperature
- 1½ cups whole milk
- 12 ounces sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, finely minced

## Directions

- Combine water, brown sugar, and yeast in stand mixer bowl with dough hook. Mix until combined and let sit 5 minutes.
- Add beer, melted butter, salt, and flour. Mix on low until combined, then increase to medium speed for 3-4 minutes until smooth dough forms. Add flour as needed if too wet. Hand-knead into a ball, coat with oil, cover, and let rise 1 hour until doubled.
- Preheat oven to 425°F. Bring large pot of water to boil.
- Divide dough into 8 equal pieces and roll each into a long rope. Shape into pretzels by crossing right side over left twice, then flipping up. Slowly add baking soda to boiling water. Boil pretzels 1-2 at a time for 30 seconds, splashing tops with warm water. Remove with slotted spatula, place on baking sheets, brush with egg wash, and season generously with sea salt. Bake 10-15 minutes until golden brown.
- Meanwhile, melt butter in large skillet. Add cream cheese and milk, cooking over medium heat until smooth. Toss cheddar with flour, then gradually add to warm milk while whisking until smooth. Stir in chipotle peppers. Keep warm and serve with pretzels for dipping.
