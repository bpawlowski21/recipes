## Ingredients

- 1 small Yukon Gold potato, sliced paper-thin
- ¾ cup walnuts
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Cooking spray
- 2 oz wild mushrooms
- 1 two-piece package of whole grain naan
- 3 oz shredded fontina or mozzarella cheese
- 1 teaspoon white truffle oil

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F.
- Bring about an inch of water to a boil in a medium saucepan. Add potato slices, reduce heat to simmer, and cook for 4 minutes until softened. Remove, rinse with cold water, and squeeze out excess liquid when cool.
- Add walnuts to a food processor and pulse until finely ground. Add olive oil and salt/pepper; pulse until combined.
- Spray a skillet with cooking spray and heat over medium. Sauté mushrooms 7–8 minutes until browning. Season with salt and pepper.
- Spread walnut mixture onto naan. Layer half the cheese, potato slices, remaining cheese, and mushrooms.
- Bake 8–10 minutes until naan edges are crispy and cheese melts. Drizzle each pizza with ½ teaspoon truffle oil before serving.
