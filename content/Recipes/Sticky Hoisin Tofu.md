## Ingredients

- 1 block firm tofu
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- Salt and pepper
- 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 3 tbsp water
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 thumb ginger, grated
- 1 red chilli
- Spring onion
- Sesame seeds
- Sticky rice, to serve
- Tenderstem broccoli, to serve

## Directions

1. Tear the tofu into nugget-sized pieces, then transfer to a bowl with the cornflour, salt, and pepper. Coat well, then spread out evenly on a baking sheet, drizzle with oil, and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
2. While the tofu bakes, make the sauce by adding the hoisin sauce, soy sauce, water, lime juice, garlic, and ginger to a small jar and shaking to combine.
3. When the tofu has about 10 minutes remaining, spread the broccoli onto a baking sheet, drizzle with oil and salt, and bake 10 minutes.
4. Fry off the garlic, ginger, and chilli in some oil for 1–2 minutes before pouring the sauce into the pan on medium heat. Wait until it's bubbling and starting to thicken, then add the crispy tofu, spring onion, sesame seeds, and chilli, and stir to coat.
5. Serve the sticky tofu with rice and roasted broccoli. Enjoy.
