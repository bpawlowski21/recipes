## Ingredients

- 1 cup (227 g) unsalted butter at room temperature
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon (3 g) orange zest
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 large egg yolk at room temperature
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 2 cups (284 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup (32 g) finely chopped candied orange peel
- 1/4 cup (43 g) semisweet mini chocolate chips
- 1 cup (200 g) turbinado sugar or sanding sugar

## Directions

- Beat butter until creamy, then add granulated sugar, orange zest, and salt. Mix until light and fluffy, 2 to 3 minutes. Add egg yolk and vanilla, then flour, candied orange peel, and chocolate chips.
- Form dough into a 12-inch log, coat with turbinado sugar, wrap in plastic, and refrigerate approximately 2 hours.
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Slice dough into ¼-inch rounds, spacing them 2 inches apart.
- Bake one batch at a time for 14 to 16 minutes until edges are lightly golden. Cool completely and store in an airtight container up to 3 days.
