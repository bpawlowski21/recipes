## Ingredients

- 4 slices bacon
- 8 large eggs
- 2 tablespoons milk (or unsweetened nut milk)
- Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 6 small flour tortillas
- ¾ cup (85 g) shredded Monterey Jack cheese
- 1 medium ripe avocado, pitted, peeled, and thinly sliced
- Chipotle Salsa (recipe below)
- Fresh cilantro leaves
- Finely chopped white onion
- Lime wedges

**Chipotle Salsa** (makes 2½ cups)
- 1 (28 oz) can fire-roasted tomatoes
- ½ yellow onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- ½ jalapeño
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, plus 1 teaspoon sauce
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- ½ cup cilantro
- Juice of 1 lime

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Cook bacon in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat until crispy, about 5 minutes, flipping once. Drain on paper towels and break into pieces.
2. Beat eggs with milk in a bowl; season with salt and pepper. Remove most of the bacon fat, melt the butter in the remaining fat over medium heat. Add eggs and stir frequently until softly scrambled.
3. Char each tortilla on a gas burner over medium-high heat for 10–20 seconds per side.
4. Arrange tortillas on a baking sheet. Distribute the scrambled eggs evenly and sprinkle with cheese. Bake 3 minutes until the cheese melts.
5. Top with bacon pieces, avocado slices, salsa, cilantro, onion, and lime wedges. Serve immediately.

**Chipotle Salsa:** Combine all ingredients in a food processor; pulse until evenly blended. Adjust seasoning and refrigerate until serving.
