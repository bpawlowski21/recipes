## Ingredients

- 4 salmon filets, skin on or off
- Kosher salt and black pepper
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons salted butter
- 1 small shallot, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 cup canned full-fat coconut milk or heavy cream
- 2 ounces cream cheese, cubed
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 4 cups fresh baby spinach
- Juice from 1 lemon
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh chives, plus more for serving

## Directions

1. Season salmon with salt and pepper on all sides.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add salmon skin-side down and cook 2-3 minutes until crisp. Flip and cook another 2-3 minutes until desired doneness. Remove from skillet; peel away skin if desired.
3. In the same skillet, add butter, shallots, and garlic; cook until fragrant (about 2 minutes). Add red pepper flakes, coconut milk, and cream cheese. Season with salt and pepper. Simmer over medium heat while stirring until smooth and creamy. Add parmesan and spinach; cook 3-5 minutes until spinach wilts. Stir in lemon juice, parsley, and chives. Remove from heat and return salmon to sauce.
4. Plate each salmon filet and spoon sauce over top. Serve with additional herbs.
