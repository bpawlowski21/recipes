## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons instant yeast or active dry yeast
- 1/2 cup (113g) lukewarm milk
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups (340g) pumpkin purée
- 2 tablespoons (25g) vegetable oil
- 6 1/2 cups (780g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1/2 cup (106g) light or dark brown sugar, packed
- 2 1/2 teaspoons (15g) table salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cardamom

## Directions

- Weigh flour or measure by spooning into a cup and sweeping off excess. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl and knead (by hand, mixer, or bread machine) until smooth and soft.
- Place dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise until doubled, about 60-75 minutes.
- Gently deflate dough and turn onto a lightly oiled surface. Divide in half.
- Shape each half into a loaf for 9"x5" pans, or shape one loaf and 12-16 rolls, or make 24-32 rolls total.
- Cover and let rise until nearly doubled, about 45 minutes. Preheat oven to 350°F.
- Bake loaves 30-35 minutes until deep golden brown and internal temperature reaches 190°F. Bake rolls about 20 minutes.
- Cool on a rack. Store well-wrapped at room temperature for several days or freeze for extended storage.
