## Ingredients

- 4 spring onions, finely chopped
- 200 g organic noodles
- 3 garlic cloves
- 2 tablespoons organic crunchy peanut butter
- 2 tablespoons organic roasted chopped peanuts
- ¼ cup runny organic tahini
- 2 tablespoons organic tamari
- 2 tablespoons chilli oil
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 5 tablespoons hot water
- 2 tablespoons sesame seeds

## Directions

1. **Prep the Noodles:** cook the noodles according to the package instructions.
2. **Build the Base:** in a large bowl, combine the garlic, peanut butter, chopped peanuts, tahini, tamari, chilli oil, lime juice, water, and sesame seeds.
3. **The Emulsion:** whisk until the sauce is creamy and glossy. The hot water will help the fats from the tahini and peanut butter emulsify perfectly.
4. **Combine:** add the cooked noodles and most of the chopped scallions to the bowl. Toss thoroughly so every strand is coated in that elite sauce.
5. **The Finish:** garnish with the remaining scallions and an extra sprinkle of sesame seeds. Serve immediately.
