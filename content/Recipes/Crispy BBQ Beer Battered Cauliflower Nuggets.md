## Ingredients

**Nuggets**
- 1 large head cauliflower, cut into florets
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1/2-1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- Kosher salt
- 3/4 cup beer or sparkling water
- Oil for frying (optional)
- 3/4 cup BBQ sauce, plus more for serving

**Creamy Honey Mustard Sauce**
- 1/4 cup Dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup sour cream, Greek yogurt, or mayo
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1 tablespoon BBQ or Cajun seasoning
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- Kosher salt and black pepper

**Spicy Tangy Honey BBQ Sauce**
- 1 1/4 cups ketchup
- 3/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup molasses
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper

## Directions

1. **Preparing the cauliflower:** Mix flour, paprika, cayenne, baking powder, and salt. Add beer and stir until combined. Coat cauliflower florets evenly.
2. **To fry:** Heat 1-2 inches of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry cauliflower in batches for 4-5 minutes until golden and crispy. Drain on a wire rack with paper towels.
3. **To bake:** Preheat oven to 450°F. Oil a baking sheet and spread cauliflower in a single layer. Bake 20-25 minutes until tender, flipping halfway. Avoid overcooking.
4. **Honey mustard:** Combine all honey mustard ingredients in a glass jar and shake or whisk until smooth.
5. **BBQ sauce:** Mix all BBQ sauce ingredients in a glass measuring cup or jar. Store refrigerated up to one month.
6. **To serve:** Drizzle or dip nuggets in BBQ sauce. Serve with honey mustard for dipping alongside french fries or vegetables.
