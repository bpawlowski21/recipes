## Ingredients

**Dough:**
- 2 cups (240g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 1/4 cup (46g) potato flour or 1/2 cup (46g) dried potato flakes
- 1/4 cup (28g) Baker's Special Dry Milk or nonfat dry milk
- 3/4 cup plus 2 to 4 tablespoons (198g to 227g) lukewarm water
- 4 tablespoons (57g) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1 teaspoon King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 2 tablespoons (25g) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon (6g) table salt

**Filling:**
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1/2 cup (99g) granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon or 2 teaspoons Vietnamese cinnamon
- (Alternative: 1/2 cup Cinnamon-Sugar Plus)

## Directions

- Weigh flour or measure by spooning gently into cup and sweeping off excess.
- Sift flour, potato flour, and dry milk through strainer to prevent lumps.
- Combine all dough ingredients; mix and knead until soft and smooth.
- Place in greased bowl, cover, let rise 60 minutes until nearly doubled.
- Divide dough into four equal pieces; shape into balls, rest 15 minutes.
- Roll first piece into 10" circle on parchment; brush with egg, sprinkle 1/3 cinnamon-sugar.
- Layer second circle atop filling; repeat with egg and cinnamon-sugar.
- Place 2½" to 3" round cutter at center; cut 16 equal strips from cutter outward.
- Twist adjacent strip pairs twice so top faces upward; repeat for all eight pairs.
- Pinch paired strips together forming eight-pointed star; remove cutter.
- Transfer to baking sheet, cover, let rise about 45 minutes until noticeably puffy.
- Preheat oven to 400°F.
- Brush star with egg; bake 12 to 15 minutes until golden with dark cinnamon streaks (200°F center).
- Cool 10 minutes; dust with confectioners' sugar; serve warm or room temperature.
- Store wrapped at room temperature several days or freeze; reheat at 350°F for 15 minutes.
