## Ingredients

- 3 cups (710ml) lukewarm water
- 1 tablespoon Platinum Yeast
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt (adjustable to taste, minimum 1 teaspoon)
- 6 1/2 cups (910g) all-purpose flour
- Cornmeal or parchment for pizza peel or baking sheet

Yield: 4 pounds (1814g) of dough

## Directions

- In a 6-quart container or large bowl, combine yeast, water, and salt. Add flour and mix with a spoon, Danish dough whisk, or stand mixer paddle until uniform.
- Cover loosely (not airtight) and let rise at room temperature for ~2 hours.
- Shape and bake immediately after the 2-hour rise, or refrigerate in a lidded (not airtight) container up to 14 days — dough becomes easier to work with after 4 hours of refrigeration.
- This master dough can be shaped into four artisan bread forms: boule, baguette, pain d'épi, and fougasse (individual shaping/recipe links on source page).

## Notes

- Note: this master dough uses commercial yeast, not a sourdough starter — filed here as reference/technique alongside the sourdough recipes.
