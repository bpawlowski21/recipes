Prep Time: 4 minutes

**Equipment:** Highball glass, shaker, strainer, bar spoon

## Ingredients

- 1 ounce blue curacao
- 1 ounce peach schnapps
- 1 ounce clear rum
- 3-4 ounces club soda
- 1 maraschino cherry

## Directions

1. Fill a tall glass with ice cubes to chill it.
2. In a shaker, combine the blue curacao, peach schnapps, and clear rum. Shake well to mix thoroughly.
3. Strain the mixture from the shaker into the prepared glass filled with ice.
4. Gently pour the club soda over the mix to top off the drink. Stir lightly to combine.
5. Add a maraschino cherry on top for a festive touch.

## Notes

- Pro Tips: Use fresh ice — older freezer ice can absorb odors that affect flavor. Chill your glass beforehand for a colder serving experience. Measure ingredients carefully to maintain balance.
