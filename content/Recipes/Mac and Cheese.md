Recipe courtesy of Ina Garten (Barefoot Contessa, Show: "Like a Kid in the Candy Store" and "What Are Friends For?"; from Barefoot Contessa Family Style). Handwritten annotations throughout. Original scan:

![[Mac and Cheese (handwritten).pdf]]

## Ingredients

- Kosher salt
- Vegetable oil
- 1 pound elbow macaroni or cavatappi
- 1 quart milk — handwritten substitution: 3 cups milk / 1 cup white wine
- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter, divided
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- 12 ounces Gruyere, grated (4 cups)
- 8 ounces extra-sharp Cheddar, grated (2 cups)
- ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ¾ pound fresh tomatoes (4 small)
- 1½ cups fresh white bread crumbs (5 slices, crusts removed)
- 1 teaspoon dijon mustard (handwritten addition)

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F.
2. Drizzle oil into a large pot of boiling salted water. Add the macaroni and cook according to the directions on the package, 6 to 8 minutes. Drain well.
3. Meanwhile, heat the milk in a small saucepan, but don't boil it. Melt 6 tablespoons of butter in a large (4-quart) pot and add the flour. Cook over low heat for 2 minutes, stirring with a whisk. While whisking, add the hot milk and cook for a minute or two more, until thickened and smooth. Off the heat, add the Gruyere, Cheddar, 1 tablespoon salt, dijon (handwritten addition), pepper, and nutmeg. Add the cooked macaroni and stir well. Pour into a 3-quart baking dish.
4. Slice the tomatoes and arrange on top. Melt the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter, combine them with the fresh bread crumbs, and sprinkle on the top. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until the sauce is bubbly and the macaroni is browned on top.

## Notes

- Reprinted from Barefoot Contessa Family Style, Copyright 2002 by Ina Garten, Clarkson Potter/Publisher.
- Dated 2.9.25 on the scan (date this copy was annotated/tested).
- Handwritten freezing instructions:
  - To freeze: cool to room temp, cover well with heavy foil. Do not top with bread crumbs before freezing.
  - Thaw the mac and cheese overnight in the fridge, then top with bread crumbs and bake.
  - Tomatoes are optional — "no tomatoes, just preference."
  - Alternative make-ahead: prepare the dish a day ahead and refrigerate overnight; rest 30 minutes on the counter before baking.
  - A question noted for a future test: "sub ¼ cup white wine for some amount of milk?" — paired with the 3 cups milk / 1 cup white wine substitution written next to the milk ingredient, suggesting this batch used part white wine in place of some of the milk.
- A set of initials appears next to the freezing notes but is not clearly legible from the scan.
