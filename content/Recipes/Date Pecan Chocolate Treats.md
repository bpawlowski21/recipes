Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta") — despite the folder, contains Nutella (which has milk). "If you love dates as much as I do, these will become your newest favorite delicious and healthy snack!"

## Ingredients

- 1 cup pecans
- ½ cup almond flour
- 1 cup medjool dates, pitted (about 12 large, soft ones)
- ¼ cup Nutella
- 2 tbsp cocoa powder
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 2 cups dark chocolate chips
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil (or coconut oil) — helps the chocolate melt smoothly
- 12 pecan halves

## Directions

1. Put the pecans, almond flour, dates, maple syrup, Nutella, and cocoa powder in a food processor and blend until very smooth and soft.
2. Shape into bars and freeze about an hour. Remove from freezer.
3. Meanwhile, in the top of a double boiler, melt the chocolate and oil until smooth. Drop in each bar to coat. Immediately top with a pecan half.
4. Let them harden either on the counter or in the fridge. Store extras in the fridge.
