## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (8 ounces, 1 cup) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1 1/2 cups light brown sugar, packed
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup pumpkin puree
- 1 large egg yolk, at room temperature
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves
- 2 1/2 cups rolled oats (not instant)
- 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Cream butter and both sugars until light and fluffy (about 3 minutes). Beat in pumpkin puree, then egg yolk and vanilla.
- In a separate bowl, combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and spices. Gradually beat into butter mixture on low speed until just incorporated.
- Fold in oats and chocolate chips with a wooden spoon.
- Scoop 2-inch dough balls onto prepared sheets, spacing 1 1/2 inches apart.
- Bake one sheet at a time for 12-15 minutes until golden at edges and slightly soft in center.
- Cool on baking sheet for 20 minutes before transferring to wire rack.
