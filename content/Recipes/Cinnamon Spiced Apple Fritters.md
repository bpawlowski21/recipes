## Ingredients

**Fritters**
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tablespoons salted butter, melted
- 1/2-3/4 cup whole milk
- 1 tablespoon real maple syrup
- 3-4 Honeycrisp apples, peeled (if desired) and chopped

**Vanilla Coffee Glaze**
- 1 1/3 cups powdered sugar
- 1/4 cup whole milk
- 1-2 tablespoons real maple syrup
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2-3 teaspoons instant coffee
- Flaky sea salt

## Directions

1. Combine flour, baking powder, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and salt in a medium bowl. Add eggs, melted butter, 1/2 cup milk, and maple syrup; mix until just combined without overmixing. If batter is too thick, add remaining 1/4 cup milk. Fold in apples.
2. Heat 1-2 inches of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Drop tablespoon-sized portions of batter into hot oil. Fry 4-5 minutes until golden and crisp on all sides. Remove and drain on a wire rack lined with paper towels.
3. For the glaze, whisk together powdered sugar, milk, maple syrup, vanilla, and instant coffee powder. Add a pinch of flaky sea salt. Drizzle over warm fritters and serve immediately.
