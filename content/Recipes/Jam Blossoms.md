## Ingredients

### Dough
- 1/2 cup (57g) confectioners' sugar
- 8 tablespoons (113g) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 1/4 teaspoon almond extract
- 1 cup (120g) King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour

### Topping and Glaze
- 2 1/4 cups (255g) confectioners' sugar, sifted
- 2 tablespoons (39g) light or dark corn syrup
- 1 teaspoon King Arthur Pure Vanilla Extract
- 3 to 4 tablespoons (43g to 57g) milk
- 2/3 cup (206g) jam or preserves

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 375°F and line two baking sheets with parchment paper. Have a small bottle cap (about 1" diameter) ready for creating wells.
- Mix confectioners' sugar, butter, salt, and extracts until smooth. Incorporate the flour.
- Form dough into 1" balls and place on the prepared sheets with 1" spacing.
- Use a greased glass or measuring cup bottom to flatten balls to 1/2" thickness.
- Bake 10-12 minutes until edges brown slightly. Remove and use the bottle cap to press wells into the warm cookies for the jam.
- Whisk together confectioners' sugar, corn syrup, vanilla, and 3 tablespoons milk until thick and honey-like, adding more milk as needed.
- Fill indentations with 1 teaspoon jam each. Cool on the pan 10 minutes, transfer to a rack, then drizzle with glaze once completely cooled.
