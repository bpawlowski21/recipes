## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sriracha
- 4 tbsp maple syrup (or golden syrup)
- 1 tsp garlic
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
- Cornflour, roughly 10 tablespoons (or enough to coat the tofu)

## Directions

1. Press the tofu to drain all moisture, using a tea towel and a weighted object on top.
2. Mix all the marinade ingredients together in a container.
3. Break the tofu into small chunks.
4. Set aside half the marinade and pour the rest over the tofu in a sealable container.
5. Refrigerate the tofu for at least 1 hour.
6. Coat each piece of tofu by rolling it in a bowl of cornflour.
7. Place the tofu with a teaspoon of oil in the oven for 20–25 minutes at 200°C.
8. Turn over halfway through.
9. Add the baked tofu to a frying pan and pour in the rest of the marinade, mixing well.
10. Serve with rice or noodles.
