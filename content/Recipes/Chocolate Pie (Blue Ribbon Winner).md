## Ingredients

- 1 prepared (baked) 9" pie crust
- 1½ cups (300 g) granulated sugar
- 5 tablespoons (40 g) cornstarch
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 5 large egg yolks
- 2 cups (473 ml) whole milk
- 1 cup (236 ml) heavy cream
- 6 oz (170 g) dark chocolate bar, finely chopped
- 4 tablespoons (56.5 g) unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 1½ teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 batch homemade whipped cream, for topping

## Directions

- In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, whisk together sugar, cornstarch, and salt until combined.
- In a separate bowl, whisk egg yolks, milk, and cream until well-combined, then slowly drizzle into the dry ingredients while whisking.
- Transfer to stovetop over medium heat and whisk frequently until thickened to a pudding-like consistency.
- Remove from heat and immediately add chocolate, butter, and vanilla, stirring until smooth and melted.
- Pour filling through a fine mesh strainer into a heatproof bowl to catch lumps.
- Pour into prepared pie crust and press plastic wrap directly against surface to prevent skin formation.
- Refrigerate at least 6 hours or overnight before serving with whipped cream.
