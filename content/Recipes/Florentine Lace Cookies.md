## Ingredients

- 2 cups slivered almonds
- ¾ cup heavy cream
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into 4 pieces
- ½ cup (3½ ounces/99 grams) sugar
- ¼ cup orange marmalade
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- ¼ teaspoon grated orange zest
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 4 ounces (113 grams) bittersweet chocolate, chopped fine

## Directions

- Adjust oven racks and heat to 350°F. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper. Process almonds until they resemble coarse sand (about 30 seconds).
- Bring cream, butter, and sugar to boil over medium-high heat. Cook while stirring frequently for 5-6 minutes until thickened. Continue cooking, stirring constantly, until mixture browns at edges and leaves a trail when scraped along pan bottom (1-2 minutes). Remove from heat and stir in almonds, marmalade, flour, vanilla, orange zest, and salt.
- Drop 6 level tablespoons of dough at least 3½ inches apart on each sheet. Once cool enough to handle, press each into a 2½-inch circle using damp fingers.
- Bake 15-17 minutes until deep brown, switching and rotating sheets halfway through. Cool on wire racks. Repeat with remaining dough.
- Microwave 3 ounces chocolate at 50% power for 1-2 minutes, stirring frequently. Add remaining 1 ounce and stir until melted. Transfer to a piping bag and pipe zigzag stripes over cooled cookies.
- Refrigerate approximately 30 minutes until chocolate sets before serving.
