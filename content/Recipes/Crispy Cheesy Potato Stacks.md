## Ingredients

- 4-6 medium Russet or Idaho potatoes, cut into 1/8 inch thick slices
- 1 stick (1/2 cup) salted butter, melted
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh oregano
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme
- 1-2 cloves garlic, grated
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup shredded sharp cheddar or gruyere cheese
- Kosher salt and black pepper to taste

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 400°F. Lightly grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
- Cut potatoes using a mandoline or very sharp knife into 1/8 inch thin slices.
- In a large bowl, combine melted butter, oregano, thyme, garlic, both cheeses, and a large pinch of salt and pepper. Add potatoes and toss thoroughly to coat. Layer potatoes evenly among prepared muffin tin, stacking layers all the way to the top. Potatoes will shrink during cooking.
- Cover with foil and place on a baking sheet. Roast for 30 minutes. Remove foil and continue cooking 20-25 minutes until potatoes are tender and golden. Run a butter knife around edges of each stack to release. Serve immediately, sprinkled with sea salt.
