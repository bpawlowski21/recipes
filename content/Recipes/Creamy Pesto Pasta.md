## Ingredients

- 1 large head of cauliflower, cut into florets
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- Kosher salt and black pepper to taste
- 3-4 cloves garlic, finely chopped or grated
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest, plus 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 pound pasta (any variety)
- 1 1/2 cups fresh basil
- 1 cup baby arugula, spinach, or a mix
- 1 jalapeño, seeded (optional)
- 1/4 cup toasted nuts (pine nuts, walnuts, or pistachios)
- 1/3 cup grated parmesan cheese or nutritional yeast
- 1/4 to 1/3 cup canned full-fat coconut milk (or cream/milk of choice)

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss cauliflower with 1/4 cup olive oil, paprika, chili flakes, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet. Roast 20 minutes until tender. Add 2 cloves garlic and lemon zest; roast another 10 minutes until charred.
- Make the pesto by combining basil, arugula/spinach, jalapeño, 1-2 cloves garlic, 2 tablespoons lemon juice, nuts, parmesan, and 1/4 cup olive oil in a blender or food processor. Pulse until sauce forms, adding water to thin if needed. Season with salt.
- Boil salted water and cook pasta according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining. Return pasta to hot pot.
- Add pesto and coconut milk to pasta, tossing well. Thin sauce with reserved pasta water as desired.
- Divide pasta among bowls and serve with roasted cauliflower on the side. Top with fresh herbs and greens before serving.
