Colorful red dough is twisted with peppermint-flavored dough to create melt-in-your-mouth cookie candy canes. Emailed to Wendy by herself, December 17, 2007, sourced from landolakes.com.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 cup (1 1/4 cups printed, but see notes — use 1 cup / 14 tablespoons) LAND O LAKES® Butter, softened
- 2 eggs (printed recipe calls for 1)
- 1 teaspoon peppermint extract
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour (Wendy has used as much as 3 1/2 cups; see notes)
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/3 cup finely crushed candy canes or red and white peppermint candy (Wendy uses 4 candy canes)
- 1/4 teaspoon red food color

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Combine powdered sugar, butter, egg, peppermint extract and vanilla in large bowl. Beat at medium speed until creamy. Reduce speed to low; add flour and salt. Beat until well mixed.
2. Divide dough in half. Stir candy into half of dough. Beat food color into remaining half of dough.
3. For each candy cane, roll 1 teaspoon of each dough to a 4-inch rope. Place 2 ropes side by side; twist gently and roll dough together. (For best results, complete cookies one at a time to prevent drying out.)
4. Place 2 inches apart onto ungreased cookie sheets. Curve one end to form handle of cane. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes or until set. Let stand 1 minute; remove from cookie sheets.

Tip: if dough is dry, stir in 1 teaspoon milk. If dough is too soft, refrigerate 1 hour or stir in 1 to 2 tablespoons flour.

## Notes

- Wendy's handwritten notes at top: "spreads too much."
- Wendy's dated notes (12/19/16): divide dough into 2 bowls; weigh dough before adding candy cane — dough for the red half should have 1 ounce more than the white half. Make the candy canes for this recipe from the Candy Cane Cookies (candy/peppermint bark) recipe at the same time; use the entire box of canes (18).
- Wendy's ingredient adjustments (also dated 12/19/16): 1 cup butter (14 tablespoons, not 1 1/4 cups); 2 eggs (not 1); flour adjusted between 2 cups and 3 1/2 cups across different attempts — final settled amount unclear from the scan, use 2 3/4 cups as printed and adjust by feel; crush 4 candy canes for the mix-in.
