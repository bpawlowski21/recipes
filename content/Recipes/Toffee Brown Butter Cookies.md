A different recipe from the other saved [[Brown Butter Toffee Cookies]] (Sam Merritt) and [[Brown Butter Toffee Chocolate Chip Cookies]] (Sofi) — this one has no chocolate at all, just toffee bits (plus optional pecans/walnuts).

"Chewy, nutty, and perfectly sweet, these Toffee Brown Butter Cookies are made with rich browned butter, crunchy toffee bits, and a hint of sea salt. With crisp edges and soft centers, they're the ultimate bakery-style cookie to keep on hand."

Prep Time: 20 minutes · Cook Time: 12 minutes · Resting Time: 1 hour · Total Time: 1 hour 32 minutes · Calories: 230kcal

## Ingredients

- 1 cup unsalted butter (226g)
- 1 cup brown sugar (220g)
- ½ cup granulated sugar (100g)
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2¼ cups all-purpose flour (287g)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ¼ teaspoon baking powder
- ¾ teaspoon kosher salt (or ½ teaspoon fine salt)
- 1 cup toffee bits (Heath Bits o' Brickle or chopped toffee bars, 150g)
- Optional: ½ cup chopped pecans or walnuts

## Directions

1. In a light-colored saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat, stirring constantly. Once golden brown (not burnt) and nutty-smelling, remove from heat and pour into a mixing bowl. Cool for about 30 minutes.
2. Add brown sugar and granulated sugar to the browned butter. Beat with a hand or stand mixer (paddle attachment).
3. Once the sugar and butter are incorporated, add the eggs one at a time, beating until fully incorporated before adding the next. Add the vanilla. Beat until smooth.
4. Add the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Mix just until combined.
5. Fold in the toffee bits and nuts, if using.
6. Cover and refrigerate the dough for 1 hour to firm up.
7. Scoop dough into 2-tablespoon balls onto a parchment-lined tray. They can be placed close together since they'll be chilled before baking. Freeze the tray, then transfer frozen dough balls to ziplock bags to bake whenever you like.
8. If not freezing, keep the scooped cookies in the fridge (covered) for at least another hour before baking, ideally overnight. The longer the chill, the richer the flavor and better the texture.
9. You can bake some right away, but they might spread without proper chilling time.
10. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Place chilled cookies on a parchment-lined baking sheet, 2 inches apart.
11. Bake for 10-12 minutes, until edges are golden and centers still look slightly underbaked. They'll finish setting on the tray.
12. Sprinkle with a touch of sea salt (such as Maldon) once out of the oven.
13. Let cool on the tray for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack.
