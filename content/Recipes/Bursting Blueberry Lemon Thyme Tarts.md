## Ingredients

- 2 1/2-3 cups fresh or frozen blueberries
- 1-2 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1/4 cup honey or granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest + 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves (or 2 teaspoons dried thyme)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 sheet frozen puff pastry, thawed
- 1 egg, beaten
- Coarse sugar for sprinkling (optional)
- Vanilla ice cream for serving (optional)

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a medium bowl, combine blueberries and 1 tablespoon cornstarch. Add honey, lemon zest, lemon juice, thyme, and vanilla.
3. On a lightly floured surface, roll puff pastry to 1/4 inch thickness. Cut into 4 rectangles and place on prepared baking sheet. Distribute blueberries over each rectangle, leaving a 1/2 inch border. Fold edges up and over berries.
4. Brush pastry edges with beaten egg and sprinkle with coarse sugar. Bake 20-25 minutes until golden brown.
5. Cool slightly and serve with ice cream.

Note: Use 1 tablespoon cornstarch for runny filling; use 2 tablespoons for thicker filling.
