Morton's Steakhouse Creamed Spinach (Copycat) is a decadent side ready in minutes, with fresh spinach, butter, garlic, and three cheeses. Recipe adapted from Morton's Steak Bible — this adaptation adds cream cheese and mozzarella cheese; for a perfect copycat, omit these.

Prep Time: 20 minutes · Cook Time: 15 minutes · Total Time: 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1.5 medium yellow onion, minced
- 4.5 cloves garlic, minced
- 6 teaspoons kosher salt, divided
- 1.5 teaspoon coarse ground black pepper
- 0.75 teaspoon nutmeg, freshly grated
- 0.38 cup flour
- 5.25 cups half-and-half
- 3.38 pounds fresh leaf spinach, or one pound frozen
- 1.5 cup mozzarella cheese
- 6 ounces cream cheese
- 0.75 cup Parmesan cheese, grated

## Directions

1. In a large skillet melt the butter with the garlic and onions.
2. Cook on medium low for 8-10 minutes or until the onions are translucent.
3. Add in the remaining kosher salt, black pepper and nutmeg.
4. Add in the flour and mix with a wooden spoon over low and cook for 2-3 minutes until the aroma is a bit nutty.
5. Add the half and half and cook until boiling.
6. Add in the mozzarella cheese and cream cheese.
7. Add the chopped fresh spinach (no need to blanch/chop separately) and just let it wilt and cook with the cheese mixture.
8. Cook for 6-8 minutes or until the mixture has thickened.
9. Add in the Parmesan cheese and stir to combine.

## Notes

- Wendy's tested adaptation (handwritten on the printed copy): skip the original steps of boiling/blanching the spinach separately, rinsing it in cold water, and chopping it into bundles — instead add the fresh spinach directly to the cheese mixture and let it wilt and cook right in with the cheese.
