## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 onion, minced
- 1 tbsp Madras curry powder
- ¼ tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 2-inch piece of ginger, minced
- ¾ cup red lentils, rinsed and drained
- 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 1¼ cups cilantro (stems and leaves), roughly chopped
- 2½ cups water
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1 (13.5 oz) can coconut milk

## Directions

- Heat oil in a medium pot over medium-high heat. Add onion and sauté for 8 minutes, stirring frequently, until tender and caramelized.
- Stir in curry powder, red pepper flakes, garlic, and ginger; sauté 2 minutes more.
- Add lentils, tomatoes, cilantro, water, 1 tsp salt, and black pepper.
- Reserve ¼ cup coconut milk; pour the remainder into the pot. Bring to a boil, then lower heat to medium and simmer 25 minutes until lentils are soft. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
- Divide soup among four serving bowls. Drizzle with reserved coconut milk and serve warm.
