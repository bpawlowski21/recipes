## Ingredients

**Crumbs**
- 1 cup (200 grams) granulated sugar
- Finely grated zest of one lemon
- 3 cups (400 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 cup (8 ounces or 225 grams) cold unsalted butter, cut into chunks
- 1 large egg

**Berries**
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1/2 cup (100 grams) granulated sugar
- 4 teaspoons cornstarch
- 4 cups fresh blueberries

## Directions

1. Heat oven to 375°F (190°C). Coat a 9x13 inch pan with butter or nonstick spray, or line with parchment paper for easy removal.
2. Combine sugar, flour, baking powder, salt, and lemon zest in a large bowl. (Optional: rub zest into sugar first with fingertips to release maximum flavor.)
3. Use a fork or pastry cutter to work cold butter into flour mixture until a rubbled texture forms. Stir in egg. Dough will be crumbly.
4. Pat half the dough into prepared pan.
5. In a medium bowl, mix blueberries, sugar, cornstarch, and lemon juice. Spread mixture evenly over crust.
6. Crumble remaining dough over berry layer.
7. Bake 45 minutes until top is slightly brown. Cool completely before cutting into squares.

## Notes

- Saved from Apple Notes folder "SMITTEN KITCHEN" (note suggested adding a little extra salt to the crumb, maybe 1 1/2x the dough/crumb amount, and refrigerating overnight — said to be better the next day). Yield: 36 small bars; 1 hour. Keep refrigerated up to one week to maintain firm texture.
