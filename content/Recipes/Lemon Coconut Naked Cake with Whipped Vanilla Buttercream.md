## Ingredients

**For the Cake:**
- 3/4 cup melted coconut oil, cooled
- 1/2 cup plain greek yogurt
- 3 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup canned full fat coconut milk
- 3 cups all purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest
- 1 cup shredded sweetened coconut, plus more for layering

**Honey Lemon Syrup:**
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/3 cup honey

**Whipped Vanilla Buttercream:**
- 3 sticks (1 1/2 cups) salted butter, at room temperature
- 2 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, or vanilla bean powder

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease 3 (6-inch) or 2 (8-inch) round cake pans. Line with parchment paper and spray with cooking spray.
2. In a stand mixer, beat coconut oil, yogurt, eggs, sugar, vanilla, and coconut milk. Add flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt; mix until combined. Fold in lemon zest and shredded coconut.
3. Divide batter among pans and bake 30-35 minutes until tops are set. Cool 5 minutes, then turn out onto cooling racks. Poke 3-5 holes in each cake top.
4. For syrup: Bring lemon juice and honey to a boil over high heat. Boil 1-2 minutes until slightly thickened. Cool to warm, about 10 minutes.
5. For buttercream: Whip butter and cream cheese until light and fluffy (3-4 minutes). Add powdered sugar and honey; beat about 2 more minutes. Add vanilla and combine. Add more powdered sugar if needed.
6. Assemble: Place one cake layer on serving plate, drizzle lightly with syrup, spread 1/3 buttercream, add coconut. Repeat with remaining layers. Refrigerate up to 1 day. Decorate with edible flowers if desired.
