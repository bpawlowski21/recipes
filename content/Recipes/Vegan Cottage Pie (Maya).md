Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post by the same author as the other "Maya" recipes in this vault. "An Irish Classic, pure comfort food but made vegan! I used lentils and oh my... it was so delicious." Prep time: 50 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1 onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 small carrots
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp flour
- 1 can lentils, with the liquid from the can
- A handful frozen green peas
- 1 tsp rosemary and thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 tbsp vegan Worcestershire sauce

**Potato mash**
- 800g potatoes
- ½–1 cup (125–250ml) plant milk
- 2 tbsp vegan butter
- Salt to taste

## Directions

1. Fry the onion, garlic, and carrots in some oil.
2. Stir in the tomato paste and flour.
3. Add the lentils with the liquid from the can, peas, rosemary, thyme, salt, pepper, and vegan Worcestershire sauce. Stir everything together and spread evenly in an oven-proof dish. Turn off the heat.
4. For the potato mash: chop the potatoes and cook until soft. Mash with the plant milk, vegan butter, and salt until creamy.
5. Spread the mash on top of the filling and make a pattern with a fork. Optionally brush with vegan cream (helps browning in the oven).
6. Bake at 200°C/400°F for 30–35 minutes (broil the last 2–3 minutes).
