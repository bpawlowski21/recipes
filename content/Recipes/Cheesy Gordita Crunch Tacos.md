## Ingredients

**Tacos**
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 pound ground chicken, beef, or turkey
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 2-3 teaspoons chipotle chili powder
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1½ teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ¼-½ teaspoon crushed red pepper
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt

**For Assembling**
- 1½ cups shredded Mexican cheese blend
- 2 tablespoons Doritos seasoning (optional, recipe in original post notes)
- 8 mini taco-size whole wheat or gluten-free tortillas
- 8 hard taco shells
- Spicy ranch dressing (homemade recipe in original post notes)
- 2 cups shredded romaine lettuce
- ½ cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- Fresh cilantro, limes, avocado, and hot sauce for serving

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add ground meat and onion; cook until browned (about 5 minutes), breaking up meat as it cooks. Add chili powder, paprika, cumin, garlic powder, red pepper flakes, oregano, and salt. Pour in ¾ cup water, reduce heat to medium, and simmer until sauce thickens around meat (about 10 minutes). Keep warm over low heat.
2. Lay flour tortillas flat on two baking sheets. Divide Mexican cheese blend evenly among tortillas. Sprinkle with Doritos seasoning if desired. Bake 3-5 minutes until cheese melts. Add hard shells to center of each tortilla, pressing one side into cheese. Bake another 2-3 minutes. Fold remaining cheesy side onto hard shell, pressing shell into cheese.
3. Spoon meat evenly into each shell. Top with spicy ranch, lettuce, and cheddar cheese. Serve immediately with desired toppings like avocado, hot sauce, yogurt, cilantro, or salsa.
