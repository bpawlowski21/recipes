## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cups (170g) all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup (28g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 11 tablespoons (5 1/2 oz; 155g) unsalted butter, room temperature, chunked
- 2/3 cup (134g) packed light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup (50g) sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon fleur de sel or 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 5 ounces (142g) best-quality bittersweet chocolate, chopped

## Directions

- Sift together flour, cocoa, and baking soda.
- Using a stand or hand mixer, cream butter and both sugars on medium speed for 3 minutes until soft and homogenous. Add salt and vanilla.
- Pulse in dry ingredients, then beat on low until dough forms moist curds. Fold in chocolate pieces and mix to incorporate.
- Knead dough together if needed. Divide in half and shape into 1 1/2-inch diameter logs.
- Wrap logs in plastic and freeze for at least 2 hours or refrigerate for at least 3 hours.
- Preheat oven to 325°F. Line baking sheets with parchment or silicone mats.
- Slice logs into 1/2-inch rounds using a sharp knife. Arrange on sheets with 2-inch spacing.
- Bake for 12 minutes without opening oven. Transfer to cooling rack until warm or room temperature.
- Repeat with remaining dough.
