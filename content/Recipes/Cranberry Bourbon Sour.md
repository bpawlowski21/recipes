## Ingredients

**Cranberry Syrup:**
- 1/2 cup maple syrup
- 2 cups fresh cranberries
- 1/2 cup water
- Zest of 1 large orange

**Single Serving:**
- 2 ounces bourbon
- 1 ounce fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 cup 100% cranberry juice
- 1-2 dashes orange bitters
- Orange slices, for garnish
- Candied rosemary, for garnish
- Ice

## Directions

**Cranberry Syrup:**
1. Combine water, maple syrup, and cranberries in a medium pot. Bring to a boil over high heat.
2. Boil 5 minutes until cranberries burst. Remove from heat; stir in orange zest. Cool completely. Optionally strain out cranberries for a smoother texture.

**Cocktail:**
1. Add 2-3 tablespoons cranberry syrup to a cocktail shaker.
2. Add bourbon, lemon juice, cranberry juice, and orange bitters. Fill with ice and shake vigorously.
3. Strain into a chilled rocks glass over ice.
4. Garnish with an orange slice and candied rosemary.

**Candied Rosemary Garnish:** Dampen fresh rosemary sprigs with water, coat thoroughly with granulated sugar, and dry on a parchment-lined baking sheet for 1 hour at room temperature. Can be made up to 1 day ahead.
