## Ingredients

**Wrappers**
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
- 1 cup milk
- 4 large eggs
- 1 cup all-purpose flour (can substitute 1/3 to 1/2 with whole wheat)
- Few pinches of salt

**Filling**
- 2 cups farmer's cheese, quark, thick cottage cheese, or ricotta
- 6 tablespoons sour cream, mascarpone, creme fraiche, or softened cream cheese
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar (or more to taste)
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)
- Few gratings fresh lemon zest (optional)
- 2 large egg yolks or 1 large egg (optional)

## Directions

1. **Make wrapper/crêpe batter:** Combine wrapper ingredients in a blender or with an immersion blender until smooth. Cover and refrigerate for one to two hours.
2. **Cook wrappers/crêpes:** Heat a medium skillet over medium-high heat. Brush lightly with butter or oil. Pour 3-4 tablespoons batter into skillet, swirling to coat bottom evenly. Cook undisturbed until edges turn golden or pale. Loosen edges with an offset spatula and flip using a fish spatula. Cook reverse side for 20 seconds. Slide onto a plate to cool. Stack crêpes while hot — they won't stick. Use immediately or refrigerate up to 2 days.
3. **Make filling and fill wrappers:** Mix filling ingredients until smooth. Place 3 tablespoons filling across the center of a crêpe. Fold bottom portion over filling; fold sides inward; fold top to create an egg-roll shape. Refrigerate up to 2 days or freeze between waxed paper for up to 2 months.
4. **Serve the blintz:** Heat butter in a skillet over medium heat. Fry blintz until browned on both sides. Transfer to a plate and serve with sour cream, fruit sauce, or jam.
