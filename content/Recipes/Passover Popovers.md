## Ingredients

- 3 cups (680g) water
- 1 cup (198g) vegetable oil
- 1 to 1¼ teaspoons table salt
- 2 tablespoons (25g) granulated sugar
- 1 cup (145g) cake meal
- 2 cups (248g) matzo meal
- 12 large eggs

## Directions

1. Combine water, oil, salt, and sugar in a saucepan and bring to a boil.
2. Remove from heat. Stir in cake meal, then matzo meal until a very stiff dough forms. Transfer to a mixing bowl and cool to lukewarm (about 1 hour).
3. Preheat oven to 400°F. Grease a muffin tin, popover pan, or mini-popover pan with shortening or cooking spray.
4. Beat eggs gradually into the lukewarm batter using a mixer until smooth. Scrape bowl and beat again until smooth.
5. Fill prepared pan cups about ¾ full. (Recipe yields 28 muffin-size, 15 standard, or 30 mini-popovers; bake in batches as needed.)
6. Bake 10 minutes at 400°F. Reduce heat to 325°F and continue baking: 35 minutes for muffin-pan size, 40 minutes for standard, or 25 minutes for mini-popovers. Popovers should be medium-golden brown with mildly moist interiors.
7. Remove and tilt in pan to cool. Serve warm or at room temperature.
8. Store wrapped at room temperature for one day or freeze for longer storage.
