## Ingredients

**Crust**
- 1 1/4 cups (165 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea or table salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons (6 grams) granulated sugar
- 8 tablespoons (4 ounces or 115 grams) cold unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 1/4 cup (60 grams) plain yogurt or sour cream
- 3 to 4 tablespoons (45 to 60 ml) cold water

**Filling**
- 3 1/2 cups berries, stone fruit, or other fruit, chopped or thinly sliced, or any combination thereof
- Pinch of salt
- Juice of half a lemon or lime (optional)
- 1/4 cup (50 grams) granulated sugar
- 1/2 to 1 1/2 tablespoons tapioca flour/starch

**To Finish**
- 1 egg beaten with 1 teaspoon water (optional)
- Turbinado or coarse sugar for sprinkling
- Softly whipped cream or vanilla ice cream, to serve

## Directions

1. **Make the pastry:** Combine flour, sugar, and salt in a large bowl. Sprinkle butter over the mixture and, using a pastry blender or fingertips, work it into the flour until it resembles small peas. Sprinkle sour cream and 3 tablespoons water over the mixture and stir/mash together; it should form large clumps. Add remaining water if needed. Bring dough together into a single mass, transfer to parchment paper, pat into a flatter packet, wrap tightly, and chill until firm, 1 to 2 hours or up to 4 days (or about 20 minutes in the freezer).
2. **Make the filling:** Combine fruit, salt, citrus juice (if using), sugar, and starch in a medium bowl and set aside.
3. **Assemble:** Heat oven to 400°F. Flatten parchment paper on a large baking sheet. On a floured counter, roll dough into a large round-ish shape, about 14 inches across. Gently transfer to the parchment paper. Spoon fruit filling and juices into the center, leaving a 2- to 3-inch border uncovered. Fold border over fruit, pleating the edge to fit; center remains open. Brush crust with egg wash if desired and sprinkle with coarse sugar.
4. **Bake:** 30 to 35 minutes until golden all over and fruit is bubbling and juicy. Cool for at least 20 minutes on a wire rack. Serve warm or at room temperature.
