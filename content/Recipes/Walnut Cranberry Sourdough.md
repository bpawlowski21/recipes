## Ingredients

### Levain
- 40g medium-protein bread flour or all-purpose flour
- 40g whole rye flour
- 80g water
- 8g ripe sourdough starter

### Main Dough
- 600g medium-protein bread flour or all-purpose flour
- 120g whole wheat flour
- 40g whole rye flour
- 160g walnuts, shelled and toasted
- 112g cranberries, sweetened and dried
- 24g walnut oil
- 15g fine sea salt
- 520g water (for autolyse)
- 80g water (reserved for mixing)
- 168g ripe levain

Yield: 2 loaves (1,800g total dough weight)

## Directions

- **Day One, evening (9:00 p.m.):** combine levain ingredients, mix, cover, rest overnight at ~77°F.
- **Day Two, morning (7:30 a.m.):** preheat oven to 350°F; toast walnuts 10-15 min until fragrant; cool.
- **Autolyse (8:00 a.m.):** mix bread flour, whole wheat flour, and 520g water until no dry bits remain; cover, rest 1 hour.
- **Mix (9:00 a.m.):** add levain and a splash of water to autolyse; mix thoroughly, slap-and-fold 5 min to strengthen. Rest 10 min. Sprinkle salt with another splash of water, mix in. Slowly incorporate walnut oil while folding. Transfer to bulk container, cover.
- **Bulk fermentation (9:30 a.m.-12:30 p.m.):** three sets of stretch and folds over 3 hours; incorporate toasted walnuts and cranberries gradually during the first set (about 1/4 of mix-ins before each fold). Two more gentle fold sets, then rest until bulk complete.
- **Divide and preshape (12:30 p.m.):** turn dough out, divide in half, preshape into loose rounds, rest uncovered 30 min.
- **Shape (1:15 p.m.):** shape into batard or boule; place seam-side up in lined proofing baskets; cover with plastic bag.
- **Cold proof (1:30 p.m. to 9:00 a.m. next day):** refrigerate covered baskets overnight.
- **Bake (9:00 a.m. next day):** preheat oven to 450°F with baking stone/steel. Score dough, bake with steam 20 min, remove steam source, bake 30 more min until internal temp reaches 204°F. Cool on rack 1-2 hours before slicing.
