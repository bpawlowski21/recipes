## Ingredients

- 1 10-ounce package frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
- 1 cup chopped artichokes (Wendy's swap for the printed frozen corn)
- 8 ounces chopped mushrooms (Wendy's addition)
- 6 ounces Cheddar, grated (1 1/2 cups)
- 3 4.5-ounce cans chopped green chilies
- 1 1/2 cups light cream
- kosher salt and black pepper
- 8 6-inch corn tortillas, warmed
- 1/4 small red cabbage, thinly sliced (about 4 cups)
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup pepitas (roasted hulled pumpkin seeds)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice

## Directions

- Heat oven to 400° F. In a medium bowl, mix together the spinach, mushrooms, artichokes, 1 cup of the Cheddar, and 1 1/2 cans of the chilies. In a small bowl, stir together the cream, the remaining 1 1/2 cans of chilies, 3/4 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper.
- Layer the tortillas to fit the dish, put half the spinach mix on top, layer with more tortillas, top with the rest of the spinach mix, top off with tortillas, then cream sauce, then cheese. Cover with foil and bake until bubbling, 15 to 20 minutes. Uncover and bake until golden, 10 to 15 minutes more.
- Meanwhile, in a large bowl, toss the cabbage and scallions with the pepitas, oil, lime juice, and 1/4 teaspoon each salt and pepper. Serve with the enchiladas.

## Notes

- Tip: The enchiladas can be assembled (but not baked) up to 12 hours in advance; refrigerate, covered. Increase the covered cooking time by 5 to 10 minutes.
- Wendy's handwritten adaptation turns this into a layered casserole rather than rolled enchiladas: swaps the printed corn/frozen-corn ingredient for 1 cup chopped artichokes and adds 8 oz chopped mushrooms; increases chilies from 1 can to 3, and swaps heavy cream for light cream. She rewrites the assembly as a layered "lasagna-style" bake — tortillas, spinach mix, tortillas, rest of spinach mix, tortillas, cream sauce, cheese — labeling the dish "Casserole" at the top of the page instead of rolling individual enchiladas.
