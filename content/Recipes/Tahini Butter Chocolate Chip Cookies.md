## Ingredients

- 1 stick (1/2 cup) salted butter, at room temperature
- 1/4 cup dark or light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/3 cup tahini
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup almond flour
- 1/3 cup coconut flour (or additional almond flour)
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 cup semi-sweet or dark chocolate chips or chunks
- Flaky sea salt for sprinkling

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- Beat together butter, brown sugar, honey, and tahini until combined. Beat in egg and vanilla until creamy. Add almond flour, coconut flour, baking soda, cinnamon (if using), and salt. Fold in chocolate chips/chunks.
- Scoop dough into rounded 1-tablespoon balls. Place 2 inches apart on prepared baking sheet.
- Bake 5 minutes. Remove pan and tap baking sheet on counter twice to flatten. Return to oven and bake another 2-3 minutes until edges brown but centers remain doughy. Tap baking sheet once more.
- Cool on baking sheet. As they cool, they'll set properly. Sprinkle with flaky salt. Eat warm or store in an airtight container up to 4 days.
