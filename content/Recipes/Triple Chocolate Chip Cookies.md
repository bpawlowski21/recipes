"These bakery-style cookies are rich, chewy, and loaded with layers of chocolate flavor. Made with nutty browned butter and a blend of dark, semi-sweet, and milk chocolate chips, they have crisp edges, soft centers, and an indulgent, melt-in-your-mouth texture that makes them truly irresistible."

Prep Time: 25 min · Cook Time: 12 min · Inactive Time: 12 hour · Total Time: 12 h & 37 m

## Ingredients

- 1 c browned butter, cooled for 30 min
- 3 tbsp water or coffee
- 1/2 c cane sugar
- 1 c brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 1/2 - 2 3/4 c all purpose flour (Italian flour recommended)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 c dark chocolate chips
- 1 c semi sweet chocolate chips
- 1 c milk chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Browned butter: place 1 cup of butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir every 30 seconds as it melts and begins to foam. Continue cooking until it turns a rich golden brown and releases a nutty aroma, about 5–6 minutes. Remove from heat, pour into a heatproof bowl, and let cool to room temp.
2. Once the browned butter has cooled, add it to a mixing bowl along with water/coffee, cane sugar, and brown sugar. Whisk together until combined.
3. Add the eggs and vanilla. Mix again until combined.
4. Add the remaining dry ingredients and mix until everything is evenly incorporated.
5. Scoop the dough into 1/4-cup rounded balls and place them on a baking sheet.
6. Cover with plastic wrap or a large Tupperware lid.
7. Refrigerate overnight or up to 2 days.
8. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Arrange cookies 2–3 inches apart on a baking sheet.
9. Bake for 12–13 minutes, until cookies are just set but still slightly soft in the center.
10. Let cookies cool for about 20 minutes, then enjoy!

## Notes

- Optional: sprinkle with extra flaky salt after removed from the oven!
