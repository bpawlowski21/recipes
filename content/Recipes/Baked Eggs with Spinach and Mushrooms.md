## Ingredients

- 2 pounds fresh baby spinach or regular spinach leaves
- 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- 3 small garlic cloves, minced
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 pound mushrooms, thinly sliced (creminis recommended)
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 3/4 teaspoon table salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg (optional)
- 12 large eggs
- 6 tablespoons finely grated Parmesan cheese

## Directions

1. Wilt spinach in a large ovenproof skillet with boiling water if dry, or without added water if freshly washed. Cook covered over moderately high heat for about 1-2 minutes until tender. Drain in a colander and cool under cold running water. Squeeze out excess moisture by hand and coarsely chop to yield approximately 2 cups packed cooked spinach.
2. Wipe skillet dry and melt butter over medium-low heat. Sauté onion and garlic until softened (2-3 minutes). Add mushrooms and increase heat to medium-high, stirring until softened and liquid evaporates (about 5 minutes).
3. Stir in cream, salt, pepper, nutmeg if using, and chopped spinach. Bring to a simmer and remove from heat.
4. Make 12 large indentations using two teaspoons pressed together to form tall walls. (Can refrigerate covered for several hours or overnight.)
5. Heat oven to 450°F with rack in upper third. Crack one egg into each well and bake 15-30 minutes until whites are firm but yolks remain runny. Check with a toothpick.
6. Remove from oven, sprinkle with additional salt, pepper, and Parmesan. Serve immediately.
