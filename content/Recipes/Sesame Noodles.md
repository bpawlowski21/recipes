Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post. "Hear me out, peanut noodles are great but sesame noodles are BETTER!" Prep time: 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 75g noodles
- 150g veggies of choice (post used broccoli)

**Protein topping**
- 75g tofu or tempeh
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp soy sauce

**Sauce**
- 1 tbsp tahini
- ½ tbsp agave or maple syrup
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- Salt to taste
- Juice of ½ lime
- ½ tsp curry powder

## Directions

1. Boil the noodles and veggies together in water 5–7 minutes (depends on noodle brand).
2. Crumble the tofu or tempeh in a pan with some oil, add the paprika, cumin, and soy sauce, and toss around 3–4 minutes.
3. Mix the sauce ingredients together.
4. Add the tofu, noodles, and veggies to the sauce, mix, and serve with cilantro if you want.
