## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup (170g) lemon juice, freshly squeezed
- 1 cup (198g) granulated sugar
- 8 tablespoons (113g) unsalted butter, melted
- 2 large eggs, plus 2 large yolks
- Zest of 1 lemon

## Directions

- In a large microwave-safe bowl, vigorously whisk together all ingredients. Contents should fill no more than 1/4 of the bowl; an 8-cup measure works well.
- Microwave in 1-minute increments, stirring after each minute. Once thickening begins, reduce to 30-second intervals. Curd is done when it coats the back of a spoon, starts to mound as you stir, and reaches at least 185°F — about 4 to 8 minutes total.
- Stir once more, then strain through a fine-mesh strainer into a clean bowl to remove zest and any cooked egg pieces.
- Cool briefly, re-whisk, and press plastic wrap against the surface to prevent a skin from forming. Cool at room temperature until barely warm, then refrigerate until set, about 3 hours.
- Keep curd refrigerated for up to 3 weeks; freeze for longer storage.
