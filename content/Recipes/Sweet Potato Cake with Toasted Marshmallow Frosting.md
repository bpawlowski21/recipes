## Ingredients

**Cake**
- 1 1/2 pounds sweet potatoes (about 2 to 3 medium or 2 large)
- 2 cups (250 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1 3/4 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- Two pinches ground cloves
- 1/2 cup (1 stick or 115 grams) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1 cup (190 grams) packed light brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs

**Frosting**
- 3 large egg whites
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cream of tartar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Directions

1. **Roast sweet potatoes:** Set oven to 375°F. Prick potatoes with a fork, place on a baking sheet, and roast 45 minutes to 1 hour, turning occasionally, until soft. Cool completely (may be prepared 3 days ahead).
2. **Make cake:** Preheat oven to 350°F. Line an 8- to 9-inch square pan with parchment, butter the paper and sides. Peel cooled potatoes and pass through a ricer or mash until smooth. Measure 1 1/2 packed cups sweet potato mash. Whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and spices. Beat butter and brown sugar until light and fluffy (2-3 minutes). Add vanilla and eggs, beat until combined. Mix in sweet potato puree, then fold in dry ingredients. Spread in prepared pan and bake 35-40 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool in pan 5 minutes, then invert onto a rack to cool completely.
3. **Make frosting:** Place egg whites, sugar, salt, and cream of tartar in a heatproof bowl over gently simmering water. Whisk 3 minutes until sugar dissolves. Remove from heat and beat with an electric mixer on high speed 4-7 minutes until stiff, glossy peaks form. Add vanilla and mix until combined.
4. **Frost and decorate:** Spread a thin layer of frosting over the cooled cake. Using a large piping tip, pipe marshmallow-like dollops over the base layer. Torch dollops lightly to brown.
5. **Serve:** Cut into squares at room temperature. Cake keeps 2 days at room temperature; refrigerate longer.
