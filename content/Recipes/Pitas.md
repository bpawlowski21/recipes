Saved from Apple Notes ("VEGAN - insta"), an Instagram post by the same author as the other "Maya" recipes in this vault. "I'm eating pitas nearly every single day, so I decided it's time to make them myself... It's honestly so easy yet so delicious and fluffy!" Prep time: 90 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups (500g) flour
- 1¼ cup (300ml) lukewarm water
- 2 tsp instant yeast
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt

## Directions

1. First mix the water, yeast, and sugar.
2. Add the remaining ingredients. Knead 5–8 minutes.
3. Let it rest 1 hour.
4. Shape into 8 balls and let them rest another 15 minutes.
5. Flatten them, then fry on medium heat without oil for a few minutes, flipping every 30 seconds or so.

## Notes

- Can be frozen for up to 3 months.
