## Ingredients

- One batch DIY almond paste, prepared
- 1 cup (198 g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1/2 cup (48 g) almond flour
- 2 large egg whites, lightly beaten
- 1 teaspoon almond extract
- Confectioners' sugar, for dusting

## Directions

- Preheat oven to 325°F. Lightly grease two baking sheets or line with parchment.
- In a stand mixer bowl, blend the almond paste, sugar, salt, and almond flour until uniformly crumbly. Add egg whites one at a time, mixing until smooth. Stir in almond extract.
- Scoop dough by heaping tablespoons onto prepared pans (about 24 cookies total).
- Dust cookies generously with confectioners' sugar.
- Bake 18 to 22 minutes until brown around edges. Cool on pan on a rack.
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 10 days; freeze for longer storage.
