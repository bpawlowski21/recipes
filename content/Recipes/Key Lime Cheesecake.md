## Ingredients

**Crust**
- 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 8 tablespoons butter, melted

**Key Lime Cheesecake**
- 5 blocks full-fat cream cheese (40 ounces), softened to room temperature
- 1½ cups granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- 2 teaspoons lime zest, finely grated
- 5 large eggs plus 3 egg yolks, at room temperature
- ¼ cup heavy cream
- ½ cup key lime juice

## Directions

**Crust**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Spray a 9-inch springform pan with non-stick spray. Wrap the bottom and sides with multiple layers of heavy-duty foil to prevent water infiltration.
2. Mix graham cracker crumbs, sugar, and melted butter; firmly press into pan.
3. Bake for 10 minutes; cool on rack. Reduce oven temperature to 300°F.

**Filling**
4. Beat softened cream cheese until completely smooth.
5. Add sugar, vanilla, and lime zest on medium speed until smooth.
6. On low speed, add eggs and yolks one at a time, avoiding overmixing.
7. Incorporate cream until just combined. Fold in lime juice until evenly distributed.
8. Pour filling into prepared crust and smooth the top.
9. Place springform pan into a large roasting pan; add 2 inches hot water. Bake at 300°F for 1 hour 25 minutes.
10. Turn oven off; leave cheesecake inside for 30 additional minutes with door closed.
11. Remove from oven; run a knife around edges. Cool completely on rack, then cover loosely and refrigerate minimum 6 hours.
