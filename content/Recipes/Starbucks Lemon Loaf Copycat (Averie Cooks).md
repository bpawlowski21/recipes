## Ingredients

**Loaf**
- 3 large eggs
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup (8 ounces) sour cream or Greek yogurt
- ½ cup canola or vegetable oil
- 2 tablespoons lemon zest
- 1 to 2 tablespoons lemon extract (to taste)
- 1½ cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt

**Lemon glaze**
- 1 cup confectioners' sugar
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Prepare a 9x5-inch loaf pan with cooking spray or greasing.
2. Whisk together eggs, sugar, and sour cream until smooth.
3. Drizzle in oil while whisking to incorporate.
4. Add lemon zest and extract; whisk to combine.
5. Stir in flour, baking powder, and salt until just combined; avoid overmixing.
6. Pour batter into prepared pan and smooth the top.
7. Bake 50-52 minutes until the top is domed and a toothpick emerges clean or with moist crumbs. Tent with foil during final 10 minutes if browning too quickly.
8. Cool in pan for at least 30 minutes, then turn onto rack to cool completely.
9. For glaze: whisk confectioners' sugar with lemon juice until smooth.
10. Drizzle glaze over cooled loaf before serving.
