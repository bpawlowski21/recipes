## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- 2 inches fresh ginger, sliced
- 4 bags chamomile tea (or 2 tablespoons loose tea)
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cayenne pepper
- 1-1.5 cups vodka (optional)
- 1 cup pure mango juice
- 1/3 cup fresh lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- Sparkling water, for topping

## Directions

1. Bring water and ginger to a boil. Remove from heat, add tea, cover and steep 5-10 minutes. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a pitcher.
2. Add honey, turmeric, cayenne, vodka (if using), mango juice, lemon juice, and apple cider vinegar to the pitcher. Stir well and adjust sweetness as needed. Chill until ready to serve.
3. Pour over ice and top with sparkling water.

## Notes

- Yields 6 servings (pitcher) or 2 servings (individual). Can be made without vodka as a mocktail; keeps refrigerated up to 1 week.
